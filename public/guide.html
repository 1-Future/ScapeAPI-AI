Guide

What is Scape?

A text-based MMORPG engine inspired by Old School RuneScape. Play by typing commands. Built in Node.js with AI-powered NPCs, a full skill system, combat, quests, and a Grand Exchange.

How to Play

Connect at sc4p3.com/play. Type your name and password to log in (or create an account automatically). Use commands to explore the world, fight monsters, train skills, and talk to NPCs.

The Inferno

69 waves of combat. An AI agent is learning to beat it through reinforcement learning. Watch it train live at sc4p3.com — each episode it gets a little better at prayer switching, eating, and surviving.
