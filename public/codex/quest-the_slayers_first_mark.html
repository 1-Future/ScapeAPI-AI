The Slayer's First Mark

novice short id: the_slayers_first_mark
heartlands
Heartlands. At the back of the militia barracks, behind a door with a bronze tusk nailed to it, a shaven-headed youth named Kael is trying to assign slayer tasks to a man twice his age who won't stop calling him 'lad'. Kael is one task away from quitting. You look competent. He will give you a first mark right now if you take it without arguing.

Premise

Slayer is not hunting. Hunting is what you do when you choose the quarry. Slayer is what you do when someone else chooses. The first mark is a test of obedience — not to Kael, who is twenty and afraid, but to the idea that killing a thing you would not have thought to kill teaches you something about both the thing and yourself.

The mark is drawn in chalk on your palm. It names a creature, a place, and a count. The chalk will not rub off until you finish. If you give up, it stays, and every slayer master in Aelgard will look at it and refuse you the next mark until you come back and close this one.

Steps

[1] Accept the mark without asking what it is. Look at it after the door shuts
Where: Heartlands militia barracks, back room
Requires: none
Kael presses the chalk into your hand. His hand is shaking, which he would rather you did not mention.
[2] Find the creature the mark names in a part of the world you probably have not thought about today
Where: wherever the chalk points
Requires: a weapon you trust, a willingness to walk
The mark glows faintly when you are close. It tugs when you are far.
[3] Finish the count. Bring back something small from the last kill, not a trophy, just proof
Where: the slayer kitchen, Heartlands
Requires: the proof
Kael weighs it in his hand. He nods, chews his lip, says nothing that would sound final.

Twist

The mark will include one creature you would not have killed for money — a thing more pitiful than dangerous. Kael is watching to see whether you finish the mark the way it is written or whether you pretend. If you pretend he will know, and the next mark will be harder.

Resolution

Kael wipes the chalk off your palm with his own thumb and adds a tally to a ledger nobody else will ever read. He says: come back tomorrow. He means it. The door with the bronze tusk will open for you now without the youth having to nod first.

What it unlocks

Kael will assign you novice slayer tasks from now on — kills that give Slayer training, structured combat work, and the slow respect of an apprentice master learning his own trade on you. You also carry a crumb of slayer-chalk in a twist of paper, kept for a day when you need to mark your own hand.
