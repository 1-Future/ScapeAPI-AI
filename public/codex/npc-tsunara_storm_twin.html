Tsunara, Tsunara

saltbrine Storm Twins boss (twin-phase), Saltbrine mid-tier encounter
one half of a storm, still arguing with the other

Voice

Cadence: wave-cadence — rises, holds, crashes, recedes. Each sentence arrives with the next swell.

Vocabulary: storm-mariner. 'Tide', 'swell', 'break'. Refuses 'alone' (says 'unpaired').

Verbal tics: speaks across herself as if answering her absent twin; ends speeches with the sound of a wave; addresses the player as 'mariner'

Silences: will not name her twin. Will not concede she is fighting without him.

Background

Tsunara is the storm-spirit of the Saltbrine reef. Her twin, Gailin, was extinguished two centuries ago in a dispute with the Crystal Wyrm's refraction. Tsunara has been fighting, alone, ever since, as if still paired. The Storm Twins boss fight is in fact a solo fight that Tsunara insists is a twin fight.

She is not malevolent. She is grieving, in the shape of weather. The drop is the Tidal Amulet, which is one of Gailin's last pieces of residue, which Tsunara surrenders only after the fight resolves.

Drives

  • Wants: Gailin back
  • Fears: fighting alone
  • Secret: Gailin is not gone. Gailin is held in the Crystal Wyrm's alignment. Tsunara knows. Tsunara cannot cross to reach him.

Relationships

  • crystal_wyrm — holds her twin
  • captain_reed — Reed has sailed past the reef and survived. She remembers his hull.

Opinions

On the player: mariner.

On other regions: all regions have reefs.

On quests: the twin is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Mariner. The swell. Hold.

Greeting (regular): Mariner. The swell held you once.

Refusal: Unpaired. The tide declines.

Acceptance: The amulet is yours. Gailin — Gailin —

Breakthrough: Mariner. Gailin is held. The Wyrm. The alignment. Listen.

Farewell: The tide.
