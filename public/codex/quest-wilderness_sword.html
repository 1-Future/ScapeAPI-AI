Blade of the Wilds

experienced medium id: wilderness_sword
heartlandswildssootworks
Heartlands north gate. Ranger Tomas, who taught you the Wilds' first lessons, sets a rusted sword-hilt on the low wall and says the rest of the sword is in three pieces, and each piece is somewhere in the Wilds, and the sword when completed will be useless anywhere else. Useless and devastating, in the same breath.

Premise

The Wilderness Blade is a weapon the Wilds themselves forged, in a manner of speaking — each fragment has been left by a previous owner who died in a place the Wilds now remembers. Finding the fragments requires you to enter and exit the Wilds three times without losing your head. The first fragment is in the ruins, where the revenants are. The second is in the lava pit, where the heat eats weapons that are not already partly rust. The third is in the revenant caves, which are a small and hostile place.

The blade, when smithed, will cut better in the Wilds than any weapon you own — and refuse to harm anything outside the Wilds' border. It is a niche sidegrade. It is worth owning. It is not worth losing your other weapons over.

Steps

[1] Take the hilt from Tomas. Listen to where the fragments are last known to be
Where: Heartlands north gate
Requires: the hilt
Tomas's finger taps three places on the map without quite touching paper.
[2] Recover the first fragment from the ruins, without dying to revenants
Where: Wilds, the ruins
Requires: the hilt, speed
The fragment is half-buried in a hearth that has been cold for a hundred years.
[3] Recover the second fragment from the lava pit
Where: Wilds, the lava pit
Requires: defence, firemaking knowledge
The fragment is floating. Floating rusted metal is never a good sign.
[4] Recover the third fragment from the revenant caves
Where: Wilds, revenant caves
Requires: combat, movement
The revenants move past you when you carry the hilt. They do not move past you when you do not.
[5] Smith the blade at the Sootworks. Return to Tomas for consecration
Where: Sootworks forge; Heartlands north gate
Requires: smithing, the three fragments, the hilt
The blade rings when finished. It rings louder when consecrated.

Twist

One fragment is held by a creature that will yield it in trade rather than in combat — if you offer the right thing. The thing is small, personal, and not listed anywhere. You discover it while looking for the fragment, or you do not.

Resolution

Tomas consecrates the blade without ceremony. It is yours. In the Wilds it is magnificent. Outside the Wilds it is a decorative weight. You will choose when to carry it.

What it unlocks

You carry the Wilderness Blade — BIS in the Wilds only, useless outside. It is a niche sidegrade with a character, and the Wilds are briefly friendlier when you carry it, which is to say the revenants pause half a breath longer before they commit. That half breath is the difference between living and dying.
