The Forge of Four Fires

experienced epic id: the_forge_of_four_fires
sootworksboneyardmoryskahheartlands
Sootworks. Retired Forgemaster Durrak sits in a low chair in a forge that is too hot for a man his age, and he tells you the old smiths used four kinds of fire to temper the best blades. Lava. Desert sun. Hellfire. Hearth. If you learn all four, he says, you can make dragon-tier weapons anywhere.

Premise

The Four Fires technique requires one bar of steel, tempered in four different fires in sequence. Each fire is in a different region — Sootworks lava forge, Boneyard sun-stone at high noon, Moryskah hellfire brazier at the Slayer Tower, Heartlands hearth fire. Each temper changes the bar's character. By the fourth temper, the bar is a quad-tempered ingot that can be smithed into a dragon-tier weapon at any anvil.

You will need strength to endure the Sootworks heat, firemaking to light the hellfire, and a willingness to burn your hands. Durrak will not carry you. He will, however, drink your health when you return.

Steps

[1] Hear Durrak out. Forge a raw steel bar in the Sootworks magma forge
Where: Sootworks
Requires: smithing, strength
The magma speaks. Durrak translates.
[2] Travel to the Boneyard Wastes. Temper the bar on the sun-stone at high noon
Where: Boneyard sun-stone
Requires: smithing, heat tolerance
The sun is direct. The bar glows pink.
[3] Travel to Moryskah. Light hellfire in the cursed brazier at the Slayer Tower. Hold the bar in it
Where: Moryskah Slayer Tower
Requires: firemaking, endurance
Your hands burn. The bar takes the temper.
[4] Return to the Heartlands. Complete the final temper at a simple hearth fire
Where: Heartlands hearth
Requires: firemaking
The hearth fire is familiar. The bar sighs into it.
[5] Smith the quad-tempered ingot into a dragon-tier weapon of your choice
Where: any anvil
Requires: smithing
The weapon finishes itself under your hammer.

Twist

The hellfire temper will mark your hands with a burn that does not fully heal. It is cosmetic, but visible. Durrak did not tell you. He knew.

Resolution

Durrak toasts you. The weapon cuts through steel like parchment. You can forge dragon-tier weapons at any anvil now. Your hands have a scar you will always notice.

What it unlocks

You carry a quad-tempered dragon longsword (or weapon of your choice from your first forge). You can smith dragon-tier weapons at any anvil, which is a significant mid-game unlock. Your smithing XP at high tiers is better. Your hands have a mark.
