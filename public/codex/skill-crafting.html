Crafting

32 training methods. 6 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 10 Spinning flax into bowstrings. Your first cross-skill connection. Crafting feeds Fletching.
    unlocks: Spin flax into bowstrings
  • level 20 Cut sapphires. Gem cutting begins. Uncut gems from mining become cut gems for jewelry.
    unlocks: Cut sapphires
  • level 43 Diamond jewelry. The first serious crafting money. Diamond rings, necklaces, amulets.
    unlocks: Diamond ring and other diamond jewelry
  • level 63 Green dragonhide bodies. The profitable crafting method. Buy hides, craft bodies, alch or sell.
    unlocks: Craft green dragonhide bodies
  • level 80 Inner Sanctum crystal armour crafting. Endgame armour path — crystal shards meet court-cloth meet moonsong. Veilwood's crafting peak.
    unlocks: Crystal armour crafting
  • level 84 Dragonstone amulets and fury amulets. The best combat amulet you can make.
    unlocks: Craft amulet of fury (onyx + gold bar)
