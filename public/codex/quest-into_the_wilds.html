Into the Wilds

intermediate medium id: into_the_wilds
heartlandswilds
Heartlands, the north gate. Ranger Tomas wears a coat that has been patched in three colours and does not match his hair. He is handing out warnings to anyone who looks like they might walk north, and he is not stopping anyone. He will, however, talk to you about the Wilds, and about the one supply cache that nobody has come back for in two months.

Premise

The Wilds are lawless because the laws do not survive being written down there. Ranger Tomas teaches the survival the way it is taught in the Wilds themselves — short, cold, and with a willingness to walk away from anything that is not moving in the direction you need. The supply cache was left by a forward party that did not come back. The party is dead. The cache is not.

You will walk in, pick up the cache, and walk out. Everything else about the Wilds — the player-against-player rule, the predator density, the absolute indifference of the land to your name — you will either learn on the way or you will not come back. Tomas is not your friend and does not pretend to be. He will, however, teach you the one thing that keeps rangers alive: the trick of not looking at what is looking at you.

Steps

[1] Hear Tomas out. Do not ask questions that Tomas will not answer
Where: Heartlands north gate
Requires: a calm face
He does not sit. He paces. He never finishes a sentence in one breath.
[2] Walk to the abandoned ruins. Do not run. Running in the Wilds means something to the things that watch
Where: Wilds, the abandoned ruins
Requires: walking boots, a short sword, resolve
The sky here is a flat grey. It does not change colour when the sun moves.
[3] Recover the supply cache from wherever the party hid it when they realised they were not going to come back
Where: Wilds, the ruins
Requires: a sharp eye
The cache is behind a fallen beam, under a folded cloak, still folded, as if someone meant to come back for both.
[4] Escape back to the border without being seen more than once. The agility shortcut exists if you find it
Where: Wilds, south
Requires: a map you mostly remember, hands ready
You will be seen once. Only once is survivable.

Twist

The cache contains a sealed envelope addressed to Tomas by name, in a hand he recognises. He will not open it in front of you. He will, however, give you the survival-kit he had been saving for the previous party.

Resolution

Tomas does not thank you. He nods. You have walked in and walked out, and you have learned how to stand next to the Wilds without leaning in. The border gate is your door now; he will let you through without the warning.

What it unlocks

The Wilderness Agility Course is open to you, and it is the best agility training-per-hour in Aelgard — if you can stand being in a PvP zone while you use it. You carry a Ranger's Survival Kit: a one-click teleport straight to the Wilds border, usable once, which has saved better rangers than you.

Dialogue

Ranger Tomas
Do not look at the things that are watching you. If you see them, they are now real to you. If they are real to you, they can reach you. Eyes down, walk past.
Ranger Tomas
The envelope is my problem. Your problem is whether you come back tomorrow.
