Prestige Goals

Prestige Goal: The Kraken Hunt

Assemble a crew, charter a ship, and hunt the Deep Kraken in its lair. BIS fishing and combat gear required.

Required skills: fishing 80 attack 70 ranged 75 prayer 60

Bosses: deep_kraken_prime

Unique rewards: kraken_tentacle_whip, sailors_necklace

Sub-areas

  • Saltbrine Reach — Pirate coast. Ships, smugglers, deep-sea fishing. Water travel hub. [requires: fishing 20, cooking 15, agility 10, quest: pirate_king]
  • Saltbrine Deep Waters — High-level fishing. Anglerfish, dark crabs. Accessed by charter ship. [requires: fishing 62, cooking 45, quest: the_anglers_challenge]
  • Smuggler's Cove — Black market shop. Higher sell prices but requires Thieving to access. [requires: thieving 40, quest: the_counterfeit_ring]

Quirky Interactions (4)

Ambient world objects that grant tiny XP. Discoverable, flavorful, mathematically terrible — but sometimes a lifeline.
  • Climb the Rigging [agility] — 1.5 xp/click. "You climb like a landlubber," the captain says, smirking.
  • Practice Knot Tying [crafting] — 0.5 xp/click. "Thats a granny knot, not a bowline. Try again."
  • Clean Pearls for the Jeweller [crafting] — 0.7 xp/click. She watches your technique. "Not bad. Keep at it."
  • Stare Down the Seagulls [hunter] — 0.2 xp/click. This is somehow the most flavorful content in Aelgard.
