Lunar Diplomacy

master long id: lunar_diplomacy
inkweald
Inkweald. Lucid Keeper Yara, who you have met before, is leaning over a bowl of moon-lit water and tracing circles that do not close. She says a word you do not know, and then she says it again, and then she asks if you would like to visit a place that is not usually a place. The place is called the Lunar Plane. The visit requires sleep.

Premise

The Lunar spellbook is not foreign magic. It is domestic magic, of the quietest sort. Heal a friend, cure a poison, call a distant NPC, condense humid air into a tool you forgot to bring. The Lunar elders live on a plane reached only through deep sleep, and they are not enthusiastic about visitors. They are, however, contract-minded. If you come to them on a business footing and complete the trials they have written down (literally written, which is rare, because they usually leave things implied), they will teach the spellbook.

The trials are not combative. They are demonstrations. Mine a lunar rock, which you will find is as much about listening as swinging. Light a sacred flame, which will go out if you take yourself too seriously. Craft a staff from dreamwood and crystal, which requires the staff's permission. You can brute-force none of it. Yara will teach you how to brew the dream-potion that gets you there, but after that the Lunar Isle is yours to prove yourself to.

Steps

[1] Brew the dream potion under Yara's eye. Do not substitute ingredients
Where: Inkweald, the Keeper's fire
Requires: Lucid essence, Moonpetals, herblore
The potion smells of a distant room. When it is ready, Yara nods once.
[2] Sleep. Reach the Lunar Plane. Introduce yourself politely to whoever is there
Where: Lunar Plane
Requires: the potion, sleep
The Plane is a long white beach and three low buildings. The tide is the wrong direction.
[3] Mine a lunar rock. Do not strike it. Ask it for a piece, and wait
Where: Lunar Plane, the rockfall
Requires: a pick held correctly
The rock gives up a chip when it is ready. You will not know it is ready until it is.
[4] Light the sacred flame. Do not use a tinderbox first. Use breath
Where: Lunar Plane, the hearth
Requires: the chip, breath
The flame catches on the third try. The first two tries are allowed only because everyone's first tries are.
[5] Craft the lunar staff. Ask before shaping. Thank after
Where: Lunar Plane, the long hall
Requires: dreamwood, crystal, crafting
The staff vibrates when you are doing it right. It goes still when you are doing it wrong.
[6] Convince the Lunar elders to teach you. Speak from below, not across
Where: Lunar Plane, the elder hall
Requires: a reason that is not about power
They already know. They want to hear you say it.
[7] Wake up. Bring the spellbook back. Tell Yara what you saw, and what you said
Where: Inkweald, the Keeper's fire
Requires: the spellbook
Yara's circles close for the first time while you tell her.

Twist

The Lunar elders will offer you two spells as a parting gift — one that is listed in the spellbook, one that is not. The unlisted spell has a cost the book does not name. You will have to decide, in the moment, whether to accept both. The correct answer differs by player.

Resolution

The spellbook is yours. The Plane remains accessible through sleep, not easily, but reliably. Yara returns to her bowl of moon-lit water and goes on tracing circles that close now. You have a new kind of quiet to draw on.

What it unlocks

The Lunar spellbook is yours — cure, vengeance, heal group, NPC contact, humidify, and the others, the quiet spells, the ones that save a friend's life at fifteen paces. The Lunar Plane is accessible through sleep and ritual, and the Plane has a runecrafting altar nobody in Aelgard has published directions to.

Dialogue

Lucid Keeper Yara
The Lunar people will not fight you. That is not a reassurance. It means that if they refuse you, there is no fight to win. Walk softly.
Lunar elder
We teach because you asked from below. Those who ask from above do not visit twice.
