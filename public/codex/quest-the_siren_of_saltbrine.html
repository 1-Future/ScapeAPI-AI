The Siren of Saltbrine

master long id: the_siren_of_saltbrine
saltbrine
Saltbrine harbour. Ships have been vanishing off the coast for three months. Sailors who survived speak of a voice that told them to sail into the rocks, and say the voice was not unkind. Harbourmaster Quinn has a dossier and a list of widows, and he would like the voice to stop, and he would like the voice, if possible, to be heard before it is silenced.

Premise

The Siren is real, ancient, and cursed. She was bound to the coast in an older war to lure enemy fleets to their deaths, and the war has been over for a thousand years. Nobody told her. She has been doing her job. The cure for her is not a blade; it is a name spoken back to her correctly, which nobody alive knows.

You will craft enchanted earplugs of wax and dream-coral to resist her song, sail out at night, lure her minions with fish, and fight them in three phases. Her final phase has her in human form on the deck, asking questions instead of singing. Two choices: break the curse (she becomes an ally NPC, offering coastal knowledge) or banish her permanently (she is gone, the coast is safer, the song stops forever).

Steps

[1] Speak with Harbourmaster Quinn. Craft enchanted earplugs from wax and dream-coral
Where: Saltbrine harbour; craft workshop
Requires: crafting, dream-coral
The earplugs hum in your pocket. They are ready to refuse.
[2] Sail out at night. Fish to lure the Deepwater Thralls
Where: coast at night
Requires: fishing
The fish jump in nets that are not theirs.
[3] Fight off three waves of Deepwater Thralls on the ship deck
Where: ship deck
Requires: combat
The thralls are not trying to kill you. They are trying to draw you into the water.
[4] Phase 1: counter the Siren's sonic blasts with magic barriers
Where: deck
Requires: magic
The barriers ring like struck bells.
[5] Phase 2: shoot her with enchanted bolts when she surfaces from the whirlpool
Where: deck
Requires: ranged
She surfaces briefly, three times. The third time is your shot.
[6] Phase 3: she takes human form and fights on deck. Pure ranged combat
Where: deck
Requires: ranged
She is smaller than she sang. Her eyes are tired.
[7] Defeat her. Decide: break the curse, or banish her permanently
Where: deck
Requires: a decision
She will not beg. She will answer if asked.

Twist

Breaking the curse requires speaking her real name. It is not written in any dossier. A minor sailor at Saltbrine — a woman who has been coming to the harbour every day for thirty years — knows it. She will tell you if you ask properly, and she will not tell you if you ask the wrong way.

Resolution

The Siren is an ally or she is gone. Quinn thanks you either way. You carry a conch that still sings faintly when held to the ear. The coast is quieter. The widows' list is closed.

What it unlocks

You carry the Siren's conch, which lets you call fair weather on a coastal voyage once per day. If you broke the curse, the Siren is an NPC at the harbour who sells you navigational charts and deep-sea fishing tips. If you banished her, the harbour carries a small permanent blessing against storms.
