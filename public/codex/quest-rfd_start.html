Recipe for Disaster — Prologue

intermediate short id: rfd_start
heartlands
Drifting Market, inaugural feast. The guests have taken their seats, the bread is rising, and a wave of cold runs through the deck. When the wave passes, every guest at the high table is frozen mid-gesture, perfectly silent, eyes open. The Culinaromancer — a chef trapped between dimensions — is laughing in a kitchen nobody can find.

Premise

Merchant Hilde's inaugural feast has been cursed by a chef she once dismissed. Each guest has been frozen in time, awaiting a specific dish. To free them, you must cook the correct meal for each — a Heartlands meat pie for the Captain, a sanctified stew for the Priest, a clockwork cake for the Engineer, and progressively stranger meals for the other guests. The Culinaromancer wants the feast to go on forever.

The prologue is a test of whether you can identify the problem. You will attend the feast, watch the curse strike, speak with the Culinaromancer through a dimensional gap, and learn his rules.

Steps

[1] Attend the feast at the Drifting Market. Witness the curse strike
Where: Drifting Market feast hall
Requires: presence
The cold runs the length of the table. The guests freeze mid-sentence.
[2] Speak with the Culinaromancer through the dimensional gap
Where: feast hall, beside the frozen guests
Requires: patience
His voice comes from nowhere. It is warm, which is worse.
[3] Learn each guest requires a specific dish to be freed
Where: feast hall
Requires: listening
The Culinaromancer names the dishes like a sommelier names wines.

Twist

The Culinaromancer will offer you an alternative: cook for him instead, and he releases the guests immediately. Accepting ties you to his kitchen for a future quest chain that is harder than the standard path. Refusing proceeds normally.

Resolution

You leave the feast with a cook's apron and a list. The curse will not lift for anyone else — Hilde has tried. You are committed.

What it unlocks

The Recipe for Disaster chain opens to you — five more quests, each freeing a different guest, culminating in a boss fight against the Culinaromancer himself. The Barrows Gloves are the chain's final reward, and the chain is Aelgard's longest.
