The Inkweald Second Door

master long id: the_inkweald_second_door
inkweald
A door, there was, once; remembered, it was, by you, opening. Sleeping behind the first, a second door is, and waited, it has. Remembered, you have been, in turn, by what you did not see the first time you opened the first door, which was watching, and which, waiting, has been, for you to come back.

Premise

The Inkweald grammar is a dream-blur; specific as a hand and vague as a word. The first door led you into the Inkweald. The second door was behind it, sleeping, and will wake only for someone who has already walked through once. Only a few per decade do. You will need to return to the first door, sleep on its threshold, see the second door reveal itself, pass through it without waking the sleeper who guards it, and retrieve whatever the second door offers — an item, a passage, a piece of knowledge, which takes the form of a small living thing that must be carried home without being looked at, and named on arrival.

The Lucid keepers, who tend the first door, cannot tend the second. They do not know how. They will offer you their three dream-fragments, kept in vaults nobody has opened in several generations, and a dream-net woven from moonlit spidersilk — both of which you will need. The sleeper who guards the second door is neither hostile nor friendly; it is a sleeper, and the only rule is that it must remain asleep.

Steps

[1] Returned to the first door, you must be; sleep on its threshold, you must
Where: Inkweald, the first door clearing
Requires: none
The threshold is warm. The dream comes quickly.
[2] The second door, appearing, will be; pass it, you must, without waking the sleeper
Where: behind the first door
Requires: a light tread, agility 60
The sleeper is enormous. Its breath is warm on your shin.
[3] From the Lucid keepers' vaults, three dream-fragments gather
Where: three Lucid vaults
Requires: magic 75, thieving 40
The vaults are dusty. The fragments are not.
[4] A dream-net from moonlit spidersilk, weave — the spidersilk must be collected at moon-rise
Where: Inkweald canopy
Requires: runecrafting 55, herblore 65
The silk is cold. The weaving warms it.
[5] Beyond the second door, catch what crawls out at the moment it opens
Where: the second door threshold
Requires: agility 60, the dream-net
It crawls. It chirps. It is small. Do not look at it.
[6] Home, carry it; without looking at it, carry it. Named on arrival, it must be
Where: wherever you call home
Requires: a covered basket
Whatever name comes to mind on arrival is correct. Do not overthink it.

Twist

The small living thing you carry home is not a reward; it is a sigil that must be placed at the threshold of your own home, wherever that is, so that the second door can learn the way back to you. Once it has learned, you may cross from your own threshold to the second door at will, but the sigil will not move from your home. Choose your home carefully. Many players pick poorly and then regret it.

Resolution

The sigil sits at your threshold. The Lucid spellbook, deeper than Dream Magic, is now available to you — Borrow Dream, Rewind Minute, Shared Eyes, among others. The Second Door Chambers are yours to explore: seven layered dream-rooms, each with a unique puzzle, each accessible only by passing the sleeper, which remains asleep as long as you are discreet.

What it unlocks

The Lucid spellbook adds spells that are more utility than damage — Borrow Dream lets you replay another player's recent action once per day, Rewind Minute undoes the last sixty seconds of your own actions (combat excepted), Shared Eyes lets you see through a party member's eyes for thirty seconds. The Dreamer's Sigil, equipped, grants the Lucid spellbook outside Inkweald. The Second Door Chambers are seven dream-rooms with puzzles that reward unique lucid-runes and a private dream-bank.
