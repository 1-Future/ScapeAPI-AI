Admiral's Last Voyage

grandmaster epic id: admirals_last_voyage
saltbrine
You are a pirate king now, and the coastline is yours to protect, and the Kraken — the actual Kraken, not one of her children or her cousins or the thing Saltbrine fishermen call a Kraken when they are drunk — has been seen off the deep grounds. Your council of captains will not raise the question themselves, because they are a little afraid of you, but they are a little more afraid of the Kraken, so you can see it in the way they are not raising the question. You will have to.

Premise

The Kraken is a tier-five boss encounter that takes an entire fleet to pull off. You will convene the council at the Throne Rocks, charter every ship flagged under the Broken Crown (six vessels, each named), sail in formation to the deep grounds, and conduct the Kraken hunt in three phases: a harpoon strike at five hundred fathoms, a boarding action on the surface tentacle before it drags a ship under, and a finish in the open water, captain to captain. This is a fleet engagement; you will coordinate fire, issue orders to named captains, and survive the death of at least one crew in at least one simulation of the fight — the Kraken does not lose cleanly.

The reward is not just the Kraken; it is what Saltbrine becomes afterwards. A month's meat for the town; a repeatable boss for you; the Kraken Tentacle Whip, which is BIS one-handed slash until the Wyrm tier. It is also the last voyage you are expected to lead as admiral — from here on, you are a king in port, and the open sea is for your captains. The council will toast you ashore.

Steps

[1] Summon the council of captains at the Throne Rocks and convince them you are serious
Where: Throne Rocks council chamber
Requires: the crown, a plain voice
Drex speaks first. He is in favour. The others follow — reluctantly, then fiercely.
[2] Charter every ship flagged under the Broken Crown — six vessels, each by name
Where: Saltbrine wharves
Requires: the charter, gold
The six names are carved on your crown's inner band. Read them aloud to hire them.
[3] Sail to the deep grounds in formation — the Kraken will know you are coming
Where: the deep grounds
Requires: calm winds, a lot of rope
The sea darkens half a furlong before the Kraken appears.
[4] First strike: harpoon the Kraken at five hundred fathoms
Where: deep grounds, surface
Requires: fishing 85, ranged 80, the great harpoon
The harpoon is the size of a spar. The rope is longer than you have ever hauled.
[5] Second strike: board the surface tentacle before it drags a ship under
Where: a flagship deck, mid-combat
Requires: attack 80, a long axe
The tentacle is thicker than the mainmast. Cut the right knot or the ship goes under with your captain.
[6] Third strike: finish the Kraken in the open water, captain to captain
Where: open water
Requires: prayer 65, hitpoints 85, everything you have
The Kraken will come up to die. You will go down to meet her.
[7] Tow the body home. Feed Saltbrine for a month. Drink on the Broken Crown until you cannot stand
Where: Saltbrine harbour, then the Broken Crown
Requires: none
The town turns out. The old pirate king pours. You drink, and you sit, and you are king in port now.

Twist

The Kraken is one of the Siren's descendants — the largest, oldest, and proudest — and has been waiting for a worthy admiral to come out. She does not resist the third strike. She meets it. When she dies, the sea itself exhales, and Saltbrine's fishing grounds are replenished for the first time in a generation. The council toasts you, and Drex toasts Yula's empty glass, and the old pirate king sits down next to you and says, very quietly, that his own last voyage was not nearly this well done.

Resolution

Saltbrine is fed for a month on kraken-meat. The Throne Rocks get a new statue — not of you, because that would be gauche, but of the great harpoon. You gain the Kraken Tentacle Whip, a BIS one-handed slash weapon you will use until the wyrm tier; the repeatable Kraken fight, for tentacle drops; and an AFK-capable high-level fishing method on the deep grounds. The Broken Crown retires to harbour use only. You are king in port, now, and the open sea is for your captains.

What it unlocks

The Kraken Tentacle Whip is a BIS slash weapon in the one-handed slot; your repeatable Kraken runs refresh its charges. The Deep Grounds fishing method is AFK-capable at level 85+ and occasionally rolls rare kraken-meat. Your Admiral's title sits above Pirate King on documents, though you will rarely use it; the only time it comes up is the annual council reunion at the Throne Rocks, which is a roleplay anchor for Saltbrine year-round.
