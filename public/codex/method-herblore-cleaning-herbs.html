Breakpoints in Range

  • level 3 Attack potions. Your first potion. Herblore stops being a mystery.
  • level 38 Prayer potions. The most important potion in the game. Sustains protection prayers. Changes ALL bossing.
  • level 45 Super attack potions. 5 + 15% attack boost. The super potion series begins.
  • level 63 Super restores. Restores prayer AND all stats. The premier sustain potion for bossing.
  • level 69 Antifire potions. Required to fight dragons. Without this, dragonfire kills you. Whole boss category unlocked.
  • level 81 Saradomin brews. Heals 16 per dose. The endgame healing potion paired with super restores.
  • level 90 Overloads (if raids herblore). The ultimate combat potion. Boosts all combat stats massively.
  • level 92 Prism elixir distills. +15% spell damage for five minutes. The BiS magic consumable in Aelgard.
