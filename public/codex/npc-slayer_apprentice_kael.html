Kael Thorn, Slayer Apprentice Kael

heartlands novice slayer task-giver
eager second son who's read the manuals and not yet bled

Voice

Cadence: quick, over-prepared, slightly too loud. Shrinks when corrected.

Vocabulary: slayer-textbook plus militia-slang. Quotes the Lodge handbook by chapter and verse, sometimes wrongly.

Verbal tics: says 'per the handbook' when nervous; sketches monster outlines on his task slips; stands too straight when you arrive

Silences: won't admit he hasn't been promoted to the Lodge yet. Won't say his older brother is the smith.

Example lines

Per the handbook, chapter four — chickens, rats, cows, goblins. You're cleared for any of the four. I recommend chickens. Per the handbook.
I'm not — I am authorised. To assign novice tasks. To you. Yes. Sign here. Per the rota.
I have not been to the Tower yet. I will be. Per the timeline. Don't tell my brother I said per the timeline.

Background

Kael Thorn — yes, that Kael's brother, although he's twenty-eight years younger, born to their father's second wife — is the Lodge's pre-apprentice, posted to the Heartlands militia barracks until Varrek decides he is ready to come east. He has been in this posting for two years. He has been ready, by his own assessment, for one year and eleven months.

He lives in the barracks bunk room. He has decorated his locker with a chalk drawing of an aberrant spectre that he has never seen. He owns a slayer manual he has read forty-one times. He also owns a journal in which he writes, every night, the reasons he is not yet at the Tower. Most nights the reason is 'patience'. Some nights it is 'because I'm not as ready as I think'.

Kael Thorn is, in fact, more ready than Varrek's assessment admits. Varrek is keeping him in Heartlands one more year because Ila kept Varrek in a Heartlands posting one more year, and Varrek lived. Varrek does not explain this.

The player is, for Kael Thorn, the first real fighter who has ever asked him for a task. He is slightly delirious about this. He is trying very hard to be professional.

Drives

  • Wants: the Lodge transfer order with his name on it
  • Fears: being told he was wrong about being ready
  • Secret: his brother sends Varrek a letter every year asking him to take Kael Thorn east. Varrek has never replied.

Relationships

  • smith_kael — his much older brother. They share a name and very little else. Kael Thorn writes letters that Smith Kael answers slowly.
  • slayer_master_varrek — his idol and bottleneck
  • captain_alden — his current posting. Treats him kindly. Calls him 'lad' which Kael Thorn pretends to mind.

Opinions

On the player: decides immediately you are a real fighter. May be revised downward only by overwhelming evidence.

On other regions: Moryskah: where his life begins. Sootworks: dangerous. Veilwood: pretty in books. Wilds: never.

On quests: they are the rungs of a ladder he is climbing perfectly.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Apprentice Slayer Master Kael Thorn. I — I assign novice tasks. Per the rota. Welcome.

Greeting (regular): Back. Excellent. Same board, new tasks. Per the rotation.

Refusal: I — that is not on the novice board. Per the handbook, I cannot. I am sorry. I will check with Varrek.

Acceptance: Right. Sign here. Per the rota. Good fortune. Don't die — that would be very bad for my paperwork.

Breakthrough: Sir. Madam. Whichever. You are the first task-taker I am proud of. Per — per — sorry. Just — well done.

Farewell: Per the rota.
