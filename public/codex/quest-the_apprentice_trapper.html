The Apprentice Trapper

novice short id: the_apprentice_trapper
veilwood
Veilwood. The canopy above your head is so thick the sun only reaches the floor as coin-sized pools. Someone has been climbing up there, and they have been leaving traps that go off in daylight and catch birds instead of chinchompas. A furious elf called Sylven Brightknot is confiscating any trap she finds and she would like a word with you about the word 'permit'.

Premise

The Veilwood canopy is not legally a place. It is a negotiated understanding between the trees and the people allowed to climb them. Outsiders who set traps up there catch things they should not, and disturb things that had agreed not to be disturbed. Sylven is not angry because you broke a law. She is angry because you nearly broke a promise that predates your grandparents.

To set traps in the canopy you need a permit, which is not a paper. It is a length of spidersilk tied round the wrist, and the spider it came from has to remember you tying it. Sylven will take you to meet the spider. The spider is the size of a cart. You will be fine, probably, depending on what you have in your pockets.

Steps

[1] Surrender every trap you have set and listen to Sylven tell you what you nearly killed
Where: Veilwood canopy, the ropewalk landing
Requires: none
She pulls a sparrow from a bent wire and strokes its head until it stops trembling.
[2] Bring the old spider something it will accept from a stranger
Where: Veilwood canopy weaver-glade
Requires: a pelt from a creature you hunted and ate yourself, a comb carved from bone, a word you do not often say out loud
The spider does not speak. It regards. There is a difference.
[3] Set your first legal trap where Sylven points, and wait for whatever comes
Where: Veilwood canopy, the moonhawk run
Requires: spidersilk wristband, a box trap of the right shape
A moonhawk lands on your wrist like a held breath.

Twist

The spider accepts two of your three gifts but demands a fourth: the next thing you catch, whatever it is, belongs to her, and you must carry it home uneaten. If you try to cheat by catching something small, she knows, and the band tightens.

Resolution

You bring her a chinchompa. She eats it in a way you will dream about for weeks. The spider nods once. Sylven pretends not to have seen. You are now among the few outsiders who may set traps above the moss line.

What it unlocks

A thread of spidersilk sits tight around your wrist, and it has a memory. In the Veilwood canopy, traps you set now hold. Rare chinchompas, moonhawks with feathers like split mirrors, and the slow bright-eyed lemur nobody has named yet — they are yours to catch, within reason, and the permit does not come off except by the weaver's own legs.
