The Tide Pool Collector

novice short id: the_tide_pool_collector
saltbrine
Saltbrine Reach. Low tide. A barefoot woman with a belt full of jars is kneeling in a rock pool, hissing at a crab. She is Lenna Fathomkeep, and she collects the fish that only show themselves for twenty minutes a day. If you can stand the smell and the seaweed underfoot, she will teach you how to see them.

Premise

Saltbrine's proper fishermen go out deep, for lobster and kraken and what nets bring up. Lenna fishes the margins — the six inches between wet rock and dry rock, the puddle-left-behind, the crack no bucket fits into. The fish she catches are tiny, strange, and worth more than cod to the right cook. Nobody has catalogued them properly because nobody else can be bothered.

She wants a net. Not any net — a net of the exact wrong size for the open sea, woven with a loose middle so a sand-gudgeon can stay tangled but a seacup can pass through. She cannot weave it herself because her hands shake now. She also cannot bend down as long as she used to, and that is why she is hissing at the crab instead of catching it.

Steps

[1] Let the crab go and hear Lenna out
Where: Saltbrine Reach, the shingle north of the harbour
Requires: none
Gulls argue overhead. The tide is turning, which means the lesson has a deadline.
[2] Find somebody who still remembers how to weave a shallow-net with a torn heart
Where: Saltbrine Reach backstreets
Requires: a coil of light twine, two cork floats
The old weaver's hands will tremble, but his mouth remembers every stitch.
[3] Fish one of each thing Lenna names, without touching any of them with bare skin
Where: the tide pools at true low water
Requires: the new net
The sandgudgeon bites. The seacup stings. The glasseel will make you forget your own name for an hour.

Twist

Two of the species Lenna lists do not exist in any Saltbrine records. You will have to decide whether to tell her the tide pools are changing, or pretend you caught exactly what she described. She is watching your face when you come back.

Resolution

Honesty wins her; she laughs, and admits she stopped updating her list thirty years ago because listing a thing is the beginning of the end of it. She hands you the net. The tide pool spots are yours to work whenever the water falls low enough, and the catch will be yours alone.

What it unlocks

You walk away with a slack-hearted net, the only one of its kind in Saltbrine, that catches fish nobody sells at market. Cooks in three regions will pay you silly for a jar of glasseel. The tide pool shelves are open to you at every ebb — you just have to know when the water goes, and nobody will tell you twice.

Dialogue

Lenna Fathomkeep
Not a seine, you great lump. A seine will drown a seacup. I want a net with a hole in its soul.
Old Weaver Mollesh
Haven't woven a margin-net in... oh. Since my Berta died. She kept them on the line. They dry funny.
