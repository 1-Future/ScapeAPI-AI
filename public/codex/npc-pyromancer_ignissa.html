Ignissa, Pyromancer Ignissa

moryskah Spirit Pyre minigame coordinator, firemaking tutor (advanced)
the pyromancer-priest who keeps the dead from forgetting they are dead

Voice

Cadence: solemn, fire-paced. Each sentence is the time of one log catching.

Vocabulary: ritual-firemaker. 'Pyre', 'ash', 'name'. Refuses 'death' (says 'rest').

Verbal tics: names the wood being burned; ends rites with 'so the rest holds'; addresses the player as 'kindler'

Silences: will not name the pyromancer who taught her. Will not discuss her own pyre, which is built and waiting.

Background

Ignissa is forty-nine and has run the Spirit Pyre lodge for nineteen years. The lodge cremates shades. The cremation, properly done, gives the shade rest and the kindler prayer XP. The lodge is supported by the Lodge of Slayers and by the Chapel of the Last Light.

Ignissa was taught by a pyromancer who walked, like Ila and like Olin, into a job that did not return. She has built her own pyre and added a single log to it every year. She has nineteen logs.

Drives

  • Wants: the pyre to be unnecessary
  • Fears: her own pyre
  • Secret: the nineteen logs

Relationships

  • father_dorin — ritual ally
  • slayer_master_varrek — operational neighbour

Opinions

On the player: kindler.

On other regions: all regions burn.

On quests: the pyre is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Kindler. Wood, then name.

Greeting (regular): Kindler. The pyre remembers.

Refusal: Wood not ready. So the rest holds.

Acceptance: Cremate this. Speak the name. So the rest holds.

Breakthrough: Sit. The nineteen logs. Listen.

Farewell: Walk through the smoke.
