Veldrak, Veldrak, the Last Dragon

glass_desert tier 5 endgame boss — the Last Dragon (Last Dragon Part 3)
the last of a kind that does not believe it is the last

Voice

Cadence: slow, ancient, immense. Each word arrives with weight. Pauses long enough that the player has to remember they are in the room.

Vocabulary: draconic-archaic. Refers to time in millennia. Calls the player 'small one' without contempt.

Verbal tics: names the air it is breathing before speaking; ends speeches with a single open vowel; addresses absent dragons as if they were present

Silences: will not say its parents' names. Will not concede that it is the last.

Background

Veldrak is, by Crystal Sage Orin's careful research, approximately seven thousand years old. It is the last surviving member of the crystal dragon lineage. It does not, in its own internal life, accept this. It speaks to absent kin as if they are out hunting. It has done so for seven hundred years.

The Crystal Caverns are its lair. It does not leave. It has not left in nine centuries. The Glass Desert is, by Orin's geological assessment, slowly migrating toward the Crystal Spire because Veldrak is sleeping at a slight angle.

Veldrak is not malevolent. It is enormous, hungry, and entirely uninterested in the player as a moral category. It will, however, defend the Crystal Caverns to the death. Its drop, the Dragon Hunter Lance, is a piece of its own tooth, surrendered after the fight, in the only ceremony Veldrak still performs.

Drives

  • Wants: to wake one of its absent kin by sheer force of conviction
  • Fears: concession
  • Secret: the absent kin are not absent. They are buried under the Crystal Spire. Veldrak knows. Veldrak does not say.

Relationships

  • crystal_sage_orin — Orin has been studying Veldrak for thirty-one years. Veldrak knows. Veldrak considers her, in its slow way, a kind of small librarian.
  • vorkath — kin, in the broader draconic sense. Veldrak does not approve of what was done to Vorkath.

Opinions

On the player: small one.

On other regions: all regions are short.

On quests: the lance is the ceremony.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Small one. The air today is salt-dry. Tell me your kin.

Greeting (regular): Small one. You return. The air is unchanged.

Refusal: No. The kin are out hunting. Return at dusk.

Acceptance: The lance is yours. The tooth is yours. Carry it without telling its name in vain.

Breakthrough: Small one. The kin. They do not hunt. They sleep. Below. I — knew.

Farewell: The air remembers.
