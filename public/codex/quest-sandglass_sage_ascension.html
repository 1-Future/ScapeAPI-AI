Sandglass Sage Ascension

grandmaster epic id: sandglass_sage_ascension
glass_desert
Glass Desert. Orin's tower. Empty. The prophecy is unrolled on the reading-table, held down by a single shard of the wyrm. A note is pinned by the shard. The note has your name, the date, and a direction. The glass is listening more loudly today than it has in a lifetime.

Premise

Orin has walked out of his tower for the first time in decades. He has gone to the Glass Lake, which only forms when the sun has not set for three days — a phenomenon predicted, in the prophecy, to happen exactly once. The note is for you. It is not a goodbye. It is a summons. Orin, it turns out, is not only a sage; he is a sandglass sage — a specific and rare office, held by one person per generation, and held, now, by him for his final hour. The office is about to pass. He has chosen you, provisionally, as a candidate.

The ascension rite is simple to describe and difficult to do: walk the Glass Lake without breaking the surface, meet Orin at its centre, challenge him in three rounds of magic and one round of silence, and then choose — to succeed him as the next sandglass sage, to walk away and let him remain (which he will accept without bitterness, because the office is old and tired), or to break the glass at the lake's centre, ending the sandglass line forever and letting Aelgard find a new balance without it. Each choice is permanent.

Steps

[1] Return to Orin's tower. Read the note. Follow its direction to the Glass Lake
Where: Glass Desert, Orin's tower then Glass Lake
Requires: none
The tower smells of dry paper. The lake will smell of hot clean air.
[2] Walk across the Glass Lake without breaking the surface
Where: Glass Lake
Requires: prayer 80, a light tread
The surface is perfect. One misstep cracks a square mile.
[3] Meet Orin at the lake's centre. He is smaller than you remember, and clearer
Where: lake centre
Requires: none
He has been waiting. He is not tired. He is finished.
[4] Challenge him: three rounds of magic (ice, fire, silence-inverted), one round of silence
Where: lake centre
Requires: magic 90, concentration
The silence round is the hardest. Hold it past the urge to speak.
[5] Make the choice: succeed him, walk away, or break the glass
Where: lake centre
Requires: honesty
Each choice is permanent. The lake does not grant second chances.
[6] Carry the outcome home. The desert remembers; your inventory reflects the choice
Where: Glass Desert to wherever you live
Requires: none
If you succeeded him, his staff is in your hands. If you walked, your coin pouch is heavier by a cryptic amount. If you broke the glass, the horizon is, subtly, different.

Twist

The third round of magic is inverted-silence, which means you must cast a spell without moving your lips or your hands. Most players fail this round first and do not reach the silence round. If you succeed at inverted-silence, Orin will pause the rite and tell you, privately, that he did not succeed at it when he ascended — his predecessor accepted him anyway, because the inverted-silence round was only added after Orin took office. You are held to a harder standard than he was. Whether that is fair depends on your patience.

Resolution

Whatever you chose, you carry it home. The sandglass sage office either passes to you, remains with Orin, or ends. Orin himself either retires to the tower, continues in it, or walks out into the desert, depending on the outcome; in two of the three paths, you will see him again in future content. The desert is changed in small ways that the Codex will record.

What it unlocks

The Sandglass Staff is BIS magic in the Glass Desert region and resets one cooldown per minute — niche outside, transformative inside. The Prophecy Magic spellbook is a fourth spellbook; its spells read the server log and foretell one boss mechanic per day, which is a breakpoint for high-end grouping. The Sage's Tower is yours to use if you chose to succeed Orin; otherwise it is still visitable and you may sit at the reading-table without the staff.
