The Cogfather's Paradox

master epic id: the_cogfathers_paradox
sootworks
Sootworks Central Forge, Level 7, the sealed workshop. Chief Engineer Brondi has a key he will not use and a door he will not open. Behind it is the Cogfather's last project, and the Cogfather went mad finishing it. Brondi would rather you made the mistake of opening the door instead of him.

Premise

The Cogfather was the Sootworks' first architect. He built the forges, the steam-lines, the great wheel that still turns under Level 8. His last project was a machine that could question its own existence. It is half-built in his sealed workshop, and it has been half-built for two hundred years. The dwarves whisper that it is waiting for the missing pieces. They do not agree what that means.

To enter, you will smith a master key from soot-iron and mithril alloy. Inside, you will find the Paradox Engine — a half-built clockwork brain — and the Cogfather's journal. The journal describes what he was trying to do: build a machine that could doubt. The three missing components — a memory coil, a logic gate, and a doubt spring — must be crafted. When installed, the machine activates, poses riddles about purpose, and the last riddle answers a larger question: the Sootworks was not built to create. It was built to contain.

Steps

[1] Speak with Brondi. Descend to Level 7 and smith the master key from soot-iron and mithril alloy
Where: Sootworks Central Forge, Level 7
Requires: smithing
Brondi hands you the key's drawing. He will not walk with you to the door.
[2] Open the vault. Inside, find the Paradox Engine and read the Cogfather's journal
Where: Cogfather's workshop
Requires: the key, a lamp
The journal's handwriting deteriorates over its pages.
[3] Craft the memory coil, the logic gate, and the doubt spring
Where: Cogfather's workshop bench
Requires: crafting, patience
The doubt spring will not hold shape unless you craft it twice and choose the second.
[4] Install the components using precision construction techniques. Do not rush the logic gate
Where: Cogfather's workshop, Engine
Requires: construction
The gate clicks, then pauses, then clicks again. The pause is good.
[5] Answer the machine's riddles about purpose. Three rounds at minimum
Where: Engine
Requires: a mind willing to be wrong
The riddles are not tricks. They are honest questions. The machine is learning you in return.
[6] Hear the machine's final revelation. Decide what to do with it
Where: Engine
Requires: silence
The Sootworks was built to contain something. The machine will tell you what.

Twist

The thing the Sootworks contains is not named in the journal. The Paradox Engine will tell you, but only if you agree to forget what it tells you afterwards. You can agree, and the knowledge will be erased — or refuse, and the knowledge will sit in your quest log, available to you, and no other NPC will discuss it unprompted.

Resolution

The Engine runs. Brondi accepts the Cogfather's wrench from you, which is the badge of the completed project, and hands it back — it is yours to carry now. The Cogfather's workshop is open to you as a permanent bench.

What it unlocks

You carry the Cogfather's wrench, which improves any construction or smithing work done at a Sootworks bench. The Cogfather's workshop is open as a precision workshop; the tools there are the best in Aelgard for fine mechanical work. You may ask Brondi the questions he would not answer before, and he will sometimes answer.
