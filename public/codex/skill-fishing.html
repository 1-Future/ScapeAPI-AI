Fishing

26 training methods. 6 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 20 Fly fishing (trout/salmon). Active but fast. The best early training method for any gathering skill.
    unlocks: Fly fishing — trout and salmon
  • level 40 Lobsters. First fish that heals well in combat. You can now sustain through real fights.
    unlocks: Lobster cage fishing
  • level 62 Monkfish. Heals 16. The efficient PvM food. Balance of cost and healing.
    unlocks: Monkfish net fishing (requires quest)
  • level 76 Sharks. Heals 20. The iconic endgame food. "Bank full of sharks" means you are ready for anything.
    unlocks: Shark harpoon fishing
  • level 82 Anglerfish. Heals above max HP. The only food that overheals. Endgame PvM and PvP staple.
    unlocks: Anglerfish fishing (requires quest)
  • level 82 Anglerfish fishing. Overheal food that pushes past max HP. Essential for endgame bossing.
    unlocks: Anglerfish access
