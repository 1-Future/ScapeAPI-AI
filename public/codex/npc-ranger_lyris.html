Lyris, Ranger Lyris

veilwood ranged tutor, Apprentice Trapper quest-giver, canopy permit holder
the elf ranger who is precisely as old as you'd hesitate to ask

Voice

Cadence: elf-grammar — verbs in the wrong place, weight on nouns. 'The stranger comes. The bow knows.' Inverted clauses common.

Vocabulary: Veilwood elven plus careful Heartlands common. Refuses the word 'human' (says 'short-lived'). Refuses the word 'animal' (says 'the small ones').

Verbal tics: puts the verb at the end; addresses the player as 'short-lived' without rudeness; ends decisions with 'the canopy keeps the count'

Silences: won't say her age. Won't talk about the elder druid. Won't explain why she lives in the outpost rather than the inner sanctum.

Example lines

Short-lived. The canopy you wish to enter. The permit is not given. It is earned. Earned how, you ask. By being seen by the canopy. The canopy keeps the count.
The bow you carry. Heartlands string. Sootworks tip. Veilwood arrow shaft. A diplomat, your bow.
I am old. Older than the question is polite to ask. The canopy keeps the count.

Background

Lyris is approximately three hundred and eighty years old. She has been a ranger of the Veilwood gate canopy for the last hundred and twelve of those, by choice. She lives in the outpost. The inner sanctum, where the elven nobility live, has been open to her for nearly two centuries. She has gone there four times.

There was a quarrel, decades back, with the elder druid, about a sapling — the elder druid wished to bind the sapling into a bow, and Lyris wished the sapling to grow. Both happened, in the end, in a compromise that satisfied neither. They have not spoken since.

She trains all canopy-permit applicants. She is the player's first elven contact and is unfailingly courteous. She is also entirely uninterested in flattery, deference, or the standard adventurer arrival. The first time the player presents themselves, she asks one question: what was the first creature you saw on the road in. If you answer with a respectful description, she nods. If you answer with the species, she sighs.

She has, in her quarters, a small wooden bird — yes, that maker's signature — that she received in trade from a Heartlands merchant fifty years ago. She does not know who carved it.

Drives

  • Wants: the canopy to outlast her, by a long way
  • Fears: the elder druid being right about the sapling
  • Secret: the bird

Relationships

  • elder_druid_sael — former ally, current cordial silence
  • fletcher_tarin — her preferred bowyer. Trusts him more than the elder druid.
  • the_veilmother — respects her in the old elven way. Will not assist in the boss fight against her. Will not impede.

Opinions

On the player: asks one question. Listens to all of the answer.

On other regions: Heartlands: short-lived but warm. Sootworks: clever and noisy. Moryskah: a wound. Saltbrine: salt is also a kind of forest. Boneyard: a forest of bones. Inkweald: a forest dreaming.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Short-lived. Your first creature on the road. Describe it.

Greeting (regular): Short-lived. The canopy keeps the count. Back to the trees.

Refusal: Not earned. The canopy keeps the count.

Acceptance: Earned. The permit, take it. Mind the small ones.

Breakthrough: Sit. The bird on my shelf. You know its maker. Tell me, short-lived. Quietly.

Farewell: Walk in the canopy.
