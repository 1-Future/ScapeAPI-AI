The Barrows Brothers

experienced medium id: barrows_brothers
moryskah
Moryskah. Six burial mounds sit in a clearing south of the village, arranged like a six-pointed star. Since you completed Blood Rites the mounds have hummed at you when you walk past. Father Dorin says: they are ready to be visited. They will not open for anyone else.

Premise

The Barrows Brothers — Dharok, Guthan, Verac, Ahrim, Karil, Torag — were ancient warriors whose kit was buried with them. The seals Blood Rites stabilized are the seals that let you enter without the Brothers leaving. Each brother uses a different combat style; each brother drops a part of his set. The set is degradable, which is to say it needs repair after a fight and wears out eventually, which is the only way to keep it from breaking the game.

The run is repeatable. It is the first proper farming activity of the mid-game. Each run takes about twenty minutes if you are efficient and have pray switches mastered. The loot roll is weighted by which brothers you killed. After all six brothers are killed, the central crypt opens. A puzzle door in the crypt leads to the chest. The chest does not care how elegantly you fought; it only cares that you stood.

Steps

[1] Walk the mounds. Open each. Defeat the brother inside
Where: Moryskah, the Barrows
Requires: attack, magic, prayer, style switches
Each brother greets you in the old language. Each greeting is a threat couched as etiquette.
[2] Enter the central crypt after all six have fallen
Where: Moryskah, Barrows central crypt
Requires: all six brothers down
The central crypt is quiet in a way that implies it has been quiet before.
[3] Solve the puzzle door. It is a different puzzle each run
Where: Barrows crypt, puzzle door
Requires: a moment of thought
One puzzle is tiles. One is levers. One is a sum. They rotate.
[4] Loot the chest. Leave. Return tomorrow if you like
Where: Barrows chest
Requires: a bag with room
The loot is what it is. Some days it is the set piece. Most days it is a bit of coin. You come back anyway.

Twist

On rare runs, a seventh brother — older than the others, and not on any official list — opens a seventh mound you did not see. He is an optional fight. His loot is cosmetic. His existence is a matter of discussion at taverns.

Resolution

The Barrows become a rhythm in your life. Some weeks you do them. Some weeks you do not. The equipment you pull from the chest is BIS at its slot for the bracket it targets, and it wears out honestly.

What it unlocks

The Barrows run is open as a repeatable training method — degradable set equipment, the mid-game's core farming, with loot tables that carry real drops. You keep coming back. The mounds keep opening. The loot respects your time.
