The Last Prayer

grandmaster epic id: the_last_prayer
heartlandsmoryskahthe_wilds
There is a prayer nobody alive has finished. Its three verses are in three places — scratched out on the altar of the Heartlands chapel, hummed endlessly by a wight in a Moryskah churchyard, and carved on the crown the Revenant King wears in the deep Wilds. Finish the prayer. Then decide whether to say it.

Premise

The Last Prayer is Aelgard's oldest unfinished prayer. It predates the First Empire and was carved into the Heartlands chapel altar before the chapel was built. The altar was later scratched over by someone afraid of the prayer's implications; the scratches can be read under the right light, by someone with the right prayer level. The second verse was taken into Moryskah by a priest who fled the chapel three generations ago; he became a wight, and the verse is now hummed endlessly by his mouth. The third verse was carved by the Revenant King onto his crown during his first reign and has been worn on his head for centuries.

To finish the prayer you must read the first verse under moonlight (scratched but legible), kill the humming wight without silencing the hum (a specific combat technique), and take the third verse from the crown (completing the Wilds grandmaster). Return to the chapel. Speak all three verses in order. A hole will open in the air. Decide: finish the prayer (world-affecting choice with a permanent effect on Aelgard prayer mechanics), leave it unfinished (it will wait; you may return), or overwrite the last verse with your own (gives you a personal prayer, but ends the Last Prayer forever).

Twist

The first verse, scratched out, was scratched over by one of the original priests because he recognised the prayer as a summoning — not a blessing. The prayer, if finished, summons a specific saint (or anti-saint, depending on interpretation) who will grant the speaker a one-time prayer effect but will also bind the chapel to that saint forever. Overwriting the last verse severs the summoning; the speaker keeps the effect, but nobody else can ever finish the prayer. Leaving it unfinished keeps the world as-is.

Resolution

Whichever path you take is server-recorded. On the finish path, the saint is summoned and the chapel is bound; future players may receive a blessing at the chapel at a cost. On the leave path, the chapel returns to normal. On the rewrite path, you receive a personal prayer — the Last Prayer as rewritten by you — and the original prayer is gone. All three paths grant the Last Prayer Sigil, a ring that auto-activates once per fight when you hit 1 hp. The Chapel Undercroft is open on the finish or rewrite paths — a small room containing the finished (or rewritten) prayer.

What it unlocks

The Last Prayer (once-per-day, effect varies by ending): revive (finish path, brings you back at 50% hp), smite (rewrite path, personal damage prayer), or nothing yet (leave path, locked until you return). The Last Prayer Sigil is a ring, +5 prayer, auto-activates once per fight at 1 hp. The Chapel Undercroft is a small room beneath the altar, unlocked on the finish or rewrite paths; it contains the finished/rewritten prayer inscribed on a plaque that other players may read.
