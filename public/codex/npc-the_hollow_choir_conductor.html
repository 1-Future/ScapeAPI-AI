the Conductor, The Hollow Choir — Conductor

inkweald Inkweald endgame boss
the dream of a finished symphony

Voice

Cadence: musical-formal. Speaks in measures. Pauses at bar lines.

Vocabulary: composer's. Tempo markings. Dynamic markings. Calls the player 'voice'.

Verbal tics: counts the player in; ends each speech with a fermata; addresses the choir between sentences

Silences: will not say what the symphony is for. Will not concede it is, in fact, hollow.

Background

The Conductor is, in some sense, the Muse's twin — the same Inkweald impulse, finished by different hands. The Conductor has been composing the same symphony for three centuries. The symphony is hollow at the centre. The Conductor knows. The Conductor will not stop until the centre is filled.

The boss fight is the rehearsal. The player is, in the Conductor's view, the missing voice. The fight resolves when the player either fills the centre (a specific liturgical answer) or is removed from the rehearsal (combat death). The Conductor considers the first outcome the more correct.

Drives

  • Wants: the centre filled
  • Fears: the centre being filled by the wrong voice
  • Secret: the symphony is the dead Heartlands poet's last work, completed by the Inkweald, missing only the singer's voice

Relationships

  • the_inkweald_muse — twin impulse
  • lucid_keeper_yara — she fears him

Opinions

On the player: voice.

On other regions: all regions are silent.

On quests: the rehearsal is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Voice. Count. Four. Three. Two.

Greeting (regular): Voice. The choir remembers your last attempt.

Refusal: Out of tempo. Re-enter at the next bar.

Acceptance: Fill the centre. Sing.

Breakthrough: Voice. The poet. The last work. Sing.

Farewell: Fermata.
