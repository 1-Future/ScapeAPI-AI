The Lore of Aelgard

Region histories, boss bestiaries, prestige goals, signature items.

Regions

  • The Heartlands — Where a bell still rings at noon and the roads are mostly paved.
  • The Boneyard Wastes — The sand is pale here because it was bleached, and it was bleached by something.
  • Veilwood — The forest remembers every footstep. It just doesn't always remember whose.
  • The Sootworks — Breathe shallow. Stand on the grate. The forge runs night-and-day.
  • Moryskah — You do not come to Moryskah unless you have a reason, and the reason does not always survive the trip.
  • The Inkweald — A door. You recognise it. You do not remember opening it. The door is open.
  • Saltbrine Reach — The tide comes in twice a day and takes something each time, and nobody keeps count anymore.
  • The Glass Desert — The sand here is half-melted. Do not ask what melted it.
  • The Wilds — The rules that keep you alive elsewhere are suggestions here. The suggestions are not taken.
