The Werewolf's Lineage

experienced long id: the_werewolfs_lineage
moryskahheartlands
A woman in a heavy coat stands outside the chapel on the Heartlands-Moryskah border, waiting for the bells to ring wrong — which they do here, if you are patient, at one minute past midnight. She is not dressed for the weather. She is dressed for the conversation. She is here because of what you chose to do at the end of The Werewolf's Dilemma, and her family, which is long, would like to speak with you about the next generation.

Premise

Whatever choice you made at the end of The Werewolf's Dilemma — to cure the werewolf, or to empower them — the consequence has reached down the family tree, in the way consequences in werewolf families do, and has arrived, now, at a child. The woman in the heavy coat is the child's aunt, on the cured path, or her mother, on the empowered path; in both cases, she has come to find the person who made the first choice, and she is here because the family policy, in her words, is to pay debts in the order they were incurred.

The family has a ledger. The ledger is kept in a vault beneath a cousin's house, which was burnt down four generations ago by a mob that did not know what they were dealing with. The ledger survived because it is made of something that does not burn. You will need to recover it; you will need to read the relevant entry with the aunt-or-mother standing beside you; and you will need to help perform, for the heir, the same kind of ceremony you helped with the first time, because whatever you chose then must be chosen now for the child — the family does not mix outcomes down the same bloodline.

Steps

[1] Meet the woman in the heavy coat at the chapel on the border at the hour the bells ring wrong
Where: Heartlands-Moryskah border chapel
Requires: none
The chapel is lit, which is unusual, and the woman is not inside it, which is not.
[2] Hear the family history out, standing, because sitting in front of an outsider is forbidden
Where: the chapel steps
Requires: patience, and a steady voice of your own
She will tell it in chronological order, and she will not stop when she gets to embarrassments.
[3] Recover the family ledger from the ash-vault beneath the burnt cousin's house
Where: Heartlands, a specific ruin
Requires: thieving 45, a lantern, a readiness to be underground
The vault is a puzzle the mob did not find. The cousin was a methodical person.
[4] Read the relevant entry aloud, with her beside you, and agree on whether it is accurate
Where: wherever you read it
Requires: none
She will correct you twice. Both corrections will be minor. Both will matter to her.
[5] Cure or empower the next heir, using the same method you chose the first time
Where: the family estate
Requires: herblore or prayer, depending on path
The heir is a child. The child will not remember any of this. That is the point.
[6] Stand witness at the ceremony without speaking until the end
Where: the estate chapel
Requires: prayer 50
The ceremony is short. It does not feel short.
[7] Accept the family token, which is not a reward; it is a debt being paid
Where: the estate
Requires: open hands
The token is heavier than it looks. It is heavier every time she looks at you.

Twist

The ledger entry you read aloud is not only about the first werewolf you helped; it is about a second werewolf, older, unrelated to this family, whose path has been tied to the first one by a covenant nobody living remembers making. When you finish reading, the older werewolf — wherever she is — knows your name and your choice, and in a future quest she will find you. The aunt/mother does not warn you. She assumes you will work it out.

Resolution

The heir is settled. The ceremony was proper. The family, which is long, acknowledges you in the ledger on a line of its own, beneath the first line — an unusual honour. You leave with the family token, a silver-backed disc about the weight of a pocketwatch. It will summon the heir, once grown, as a combat ally of scaling power, once per week, for as long as you keep it.

What it unlocks

You carry the werewolf family token — silver-backed, warm to the touch, bearing a sigil nobody in Aelgard sells. The Moryskah estate is now fully explorable and contains a private bank, a small shrine you may pray at, and a locked room the ledger hints at but does not describe. The family's smith, who is older than most banks, will teach you Silvered Steel: a smithing alloy recipe that lets any melee weapon count as silver against werewolves without losing its own material bonuses.

Dialogue

the aunt/mother
Policy is, debts paid in the order incurred. You first. The child doesn't know yet. She won't, until she does. That's family.
the family smith
Silver on steel. Not silver plating — silver alloy. Different work. Takes a week. You'll come back for it.
