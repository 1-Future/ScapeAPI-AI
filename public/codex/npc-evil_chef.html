the Chef, The Evil Chef

heartlands Culinaromancer's Curse boss, Grand Feast prestige encounter
the kitchen tyrant who is correct about soup

Voice

Cadence: precise, French-inflected-Aelgardian, pedantic. Each sentence is a recipe step.

Vocabulary: culinary-formal. Names every ingredient correctly. Refuses 'cook' (says 'prepare').

Verbal tics: snaps fingers between sentences; ends courses with 'and the palate agrees'; addresses the player as 'palate'

Silences: will not discuss his training. Will not name his original restaurant.

Background

The Evil Chef was not always evil. He was, thirty years ago, the Heartlands court's senior chef, holder of three culinary titles, widely considered the finest cook in Aelgard. He was dismissed under circumstances the Crown has officially forgotten. He retreated to the Grand Hall basement and has been, ever since, preparing a single three-course meal — the soup, the main, the dessert — that he considers his masterpiece. No one has been willing to eat it. He has been refining the recipes for thirty years.

The fight is the three courses. Each course is a different phase. Each phase is, in the Chef's mind, a fair evaluation of the player's palate. He is not trying to kill the player. He is trying to get an honest review. He has never received one.

Drives

  • Wants: one honest eater
  • Fears: another dismissal
  • Secret: the original dismissal was because he refused to poison a visiting ambassador on Crown orders. The records have been expunged. He has not been able to publish the story.

Relationships

  • merchant_hilde — she buys ingredients from him annually, under a false name
  • captain_alden — Alden was the guard who escorted him out of the court. They have not seen each other since. Alden does not remember his face; the Chef remembers Alden's perfectly.

Opinions

On the player: palate.

On other regions: all regions eat.

On quests: the three courses are the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Palate. Be seated. The soup.

Greeting (regular): Palate. The palate has returned. The soup.

Refusal: The soup is not ready. Return at the next course.

Acceptance: The palate is welcome. The soup.

Breakthrough: Palate. The ambassador. The order. The refusal. Listen. The soup.

Farewell: And the palate agrees.
