The Perfect Herb Run

experienced medium id: herb_run_mastery
heartlandsveilwoodsootworksmoryskahsaltbrineboneyardglass_desert
Veilwood. Herbalist Syl, who has tolerated you since The Green Thumb, has a challenge she will only offer once, and she will only offer it when your farming is ready. Plant ranarr seeds at all seven herb patches across Aelgard and return with seven grimy ranarrs in five minutes. No disease. No second try today.

Premise

A perfect herb run is the thing farmers do in their sleep. Syl's challenge is not that it is hard; it is that it is unforgiving. One patch diseased, one bad seed, one teleport mistake, one moment of admiring the sunset over the Saltbrine plot and you are out. You must know the route, you must carry the tools, you must teleport efficiently. The reward is real: the Master Farmer title, and a permanent ten-percent yield bonus on herbs forever.

The seven patches are: Heartlands, Veilwood, Sootworks border, Moryskah (Grael's), Saltbrine, Boneyard oasis, Glass Desert crystal-bloom. Each has its own quirks. The Sootworks border patch is prone to soot-damage if not compost-treated. The Moryskah patch is guarded by a chicken Grael will not restrain. The Glass Desert patch must be planted at dawn, not dusk. Syl will test you on all of this.

Steps

[1] Speak to Syl. Accept the challenge only if you have the seeds and the teleports ready
Where: Veilwood, Syl's garden
Requires: ranarr seeds x7, teleports
Syl does not explain the rules twice. She never explains them once.
[2] Plant all seven patches. Watch the time
Where: Heartlands, Veilwood, Sootworks border, Moryskah, Saltbrine, Boneyard oasis, Glass Desert
Requires: farming, agility, teleportation fluency
The Moryskah chicken gets in your way. You were warned.
[3] Wait for growth. Return when the patches are ready
Where: anywhere; check back often
Requires: patience
The wait is offscreen. The wait is the farming.
[4] Harvest all seven patches without disease. Return to Syl with seven grimy ranarrs
Where: the seven patches
Requires: farming, clean patches
Syl counts. Seven.

Twist

If a patch catches disease during your absence, you are not disqualified — you are given a second chance with a new seed at a cost. But if you take the second chance, the title you earn will be Master Farmer (Second Attempt), and Syl will not let you forget it.

Resolution

Syl nods, which is her highest gesture. The title is yours. The yield bonus is yours. Herb runs from now on are faster, richer, and carry your name in small text no other farmer can read.

What it unlocks

The Master Farmer title is yours, and with it a permanent ten-percent herb yield bonus on every herb patch in Aelgard, forever. Your herb income increases quietly and forever. Syl still will not smile at you, but she will not sigh, which is the closest she comes to approval.
