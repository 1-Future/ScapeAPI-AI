The Pirate King's Throne

master epic id: the_pirate_kings_throne
saltbrine
The Broken Crown, late. The pirate king is drinking more than he used to, which is saying something. He will not say he is tired. He will say a great many other things — about the Throne Rocks, about a captain named Drex who you have not heard of, about the meaning of a silver pin pinned to your jacket a while back — but he will not say he is tired. He is, though. He wants to abdicate. He wants to abdicate to you.

Premise

The pirate king is retiring, which is a thing pirate kings do not often do, but he intends to do it properly. Abdication on the Broken Crown requires the successor to take the Throne Rocks — a chain of three crags nobody lives on by choice — without killing anyone who is sorry about it, because the Rocks are inhabited by three of his oldest rivals, each of whom has long since stopped wanting to be a rival but has not yet been given an honourable way to stop. The pirate king, who is a sentimental man under the drink, would like them all to retire with him, which is why the rules are set so specifically.

First rock: a captain duel at dawn against Drex, who is better than you, but who is also sorry. Second rock: outshoot a sniper from three hundred paces — she has been waiting sixty days for the shot to come, and she will lose it on purpose if you deserve the loss. Third rock: take it without being seen, because the third rock is held by a captain who will not concede to any person she has laid eyes on. Eat a last meal with the pirate king afterwards, because that is also a rule, and accept the crown, which is surprisingly light, and which he will not put on your head for you — he will set it on the table between you, and you will put it on yourself, and that will be the abdication.

Steps

[1] Sail to the Throne Rocks on the Broken Crown, alone with the pirate king
Where: Saltbrine open sea, Throne Rocks
Requires: the charter, a steady stomach
The Broken Crown has been waxed. The sails are new. He has been saving this.
[2] Take the first rock by captain-duel at dawn against Captain Drex
Where: first Throne Rock
Requires: attack 70, honour, one good blade
Drex is faster than you, and will let you catch up. You must notice without showing you have noticed.
[3] Take the second rock by outshooting the sniper Mazen from three hundred paces
Where: second Throne Rock
Requires: ranged 65, still hands
She is sixty days into a shot. Do not waste the sixtieth day.
[4] Take the third rock without being seen at all by Captain Yula
Where: third Throne Rock
Requires: thieving 65, agility 60, a light tread
She has cataracts and a spyglass. She uses the spyglass as an ear. Be quieter than you would expect.
[5] Return to the Broken Crown. Eat the last meal with the pirate king
Where: Broken Crown quarterdeck
Requires: none
The meal is small. The meal is correct. The pirate king does not drink during it.
[6] Accept the crown — he will set it on the table, and you will put it on yourself
Where: quarterdeck table
Requires: open hands
It is lighter than you expected. That is part of the test.

Twist

Captain Yula, who you defeat the third rock without being seen by, was the pirate king's first partner, from before he was the pirate king; they had not spoken in decades, and the third rock was the way she kept from having to. When you take the rock without her seeing you, you do not actually defeat her. You give her the one ending she can accept: to have lost to someone she did not have to look at. The pirate king will not mention her when you return. He will, however, pour her an empty glass and leave it on the table.

Resolution

You are the pirate king, which — as the previous one will tell you, on the way back — is mostly a burden and slightly a pleasure. You get the crown, the throne rocks, and the loyalty of three captains who are now free to retire with honour. The Broken Crown remains his flagship by his choice; you will have your own ship, which will be given to you in the next part of this arc, if you are willing to go out one last time as an admiral.

What it unlocks

You wear the Pirate King's Crown: a head-slot, a +3 prayer, and enough respect in any coastal town that guards will turn a blind eye to minor offences involving your person. The Throne Rocks are yours now — a private bank on the first, a small shrine on the second, and a balcony on the third overlooking the open sea. Your quartermaster, whose loyalty is to the crown and not to you, will charter any vessel you name, for a fee, whenever you ask.
