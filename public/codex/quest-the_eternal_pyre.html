The Eternal Pyre

intermediate long id: the_eternal_pyre
moryskahboneyard
Moryskah, Temple of Embers. The sacred flame has gone out for the first time in a thousand years. Priestess Vanya is not panicking because panic is beneath her station, but her hands shake when she lights a candle. The flame cannot be relit with ordinary fire. It must be fed from the sun.

Premise

The Temple of Embers keeps a flame that has been burning since the temple was built. It is not decorative. The flame holds a covenant with the Moryskah dead — while it burns, they rest. It has gone out because the sun has dimmed, and the flame has always fed on the sun's surplus in a way nobody mentioned. To relight it you must carry a living ember from the Boneyard's Sunscorch Crater back to the Moryskah temple, through a desert and a swamp, without letting the ember starve.

The Solar Wisp is the living ember. It is captured in a blessed lantern. It eats logs the way you eat bread. If you let it go out on the road, the covenant breaks, and the Moryskah dead remember what they were. Priestess Vanya will teach you the relight prayer, Priest Dorin will consecrate the blessed lantern for you, and the old firemaking tricks — shade fires along the desert path, the habit of building a windbreak before you need one — will keep the ember alive.

Steps

[1] Witness the dead flame at the Temple of Embers. Hear Priestess Vanya's ritual of rekindling
Where: Moryskah, Temple of Embers
Requires: attention
The hearth is cold in a way a hearth is not supposed to be.
[2] Chop sanctified yew logs from the cemetery grove
Where: Moryskah cemetery grove
Requires: woodcutting, permission
The yew takes the axe with a sigh.
[3] Travel to the Boneyard Wastes and walk to the Sunscorch Crater, building shade fires along the path
Where: Boneyard Wastes, Sunscorch Crater
Requires: firemaking, water
The shade fires are for you, not the ember. The ember is not walking yet.
[4] Light the signal pyre at the crater core to summon the Solar Wisp, and capture it in the blessed lantern
Where: Sunscorch Crater
Requires: firemaking, prayer
The wisp lands on the lantern rim. It weighs nothing. It weighs everything.
[5] Return to Moryskah through the desert night, feeding the wisp logs as it dims. Do not let it go out
Where: road home
Requires: yew logs, patience
At midnight the wisp sputters. You feed it. It bows, faintly.
[6] Relight the sacred flame at the Temple of Embers. Speak with Priestess Vanya
Where: Moryskah, Temple of Embers
Requires: the wisp
The flame catches. Vanya lets out a breath she has not taken in a week.

Twist

Along the road, a Moryskah pilgrim will ask you to light their candle from the wisp. Lighting their candle will not dim the wisp perceptibly — but it will change what happens at the temple when you arrive, and Vanya will know. The choice is yours, and both outcomes are valid.

Resolution

The flame burns again. Vanya hands you a tinderbox that is warm even when not struck. Dorin removes the blessing from the lantern, gently, so you may keep it. The Moryskah dead settle. The covenant holds.

What it unlocks

You carry the Tinderbox of the Pyre, which catches any fire on the first strike, including blessed and cursed woods other tinderboxes refuse. Your firemaking XP is faster when you are working for someone else. The Temple of Embers is open to you as a training hall for prayer and firemaking, both, and the Moryskah cemetery grove will let you harvest sanctified yew at the rate it can regrow.

Dialogue

Priestess Vanya
