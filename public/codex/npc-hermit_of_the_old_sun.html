the Hermit, Hermit of the Old Sun

boneyard Last Light of the Old Sun quest-giver, theological consultant, post-game riddle-keeper
the desert ascetic who is one straight question away from sanity and chooses the riddle

Voice

Cadence: broken-prophet — sentences in the wrong order, half answered. Pauses long enough that you assume the conversation is over and then resume.

Vocabulary: religious-archaic. Refuses anyone's name. Calls everyone 'the seeker' even after fifty visits.

Verbal tics: closes one eye when listening; ends speeches with 'the Old Sun knows'; responds to direct questions with reference to the questioner's hands

Silences: won't tell you their name. Won't tell you their gender. Won't tell you when they came to the shrine. Will tell you absolutely anything about the Old Sun cult, which has been dead for seven centuries.

Background

The Hermit has been at the Old Sun shrine for, by Razak's best estimate, forty-one years. The Hermit's voice doesn't match any one age. The Hermit's face is wrapped against the sun in a manner that has, over decades, become indistinguishable from the wrappings.

The Hermit was, at some point in a former life, a scholar of the Old Sun cult, which worshipped a sun that — by their theology — preceded the current sun and was extinguished by an eclipse from which the world has not yet recovered. The Hermit believes the eclipse is reversible. The Hermit has spent forty-one years in the desert preparing the rite. The rite is the Last Light of the Old Sun quest.

The player is not, in the Hermit's view, special. The player is, however, the first seeker in eight years to ask the right second question. The first question — 'do you have a quest' — is asked by everyone. The right second question is not common.

Drives

  • Wants: the rite to be performed by a seeker who understands the price
  • Fears: the rite being performed by a seeker who does not
  • Secret: the Old Sun is not the sun the world remembers. The Old Sun is the moon.

Relationships

  • razak — the Hermit's bread comes by Razak. They argue theology.
  • archaeologist_veris — the Hermit thinks Veris is, separately, also right about the Pyramid. The Hermit has not told her.

Opinions

On the player: the second question.

On other regions: all regions are different angles of the same eclipse.

On quests: the rite.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Seeker. Your hands. Show me your hands.

Greeting (regular): Seeker. The hands have changed. The Old Sun knows.

Refusal: Not yet. The Old Sun knows.

Acceptance: The rite. The price. The seeker. Three. Begin.

Breakthrough: Sit. Wrappings off. No, your wrappings. The Old Sun. Listen.

Farewell: The Old Sun knows.
