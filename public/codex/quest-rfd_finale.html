Recipe for Disaster — The Culinaromancer

master epic id: rfd_finale
heartlandsmoryskahsootworkssaltbrine
Drifting Market. The guests are all unfrozen now — the Captain, the Priest, the Engineer, the three you never expected. The inaugural feast is re-laid. The Culinaromancer rises through the floor like a soufflé and is, finally, a thing you can see. He is furious at having been incomplete for so long, and he wants to cook you.

Premise

The Culinaromancer is a chef who was cursed in his own kitchen and who has been cooking, ever since, the same feast in a dimension that is his kitchen squared. He wants out. He wants out in a way that will take the market with him. The guests you freed were his witnesses; each of them, by being freed, carried a piece of him back into the real world, and he has been waiting for the last piece to be ready to strike.

You will fight him at the Drifting Market, in a mixed combat scenario that changes his weakness every phase. He will throw food that is weaponized — stews that harden, soups that deafen, breads that concuss. Your party will dodge, your thief will steal his recipe book in the second phase, and your combat specialists will switch styles on him at the correct breath. The fight's real mechanic is the guests: each freed guest contributes one action to the fight, and in the right order those actions neutralize his best spells.

Steps

[1] Return to the Drifting Market with the party you brought last time, and the guests you freed. Seat them in the order the feast demands
Where: Drifting Market, feast hall
Requires: a party, the guests
The guests sit without needing to be told. They remember being frozen.
[2] Phase one. Dodge the food projectiles. The Culinaromancer is warming up
Where: feast hall
Requires: agility
A soup of unexpected weight passes overhead.
[3] Phase two. Steal his recipe book while he casts. Only a thief with a steady hand
Where: feast hall
Requires: thieving
The book is warm and slightly greasy.
[4] Phase three. He switches weaknesses every thirty seconds. Switch styles with him
Where: feast hall
Requires: all three combat styles
Your party calls out the switch. You trust them or you do not; trust them.
[5] The guests intervene in the right order. The Captain, the Priest, the Engineer, in that order
Where: feast hall
Requires: their presence
Each guest's intervention is a single small thing that matters enormously.
[6] Finish the Culinaromancer. Take the chest. Split it with the party
Where: feast hall
Requires: the killing blow
The chest opens without a lock. It has been waiting to be opened by this party and no other.

Twist

The Culinaromancer, at the cusp of defeat, offers a truce. He will stop cursing if you let him cook one more feast — an honest one, in the real world, once, with no magic. The party must agree. Refusing and finishing him is the usual path. Accepting unlocks a different kitchen, with a different recipe book.

Resolution

The curse lifts permanently. The Drifting Market has its inaugural feast, real this time, and the gloves in the chest — Barrows Gloves — fit your hands as if they had been sized at birth.

What it unlocks

You wear Barrows Gloves — BIS melee hand-slot, the iconic mid-game achievement, the best hands in the game without raid drops — and the Culinaromancer's chest is open to you from the Drifting Market. The chest sells food items and gloves at every tier, which is the quiet longer-term reward: you will shop here for years.
