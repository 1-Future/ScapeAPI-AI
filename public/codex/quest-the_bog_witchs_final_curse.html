The Bog Witch's Final Curse

grandmaster epic id: the_bog_witchs_final_curse
moryskahheartlandsthe_wilds
Moryskah, the dark of the moon, and the Bog Witch is smaller than she was last time, a little smaller again than the time before that, and she has decided, finally, after a good deal of sitting with the question, that she would like to stop being a witch before the thing that is making her one finishes making her one; she has asked for you by name, and you are perhaps the only person in Aelgard who would answer to it.

Premise

The Witch is under a curse that she, herself, did not recognise as a curse for most of her life, because it came to her as a gift from her own teacher, who was under the same curse, and whose teacher was under the same curse, and so on back through a line of women who have lived alone in that cottage since before the kingdom of Aelgard was a country that remembered being founded. The curse is that the witch inherits the swamp, and the swamp, slowly, inherits the witch, until they are no longer distinguishable, and the cottage moves a little each season, and the pots cook themselves if the witch is late, and the patch weeds itself, and eventually the witch is not a person but a continuity, a name on a door that has stopped needing a door.

She has decided she would like to die a person. To do this you must break the curse, which means breaking its three anchors — a locket in the Heartlands, a grave in Moryskah, and a stone in the Wilds — without anyone seeing you break them, because the anchors are held in place by the fact that people do not touch them. Then you must brew a dissolution potion, at her herb bench, over seven hours, in silence. Then you must fight what crawls out of her when the curse lets go, which will be shaped like her, and will speak in her voice, and will not be her.

Steps

[1] Come to her cottage at the dark of the moon, as she asked, and be told the shape of the curse
Where: the Bog Witch's cottage
Requires: none
She will be smaller. Her chair will be larger. The teapot will pour without her touching it.
[2] Break the first anchor — a locket in a Heartlands drawer nobody has opened in three generations
Where: Heartlands, a specific manor
Requires: thieving, discretion, no witnesses
The locket is heavy for its size. The drawer will not close again after.
[3] Break the second anchor — a grave in Moryskah that has never been visited and must not be seen to be visited
Where: Moryskah, a forgotten churchyard
Requires: prayer, quiet feet, moonless night
The gravestone does not have a name. You will leave it without one.
[4] Break the third anchor — a stone in the Wilds, which must be touched while no pker is within sight of you
Where: the Wilds, a specific cairn
Requires: agility, a long view, a steady hand
The stone is smaller than the cairn suggests. You will not take it. You will only shift it.
[5] Return to the cottage, which will have moved again, and find her at the herb bench
Where: the Bog Witch's cottage, which is not quite where it was
Requires: none
She is waiting. The bench is already laid out. The pots are still.
[6] Brew the dissolution potion at the bench in silence for seven hours
Where: the bench
Requires: herblore 85, absolute silence
The swamp will try to interrupt you. You must not answer.
[7] When the curse lets go of her, fight whatever steps out of her. It will not be her, though it will look like her
Where: inside the cottage
Requires: attack 70, prayer 70, a strong stomach
It will speak in her voice. It will know your name. Do not hesitate.
[8] Bury what remains, and plant the moryskah tree over the burial, and speak her given name for the first time
Where: the patch
Requires: a sapling, a grave, a word
She left the name on a paper on the bench, face down. You may read it only when you need it.

Twist

The thing that crawls out of her is not an enemy. It is the swamp itself, shaped like her because the swamp has forgotten any other shape. When you defeat it, you do not destroy the swamp; you simply release it from the shape of her. Moryskah remains Moryskah; it just stops walking on her legs. She is allowed to die a person. The shape she releases, however, must be settled somewhere, and the somewhere is you, briefly, for the duration of one long night that the game will remember.

Resolution

You bury her. You plant the moryskah tree over the burial, and you speak her given name — Lith — aloud, for the first time, and the sapling takes. The cottage no longer moves. Her hat, which you will find hanging on the back of the chair you always sat in, fits your head. The grove around the burial becomes a small perfect patch of her swamp, which cannot be found by accident, and which will be open to you whenever you need it.

What it unlocks

You wear her hat. It halves herblore ingredient costs at any bench in Aelgard, which is to say it halves every serious potion expense of your endgame. The cottage is yours to use as a herblore workshop; the Witch's Grove, a small perfect patch of Moryskah swamp behind the cottage, is accessible only to you, and contains rare herbs that do not grow elsewhere. The Swamp Made Flesh, the shape you defeated, returns on a weekly cadence as an optional grandmaster boss; killing it drops unique herblore secondaries the game gives out nowhere else.
