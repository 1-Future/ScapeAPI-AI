The Shattered Covenant

grandmaster epic id: the_shattered_covenant
glass_desertheartlandsboneyardmoryskahveilwoodsootworkssaltbrineinkweald
Glass Desert, Crystal Sage Orin's tower. The sky is cracking. Literal cracks, visible by daylight, running in lines that match old maps of the kingdom's borders. Crystal Sage Orin's warning is short: the covenant between Aelgard's gods is failing, and its fragments are awakening, and every faction in the world wants them.

Premise

The Shattered Covenant is the world-affecting quest. Eight fragments of divine power, each sealed in one of Aelgard's eight regions. You must collect them all. Each requires a different approach — a thieving puzzle in the Heartlands king's vault, a mining expedition in the Boneyard through cursed bedrock, a negotiation with the Moryskah vampyre council, a cure for the Veilwood Heart-Oak, a careful extraction from the Sootworks Paradox Engine, a pearl dive in Saltbrine's deepest trench, a Dreamer's absorption in the Inkweald, and a surgical removal from the Glass Desert's sleeping titan.

Combine all eight at the Covenant Altar atop Aelgard's highest peak. Then choose: restore the old covenant (gods remain, world continues) or forge a new one (gods weakened, mortals gain divine abilities). Your choice echoes across the server.

Twist

The choice is not between two options; it is between three. The third option is revealed only if you have spoken with each region's fragment-holder at length. The third option is to not combine the fragments at all, and to let Aelgard find a new balance without divine intervention. This is the hardest outcome to live with and the most interesting.

Resolution

The sky heals. The world is changed in the direction of your choice. Crystal Sage Orin will record the outcome for future generations. The Covenant Shard in your hand is warm.

What it unlocks

You carry the Covenant Shard. Depending on your choice, you gain divine abilities (in the new covenant), stability (in the old), or a new kind of agency (in the third path). The shard is a permanent quest item; future world-affecting quests branch from it.
