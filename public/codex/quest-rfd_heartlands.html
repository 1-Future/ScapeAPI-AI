Recipe for Disaster — The Captain

intermediate medium id: rfd_heartlands
heartlands
Drifting Market. Captain Alden is frozen mid-laugh, one hand still on a goblet, the other on his old sword. His wife, in a widow's coat bought in advance, tells you he will thaw only for a Heartlands meat pie — the one his mother made when he was a boy, the one she has never managed to replicate.

Premise

Captain Alden's childhood pie is a specific recipe: Heartlands allotment potatoes, yellow onions grown from a particular family seed, a crust rolled so thin it shadows the filling. His mother is long dead, but the seed survives in her garden, tended by the Wellcote village keeper. You will grow the vegetables, craft a pie dish from clay, cook the pie without burning it, and serve it with the correct placement on the plate.

The Culinaromancer will watch from the dimensional gap. He will comment. You will ignore him.

Steps

[1] Learn what the Captain's childhood pie was. Obtain the family seed from the Wellcote keeper
Where: Heartlands; Wellcote
Requires: polite persuasion
The keeper will give you the seed if you promise to grow it honestly.
[2] Grow potatoes and onions at the Heartlands allotment
Where: Heartlands allotment
Requires: farming
The onions come up yellow. The potatoes are small and sweet.
[3] Craft a pie dish from clay
Where: Heartlands potter's bench
Requires: crafting
The dish rings hollow when fired. That is correct.
[4] Cook the pie without burning it
Where: Drifting Market kitchen
Requires: cooking
The Culinaromancer narrates unhelpfully. You ignore him.
[5] Serve the pie to free Captain Alden
Where: Drifting Market feast hall
Requires: the pie
He thaws slowly. His laugh finishes. He looks at you and says nothing, then everything.

Twist

The Culinaromancer will offer you his version of the pie — better, he says, and freely given. Accepting it will free Alden faster but the Captain will never fully trust you afterwards. Refusing is slower and honest.

Resolution

Alden is free. His wife cries, once, quickly, and hides it. The Captain invites you to his ship anytime. You have one of five guests freed.

What it unlocks

Captain Alden will charter you a free passage on any Heartlands river route once a week, and his memory of your visit is long. A Heartlands allotment plot tied to his family seed is yours to use; the produce from it has a slight quality bonus.

Dialogue

Captain Alden's wife
His mother's pie was not a recipe. It was her. If you approximate the pie, you approximate her. Do your best. I will be kind.
Captain Alden
(a long breath, a swallowed tear, and then:) You have my mother's recipe down to the crust. How? Tell me later. Not now.
