Recipe for Disaster — The Priest

experienced medium id: rfd_moryskah
moryskah
Drifting Market. Father Dorin is frozen in prayer, one hand raised, lips parted around a word. He will thaw only for a sanctified stew — the kind the Moryskah priests make in the cold months — brewed with swamp herbs the Bog Witch will lend you reluctantly, blessed on a range only consecrated stones will heat.

Premise

The priest's stew requires wolfbane herbs from the Moryskah swamp, raw karambwan fish from the swamp waters, and a blessed range of your construction. Grael will lend the herbs if you phrase the request as an errand, which she still has the habit of running. The karambwan must be fished at dusk, when the swamp waters are warmest. The range must be built with stones Dorin has blessed — which he will do only once, at the feast, because he is frozen.

You will work around his absence by pre-blessing, through a specific prayer Grael taught you in her bargain.

Steps

[1] Secure wolfbane herbs from Grael. Fish raw karambwan from Moryskah swamp waters at dusk
Where: Grael's shack; Moryskah swamp
Requires: herblore, fishing
Grael hands over the herbs. She does not ask what for.
[2] Brew the sanctification agent from wolfbane herbs
Where: apothecary
Requires: herblore
The agent bubbles. It smells of something kept.
[3] Cook the stew on a blessed range
Where: Drifting Market kitchen
Requires: prayer, cooking
The range flames turn blue once, briefly. Good sign.
[4] Serve the stew to free Father Dorin
Where: feast hall
Requires: the stew
He thaws in the order of his bones: legs, hands, mouth, eyes.

Twist

The Culinaromancer will attempt to curse the stew mid-cook. You must either notice (and recook) or serve the cursed stew, which will free Dorin but leave him with a minor ailment for a week. He will not blame you; you will know.

Resolution

Dorin thaws. He finishes the prayer he started, which turns out to be for you. You are two of five.

What it unlocks

Father Dorin will teach you an advanced prayer line at his chapel on request, and the Moryskah swamp's karambwan fishing spots are now open to you as a reliable food source. Grael considers you almost-competent now, which is the highest grade she gives outsiders.

Dialogue

Father Dorin
I was mid-prayer. I was praying for you. I do not often pray for you, but I was. Continue, please.
Grael
Take the wolfbane. Do not tell me what for. I do not wish to be implicated in anything the Drifting Market does.
