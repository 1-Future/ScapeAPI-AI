The Grand Heist

master epic id: the_grand_heist
heartlandssootworkssaltbrinewilds
Heartlands, tavern basement. An old man who introduces himself as Old Fingers — thin, white, with hands that do not shake — says the Vault of Ages beneath the castle has been sealed for five hundred years, and he knows a way in. He also says he is retiring. He would like a last good heist before he does.

Premise

The Vault of Ages is the Heartlands' great secret: a vault containing artefacts from every era, sealed by the royal line and forgotten on purpose. Old Fingers spent twenty years mapping it from the outside. He has a plan with eight phases. You will gather a skeleton key from Sootworks, maps from Saltbrine, a firebeetle distraction from the Wilds border, and then navigate sewers under the castle, build a precision tunnel into the vault floor, disarm magical wards, pick the master vault door (the hardest lock in Aelgard), and take one item of your choice.

Each phase is a mini-quest. Each requires a different skill. Each fails if you rush it.

Steps

[1] Meet Old Fingers. Hear the plan
Where: Heartlands tavern basement
Requires: patience
He lays out the plan on the table with coins and salt.
[2] Sootworks: smith the skeleton key from rare alloy that mimics any lock
Where: Sootworks forge
Requires: smithing
The key looks unfinished. That is the point.
[3] Saltbrine: steal old maps from the harbourmaster's archive
Where: Saltbrine harbour archive
Requires: thieving
The maps are in an unlocked drawer. That is the distraction.
[4] Wilds border: capture a wild firebeetle for the distraction
Where: Wilds border
Requires: agility, hunter
The firebeetle is fast and enormously offended by your net.
[5] Heartlands: navigate the sewers to the vault's underside. Build a precision tunnel
Where: Heartlands sewers
Requires: agility, construction
The tunnel must be quiet. You work slowly.
[6] Disarm magical wards in each vault alcove
Where: vault
Requires: magic
The wards hum. You hum back, quietly.
[7] Pick the master vault door — the hardest lock in Aelgard
Where: vault door
Requires: thieving at its peak
The lock resists. Then it yields. Then it remembers you.
[8] Take one item (of your choice of equipment type). Escape before the alarm resets
Where: vault interior
Requires: a steady hand
The vault has too many things. You choose one.

Twist

One of the vault's artefacts is a letter addressed to the current king — not sealed, not mailed. Reading it before leaving changes a future quest. Delivering it anonymously to the king afterwards is another option. Keeping it is a third.

Resolution

You escape through the tunnel. Old Fingers seals it behind you. He retires, officially this time. The Vault of Ages is accessible to you from now on, legitimately, by the back door.

What it unlocks

You carry a Vault of Ages key — access to the BIS equipment storage, and a weekly loot chest that contains rare materials. The vault is a quiet, personal library you may visit. Old Fingers sends you postcards from a coastal town nobody has mapped.
