Slayer

16 training methods. 8 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 1 Slayer begins. The skill that makes combat structured. Kill assignments, earn points, unlock new monsters.
    unlocks: Basic slayer assignments
  • level 40 Moryskah Slayer Tower access. Aberrants, gargoyles, nechryaels — the mid-game slayer grind core.
    unlocks: Slayer tower methods
  • level 50 Scuttler Pits slayer tier open. Anchor-Sworn gives the first salt-wraith task. The Reach's slayer ladder begins here — brine-troll to salt-vampire to salt-wraith.
    unlocks: Scuttler Pits full access
  • level 55 Slayer helm craftable. 16.67% accuracy and damage boost on task. Slayer becomes THE combat training method.
    unlocks: Slayer helm — 16.67% boost on task
  • level 72 Skeletal wyverns. First serious slayer money-maker. Drops are worth millions per task.
    unlocks: Skeletal wyvern tasks
  • level 85 Abyssal demons. Drops the Abyssal Whip — the iconic weapon. This is the goal people grind slayer for.
    unlocks: Abyssal demon tasks — whip drops
  • level 91 Cerberus. Drops primordial, pegasian, eternal crystals. BiS boots for all 3 combat styles.
    unlocks: Cerberus slayer boss — BiS boot upgrades
  • level 95 Hydra. Alchemical Hydra drops Hydra claw (Dragon Hunter Lance component). Best slayer gp/hr.
    unlocks: Alchemical Hydra — best slayer gp/hr
