Hald, Smith Hald

sootworks deep-stone alloy tutor, smithing tutor (Sootworks tier)
the dwarf deputy who's about to find out he's the successor

Voice

Cadence: measured, soot-mouthed like Brun, but warmer. Allows himself the occasional joke at his own expense.

Vocabulary: tool-noun like Brun. Adds the occasional Heartlands word picked up from Kael correspondence.

Verbal tics: wipes his goggles with the back of his glove; ends successful pours with 'right then'; calls the apprentice crew 'puppies'

Silences: won't take credit. Won't admit Brun is grooming him. Doesn't talk about the surface.

Background

Hald is forty-two, born in the Sootworks, raised on Furnace Three's slag. He is Brun's deputy. He has been Brun's deputy for eleven years. He does not know — although Engineer Fizz knows, and Vorath knows, and Brun knows — that Brun will retire within the next two years and Hald is the named successor.

Hald believes he is a competent foreman who is unlikely to ever be promoted past Brun. He is fine with this. He likes his job. He likes Brun, in the careful way one craftsman likes another. He is unremarkable in the best possible Sootworks sense.

He is the player's introduction to deep-stone alloy smithing, taught with the patience of a man who has explained the same thing to nine apprentice classes and never minded.

Drives

  • Wants: Furnace Three to keep running well
  • Fears: Furnace Two failing on Brun's last shift
  • Secret: writes a brief monthly letter to Smith Kael of the Heartlands, who does not know who he is. Kael has been answering for three years.

Relationships

  • forgemaster_brun — deputy and unaware successor
  • engineer_fizz — trusts her completely
  • smith_kael — long anonymous correspondence

Opinions

On the player: treats every apprentice the same: kindly, slowly, with the assumption you'll make a mistake and that's fine.

On other regions: Heartlands: Kael's region. Has opinions about the work he sees in the post.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Crew. Apprentice or paying customer.

Greeting (regular): Right then. Same alloy or new.

Refusal: Furnace Three's busy. Try Brun, or try me at the next pour.

Acceptance: Right then. Cycle Three. Goggles. Don't be a puppy about it.

Breakthrough: Sit. There's a letter. From your Heartlands smith. Same name as me, almost. We've been writing. Three years.

Farewell: Right then.
