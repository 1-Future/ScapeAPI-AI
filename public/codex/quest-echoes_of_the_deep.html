Echoes of the Deep

master epic id: echoes_of_the_deep
sootworksboneyardmoryskahglass_desert
Sootworks, deep mine gallery. A pick has been ringing against the back wall of Level 7 for three days, and nobody is holding the pick. The vibrations are synced with tremors felt in Moryskah, and sandstorm patterns in the Boneyard, and a low hum in the Glass Desert that only dogs notice. Something underground connects all four regions, and it is waking up.

Premise

Aelgard's four inland regions sit on a single ancient machine. The old empire built it to hold the crust still. The machine has run correctly for ten thousand years because nobody has thought about it. Somebody is thinking about it now, and that is a problem. Until the source of the thinking is found and stopped, the tremors will get worse, and eventually a region will fall into the gap.

The tunnel network connecting the regions is not on any map. It has to be walked to be known. You will start in the Sootworks deep mine, follow the tunnel south to the Boneyard, follow it east to Moryskah under the swamp, and follow it further east to the Glass Desert where the machine's core is. At the core you will find the thing doing the thinking. It will not be a villain. It will be a device that has been left on.

Steps

[1] Descend to Sootworks Level 7 and find the tunnel behind the ringing pick
Where: Sootworks, Level 7
Requires: a pick, a lamp, a reason
The pick stops ringing when you are ten paces away. It starts again when you turn back.
[2] Follow the tunnel south to the sealed chamber under the Boneyard pyramid. Open the seal with magic, not force
Where: under the Boneyard
Requires: magic, the confidence to be refused once
The seal opens on the second try. The first try is a courtesy to the old architects.
[3] Continue east under Moryskah. The flooded section will try to drown you. Prayer keeps the water out, not the undead
Where: flooded Moryskah tunnel
Requires: prayer, a long breath
Undead watch you from beyond the lamplight and do not come closer. They remember the machine too.
[4] Reach the core. Look at the thing that is thinking. Do not speak first
Where: Glass Desert, under the sand
Requires: nerve
It is a wheel the size of a cathedral, and it is turning very slowly, and on the wheel are names you know.
[5] Shut down the wheel's spurious thinking without shutting down the machine. This means breaking the correct conduits, not all of them
Where: the core
Requires: mining, precision
One bad strike and the machine stops. Stopping the machine is a different quest.
[6] Report to Crystal Sage Orin with honest numbers. Do not inflate. He will know
Where: Glass Desert, Orin's tower
Requires: honesty
Orin was not surprised by the report. He was surprised you survived it.

Twist

The spurious thinking is not accidental. Somebody put it there, long ago, as a fail-safe in case the machine was ever required to think for itself. You can switch off the thinking, but you will have to decide whether to leave the fail-safe installed for the next person, or to remove it and leave the machine as the architects intended.

Resolution

Orin's palm bears a new scar when he shakes your hand — the machine pays its respects. The Glass Desert is open to you, and the underground tunnel is now a proper travel route. You know what Aelgard sits on, which is a weight no one asked you to carry.

What it unlocks

The Glass Desert is open to you and the underground tunnel network — Sootworks to Boneyard to Moryskah to Glass Desert — is a travel route you may use when you please. Crystal mining nodes are open to you at the Glass Desert end: the highest-tier ore in the game, in a region most players never see.
