Target Practice

novice short id: target_practice
heartlands
Heartlands militia range. Captain-at-Arms Odette Brume is shouting at a line of recruits who cannot hit a hay bale at twenty paces, and she needs someone to shoot decent enough to shame them into competence. If you can put three arrows on a moving mark without flinching when she barks, she will give you a badge that means something.

Premise

The militia range has a daily practice hour nobody but regulars attend, because the regulars are famously mean to walk-ins. Odette runs it. She was an archer in a war she does not name and she trains the way she was trained, which is harder than necessary and better than civil. The badge she hands out is stamped on pewter and it looks like a child's toy. It is not.

To earn it you have to prove you can learn, not prove you can shoot. Odette will make you do something deliberately awkward — shoot left-handed, shoot in wet gloves, shoot at a target she has numbered backwards — and she will watch whether you complain. If you complain, the badge goes to someone else, and you will not get another chance this season.

Steps

[1] Turn up at the morning hour and take whatever odd instruction Odette hands you
Where: Heartlands archery range
Requires: a bow you can actually draw
Rain spatters the hay. The recruits smirk. They are going to lose that smirk.
[2] Shoot the awkward shot she designs for you without comment
Where: Heartlands archery range, soaked through
Requires: three arrows, patience
Your gloves are full of water. The string feels wrong. Good.
[3] Do it again tomorrow without being asked
Where: Heartlands archery range at dawn
Requires: yesterday's bow, today's discipline
Odette nods once. The recruits do not smirk today.

Twist

On the second morning Odette will be absent. A sergeant will lie and say the drill is cancelled. If you leave, you fail. If you stay and practise on your own, Odette will appear from behind the targets — she was watching — and hand you the badge in silence.

Resolution

The badge pins to the inside of your coat, close to the heart, which is where the militia say it belongs. Odette tells you the range is open to you every morning now, and that if you bring a friend who shoots worse than you, the friend will be laughed at, but tolerated.

What it unlocks

You carry a Militia Archer's Badge, dented and warm, and at the Heartlands range your arrows fly truer than they ought to. A daily bonus of steady ranged training is yours whenever you can spare the hour — low attention, high effect — and the sergeant has learned your name, which matters more than it sounds.
