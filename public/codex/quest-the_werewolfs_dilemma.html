The Werewolf's Dilemma

experienced medium id: the_werewolfs_dilemma
moryskah
Moryskah forest. A young werewolf bleeds into the moss and does not turn when the moon hits him. He is tired of turning. He is also tired of not turning. He asks you — because you are there, because you smell of Grael's incense, because he will ask the next person otherwise — to choose for him.

Premise

The young man's name is Rask. He has been a werewolf for four years and he has been lonely for three of them. The cure exists, as does the empowerment. Curing him turns him human, and he loses the form, and he goes back to a village that might not take him. Empowering him seals him into the form permanently, stronger and faster, and he becomes part of the Moryskah wolf-pack that hunts on the Hollow Mire's far side. Both paths are honest. Neither is safe.

Grael has left ingredients for both potions in a locked box in her shack, and she is making tea and will not be drawn on the matter. Father Dorin has offered to perform the cure's prayer, but only if Rask asks for it himself. Grael has offered to perform the empowerment's ritual, but only if Rask asks for it himself. Rask will not ask. He has delegated the ask to you. That is the dilemma.

Steps

[1] Sit with Rask. Listen to his story as he tells it, not as you expect to hear it
Where: Moryskah forest, clearing
Requires: patience, time
Rask's hands shake only on the odd-numbered sentences.
[2] Visit Father Dorin and Grael separately. Hear each of them without telling the other what the first said
Where: Moryskah chapel; Moryskah bog
Requires: discretion
Dorin is careful. Grael is indifferent. Both care, differently.
[3] Gather the ingredients for both paths. Decide as late as possible
Where: Moryskah bog and chapel
Requires: herblore, crafting
The cure potion smells like childhood. The empowerment amulet is warm in your pocket.
[4] Return to Rask. Ask him, again, which he wants. If he still will not answer, choose — it is the job he assigned
Where: Moryskah forest, clearing
Requires: the potions, the ability to decide
Rask watches your mouth, not your eyes, when you speak the choice.
[5] Administer the chosen outcome. Walk him back to whichever community will have him
Where: wherever the choice leads
Requires: both hands
The walk is quiet. You do not fill it with talk.

Twist

There is a third path, which Grael does not mention and Dorin does not know. Rask can be left as he is, with a ward tied around his throat that keeps him alive for precisely one more season. If you notice this during the ingredient-gathering — Grael's shelf has a third bottle that is marked in ink you can read, if you look — Rask will accept the third path if offered. It is neither cure nor empowerment. It buys him time to decide himself.

Resolution

Whichever path is taken, the outcome is permanent. Rask's future is fixed by your hand. The village you return him to changes by the choice: cured, he joins a human community that now sells you herblore supplies; empowered, he becomes an ally in the Moryskah pack, summonable for hard fights. The third path, if you found it, returns him to Grael as her student.

What it unlocks

If you cured him, a human shopkeeper in werewolf territory now sells herblore supplies nobody else carries. If you empowered him, a werewolf ally answers a summoning horn once per day for Moryskah bosses — a real second in a hard fight. If you took the third path, Grael has a new apprentice and you have a standing invitation to her table, which is not nothing.

Dialogue

Rask
I will not ask. If I ask I will be wrong. If you choose, I will agree with the choice, and I will not blame you, which is the best I can do for both of us.
Grael
There is a third bottle. You have not asked about it. I will not offer it. If you ask, I will speak.
