The Hollow Choir Conductor

inkwealdtier 5

Bestiary

The Conductor is the leader of the Choir and is possibly not a separate entity from the Choir but is, for purposes of engagement, addressed as one. The Choir sings. The Conductor directs. The singing reshapes the dream forest in real time, and the reshaping is a weapon. Players who enter the Resonance Chamber encounter the Choir first — a mass of singers arrayed in tiered ranks — and the Conductor last, after the tiers have been thinned and the chamber has been cleared of enough sound to make the Conductor's own voice audible.

Eight players. Three phases. The phases are distinguished by what the Conductor's weakness is, and the weakness cycles: phase one is crush, phase two is magic, phase three is ranged. Players who prepared only one combat style will fail. Players who prepared three will find the fight manageable and the survival rate acceptable.

The first phase is a close-range encounter in which the Conductor wields a baton that crushes what it touches. Shatter the resonance with crush weapons — maces, warhammers, mauls. The second phase takes place at middle range; the Conductor retreats to a dais and sings in a register that disrupts physical matter, and spells are the only attacks that reach him. The third phase is at extreme range; the Conductor ascends, and the Choir begins a harmonic that compresses the air between himself and the players, and only ranged weapons can punch through the compression at a distance that keeps the team safe.

The Conductor drops the Choir Sigil (a neck-slot item that resonates with impossible harmonics and grants a bonus to all combat styles simultaneously), the Harmonic Blade (a sword whose frequency disrupts armour), and the Silence Bow (arrows that produce no sound until impact). The drops are not all dropped in a single kill. The Conductor drops one of the three per defeat, and players who want the full set must clear him three times, and most teams find after the second time that they do not, in the end, want the full set, because the third run is when the Inkweald begins to notice them.

Silence. Actual silence. For several seconds afterward, even players' own breathing sounds muted.
