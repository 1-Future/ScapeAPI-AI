The Bog Witch's Bargain

intermediate long id: the_bog_witchs_bargain
moryskah
Moryskah. Father Dorin has finally walked out to the bog witch's shack because the wolves at the village edge have stopped being wolves. He wanted to pray over it. The witch Grael has told him praying is a fine thing but a boring thing, and that she will help him if, and only if, he brings her an adventurer who will run three errands she does not trust a priest with.

Premise

Grael does not do bargains lightly. A bargain is a debt she can call in, and a bargain with a priest is a debt with interest. With an adventurer she can trade by the hour. The werewolves in the Moryskah woods are not cursed — they are remembering a shape they used to wear, and something is reminding them. Grael knows who. She wants that person dealt with in a way Dorin would not approve of, and she is happy to teach you herblore properly as payment.

To prove you are worth teaching she wants three things done: a wolfbane incense blended from plants she will point at but not name, a name pulled out of a witness who will not give it to a priest, and a visit to a grave that has to be walked to, not ridden to. If you do all three in the right order, she will tutor you in proper swamp herblore, and the Moryskah as a region will stop eyeing you sideways.

Steps

[1] Meet Grael at the shack she did not have last week. Hear the bargain. Do not sign anything
Where: Moryskah swamp, Grael's shack
Requires: a strong stomach, nothing written down
Father Dorin is waiting outside, looking at his shoes. Grael has three chickens on her roof.
[2] Blend wolfbane incense from plants you gather in the Moryskah deep swamp. Grael will watch from a distance and say nothing until you finish
Where: Moryskah deep swamp
Requires: gathering bag, mortar, a kettle
Three of the plants bite. One is a mushroom that watches you back.
[3] Visit the witness. Do not threaten them. Get the name out using only words and a cup of tea
Where: a hut behind the Moryskah windmill
Requires: patience, the right sort of tea
The witness is a girl of nine with a grandmother's mouth. She drinks her tea in one long sip.
[4] Walk the long graveyard path to the grave Grael has circled on a scrap of map. Do not let anyone carry you any part of the way
Where: Moryskah cemetery, the old section
Requires: your own two feet
The graves along the path belong to every family in the village. Half the names are crossed out in paint that has not weathered.
[5] Return to Grael with the incense burning and the name on your tongue. Hand them both over
Where: Grael's shack
Requires: the lit incense, the spoken name
Grael does not touch the incense. She bows to it, a fraction.

Twist

The name is the name of a man Father Dorin baptized, and he was not told because Grael knew he would refuse to let her act. Dorin will find out. You will have to choose whether to stand between them when the argument starts, or to step back and let the swamp arbitrate.

Resolution

Grael burns the incense for three nights. The wolves at the village edge go back to being wolves. Father Dorin and Grael are not friends, but they are cordial on feast days now. Grael opens her pantry to you and starts teaching properly, which turns out to be harder than the bargain.

What it unlocks

Moryskah is open to you as a region — all of it, the wet parts and the dry, and the bits with signs — and Grael the Bog Witch is your herblore tutor for the advanced swamp potions nobody else will teach you. You know the wolfbane incense recipe; burn it and werewolves find somewhere else to be for a good ten minutes. The incense stains your sleeves and it stains your thinking, which Grael says is the point.

Dialogue

Grael the Bog Witch
A priest is a rope with a knot tied at both ends. He cannot do the untidy bits. That is what we are for, you and me. Only you have a shorter memory, which helps.
Father Dorin
I asked you to come here to pray. I see you have done something else instead. I will not ask what.
