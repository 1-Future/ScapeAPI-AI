The Fight Caves

grandmaster epic id: fight_caves
glass_desert
Glass Desert, far eastern edge. A crack in the glass floor opens on the hour. Heat comes out of it in a breath that tastes of copper. The local custom is to nod at it and go on walking. You have heard, from a veteran with scars like written records, that the crack leads to the Fight Caves, and the Fight Caves end in a Fire Cape.

Premise

The Fight Caves are a solo combat trial. Sixty-three waves of monsters designed to test prayer switching, positioning, and self-control under fire. No party. No second chances. No reloading a save. You bring what you can carry, you kill what you can reach, you die if you get cocky. The final wave is a boss that has killed more adventurers than any other single fight in Aelgard. Completing it earns the Fire Cape, which is a prestige item visible on you forever, and opens the door to the Inferno, which is the harder version, which most people do not survive.

There is no meta-quest around this. You do it or you do not. The veteran who told you about it will not come with you. Nobody will. The only way to earn the cape is alone.

Steps

[1] Enter the cave through the hourly crack. Bring what you plan to bring
Where: Glass Desert, the crack
Requires: preparation
The crack seals behind you. The seal is not dramatic, which is worse.
[2] Survive waves 1-62 with prayer switches, positioning, and patient damage output
Where: Fight Caves
Requires: attack, ranged, prayer, hitpoints, hours
Waves 1 through 30 are the warm-up. You will not feel warmed up.
[3] Defeat the final boss on wave 63 with prayer switching and attention that does not falter
Where: Fight Caves, wave 63
Requires: prayer switches, rangers' discipline
The final boss changes attack style each half-second. Blinking is a decision.
[4] Claim the Fire Cape from the reward chest
Where: Fight Caves, reward chamber
Requires: being alive
The cape unfolds when you lift it. It is warm. It does not burn.

Twist

The reward chest offers you the cape or a second attempt at the trial with a handicap you pick. Choosing the handicap changes the cape's colour and adds a small passive to it. Most players take the cape on the first offer. Some come back for the recolour later, and the chest remembers their first answer.

Resolution

You walk out of the crack with a Fire Cape over your shoulders. The veteran at the camp claps, once, slowly. You are now a person who has done the Fight Caves. That will be true forever.

What it unlocks

The Fire Cape is yours — BIS melee cape until the Infernal Cape, the prestige item, the thing people notice when they stand next to you. The Inferno is now accessible, and entering it requires sacrificing your Fire Cape, which is a weight, which is the point.
