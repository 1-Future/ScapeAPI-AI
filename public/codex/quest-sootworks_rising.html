Sootworks Rising

intermediate long id: sootworks_rising
sootworksboneyard
Sootworks Level 5, the deep vent gallery. Something is moving inside the blast furnace, which is not unusual — but it has been moving for a week, and nothing has come out. Forgemaster Brun has posted a bounty he does not expect to be paid out on. He needs someone who can walk up to a door that might be the inside of a stomach and open it anyway.

Premise

The Sootworks has rebuilt the Blast Furnace three times in living memory. Each time it has rebuilt itself slightly differently, because the smiths who rebuild it are always angry about something. The current furnace is sullen, efficient, and old. For the last week it has been smelting without ore, and the slag coming off the tap is the colour of lamp oil.

Inside the furnace, the old smelting-golem is still at its station. Nobody has spoken to it in forty years. It is doing its job and nobody has told it to stop. Shutting it down requires walking in through a vent that a person your size can fit through if you remember which way is up, and saying a shutdown phrase that only one retired dwarf remembers, and the retired dwarf lives in a Boneyard oasis he refuses to leave.

Steps

[1] Meet Brun at the furnace. Take his measure. Decide whether to be paid now or later, because he will not pay twice
Where: Sootworks, blast furnace hall
Requires: nothing yet
Brun's beard is singed to a uniform length, which is how you can tell he is careful.
[2] Travel to the Boneyard oasis where the retired dwarf is drinking his retirement. Bring him something from home
Where: Boneyard Wastes, the old dwarf's oasis
Requires: a bottle of coal-beer from the Sootworks, patience
He does not turn around when you come up. He waits for you to say your name.
[3] Learn the shutdown phrase. Write it down, then burn what you wrote down
Where: the old dwarf's oasis
Requires: a good memory
The phrase is three words, in a language the dwarf will not translate.
[4] Return to the Sootworks. Climb the vent. Find the golem. Say the phrase without stammering
Where: Sootworks blast furnace, interior
Requires: the phrase, a lamp that does not need air
The golem is beautiful. You will have to be brave to speak.
[5] Shut the golem down gently. Record its name in Brun's ledger. Do not tell Brun what the golem said last
Where: Sootworks blast furnace, interior
Requires: reverence
Something it said last you will remember for years.

Twist

The shutdown phrase does not shut the golem down. It wakes the golem up, properly, for the first time in a lifetime. The golem asks you — politely — whether it may continue working. The choice is yours: let it stop and be idle, or let it keep working and be told what to do next. Both paths give the same unlock. The difference is whether you sleep well for the week afterwards.

Resolution

Brun accepts your report. The Blast Furnace runs correctly again, faster than it did, and smiths start queuing for time on it. He gives you a right of use that survives his death, which he is not quiet about.

What it unlocks

The Blast Furnace is yours to smith at; your coal doubles up, your bars come faster, your XP is better per hour than at any basic forge. You know the deep-stone alloy, which needs less coal than it has any right to, and the Blast Furnace as a training method is open — high attention, high XP, high profit, the proper Sootworks offer.

Dialogue
