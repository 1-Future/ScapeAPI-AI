The King's Last Edict

experienced long id: the_kings_last_edict
heartlands
Castle Heartstone library. Archivist Lenna has found a letter under a shelf that everyone assumed was an empty shelf. The letter is sealed with the last king's own wax, and the last king has been dead for four hundred years. The seal resists picking, resists reveal enchantments, and resists being brought to her desk: it prefers to stay in the shelf's shadow.

Premise

The last king of the Heartlands abdicated in a manner that was not recorded. The records simply say he stopped being king, and then he died, and then a council ruled until the present line. The letter under the shelf contains his last edict. Lenna, who is a careful woman, would prefer to read it quietly and file it, but she cannot open it alone.

The seal requires a reveal enchantment, a replica key cut from impressions in the letter's margins, and a willingness to walk into the throne room's hidden chamber beneath the floor. The puzzle inside the chamber is three rotating pillars that align by era — you will need to know four dates in Heartlands history and get them in the right order. The edict reads plainly once the seal parts: the king abdicated willingly, sealing something beneath the castle that he did not name. A Sealed Guardian waits in the deepest chamber, and it does not care whether you read the letter or not.

Steps

[1] Speak with Archivist Lenna. Examine the letter without touching its seal
Where: Castle Heartstone library
Requires: a magnifier
The seal is darker than the wax. It is not decorative.
[2] Attempt to pick the seal. Fail usefully. Research the king's cipher in the restricted section
Where: library restricted section
Requires: thieving, patience
Lenna unlocks the section with a look.
[3] Cast a reveal enchantment on the wax. Cut a replica key from impressions in the margins
Where: library
Requires: magic, crafting
The impressions are small and clear. Whoever sealed this left them on purpose.
[4] Enter the hidden chamber beneath the throne room. Align the three rotating pillars by era
Where: Castle Heartstone, hidden chamber
Requires: history, a clear head
The pillars turn under your hand the way a heavy door turns under the right shove.
[5] Read the true edict. Prepare for the Sealed Guardian
Where: hidden chamber
Requires: reading, combat readiness
The edict is shorter than expected. The king wrote with his own hand.
[6] Defeat the Sealed Guardian. Return to Lenna with the truth
Where: deepest chamber; library
Requires: combat, the edict
Lenna reads the edict three times. The third time she copies it into her own book.

Twist

The Sealed Guardian is not guarding a treasure. It is guarding a door the king wrote the edict to keep closed. Opening the door is an option — the quest does not force it — but the option is available, and Lenna will not tell you what to do. She will only record your choice.

Resolution

The letter is archived in Lenna's own book, under her own name. The hidden chamber is yours to visit. Whatever you did with the door below stands, and Lenna credits you as a scholar — a small title that opens a few doors elsewhere.

What it unlocks

You carry a Crown Shard, a fragment of the last king's regalia that opens specific locks in the Heartlands archives. The hidden chamber beneath the throne is a place you may enter, and Archivist Lenna counts you among her correspondents. Your Heartlands-history questions get answered properly from now on.

Dialogue

Archivist Lenna
The letter has been here the whole time. I have walked past it for fifteen years. I am not angry. I am instructed.
the Sealed Guardian
You have read the letter. You know what it says. Decide, and proceed. I am very tired.
