The Merchant Empire's Fall

master long id: the_merchant_empires_fall
heartlandssaltbrinemoryskahsootworks
The Drifting Market is docked in the Heartlands this week, and it is about to collapse. Not literally — the platform is sound — but the four-region empire of favours, fakes, and handshakes that holds it afloat is about to fall apart. Four factionheads are circling. An assassination is already drafted. Save it, inherit it, or watch it burn, but four regions are waiting on your answer.

Premise

The Drifting Market Charter was your work. The market has since grown into an empire of cross-region favours that no one person — not the original charter-holders, not the factionheads, not the Saltbrine pirate king's proxy — can hold together through the coming week. The Sootworks ore cartel wants control of distribution. The Moryskah relic broker wants the fakes channel closed. The Heartlands guild rep wants tax parity. The Saltbrine pirate king's proxy wants the seabed routes formalised. Each has a book of grievances. Each book contradicts the others.

You will negotiate, blackmail, or expose each factionhead. Your tools are inherited from prior arcs (Inspector's Loupe for fakes, the patrol cloak for Heartlands access, the pirate king's crown for Saltbrine backing, the Bog Witch's reputation in Moryskah). At the council dinner, someone will attempt to assassinate you with poisoned wine; you must catch it (Herblore skill and attention) before drinking. The ending branches by outcome: broker peace (the market drifts on), broker a coup (the market shatters into four regional markets), or broker an absorption (the market becomes yours, with you as the new charter-holder).

Steps

[1] Board the Drifting Market at its current dock (Heartlands)
Where: Drifting Market, Heartlands dock
Requires: drifting_market_charter completed
The market is bigger than it was. The platform is reinforced with Sootworks iron.
[2] Meet the four regional factionheads. Collect their books of grievances
Where: four factionhead cabins
Requires: none
Each book is written in the factionhead's own hand. The handwriting tells you a great deal about the person.
[3] Negotiate, blackmail, or expose each factionhead — player choice per head
Where: market-wide
Requires: thieving 70, magic 55, cooking 50 for the Saltbrine proxy's kitchen conversation
Each factionhead has exactly one weakness and one offer. You may use either, or both, or neither.
[4] Convene the council at the Drifting Market. Broker peace, coup, or split
Where: Market council chamber
Requires: charisma, and the evidence you gathered
The council is seated. The wine is poured. The assassination is in one of the glasses.
[5] Catch the poisoned wine at the council dinner
Where: council dinner table
Requires: herblore 55, attention
The poison smells of almonds. It is a common poison. The assassin is not.
[6] Stand by your outcome. The market drifts on, shatters, or becomes yours
Where: council chamber
Requires: none
Your outcome is recorded in the charter itself. The charter will be unreadable by anyone else after today.

Twist

The would-be assassin is the Heartlands guild rep — the factionhead who seemed most reasonable. She has been embezzling from the market for five years and needs the chaos of a transition to cover her exit. If you catch the poison before the council drinks, she will, instead of striking, turn quietly and leave; you will see her again in a future quest, in a different role, and she will owe you. This is only possible if you caught the wine in time. If you missed it, she escapes in the chaos, and the quest's aftermath is harder.

Resolution

The market is either saved, shattered, or yours. The Charter Seal is a ring; its stats depend on the outcome you chose — peace grants a discount at the market shops, coup grants unique access to four regional markets, absorption grants partial ownership and daily revenue. The four new specialty shops reopen on the Drifting Market, each region-coded, regardless of outcome — though their inventories differ per path. The Ring's teleport always returns you to the Drifting Market wherever it is currently docked.

What it unlocks

The Market Charter Seal is a ring; its stats vary by ending: peace grants a 10% Drifting Market discount, coup grants four regional market teleports, absorption grants partial ownership (daily coin revenue). Four new specialty shops reopen, one per region-factionhead, each with unique inventory — the peace path preserves all four, the coup path opens each only in its own region, and the absorption path places all four under your control. The Ring Teleport to the Drifting Market is always available regardless of dock location.
