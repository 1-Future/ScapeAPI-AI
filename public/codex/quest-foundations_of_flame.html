Foundations of Flame

experienced medium id: foundations_of_flame
sootworks
Sootworks. Brun's standing at the Crypt shaft with a fresh bruise on his jaw and a bar of iron that glows hotter than it should. Soot found a vein. Vein's hot. Vein's deeper than last time. Bring a pick. Bring a prayer. One of each will do.

Premise

Foundations of the Fallen opened the Crypt. Foundations of Flame opens the seam under it — a vein of fire-iron, an ore that stays molten until a human voice addresses it, and then, in the silence that follows, cools into a workable bar. Nobody knows why. Brun suspects the ore is shy. The mining is hot work, and the pipe that carries the ore to the Foundation Furnace must be tapped exactly three times; the Furnace must be lit from below, not above; and when all this is done, a flame-wight will crawl out of the seam to contest you for the ore. It is a short fight, if you are prepared.

The reward is the Foundation Furnace itself — a Sootworks training bench that runs 25% faster than any other Sootworks smithing bench, at the price of requiring fire-iron as a feedstock. You will also get the first fire-iron bar, which is stronger than steel and cooler to the touch, which is the ore's one polite trait.

Twist

The flame-wight is the first flame-wight ever brewed at the Foundation Furnace — not an enemy, strictly, but a worker who has been running the seam alone for a century. When you defeat it, it passes you a token: a rivet, small and glowing, that authenticates you to any fire-iron work across Aelgard. Brun pretends not to notice, but the second bar he hands you is bigger than the first.

Resolution

The Foundation Furnace is lit and yours to use. The Foundation Seam, a unique Sootworks mining area, is open — fire-iron ore respawns there, and the flame-wight returns weekly as a respawn boss for the rivet-token, which is a one-time item but which stacks with your existing smithing gear for permanent bonuses. Brun, who will never say it plainly, will nod at you for the rest of the month whenever you pass.

What it unlocks

The Foundation Furnace smithing bench runs 25% faster than any other Sootworks bench on fire-iron inputs, which is a breakpoint for smithing grinds into the 80s. The Foundation Seam is a unique mining area; the fire-iron ore it produces is both a smithing input and a quest-item gate for Sootworks grandmaster content. The fire-iron alloy recipe requires the ore-carrier to remain silent between mining and furnace, which is a gameplay-mechanic expressed as a buff/debuff (Silent Carrier) that the game tracks per session.
