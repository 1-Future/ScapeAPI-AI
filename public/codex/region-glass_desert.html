Prestige Goal: Slaying the Crystal Wyrm

The endgame. Defeat Veldrak the Crystal Wyrm — Aelgards ultimate tier 5 boss. Claim the Dragon Hunter Lance components.

Required skills: attack 90 ranged 90 magic 85 prayer 77 hitpoints 90 herblore 80

Bosses: veldrak_the_crystal_wyrm

Unique rewards: crystal_wyrm_scale_cape, dragon_hunter_lance

Sub-areas

  • The Glass Desert — Crystalline wasteland. The hardest region. Crystal Wyrm lives here. Endgame bosses and resources. [requires: mining 50, magic 45, prayer 35, agility 30, hitpoints 50, quest: echoes_of_the_deep]
  • Crystal Caverns — Crystal Wyrm lair. Tier 5 endgame boss. The hardest solo encounter in Aelgard. [requires: attack 80, ranged 80, magic 75, prayer 70, hitpoints 80, quest: the_last_dragon_p3]
  • The Fight Caves — 63 waves of combat. Fire Cape reward. The prestige challenge of Aelgard. [requires: ranged 60, prayer 45, hitpoints 60]
  • The Inferno — 69 waves. The hardest content in the game. Infernal Cape. Requires Fire Cape to enter. [requires: ranged 90, magic 85, prayer 75, hitpoints 90, quest: fight_caves]
