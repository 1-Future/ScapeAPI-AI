Bestiary

Veldrak is the last dragon of Aelgard. There were others. Veldrak is alone now because the others are dead, and Veldrak is alone now because Veldrak is what killed several of the others, and Veldrak is sleeping because the Crystal Sages — eleven of them, always eleven — each carry a fragment of the binding that holds the sleep, and the fragments are in the Sages' bodies, and they die with the Sage and are re-carved into the successor, and this has worked for eleven centuries and is beginning to slip. The Last Dragon quest chain is three parts, and the third part is the awakening, and the awakening is triggered by the player's choice. The player may attempt to keep Veldrak asleep, which is the Sages' preference, or the player may wake Veldrak, because the prophecy etched in the oldest crystal says that Veldrak must be fought, once, before a certain event it does not name, and the Sages have not decided whether they believe the prophecy. The player decides. The player does not get to un-decide. If woken, Veldrak rises, and the Glass Desert becomes a battlefield. Veldrak is the size of a small castle. His scales are crystalline and refract sunlight into a weapon. His breath is liquid fire that cools to crystal on the stone where it lands. He fights in three phases. Phase one is Crystal Breath. Veldrak stands, grounded, and exhales in long sweeping arcs. Pray Magic; the breath's damage reads as spell rather than fire. Avoid the crystallised ground, which will trap a running player. Phase two is Shattered Wings. Veldrak lifts. He becomes aerial and unreachable by melee. The ground around the player fills with crystal bombardment from above. Only ranged weapons hit him. Keep moving. Phase three is Enraged. Veldrak descends, grounded again, and fights with his wings as well as his jaws and claws. All three styles work. None of them work well. A Dragonsbane weapon — forged in the quest chain from Moryskah consecrated materials and Sootworks soot-iron — is the only weapon that reliably penetrates his hide at this stage. He is weak to stab. His scales overlap; the underbelly is vulnerable; a long pointed weapon reaches. He drops the Dragon Shard, a fragment of his crystallised heart that is the most valuable crafting material in Aelgard; Veldrak's Talon, a sword that has no rival in its slot; and Veldrak's Scale Mail, a chestplate whose weight is negligible and whose defences are absolute against dragonfire. He also drops, always, five full dragon bones. The bones do not look like the bones of other dragons. They are longer and paler. The Sages collect them quietly.

A long exhale. The sky over the Glass Desert, which had been fire-red for the duration of the fight, cools to the white it was before.
