Prayer

17 training methods. 8 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 1 Chaos Altar is accessible at ANY prayer level. 7x XP. THE wilderness economy of prayer training.
    unlocks: Chaos altar
  • level 13 Protect Item. You keep one more item on death. PvP and PvM risk calculation changes.
    unlocks: Protect Item prayer
  • level 37 Protect from Magic. The first protection prayer. Mages in PvP can no longer freeze-kill you.
    unlocks: Protect from Magic prayer
  • level 43 All three protection prayers. This is THE breakpoint. Bosses that were impossible become farmable. The game permanently changes.
    unlocks: Protect from Melee prayer · Protect from Missiles prayer
  • level 60 Chivalry. 15% attack, 18% strength, 15% defence boost. Massive DPS increase if you can sustain the drain.
    unlocks: Chivalry prayer (requires quest)
  • level 70 Piety. 20% attack, 23% strength, 25% defence boost. THE endgame melee prayer. All serious PvMers need this.
    unlocks: Piety prayer (requires quest)
  • level 74 Rigour. 20% ranged accuracy, 23% ranged strength, 25% defence. The ranged equivalent of Piety.
    unlocks: Rigour prayer (requires drop + quest)
  • level 77 Augury. 25% magic accuracy, 25% defence. The magic equivalent of Piety.
    unlocks: Augury prayer (requires drop + quest)
