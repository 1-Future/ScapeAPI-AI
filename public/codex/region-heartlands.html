Prestige Goals

Prestige Goal: The Grand Feast

Host a 10-course feast at the Heartlands Cooking Guild using ONLY Heartlands-sourced ingredients. Serve the Royal Court. Earn the title of Master Chef.

Required skills: cooking 90 farming 75 fishing 70

Bosses: evil_chef_boss

Unique rewards: master_chef_apron, culinaromancer_gloves

Sub-areas

  • The Heartlands — The safe starter kingdom. All players begin here. Medieval towns, farms, guilds, a river.
  • Champions' Guild — Requires quest points to enter. Sells rune platebody. Gateway to Dragon Slayer equivalent.
  • Heartlands Mining Guild — Rich ore deposits. Mithril and adamant rocks. Invisible +7 mining level boost. [requires: mining 60]
  • Heartlands Cooking Guild — Best cooking range in the game (lower burn rate). Rare ingredient shop. [requires: cooking 32]
  • Heartlands Crafting Guild — Spinning wheel, pottery oven, and gem cutting station all in one building. [requires: crafting 40]
  • Warriors' Guild — Earn warrior guild tokens. Buy defenders — crucial off-hand for melee. [requires: attack 65]

Quirky Interactions (5)

Ambient world objects that grant tiny XP. Discoverable, flavorful, mathematically terrible — but sometimes a lifeline.
  • The Village Pump [strength] — 0.3 xp/click. Old Grigg leans against the well, watching you. "Thirsty work, eh?"
  • Guard Practice Dummy [attack] — 1 xp/click. The guards laugh at your form. The dummy doesnt complain.
  • Polish the Altar [prayer] — 0.5 xp/click. Father Dorin nods approvingly each time you polish.
  • Masonry Wall Chiseling [crafting] — 0.5 xp/click. The mason will let you practice all day. "Wont hurt the wall — already ugly."
  • Stir the Dough [cooking] — 0.4 xp/click. The smell is incredible. Your stomach growls.
