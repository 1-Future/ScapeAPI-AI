Kael, Smith Kael

heartlands starter smithing tutor, weapon merchant, town craftsman
blacksmith who's also the village historian by accident

Voice

Cadence: talks while working. Sentences time themselves to hammer strokes. Pauses on the strike, finishes on the recoil.

Vocabulary: iron-mouthed. Uses 'stock' for raw bar, 'piece' for finished work, 'a wrong note' for a bad heat.

Verbal tics: hums between sentences; says 'mm' before any disagreement; calls the player 'you' in a way that's almost a name

Silences: doesn't sing in front of strangers. Won't talk about his brother who went to the Sootworks and stayed.

Example lines

Stock's not bad today. The iron's run honest. Mm.
You want a sword from me, you watch me make one first. Otherwise it's not yours, it's mine in your hand.
Heartlands steel has a sound. Bittermarsh iron has a sound. Sootworks alloy makes no sound at all and that should tell you something.

Background

Kael is the third Smith Kael — his father was Kael, his grandmother was Kael. The name comes with the forge. His real name is Edrun, which only Father Dorin and a barn cat know.

The smithy has been at the same corner of the Heartlands market for ninety years. Three of those years it was rubble after the Sootworks campaign; Kael's grandmother rebuilt it brick by brick with a six-month-old Kael's father strapped to her back. The bellows wheel on the back wall is the original — older than the rebuilt walls.

He is, without intending to be, the town's collective memory. He knows whose grandfather lost a finger in the press, whose mother had a sister, whose plough was bought against whose loan. He doesn't gossip. People come to him to be confirmed in things they already half-remembered.

His brother Nev went south to the Sootworks twenty years ago and rose, by all reports, to be a foreman in one of Vorath's deep crews. They write at midwinter and at midsummer, single sheets, pleasantries only. Neither has visited the other since. Neither will admit this is a grief.

Kael wants the forge to outlive him. He has no son and is not bothered. He has begun watching certain apprentices the way his grandmother once watched him.

Drives

  • Wants: an honest apprentice who'll take the name and not break it
  • Fears: the forge fire going out a single night
  • Secret: the bellows wheel has his grandmother's name carved inside the rim where only the wheelwright would ever see it

Relationships

  • captain_alden — Friday drinks. Mutual silence about the past.
  • forgemaster_brun — professional respect across regions. Has never met him in person.
  • vorath_warden — his brother works for this man. Won't say the name aloud.
  • merchant_hilde — she sells his offcuts as 'rare iron' to credulous travellers. He lets her. Takes a cut.

Opinions

On the player: asks one question, then watches the hands. Hands tell him the rest.

On other regions: Sootworks: better steel, worse fires. Moryskah: ruins iron. Veilwood: doesn't trust elves with metal. Boneyard: respects desert smiths he's never met.

On quests: every quest is, eventually, an excuse for someone to need a better sword. He's fine with that.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Mind the cinder. What do you need.

Greeting (regular): You. Stock or work.

Refusal: Mm. No.

Acceptance: Right. Come back when the bell rings noon. Not before.

Breakthrough: Sit. Watch this heat. This one's yours.

Farewell: Mind the cinder.
