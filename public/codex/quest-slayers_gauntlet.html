Slayer's Gauntlet

intermediate medium id: slayers_gauntlet
moryskah
Moryskah Slayer Tower, top floor. Slayer Master Varrek does not turn around when you come in. He is watching a spider through a window and taking notes. He will acknowledge you when you have killed a monster of each combat style and brought back the proof. He will not tell you which monsters. He will, however, offer tea.

Premise

Varrek is old, and old slayer masters do not take on students. They already have their name engraved somewhere. He is considering taking you on because Kael wrote him a letter, which is not something Kael does often. Varrek wants to see whether the apprentice's hunch holds.

The gauntlet is three kills, one per style. The monsters are your choice. If you choose badly — killing something that is obviously easy for you — Varrek will assign you harder work next time. If you choose honestly — a monster you are not confident about — Varrek will make your next work easier, because he will trust you to pick your own difficulty.

Steps

[1] Choose a melee quarry that will test you. Bring back the proof Kael told you about
Where: your discretion
Requires: a melee weapon, the right attitude
Spiders in the Moryskah cellar are the easy choice. The giant up the hill is the better one.
[2] Choose a ranged quarry. Do not pick something that stands still
Where: your discretion
Requires: a bow, arrows you like
A dark wizard moves. A training dummy does not.
[3] Choose a magic quarry. Not a thing you would ordinarily throw spells at. Something that you think will make you better if you succeed
Where: your discretion
Requires: runes, a spellbook
The hill giant is not usually killed with magic. That is rather the point.
[4] Return to Varrek in the order you killed. Set the proofs on his desk. Say nothing until he asks
Where: Moryskah Slayer Tower
Requires: the three proofs
He counts without looking up. He already knew the count.

Twist

Varrek will have watched one of your kills from a distance without your knowing. If you cheated, he knows. He will not accuse you. He will simply assign your next task as if you had failed, and you will notice.

Resolution

Varrek pours tea. He tells you the Tower's upper levels are yours to climb when you choose, and the intermediate tasks are available from his desk from now on. He gives you a gem that shows the current mark in its facets, wherever you are.

What it unlocks

You can receive slayer tasks from Varrek directly, which are harder and pay better than Kael's, and the Moryskah Slayer Tower is open to you above the second floor, where the creatures actually earn you a keep. An enchanted gem rides in your pocket; held to the light, it shows your current mark's shape, so you know what you are hunting without having to go back to ask.
