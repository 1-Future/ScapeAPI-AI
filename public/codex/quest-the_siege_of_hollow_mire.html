The Siege of Hollow Mire

master epic id: the_siege_of_hollow_mire
moryskahheartlandsveilwood
Hollow Mire. The vampyre armies have massed. Twelve hours until they arrive. The village has no army — a handful of allies, a shaking elder, and you. You will build barricades, recruit reinforcements from two regions, and hold the village through the night, or Moryskah as a whole falls.

Premise

The Siege of Hollow Mire is a four-wave defence. Build phase: barricades and archer platforms. Recruitment phase: Heartlands militia volunteers by wagon, Veilwood elven archers by invitation. Combat phase: four waves — vampyre thralls, vampyre mage, vampyre general, then a streets-level melee when the barricades fail. Dawn phase: a prayer to accelerate sunrise, repelling the vampyre army's remnant.

The village elder will grant Hollow Mire fortress to the survivor as a player-owned stronghold.

Steps

[1] Arrive at Hollow Mire. Build barricades and archer platforms
Where: Hollow Mire
Requires: construction
The villagers help. They are not builders. They try.
[2] Travel to the Heartlands and recruit militia volunteers
Where: Heartlands militia hall
Requires: persuasion
Captain Alden signs them up for you if he remembers you.
[3] Travel to Veilwood and request elven archers. They will send five
Where: Veilwood gate
Requires: respectful request
Five elven archers are, the elves claim, better than fifty human ones. You will test this.
[4] Wave 1: defend the east barricade from vampyre thralls
Where: east barricade
Requires: defence
The thralls are many. The barricade holds.
[5] Wave 2: counter-spell the vampyre mage bombarding from the tower
Where: village tower
Requires: magic
The mage's shadow fire stains the ground.
[6] Wave 3: engage the vampyre general in ranged combat from the archer platform
Where: archer platform
Requires: ranged
The general wears armour that does not reflect lamplight.
[7] Wave 4: melee combat in the village streets after the barricades fail
Where: village streets
Requires: defence, attack
The streets are narrow. The fight is close.
[8] Pray to accelerate sunrise. The vampyres retreat
Where: village square
Requires: prayer
The dawn comes three minutes early. The vampyres do not hurry. They recede.

Twist

The vampyre general will recognize you by name and ask, mid-fight, whether you remember them. Whether you respond changes a future cross-region diplomacy event. Choosing to ignore is safe. Choosing to answer is interesting.

Resolution

Hollow Mire stands. The elder grants you the fortress. The villagers sleep, finally. You have a base of operations in Moryskah.

What it unlocks

You hold the Hollow Mire fortress key. The fortress is your player-owned stronghold in Moryskah — a base with bank access, a forge, a kitchen, a guest room. The elder has put you in his will. The Heartlands militia and Veilwood archers will remember that you called on them.
