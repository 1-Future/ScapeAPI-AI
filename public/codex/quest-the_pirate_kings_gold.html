The Pirate King's Gold

experienced long id: the_pirate_kings_gold
saltbrine
Saltbrine wharf, before dawn. The Broken Crown is the only ship not pretending to be asleep. The pirate king — who now tolerates you, perhaps even likes you in a way he won't discuss sober — is standing on his own foredeck with a ledger in one hand and a bottle in the other, and would like you to sign aboard for a particular job. Two gold on the barrel, not a copper more. Bring a pick.

Premise

The pirate king's actual gold — not the Saltbrine treasury's, his, personally — was taken from him a very long time ago by a woman named Iseult Bloody Iseult, who is no longer alive, but whose drowned vault, off the Reach on the moonless side, very much is. Iseult locked the vault three times and then had the locksmith killed so that the vault would never be opened by anyone but herself; she then died in a storm, unhelpfully, and the vault has sat under three locks and a mile of coral-crust for about eighty years.

The pirate king would like it back, not because he needs the money — he has money — but because his ledger does not balance without it, and the ledger is the only thing that keeps him from becoming the kind of pirate king who drinks all day. You are signing on to a two-gold-a-head expedition, under his own captaincy, to crack the locks, mine the coral, read the inventory aloud so nobody pockets a coin, and bring the ledger home. You will get a fair share, and, if you conduct yourself well, something that is not on the ledger at all.

Steps

[1] Sign aboard the Broken Crown at the wharf for two gold on the barrel
Where: Saltbrine wharf
Requires: two gold, one good pair of boots
The pirate king writes your name in a blotted hand. He has not slept.
[2] Sail to the drowned vault off the Reach on a moonless tide
Where: Saltbrine, Reach waters
Requires: none
The sea is glassy. The crew is not singing. This is a serious trip.
[3] Dive to the vault door and spring the triple-lock
Where: vault door, 20 fathoms
Requires: thieving 55, agility 50, holding your breath
The locks are sulky with age. The third one knows it is about to lose.
[4] Mine through the coral-crust that has grown over the inner hatch
Where: the inner hatch
Requires: mining 50, a tolerance for barnacles
The coral has eaten a lot of teeth over eighty years. Do not lose yours.
[5] Read the vault inventory aloud, in full, so nobody can pocket a coin
Where: inside the vault
Requires: the ledger, a steady voice
The list is longer than it ought to be. Read every line.
[6] Return to the pirate king with his gold and his ledger. Let him weigh you
Where: Broken Crown quarterdeck
Requires: none
He weighs the gold first, then the ledger, then you. The scales are not literal.

Twist

Iseult's inventory includes one line nobody mentions on the read-through, because the line is about the pirate king himself — a debt she was owed, carried down from before she met him, that has not been paid. When you return, the pirate king does not pay it; he transfers it to you, on the grounds that you opened the vault and therefore must finish the ledger. The debt is not money. The debt is a favour to Iseult's only surviving grandchild. It will come due later.

Resolution

The pirate king pays the crew out, gives you a fair share, and then, without ceremony, pins a small silver pin to your jacket that marks you as a named friend of the Broken Crown. The Smugglers' Tunnel — a passage under the Reach, kept as a private route — is open to you now. The Quartermaster, a woman of severe patience, will sell you naval gear at thieves-cant prices. The ledger, closed and locked, sits on the pirate king's table; you will see it open again, someday, when Iseult's grandchild finds you.

What it unlocks

You walk the Smugglers' Tunnel from Saltbrine to the open sea cliffs in one minute flat, which used to take an hour. The Broken Crown Charter, a folded oilcloth, gives you free charter-ship travel to any port that respects the flag, which is most of them on the Reach. The Pirate King's Quartermaster opens her shop at odd hours for you specifically; her prices are not in any book.
