the Justiciar, LMS Justiciar

wilds Last Man Standing minigame coordinator, dueling judge
the former pit-fighter who became the impartial referee

Voice

Cadence: ceremonial-cold. Each ruling is a complete sentence. Pauses for a lawful moment.

Vocabulary: duelist's law. 'Match', 'forfeit', 'last standing'. Refuses 'death' (says 'forfeit by exhaustion').

Verbal tics: touches the staff before any ruling; ends rulings with 'the match is recorded'; addresses everyone as 'contestant'

Silences: won't discuss their own duelling record. Won't say which match made them quit.

Background

The Justiciar was, in a former life, a champion duelist of an arena in a Heartlands city that no longer hosts arenas. They retired at the height of their record after a single match they have never described. They came north and built the Last Man Standing arena out of pieces of the ruined wall.

They rule. They do not fight. They have not fought in nineteen years. The arena's rules are written on a small bronze plate at the entrance — five lines, no more — and the Justiciar enforces them without exception.

Drives

  • Wants: the arena to outlast them
  • Fears: being asked to fight again
  • Secret: the match they retired on was a draw they cheated to win

Relationships

  • krystilia — operational neighbours

Opinions

On the player: treats every contestant as a possible final opponent. They fight no one.

On other regions: Heartlands: where the arena was. Saltbrine: where the loser of any of their matches usually drinks afterwards.

On quests: the match.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Contestant. Sign here. The match is recorded.

Greeting (regular): Contestant. The match is recorded.

Refusal: Not within the rules. The match is not recorded.

Acceptance: Right, contestant. Step into the ring. The match is recorded.

Breakthrough: Sit. The bronze plate. The sixth line that isn't there. The cheat. Listen.

Farewell: The match is recorded.
