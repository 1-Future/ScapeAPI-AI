Prestige Goals

Prestige Goal: Song of the Elves

Complete the Veilwood ritual. Restore the Great Ash. Walk the path of the Moon Elder. Unlock the Inner Sanctum.

Required skills: agility 70 construction 70 farming 70 herblore 70 hunter 70 mining 70 smithing 70 woodcutting 70

Bosses: seren_shade, corrupted_elder

Unique rewards: crystal_halberd, elder_crown

Sub-areas

  • Veilwood — Enchanted elven forest. Dense canopy, hidden paths, druid circles. Requires agility to navigate. [requires: agility 15, woodcutting 10, quest: the_green_thumb]
  • Veilwood Canopy Hunting Grounds — High-level hunter area above the forest floor. Rare chinchompas and moonhawks. [requires: hunter 20, agility 15, quest: the_apprentice_trapper]
  • Veilwood Druid Circle — Sacred grove. Herblore and farming training with elven bonuses. [requires: farming 25, herblore 20, quest: roots_of_the_old_growth]
  • Veilwood Inner Sanctum — Heart of the elven forest. Crystal-enhanced magic training. Prerequisite for Prifddinas equivalent. [requires: agility 70, construction 70, farming 70, herblore 70, hunter 70, mining 70, smithing 70, woodcutting 70, quest: song_of_the_elves_aelgard]

Quirky Interactions (4)

Ambient world objects that grant tiny XP. Discoverable, flavorful, mathematically terrible — but sometimes a lifeline.
  • Chant with the Druids [prayer] — 1.2 xp/click. You dont understand the words. The druids smile anyway.
  • Trim the Sacred Vines [farming] — 0.6 xp/click. The vines seem to grow back the moment you look away.
  • Elven Archery Targets [ranged] — 2 xp/click. "Your technique is... primitive," the elf says.
  • Scry at the Moonpool [magic] — 1.5 xp/click. Brief, flickering images. A war. A throne. A broken crystal.
