The Last Light of the Old Sun

grandmaster epic id: the_last_light
heartlandssootworksmoryskahinkwealdsaltbrineveilwoodglass_desert
Heartlands observatory. The astronomer on duty says the sun is dimming on a timescale she can measure with a pocket watch. Crystal Sage Orin sends a letter by moonrunner: the old sun is dying, as was always going to happen, and the question now is whether Aelgard restores it or builds a new one, and either answer changes the map.

Premise

This is the quest that ends an era. It spans six regions and one weighty decision. The old sun was a compromise between four elder gods who have all been dead longer than the kingdom has been a kingdom. The compromise is coming apart. Orin believes it can be restored exactly as it was — same light, same seasons, same limitations. A faction in the Heartlands observatory believes a new sun can be forged, different, which is to say better in some ways and worse in others, and which will open a region that does not yet exist.

You must gather six components: a flame from the Sootworks, a tear from Moryskah, a dream from the Inkweald, a pearl from Saltbrine, a seed from Veilwood, a crystal from the Glass Desert. Each requires a small quest of its own, each calls in a favour from a region, each will test your patience. Then, at the central altar, you combine them and perform the Sun Restoration ritual. Then you choose.

Steps

[1] Hear Orin out. Hear the observatory out. Do not promise either
Where: Glass Desert and Heartlands observatory
Requires: an open mind
Orin's letter has three seals. The observatory's door has two, and one is broken.
[2] Sootworks: take a flame from the deepest hearth. The smiths will not give it freely
Where: Sootworks, deep forge
Requires: a favour owed
The flame sits in a lamp the size of your fist. It is very warm.
[3] Moryskah: take a tear from the Weeping Pool. Father Dorin must bless the vial
Where: Moryskah, Weeping Pool
Requires: prayer, herblore
The pool weeps without raining. Dorin's blessing is quiet.
[4] Inkweald: take a dream from a dreamer. Ask permission. Do not take without
Where: Inkweald, the central dreamer
Requires: magic, honesty
The dreamer nods in their sleep. You take only the colour of it.
[5] Saltbrine: take a pearl from a shell that chose you. Do not buy one
Where: Saltbrine Reach, the oyster beds
Requires: fishing, patience
The shell opens when your hand is right over it. Once.
[6] Veilwood: take a seed from the Heart-Oak. Elara must be present
Where: Veilwood, the Heart-Oak
Requires: farming, respect
The seed falls without being struck. You catch it without looking.
[7] Glass Desert: take a crystal from the Prism Throne. The throne may ask a question
Where: Glass Desert, Prism Throne
Requires: mining, courage
The throne asks what you will do with the sun. Answer it honestly.
[8] Combine the six at the central altar. Perform the Sun Restoration ritual
Where: the altar at the world's centre
Requires: herblore, crafting, prayer, magic
The altar sings. The sky listens.
[9] Choose. Restore the old sun, or forge a new one
Where: the altar
Requires: a decision
The choice is made in full daylight. There will not be another daylight like it.

Twist

The seventh component is not named on the list. It is your own certainty. Whichever choice you make, you must spend a measurable thing of yours — a year of age, a memory you were fond of, a scar — to pay for it. You will not be told the cost until you have made the choice.

Resolution

The sun comes up the next morning, either the same or different, depending on you. The world changes or does not. Your quest log records the choice. Orin writes you a letter that does not contain a reproach.

What it unlocks

If you forged a new sun, the Glass Desert's New Sun Zone opens — a region that did not exist before your hand, populated, curious, full of post-game content. Either way, the Eclipse Guardian awakens as a post-game boss with its own drop table. The Solar Blessing prayer is in your book: passive HP regeneration during the day, a quiet gift for a loud quest.
