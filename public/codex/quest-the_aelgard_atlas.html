The Aelgard Atlas

grandmaster epic id: the_aelgard_atlas
heartlandsboneyardmoryskahveilwoodsootworkssaltbrineinkwealdglass_desert
Heartlands Cartographers' Hall. Guildmaster Pell has a commission on the board that has been open for forty years. Nobody has completed it. The commission is to sketch one landmark in every region of Aelgard, from the correct vantage, and return with all eight sketches stitched into a single atlas. You, apparently, might be the fool.

Premise

The Aelgard Atlas is the Cartographers' Guild's white whale. Each landmark must be sketched from a vantage the guild specifies — a bluff, a boat, a bough, a buoy — and the sketch must be made in person, on-site, with the correct parchment and a steady hand. No region may be skipped. No sketch may be copied. The commission is not about producing a map; it is about proving a human has been everywhere.

You will sketch Castle Heartstone from the western bluff. The Great Skull in the Boneyard during a sandstorm. The Crimson Spire from across the Moryskah bog. The Crown Canopy in Veilwood, climbed with woodcutting spikes. The Grand Cogwheel from the Sootworks observation platform. The Lighthouse of Tides from a Saltbrine fishing boat. The Mirror Lake in the Inkweald, pinned with crafting tools because it keeps shifting. And the Prism Throne from the Crystal Overlook in the Glass Desert, climbed with agility most climbers lack.

Steps

[1] Accept the commission from Guildmaster Pell
Where: Heartlands Cartographers' Hall
Requires: eight blank parchments
Pell hands you the parchment with a bow you do not expect.
[2] Sketch Castle Heartstone from the western bluff, and the Great Skull in the Boneyard during a sandstorm
Where: Heartlands, Boneyard
Requires: crafting, survival
The sandstorm will not wait for you to finish. You finish faster.
[3] Sketch the Crimson Spire from Moryskah, and the Crown Canopy in Veilwood
Where: Moryskah, Veilwood
Requires: agility, woodcutting
The spire looks back. The canopy sways under you in a rhythm you begin to trust.
[4] Sketch the Grand Cogwheel from Sootworks, and the Lighthouse of Tides from Saltbrine
Where: Sootworks, Saltbrine
Requires: mining, fishing
The cogwheel's teeth are wider than your arm. You draw fewer than you see.
[5] Sketch the Mirror Lake in the Inkweald — pin it with crafting tools first
Where: Inkweald Mirror Lake
Requires: crafting, stillness
The lake keeps rearranging. Your pins hold.
[6] Sketch the Prism Throne from the Crystal Overlook in the Glass Desert
Where: Glass Desert, Crystal Overlook
Requires: agility
The overlook rewards the climb with a view you will not forget.
[7] Return to Guildmaster Pell with all eight sketches. The atlas is published in your name
Where: Heartlands Cartographers' Hall
Requires: the sketches
Pell stitches the sketches himself. He will not let anyone else touch them.

Twist

One of the landmarks will have moved since Pell last saw it — the Crimson Spire has shifted by several hundred feet, which will invalidate the guild's reference. You will have to decide whether to sketch it from Pell's old vantage (strictly correct per commission) or from the true current one (honest). Pell rewards either; differently.

Resolution

The atlas is yours as a completed item. The Guild publishes it in your name. You will be cited by future cartographers. You will walk across Aelgard and find that roadside stones mark the vantages you used.

What it unlocks

You carry the Aelgard Atlas (completed), which is a travel aid — it reveals paths you did not know about, based on the sketches it contains. The Cartographers' Guild will offer you commissions, for pay, forever. The Guild's teleport network is partially yours, in that you may use the vantages as fast-travel nodes.
