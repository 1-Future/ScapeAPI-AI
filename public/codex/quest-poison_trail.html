The Poison Trail

intermediate medium id: poison_trail
heartlandssootworksmoryskah
Heartlands. Three villages are drawing water from the same well network, and three villages are sick. The well-keeper has tested the water and found a slow poison she cannot identify. Upstream, somewhere, is a source. The source does not know it has been found yet, and you would like to keep it that way.

Premise

The poison is an industrial waste product. A rogue miner in the Sootworks has been dumping tailings into a drainage channel that feeds the Heartlands rivers. He does not know the full extent of his contamination. He is also protected by a bribery chain that leads to a secret laboratory on the Moryskah border, where someone is refining the poison for a more deliberate purpose.

You will investigate the well. Collect a water sample. Analyse it. Follow the river upstream to the Sootworks outflow. Steal the miner's logbook. Trace the laboratory. Neutralise the poison. Confront the saboteur. Return with proof.

Steps

[1] Investigate the poisoned well. Collect a water sample and analyse it
Where: Heartlands well
Requires: herblore
The water is clearer than it should be. That is the telltale.
[2] Follow the river upstream to the Sootworks mining outflow
Where: river; Sootworks outflow
Requires: tracking
The outflow stains the rocks a colour not on any map legend.
[3] Steal the miner's logbook
Where: Sootworks mine office
Requires: thieving
The logbook is in his back pocket. He does not notice.
[4] Trace the trail to a secret laboratory on the Moryskah border
Where: Moryskah border
Requires: tracking
The lab is in a farmhouse. The farmhouse is too clean.
[5] Neutralise the poison agent. Confront the saboteur. Return to Heartlands with proof
Where: lab; Heartlands well
Requires: herblore, combat
The saboteur's confession is written on a rag they wanted to eat.

Twist

The saboteur has a sick child in Moryskah. The poison was to earn money for a cure — a circular tragedy. You can still bring them to justice, or arrange a different resolution (herblore cure for the child, in exchange for confession and ceasing).

Resolution

The well is clean. The village recovers. The Heartlands constable files your report, or does not, depending on what you negotiated. You walk away with a satisfaction that does not fit neatly in a bag.

What it unlocks

You carry a herblore apprentice's badge from the Heartlands guild, a small credential that improves herblore prices and adds a handful of XP to each successful brew for a while. The Heartlands river system is slightly cleaner because of you, which is a thing you did.
