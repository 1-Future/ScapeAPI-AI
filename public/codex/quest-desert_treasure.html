Desert Treasure

master epic id: desert_treasure
boneyardglass_desertinkwealdmoryskahsootworks
Boneyard Wastes, the Pyramid's outer courtyard. An archaeologist in black silks and a sun-bleached scarf is pacing around a scale model of the pyramid she built herself, and she is counting diamonds on her fingers. Four diamonds. Four regions. Four guardians. She wants the Ancient Magicks, and she cannot get them alone, and she has been waiting for someone who can cast a novice spell without flinching to finish what she started.

Premise

The Ancient Magicks were sealed out of Aelgardi practice by the Pyramid's first priesthood, who judged that the old spells killed too freely. The seal is four diamonds, set in four shrines, in four regions; break all four and the seal parts and the spellbook becomes readable. The diamonds are not guarded in the ordinary sense. They are bound: to ice, to shadow, to blood, to smoke. Each binding has a guardian, and each guardian is not a monster — they are judges.

Azzanadra is the name of the magician whose book this is. He is not in the book. He is, according to the archaeologist, watching. The Ancient Magicks include ice barrage, blood barrage, smoke barrage — the sort of spells that change how a fight ends. The seal was put in place for a reason. Breaking it is work.

Steps

[1] Meet the archaeologist. Listen to her four-diamonds theory. Agree or disagree honestly
Where: Boneyard Wastes, the Pyramid courtyard
Requires: patience for theory
Her scale model has moved since yesterday. She swears she has not touched it.
[2] Find the Ice Diamond in the Glasswake Passage, north of the Glass Desert. Survive cold that is not temperature
Where: Glass Desert, Glasswake Passage
Requires: firemaking for warmth, a willingness to be judged
The cold asks you a question before you reach the diamond. The question is about a winter you do not often remember.
[3] Find the Shadow Diamond in the Inkweald dream labyrinth. Do not trust your reflection
Where: Inkweald, dream labyrinth
Requires: a dream anchor, an honest memory
The labyrinth is a version of the pyramid laid on its side. The labyrinth knows.
[4] Find the Blood Diamond in Castle Malachar. Defeat the blood guardian without taking the blood it offers you
Where: Moryskah, Castle Malachar
Requires: prayer, discipline
The guardian extends a goblet. Decline politely.
[5] Find the Smoke Diamond in the Sootworks deep furnace. Survive heat that is not temperature
Where: Sootworks, deep furnace
Requires: mining to dig the chamber out, nerves
The smoke asks you what you burn for. You will not like your first answer.
[6] Combine the four at the ancient altar in the pyramid. Read the spellbook while the seal breaks
Where: Boneyard Pyramid, altar chamber
Requires: the four diamonds
The altar recognises the shape you have made. It does not like the shape, and it permits you anyway.
[7] Defeat the final guardian who rises as the seal parts. Do not use the Ancient Magicks against it, unless you want your first spellbook memory to be a fight
Where: altar chamber
Requires: your existing combat, prayer, patience
It is old, and tired, and offering you the book with one hand while it fights with the other.

Twist

The fourth guardian is not a guardian. It is Azzanadra, or what remains of him, and he wants you to take the book because he is finally done carrying it. You must decide whether to accept the book at face value, or to ask what he would like done with his name once it leaves his mouth.

Resolution

The Ancient Magicks are yours. The pyramid does not close behind you — it opens further. The deeper chambers are accessible now, and they are quieter than you expect, because the book was the loud thing.

What it unlocks

You carry the Ancient Magicks spellbook — ice barrage, blood barrage, smoke barrage, and the terrifying lesser spells that are quieter than their names suggest. The Boneyard Pyramid is open to its interior; the lower floors contain training no other place offers, and secrets you will uncover at your own pace.

Dialogue

the archaeologist
Four diamonds, four shrines, four regions. The seal is clever. The seal was not meant to be permanent. If it was permanent it would not be a seal, it would be a wall.
Azzanadra
Take the book. I have been its librarian long enough. Do not read it aloud where a child might hear.
