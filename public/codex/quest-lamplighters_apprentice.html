Lamplighter's Apprentice

novice short id: lamplighters_apprentice
moryskah
Moryskah border at dusk. The lamps along the old post road are going out one by one, and the lamplighter — a thin man named Voss, who walks with a beech pole and hums an old song — is losing ground to the dark. He needs an apprentice, not for wages, but because something is following him from lamp to lamp and he would rather not be followed alone.

Premise

The Moryskah lamps are older than the post road. They were set down when the swamp was first named, by a guild of lighters who made pacts with the things that live between lit and unlit. The lamps do not run on oil. They run on being tended by a known hand. When Voss is the only hand, the thing that follows remembers him too clearly.

There is a particular tinderbox, kept in a particular box, in a particular cupboard in Voss's cottage. His master gave it to him the night she stopped coming home. It lights blessed wood on the first strike, which matters when the swamp air will not take a flame. Voss will lend it to you tonight if you walk the lamps with him, and he will let you keep it if you walk the loop alone tomorrow without dropping one.

Steps

[1] Shadow Voss on his route and learn why he hums that particular song at every third lamp
Where: Moryskah border post road
Requires: none
The wick takes the flame like a mouth takes a wafer. The thing at the edge of the light holds its breath.
[2] Gather the small wooden offerings each lamp expects — different at each post — before you go round again
Where: three Moryskah hamlets
Requires: a twist of wolfsbane, a coin minted before the war, a child's milk tooth left by parents who had no better grave
Nobody asks why you want these things. Everybody knows.
[3] Walk the route alone at moonset and light every lamp without looking directly at what is watching you from between them
Where: Moryskah border post road
Requires: the tinderbox, the offerings
The third lamp is where the hum starts. If you forget it, the lamp will not forget you.

Twist

The last lamp on the route is dark, and the metal is cold, and the offering in the pan is gone — taken before you got there. You must choose: skip it (Voss says one missed lamp is survivable, two is not), or relight it with what you carry on your own person (the bracelet, the brooch, the lock of hair from the person you miss most). Neither choice is wrong. Both are remembered.

Resolution

You return to the cottage at dawn. Voss is alive, which he was not certain he would be, and he hands you the tinderbox in both hands. He tells you to come back on the shortest night of the year to walk the route with him, or for him. The lamps along the border are yours to travel now, each to each, without the road in between.

What it unlocks

You carry a tinderbox shaped like a squat iron acorn, worn smooth by two lifetimes. It catches any wood you strike it over, blessed or cursed, and the Moryskah border lamps now recognize your hand. Touching a lit lamp lets you step to any other lamp on the route as if the road between them had been folded up and put in a drawer.

Dialogue
