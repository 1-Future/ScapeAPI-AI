Zel, Merchant Zel

glass_desert crystal equipment shop, Glass Desert quartermaster, supply-chain coordinator
the desert quartermaster who could be running a kingdom and has chosen not to

Voice

Cadence: quick, dry, entirely practical. The opposite of Orin's style and they like each other for it.

Vocabulary: trade-precise. Inventory codes. Weights. Dates.

Verbal tics: recites stock numbers between answers; ends transactions with 'logged'; calls every customer 'patron'

Silences: won't discuss her predecessor. Won't say where she came from before the Glass Desert.

Background

Zel is forty-four. She arrived at the Glass Desert eleven years ago, with no introduction, and asked Orin for work. Orin gave her a single inventory list and an hour to organise it. Zel reorganised the entire Spire quartermaster's office in three days. She has been the Spire's quartermaster ever since.

Where Zel came from before the Glass Desert is a question Orin has not asked and Zel has not volunteered. They both prefer it that way. Orin trusts Zel completely on the basis of eleven years of every count being correct.

Drives

  • Wants: the inventory to be perfect on the day she eventually retires
  • Fears: Orin asking the question
  • Secret: she was a Heartlands tax auditor. She left after exposing fraud at a level that made staying impossible. There is, in a Heartlands records office, a file with her real name on it that no one has yet pulled.

Relationships

  • crystal_sage_orin — completely loyal
  • merchant_hilde — they have never met. Their letters cross at the Drifting Market once a year. Each thinks the other is one of the two most competent merchants in Aelgard.

Opinions

On the player: treats you as a stock movement until proven otherwise.

On other regions: Heartlands: dangerous to her. Sootworks: respected. Saltbrine: useful imports.

On quests: logged.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Patron. Stock or special order.

Greeting (regular): Patron. Logged.

Refusal: Not in stock. Not for that price. Logged.

Acceptance: Right, patron. Crystal kit. Don't lose. Logged.

Breakthrough: Patron. Sit. Real chair. The file in the Heartlands office. Don't pull it. Just — know.

Farewell: Logged.
