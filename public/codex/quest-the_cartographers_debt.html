The Cartographer's Debt

experienced medium id: the_cartographers_debt
boneyard_wastes
Boneyard market, noon. Cartographer Nessa is sitting under her awning with a half-drawn map on her knees and a cup of sand in her lap. She does not look up when you approach. The desert owes no water, and the cartographer owes the desert a map. Whispered, it was, that her last map was wrong. Whispered, it was, that the dunes moved to prove her wrong.

Premise

Nessa drew the Boneyard's last official map twenty years ago. It was correct on the day she finished it. It has been gradually wrong for nineteen years, not because of her skill but because the dunes, which are a slow and patient committee, have moved six landmarks to positions that flatter no mapmaker. Nessa, who is scrupulous, considers the error a personal debt. She would like to repay it. She would also like company, because the Singing Dunes at sunset — the only time the dunes consent to be measured — are not a place a sensible person walks alone.

You will survey seven landmarks: three by agility through shifting sand, two by thieving past sand-wraiths who guard specific stones, and two by reading old glyphs that require you to be silent around them. You will burn her old map at dawn, in front of her, which is a ritual her family has done for three generations. You will redraw the new map together, with both your names on it. And you will drink the last of her water, because she is paying a debt.

Steps

[1] Find Cartographer Nessa in the Boneyard market. Do not greet her first
Where: Boneyard market, Nessa's awning
Requires: none
She will speak when she is ready. The half-drawn map has a stain on it.
[2] Walk with her to the Singing Dunes at sunset
Where: Boneyard dunes
Requires: none
The dunes hum as the sun sets. The hum is not tuneful. It is a habit.
[3] Survey three landmarks by agility through shifting sand
Where: three dune-marks
Requires: agility 55
The sand shifts as you measure. Your measurement must be faster than its shift.
[4] Survey two landmarks by slipping past sand-wraiths who guard specific stones
Where: two dune-stones
Requires: thieving 50
The wraiths guard by hearing, not sight. Walk softly.
[5] Survey two landmarks by reading old glyphs in silence
Where: two buried steles
Requires: herblore 40 for identifying desert flora on the steles
The glyphs are dry but legible. They must be read without speaking aloud.
[6] Return to her tent at dawn. Burn the old map in front of her
Where: Nessa's tent
Requires: firemaking 45
She will not light it. You must.
[7] Help her redraw the new map. Both names go on it
Where: Nessa's table
Requires: a steady hand, an honest survey
She will write your name first, which is a formal apology.
[8] Drink the water she offers. It is the last of her supply
Where: Nessa's tent
Requires: open hands
It is warm, and it is flat. She watches while you drink.

Twist

One of the seven landmarks you survey is not a landmark; it is Nessa's mother's grave, which has been slowly walking across the desert for nineteen years, carried by the dunes in the direction of the tent where Nessa has been working. You will realise this during the survey, because the glyphs at the seventh landmark will spell her mother's name. Nessa will not ask what you saw. You may tell her, or you may not. Either answer is remembered.

Resolution

The new map is finished. Nessa's Map is a dynamic quest item — right-click any Boneyard tile once per hour to teleport to it; the map updates in real time as the dunes move, which, in the Boneyard, matters. The Singing Dunes survey method opens as a repeatable training node, with bonus XP at sunset only. Nessa takes you on as her commissioning partner, which means, going forward, you may commission her to draw specific new maps — each commission being a mini-quest with a teleport unlock at the end.

What it unlocks

Nessa's New Map is a dynamic teleport tool for the Boneyard: right-click any visited tile to teleport once per hour. The map updates when the dunes move, which happens weekly in-game. The Singing Dunes survey is a repeatable agility/thieving method with bonus XP at sunset, which syncs with the desert's daily cycle. The cartographer partnership means you may request new maps for specific Boneyard regions, each of which is a small commissionable quest with a unique teleport at the end.
