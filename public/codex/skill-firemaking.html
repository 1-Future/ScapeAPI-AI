Firemaking

40 training methods. 3 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 30 Willow logs. The efficient firemaking training tier.
    unlocks: Burn willow logs
  • level 50 Access to the Wintertodt minigame. Firemaking goes from "why bother" to "this is actually fun."
    unlocks: Wintertodt minigame — group firemaking boss
  • level 70 Deep-coal furnace unlocked. 245k firemaking XP/hr. Sootworks becomes the fastest firemaking region in Aelgard.
    unlocks: Deep-coal furnace master burn
