Recipe for Disaster — The Engineer

experienced medium id: rfd_sootworks
sootworks
Drifting Market. Engineer Fizz is frozen mid-adjustment of a clockwork mechanism she was demonstrating to the Merchant Prince. Her assistant explains: she will thaw only for a clockwork cake, baked to precise specifications, in a temperature-controlled oven you must build yourself.

Premise

Fizz's cake is mechanical. The cake must be baked at exactly the right temperature. The decorative frosting must be molded from a precision frame. The tin must be made of soot-iron smithed to her own tolerances. You will need a smithing bench, a crafting bench, a construction kit, and a cooking fire calibrated to the degree.

The Culinaromancer will try to destabilize the oven. You will need to build a passive temperature regulator — a small, elegant piece of engineering that Fizz herself would approve of.

Steps

[1] Smith a precision cake tin from soot-iron
Where: Sootworks smithing bench
Requires: smithing
The tin weighs exactly what Fizz said it would. She has a note somewhere.
[2] Craft the decorative frosting mold
Where: Sootworks crafting bench
Requires: crafting
The mold is a map of a gear train in sugar-cast.
[3] Build a temperature-controlled oven
Where: Drifting Market kitchen
Requires: construction
The oven hums at the right pitch.
[4] Cook the cake at exactly the right temperature
Where: the oven
Requires: cooking
The cake rises in a spiral. That is exactly right.
[5] Serve the cake to free Engineer Fizz
Where: feast hall
Requires: the cake
She thaws faster than the others, because her clock was already running.

Twist

Fizz will, mid-thaw, point at a flaw in your oven regulator. Fixing the flaw takes a minute. Ignoring the flaw does not cost you the quest, but the oven will catch fire three days later, which is an issue for the Merchant Hilde and will cost you favour there.

Resolution

Fizz returns to her demonstration. You are three of five. The assistant applauds softly.

What it unlocks

Engineer Fizz will design you one small clockwork item of your choice — a pocket clock, a self-winding lamp, a mechanical key — on commission. The Sootworks' precision bench is unlocked for you, which is a small but notable improvement for mechanical crafting.
