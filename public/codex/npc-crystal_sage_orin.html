Orin, Crystal Sage Orin

glass_desert Echoes of the Deep quest-giver, Glass Desert faction leader, crystal mining instructor
the scholar who walked away from a dwarven Wardenship to study glass

Voice

Cadence: minimal, glass-edged. Each sentence is a shape. Cuts the air.

Vocabulary: lapidary. 'Cut', 'cleavage', 'edge'. Refuses the word 'magic' (says 'refraction').

Verbal tics: taps a small crystal on the desk between answers; ends statements with the angle of cleavage in degrees; addresses the player as 'visitor'

Silences: won't talk about the Sootworks. Won't acknowledge the Wardenship. Will not say the name 'Vorath'.

Background

Orin is — well. Orin is the missing sister of Vorath, Warden of the Deep Vein. Thirty-one years ago she walked out of the Sootworks on the morning of her ascension, walked east across the continent in a journey that took eleven months, and arrived at the Crystal Spire of the Glass Desert. She has been there ever since. She took the name Orin, which is a compressed Sootworks word for 'edge'.

She is, by any measure, a brilliant lapidary scholar. She has spent thirty-one years studying the way light cleaves through Glass Desert crystal. She has trained a small school of acolytes. She has corresponded with Vorath in code for twenty-eight of those years and has never once mentioned the Sootworks, the Wardenship, or her own birth name. Vorath has never once asked.

She wrote her brother an eleven-word note when she left. He keeps it locked in his desk. The note is in the old dwarven formal: 'I was not made to be Warden. I am sorry.' She has never reread it. She remembers every word.

Orin is an excellent quest-giver. The Echoes of the Deep quest is a careful initiation into Glass Desert work. She does not waste an applicant.

Drives

  • Wants: her brother to forgive her without her ever asking him to
  • Fears: the Crystal Spire being mined out before the work is done
  • Secret: the eleven words

Relationships

  • vorath_warden — her brother. They write in code. Neither says the word.
  • merchant_zel — her quartermaster. Trusts her completely.
  • archaeologist_veris — academic correspondent. They have not met.

Opinions

On the player: tests with a single crystal cut. The player either holds it wrong or right.

On other regions: Heartlands: too much wood. Veilwood: too much wood, more politely. Sootworks: not discussed. Moryskah: a wound. Boneyard: the desert that taught her.

On quests: Echoes of the Deep is the entry exam.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Visitor. Hold this. Don't drop it. Forty-six degrees.

Greeting (regular): Visitor. The cut held.

Refusal: The cut declines. Forty-six.

Acceptance: Right. The Echoes are yours. Bring them back unbroken.

Breakthrough: Sit. There is a brother. There are eleven words. You will not deliver them. You will know them. Forty-six.

Farewell: Edge clean.
