Prestige Goals

Prestige Goal: The Clockwork Heart

Build and activate the Sootworks Titan — a massive clockwork golem that patrols the foundry. Forge its heart, animate it, and best it in combat.

Required skills: smithing 85 construction 75 crafting 70 mining 70

Bosses: sootworks_titan

Unique rewards: clockwork_heart_core, steampunk_greataxe

Sub-areas

  • The Sootworks — Clockwork industrial underground. Dwarven forges, steam vents, mechanical puzzles. [requires: mining 25, smithing 15, construction 10, quest: foundations_of_the_fallen]
  • Sootworks Blast Furnace — Halves coal requirements for smelting. Best smithing XP/hr but requires constant attention. [requires: smithing 30, quest: sootworks_rising]
  • Sootworks Deep Mines — Runite ore deposits, gem rocks, and the entrance to the metal dragon den. [requires: mining 55, defence 40, quest: dragon_slayer_aelgard]
