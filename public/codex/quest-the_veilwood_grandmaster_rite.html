The Veilwood Grandmaster Rite

grandmaster epic id: the_veilwood_grandmaster_rite
veilwood
Whispered, this rite is — not often spoken of, because once a century only, walked it is, and most of a century's work it takes to be invited. Asked, you now are, because the covenant you made with the druids was seen to be serious, and because, counted across eight Veilwood skills, your mastery is sufficient. Ready, whether you are, the woods will decide.

Premise

The Inner Rite is an eight-gate examination, one gate per Veilwood-aligned skill: agility, construction, farming, herblore, hunter, mining, smithing, woodcutting. Each gate must be passed on its own terms, and the woods, who do not like being measured, will test your mastery in ways that will feel, slightly, like being pranked by a competent sibling. If you complete all eight, the Seren Shade — the last remembering presence of the Veilwood's oldest elder — will receive you at the Inner Sanctum, and you will be offered the Elder Crown, which will not be placed on your head by her but carried home.

The rite is not a combat challenge. It is a demonstration. You do not fight the Seren Shade; you stand before her, and she greets you in a way that is difficult to survive not because it is violent but because it is a lot of greeting. You will feel, briefly, every memory of the Veilwood pass through you. You will not remember most of it afterwards, but you will remember enough.

Steps

[1] At the eight skill-gates of the Veilwood, kneel and offer proof — mastery of each of the eight covenant skills
Where: eight gates scattered across Veilwood
Requires: the listed level in each skill
The gates will recognise you by the calluses on your hands, not by any token you might carry.
[2] Walk the canopy-bridge barefoot, with what you carry in your own hands
Where: Veilwood canopy bridge
Requires: agility 80, no bags
The bridge is friendly to bare feet. It is rude to bags.
[3] Build a shrine of Veilwood ironwood, no nails, no mortar
Where: a glade near the Inner Sanctum
Requires: construction 80, smithing 75
The joinery is particular. The wood will sigh when you get it right.
[4] Raise a Great Ash seedling to full height in one night, by farming alone
Where: a prepared plot
Requires: farming 80, herblore 80
The seedling is larger than a child by dawn. It remembers the night.
[5] Hunt the Moon Elder without weapons — only traps
Where: the high forest
Requires: hunter 75, cleverness
The Moon Elder will walk into the right trap. You must build the right trap.
[6] Mine the one crystal-node that grows inside the shrine you built
Where: the shrine you built, yesterday
Requires: mining 75, a careful touch
The node is not random. The shrine summoned it by being correct.
[7] Stand before the Seren Shade. Survive her greeting
Where: Inner Sanctum
Requires: an open mind
She will greet you with the Veilwood's entire memory. Do not flinch. Do not thank her yet.
[8] Accept the Elder Crown. Do not put it on. Carry it home
Where: Inner Sanctum
Requires: open hands
She will not place it. She will hand it.

Twist

The Moon Elder, who you hunt in step 5 without weapons, is a former druid who chose this shape instead of the covenant-tree transformation. She lets you catch her. She has been waiting for a druid-aligned outsider to catch her so that she can be released back into a human form, briefly, for a conversation with the Seren Shade in step 7 that she has been owed for a century. That conversation happens behind you while the Seren Shade greets you. You will not hear it. Afterwards, the Moon Elder will thank you, quietly, and return to her hunter form.

Resolution

You carry home the Elder Crown, never having placed it on your head. The Veilwood is open to you now in a way it was not — the Inner Sanctum is a place you can return to; the Moonwell is a place you can drink from once a day for a full prayer refill; the Seren Shade's crystal-crafting is available to you at her bench, and it makes gear nothing else in Aelgard makes. The Moon Elder will nod to you the next time you cross her path. That nod is, in Veilwood grammar, a long and effusive thank-you.

What it unlocks

You wear the Elder Crown on formal occasions and carry it otherwise; equipped, it grants +1 to every Veilwood-aligned skill level for any training method within the Inner Sanctum, which is the largest flat boost in Aelgard outside of prestige items. The Moonwell refills prayer once per day — critical for long Inner Sanctum training runs. Seren's Crystal Crafting, the Seren Shade's bench, produces the best crystal gear in Aelgard, and only at her bench; the crystals must be mined from Inner Sanctum nodes, which are now accessible.
