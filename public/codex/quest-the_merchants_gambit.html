The Merchant's Gambit

experienced long id: the_merchants_gambit
heartlandssaltbrinesootworksveilwood
Heartlands Grand Bazaar. Merchant Prince Davos has a manifest the length of his forearm and three game days to assemble it. He needs a courier. The competitors are willing to kill their rivals' couriers. He is offering better money than usual, which means the job is worse than usual.

Premise

Davos is assembling a trade-master's key — a physical set of four items from four regions that, combined, creates a key to the bazaar's inner market. Smoked kraken tentacle from Saltbrine. A prototype clockwork music box from Sootworks. An elven crystal pendant from Veilwood. And a courier (you) to deliver all four to Heartlands within three game days.

Rival merchants have their own candidates. They will ambush you at the Saltbrine docks. Sootworks guards will chase you through steam vents after the theft. Veilwood's gate will evaluate you. Heartlands will reward you. Davos will reveal what the set of four items actually is, and you will have the option to steal the key yourself — a course of action Davos is prepared for.

Steps

[1] Receive the manifest from Davos at the Heartlands Grand Bazaar
Where: Heartlands Grand Bazaar
Requires: attention
Davos hands you the manifest folded into a cone. The cone is a map.
[2] Saltbrine: acquire smoked kraken tentacle from the dockside chef. Fight or flee the rival ambush
Where: Saltbrine docks
Requires: cooking, a plan for ambushes
The chef watches you eat a sample. It is his test.
[3] Sootworks: steal the prototype clockwork music box. Escape through the steam vents
Where: Sootworks, display workshop
Requires: thieving, agility
The music box plays one note when touched. That note is the alarm.
[4] Veilwood: craft an elven crystal pendant from raw materials
Where: Veilwood gate workshop
Requires: crafting
The gate examines the pendant. It approves, barely.
[5] Return to Heartlands within three game days. Deliver all four items to Davos
Where: Heartlands Grand Bazaar
Requires: the items, the key
Davos counts. He does not thank.

Twist

Davos will reveal the combined set forms a trader's key to the bazaar's inner market. He will offer to sell you the key back. You may pay, you may refuse, or — if you were quick enough and clever enough — you may have already swapped one of the items during delivery, keeping the real key for yourself. This gives a different unlock (a permanent bazaar access card for you alone).

Resolution

Davos pays. The inner market opens to you. The trader's signet ring is yours. Whichever path you took, you are now a recognized face in the bazaar, and your prices reflect that.

What it unlocks

You wear a Trader's Signet Ring, which improves bazaar prices and unlocks the Heartlands inner-market shops. Your three-region trade route is warmer to you now; the chef at Saltbrine will slip you a free sample, the Sootworks guards will nod you past their lesser doors, and the Veilwood gate will not slow you more than twice.

Dialogue

Merchant Prince Davos
A manifest is a test of character. The items are incidental. What I will buy is what you bring back, and how.
the rival merchant
