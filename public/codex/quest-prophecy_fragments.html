Prophecy Fragments

experienced long id: prophecy_fragments
glass_desertheartlandsmoryskahveilwoodsootworkssaltbrineinkweald
The desert is thirsty. The sage's tongue is dry. Three lines of the Glass Prophecy, he holds; six he does not. Walk six regions, parched prophet says; bring six fragments. Read them, we will, together, when all nine are on the table. The glass is listening. Hurry.

Premise

Crystal Sage Orin has been reading the first three lines of the Glass Prophecy since he was a young man. They have told him, so far, that a great wyrm sleeps, that a sage will be called, and that the desert will know when the glass is listening. Only three lines. Orin has been patient. He is still patient. But he is now old, and the sky is cracking on the edges, and the other six lines will need to be found and read in company, because a prophecy is not a prophecy until it is heard.

The six missing lines are, by the sage's tables and the cartographer's maps and your own instincts after several quests worth of crossing borders, in six specific objects in six specific regions. A page in a cathedral choir-book that nobody sings from. A tooth in a Moryskah reliquary nobody opens. A leaf in a Veilwood book that closes itself. A rivet in a Sootworks wall that nobody taps. A barnacle on a Saltbrine hull that nobody scrapes. A line in a dream you have not yet had. Each one is a minor recovery — no boss, no major fight — but each one is a statement about the region it sits in, and by the time you gather all six, you will understand Aelgard in a way the prophecy has been waiting for you to understand it.

Steps

[1] Meet Crystal Sage Orin at his tower. Hear the first three lines
Where: Glass Desert, Orin's tower
Requires: none
The tower is neat, which is a surprise. Orin is tidy in a way desert men sometimes become.
[2] Recover the Heartlands fragment — a page in the cathedral choir-book nobody sings from
Where: Heartlands cathedral, choir stall
Requires: thieving 55, a voice quiet enough not to sing
The choir-book has not been opened in forty years. The page is loose; it has been waiting.
[3] Recover the Moryskah fragment — a tooth in a reliquary nobody opens
Where: Moryskah, a reliquary in an abandoned chapel
Requires: prayer 45, a hand steady around old bone
The tooth is small and yellow. It will rattle if you walk too fast.
[4] Recover the Veilwood fragment — a leaf in a book that closes itself
Where: Veilwood scholar's hut
Requires: magic 50, inverted patience
Asked, the leaf must be, before it will be surrendered. Politely, in Veilwood grammar.
[5] Recover the Sootworks fragment — a rivet in a wall nobody taps
Where: Sootworks deep-wall
Requires: a hammer, a careful ear
Tap. Listen. Tap again. Wrong rivet, whole wall falls.
[6] Recover the Saltbrine fragment — a barnacle on a hull nobody scrapes
Where: Saltbrine harbour, a specific hull
Requires: agility 40, a scraper
The hull is the Broken Crown's. The pirate king will let you scrape once you have asked.
[7] Recover the Inkweald fragment — a line from a dream you have not had yet
Where: Inkweald, second-door clearing
Requires: sleep, magic 50
You will have the dream on the threshold. The line will be short.
[8] Return to Orin with all six. Watch the whole prophecy resolve on his table
Where: Orin's tower
Requires: none
He will read nine lines for the first time. He will cry, quietly, once.

Twist

The ninth line of the prophecy is the one Orin has always held, silently, on a piece of paper under his tongue — literally, as a matter of old-desert custom. The first three are what he reads aloud. The sixth through eighth are what you recover. The fourth and fifth were written by Orin himself, years ago, because the original fragments were lost — and the prophecy appears to accept them. The ninth is his name and yours. You will see it on the table. Orin will not make a show of it.

Resolution

The prophecy is whole. Orin thanks you in a dry voice. The tower is, for an hour, less tidy than you remember it, because Orin has sat down in the middle of the floor. He gives you his spectacles — the real ones, not the spare — and hands you the reading-table key and a rune that always teleports you to the tower edge. The Prophecy Chamber, under the tower, is now open to you; it contains a reading-table that foreshadows every remaining grandmaster quest in your future.

What it unlocks

Orin's Spectacles let you read any prophecy glyph in Aelgard and reveal hidden dialogue on NPCs — a permanent Codex-reveal effect. Orin's Tower teleport rune is always available from any bank, anywhere; it teleports you to the tower edge and back. The Prophecy Chamber contains a reading-table that shows foreshadowing entries for every grandmaster quest you have not yet completed — not walkthroughs, but premonitions.
