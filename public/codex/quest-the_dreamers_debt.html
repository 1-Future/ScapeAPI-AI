The Dreamer's Debt

master epic id: the_dreamers_debt
inkweald
Inkweald. Reality is flickering. Trees blink out and return, paths fold into themselves, and somewhere at the centre of the forest a dreamer is dying in the waking world. When their body stops breathing, the Inkweald stops being a place. The Ink Keeper, who has kept the dream coherent for decades, would like your help stitching things down.

Premise

The Inkweald is a dream held open by a single dying mage. Their body lies in a hidden room in a forgotten tower, on life-support that is slowly failing. When the dreamer wakes — which, for them, means dying — the Inkweald collapses. The Ink Keeper has been maintaining the dream's coherence single-handed, and can no longer.

You must brew a Dream Anchor potion, craft ten anchor runes, place them at five cardinal points throughout the Inkweald, and cast a binding spell at each. The Dreamer will then manifest — a projection of a dying mage from a forgotten era — and you will face a choice: let the Dreamer wake (destroying the Inkweald) or keep them asleep forever (saving the forest but condemning the Dreamer to a permanent coma). Neither choice is clean.

Twist

There is a third option the Ink Keeper will reveal only if you have asked exactly the right questions: you can become a co-dreamer, sharing the burden and keeping the Inkweald alive while allowing the original dreamer to wake (die peacefully). This path permanently ties you to the forest — you can be called there in your sleep, forever, and it costs a small cosmetic stake on your character (tired eyes, a shaded portrait).

Resolution

The Inkweald persists, or does not. If it does, the Keeper hands you the Ink Keeper's quill. If it does not, she hands you a different quill, made of the last ink of the dream. Either way, you walk out carrying a writing implement that still writes in places most pens refuse to.

What it unlocks

You carry the Ink Keeper's quill, which writes on dream-fabric and on memory, and a pouch of Dream Runes for the spellbook you already know. The Inkweald is accessible under any outcome; only the colour of the trees changes, and the sound the wind makes under the canopy.

Dialogue

the Ink Keeper
A dream is not a place. It is a shared holding of breath. I have been holding mine. I would like to exhale. Help me decide whether I exhale into a forest or into nothing.
the Dreamer
I would like to wake. I would also like the forest to persist. I know these are not both possible. I am sorry.
