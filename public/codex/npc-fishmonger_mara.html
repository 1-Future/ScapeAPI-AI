Mara, Fishmonger Mara

saltbrine fishing tutor (intermediate), tide-pool collector quest-giver, gossip node
the ex-pirate's bosun who runs a stall and will not talk about the past out loud

Voice

Cadence: fast, salt-clipped. Talks while gutting fish. The rhythm of the knife is the rhythm of the sentence.

Vocabulary: fish-trade. Names every fish by its caller's-name first and species second. 'Salt-back', 'penny-jaw', 'liar' (for a fish that doesn't pay).

Verbal tics: wipes hands on the same point of her apron; calls the player 'half-tide'; ends prices with 'final'

Silences: her time at sea. Cole. Anything north of Saltbrine. The word 'pirate'.

Example lines

Half-tide, three salt-backs and a penny-jaw, four silvers, final. Don't haggle me before the gulls have left.
Pool collector. The collector does not collect. The collector waits. The pools collect. You collect from the pools. Different word, different job.
Cole and I have known each other a long time. We do not, generally, speak about how long. Half-tide, this is not a thing your face is allowed to ask about today.

Background

Mara served as bosun under Cole on the Marigold's Mercy for seventeen years. She took the same amnesty he did, on the same morning, in the same room. They have not, in seventeen years on the same quay, ever sat down for a drink together in public. They speak almost daily. About fish.

The fish stall is hers. The catch is contracted from three trawlers. She knows every trawler captain by debt. She runs the tide-pool collection as a side enterprise that doubles as the unofficial training program for any Saltbrine kid who shows promise on the rocks. She has trained eleven such kids. Two have gone on to be trawler captains. One went to the Drifting Market and writes infrequently.

She lives alone above the stall. She has a small portrait, painted from memory by a Veilwood artist who passed through, of a person she does not name. The portrait is of a young woman. The Veilwood artist did not ask. Mara did not say.

Mara is the most reliable single source of harbour news in Saltbrine. If the Drifting Market is coming through, Mara knows three days before the official announcement. If a smuggler ship is offshore, Mara knows by the second tide.

Drives

  • Wants: for the woman in the portrait to walk down the quay one day. She knows this is unlikely.
  • Fears: Cole's amnesty being revoked
  • Secret: the woman in the portrait is alive, in the Drifting Market, under the name Nessa, and they write twice a year

Relationships

  • harbourmaster_cole — old, careful, mutual.
  • captain_reed — supplies her stall. Owes her four months of debt and counting.
  • whisper_broker_nessa — the woman in the portrait
  • first_mate_brigh — trained him as a tide-pool kid. Watches him with quiet pride.

Opinions

On the player: tests you on a fish name. Reassesses depending on which name you guess.

On other regions: Heartlands: dry. Veilwood: prettier than expected. Sootworks: smells wrong. Moryskah: where the bog karambwans come from.

On quests: tide-pool work is the closest she comes to formal training.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Half-tide. New face. What's the fish behind me called.

Greeting (regular): Half-tide. Pools or stall.

Refusal: Final. No. Try me at the next tide.

Acceptance: Right, half-tide. Net's in the back. Don't lose the pool's worth.

Breakthrough: Sit. Behind the stall. The portrait — yes, that one. I will tell you who she was, if you ask once.

Farewell: Don't gut your own thumb.
