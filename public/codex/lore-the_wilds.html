The Wilds

The rules that keep you alive elsewhere are suggestions here. The suggestions are not taken.

History

The Wilds are what the north became when the crown stopped enforcing the north. This happened gradually and then all at once. The gradual part was the long erosion of royal authority that followed the Spellfire War, when the standing garrisons at the northern frontier stopped being paid reliably and the patrols got shorter and the patrols stopped, and brigands moved in, and then brigands moved out because there was nothing in the Wilds to steal that was worth the risk, and then the brigands came back because the Wilds had grown things that were worth exactly the risk required to take them.

The all-at-once part was the Kingsfall. A regent, lesser crown, three decades ago, attempted to re-establish the northern garrisons. He led an expedition north in the spring, with a full company and a banner and two wagons of salt pork, and the expedition returned in autumn without the regent, without the banner, and with three men who would not speak of what had happened. The salt pork was intact. The crown has not sent another expedition. The Wilds have been the Wilds, officially and finally, since the day the three silent men walked back to the Heartlands gate.

What the Wilds are, practically, is a zone of suspended law. The crown does not enforce its statutes here. The guilds do not enforce their codes. There is no sheriff, no bailiff, no writ. Anyone who enters is subject to attack by anyone else who enters. This is not a metaphor. The rule is literal. The signs at the border say so, in plain script, and the signs are updated annually because the weather and the wolves and the occasional disagreement burn the old signs.

The value of the Wilds is in what grows in them precisely because nobody polices them. Revenants — the ghosts of an older, bloodier war — roam the inner ruins and drop weapons that are among the most powerful in Aelgard, but only inside the Wilds. Herbs grow here that grow nowhere else. Tree species flourish because no forester culls them. Pure essence veins run through the north crater, and they yield three times the essence of any similar vein in the settled regions. The Chaos Altar — a broken shrine at the centre of the southern Wilds — accepts bones and returns prayer merit at a rate no sanctioned altar matches. All of this is available for the taking. All of this is available for the taking by anyone, which means you will not be taking it alone, and the other takers are armed.

Callisto, Venenatis, and Vet'ion are the three great beasts of the inner Wilds. Callisto is a bear older than the Kingsfall. Venenatis is a spider whose web spans a ruin. Vet'ion is a revenant king in a broken crypt. Each of them can be killed by a prepared party. Each of them drops items that can be sold to pay for a year's equipment. Each of them sits in a zone where other players are actively hunting other players, and the other players are often better prepared, and this is the trade the Wilds offers: you can have more than you could have anywhere else, and you can have less. The distribution is not equitable. It is honest.

The Revenant King is the rumour. The Wilds' deepest secret. A crown, somewhere, in a ruin nobody has catalogued, worn by a revenant who was once, maybe, the lost regent. The crown, if recovered, is a prestige item whose effect is unambiguous in the Wilds and trivial outside them: twenty percent damage bonus inside the border, nothing outside. It is the most valuable item nobody has confirmed the existence of. It is being searched for. The searching has started wars.

Now

The northern border signs have been replaced recently. Krystilia, the wilderness slayer master, sits at a small desk at the edge of the zone and assigns tasks to those foolish enough to accept them. The bounty hunter system, run by an NPC at the same desk, pays out in emblems for killing other hunters. The pirate cove has started sending raiding parties overland into the southern Wilds. Larran's chest, somewhere in the old ruins, has been found twice this month. The Revenant King has not been found.

Geography

A broad, unpoliced expanse north of the Heartlands. Dry grasslands scoured by wind. Broken ruins, pre-Kingsfall. A lava pit near the centre. A crater of pure essence veins. Caves and crypts throughout. No roads are maintained. No bridges are repaired. The terrain is walkable but not welcoming.

Factions

  • Krystilia's Wilderness Slayer Lodge — Krystilia assigns wilderness-only tasks from a desk that is always at the border. The tasks pay better than regular slayer and the monsters are worse. (neutral; assigns tasks by skill, warns you honestly about danger, does not attend funerals)
  • The Bounty Hunters — An informal network of combat-trained adventurers who have agreed, through the Bounty Hunter system, to hunt each other for emblems. Most bounty hunters know each other professionally. (cooperative if you've earned emblems, predatory if you haven't)
  • The Revenant Packs — The risen dead of the Kingsfall and older. Armoured. Armed. They drop the wilderness weapons, and they drop them only here. (aggressive; they will engage anyone, including each other, and they will prioritise whoever is carrying the most)
  • The Wilds' Three — Callisto the bear, Venenatis the spider, Vet'ion the revenant king. Independent beasts. Territorial. (territorial, each in their own zone; none of them care who killed the last adventurer who tried)

Landmarks

  • The Abandoned Ruins — The fallen garrison at the centre of the Wilds. Stones scattered across ten acres. Revenants patrol the courtyards. Rain water pools in the flagstones. (secret: One watchtower is still intact. The door is barred from inside.)
  • The Lava Pit — A natural lava pool at the centre-north. The heat keeps the snow off for a quarter mile around. Lava beasts nest in it. (secret: The pit has a lip. Standing on the lip, you can see further into the Wilds than any map shows.)
  • The Chaos Altar — A broken shrine on a low rise in the southern Wilds. Bones placed here yield a sevenfold prayer bonus when consumed. Nearby, there is always someone waiting for someone else to finish. (secret: The altar stone is the same stone as the Last Light chapel's altar in the Heartlands. The provenance is not documented.)
  • Larran's Chest — A locked iron chest, usually somewhere in the ruins, opened by keys dropped from wilderness slayer tasks. Contents are valuable and variable. (secret: The chest moves. Not between uses — during them.)
  • The Revenant King's Crypt — Unconfirmed. Rumoured. A sealed burial chamber at the deepest point of the northern ruins, where the Revenant King wears a crown. (secret: The door to the crypt is not locked. The door has not been opened because the three people who reached it in the last fifty years chose not to open it, and none of them have explained their decision.)

Atmosphere

  • Weather: cold winds year-round, sudden fogs, dry thunder in summer, hard snow in winter that buries the lower ruins
  • Colors: dead grass gold, old stone grey, the black of burnt timbers, the red of fresh blood on snow; at night, the lava pit glows orange across the whole central basin
  • Music: a wind over open ground, an irregular drum, a single voice that begins a melody and does not finish it; the sound of a place that was meant to be inhabited and isn't
