Bones of the First Empire

experienced long id: bones_of_the_first_empire
boneyard
Boneyard Wastes. A sandstorm has peeled back a mile of dune and uncovered a wall older than any on any map. Professor Sandil is already there with three students and a tent, and she would like a fourth pair of hands that is not afraid of bad history.

Premise

The Boneyard Wastes bury things. Every once in a while a storm forgets to bury one. The wall Sandil has found is not a wall — it is a ceiling, seen from above, because the building it belongs to is buried upside down. The murals inside depict a civilization that predates the gods now worshipped in Aelgard, and they depict the gods arriving, and they depict the civilization being destroyed by those arrivals.

This is not good news for anybody who worships. Sandil has a decision to make once the chronicle tablet is recovered: publish or bury. The Fossil Sentinel that guards the deepest chamber was placed there by the First Empire itself to protect exactly this knowledge, and it does not care for arguments.

Steps

[1] Meet Professor Sandil at the exposed rock face. Mine through the surface to a safe entrance
Where: Boneyard Wastes, excavation
Requires: mining
The rock gives way in sheets. Sandil examines each sheet before it falls.
[2] Navigate the collapsing tunnels, surviving three cave-in traps
Where: tunnels under the excavation
Requires: hitpoints, timing
The cave-ins are not random. They are rhythmic. You can count them.
[3] Discover murals depicting a civilization before the gods arrived. Pray at the ancient altar to translate them
Where: mural chamber
Requires: prayer
The prayer does not say anything aloud. The murals simply become legible.
[4] Defeat the Fossil Sentinel guarding the deepest chamber
Where: deepest chamber
Requires: combat, hitpoints
The Sentinel is made of bones you cannot name. You will not name them later either.
[5] Recover the chronicle tablet and decide, with Sandil, whether to publish or bury the truth
Where: deepest chamber; excavation
Requires: a choice
The tablet weighs more than a stone its size should.

Twist

A student in Sandil's team is not a student. They are a priest of the current pantheon, sent to ensure the truth is buried. You will learn this before Sandil does. You must decide whether to warn her and risk the dig being seized, or to trust Sandil to know her own team, or to confront the priest yourself.

Resolution

The chronicle tablet is yours to hold for one long night. By morning, you and Sandil have decided. Either the truth is buried with a note in the margin of the archives, or the dig is published, or a third thing — the tablet is quietly copied and the original re-buried — is arranged. Each path unlocks slightly different dialogue across Aelgard, and the First Empire amulet Sandil gives you regardless carries a small weight the gods do not particularly like.

What it unlocks

You wear a First Empire amulet, which is quieter than it sounds and stronger than it looks, and Professor Sandil invites you to assist on every major Boneyard dig from now on. The Boneyard Wastes have deeper mining veins open to you through her permits, and the Wastes' attitude toward your presence softens — they know you know.

Dialogue

Professor Sandil
I am a scholar. I publish. That is the rule. I have broken that rule exactly once in thirty years, and it still bothers me. Decide with me.
