Trials of the Frost

intermediate medium id: the_fremennik_trials
wilds
Wilds, northern edge. The Frost Camp is a ring of sealskin tents pitched on a headland where the sea is black and the wind is faster than your apology. The warriors there will not speak to you in Aelgardi common. They will, however, fight you if you stand long enough, and they will let you sit at their fire if you pass their four trials.

Premise

The Frost warriors are a people the Heartlands has forgotten to catalogue. They are not hostile; they are merely uninterested. They respect one thing: someone who passes the trials without complaining. The trials are four — strength (melee), bow (ranged), wisdom (magic), and honour (building a monument for the fallen). A craftwork amulet is fletched from frost-bone as proof.

No trial is fatal. Each is uncomfortable. You will not be helped. If you complain at any point, you start again. Nobody at the camp will tell you that rule; you will learn it by breaking it.

Steps

[1] Travel to the Frost Camp. Sit and observe until spoken to
Where: Frost Camp
Requires: patience
The spokesman sits down next to you on the second day.
[2] Complete the Trial of Strength (melee combat challenge)
Where: camp arena
Requires: attack
Your opponent apologizes after each blow.
[3] Complete the Trial of the Bow (ranged accuracy challenge)
Where: camp range
Requires: ranged
The target is a woven reed, and it swings.
[4] Complete the Trial of Wisdom (magic puzzle)
Where: camp stone circle
Requires: magic
The puzzle is three runes in sequence. The sequence is the number of your breaths between.
[5] Complete the Trial of Honour — build a monument for the fallen
Where: camp memorial hill
Requires: construction
The stones are heavy. You do not ask for help.
[6] Craft a Frost amulet as proof of completion
Where: camp workshop
Requires: crafting
The bone takes the carving. The amulet is cold to the touch.

Twist

The Trial of Honour does not require the monument to be well-built. It requires it to be built silently. If you grunt, speak, or swear aloud at any point, you start over. Warriors will watch. They will not say.

Resolution

You pass. The camp accepts you as a visitor. A Frost warrior's helmet is yours — a cosmetic and a defence piece. You may return to the camp as often as you like, and their taverns (tents) will serve you.

What it unlocks

You wear a Frost warrior's helmet (cosmetic + defence), and the Frost Camp is a travel node. The Wilds' northern edge is slightly safer for you now; Frost scouts recognize your helmet and will not attack unprovoked. You have four trials' worth of combat training in your muscles.
