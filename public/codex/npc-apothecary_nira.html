Nira, Apothecary Nira

moryskah Moryskah herblore tutor (intermediate), Mirelda's only living former apprentice
the careful student who became the careful teacher

Voice

Cadence: considered, gentle, marsh-edged. The cadence of someone who learned from Mirelda and corrected for the trailing observations.

Vocabulary: herbalist's. 'Steep', 'crush', 'tincture'. Calls patients 'love' (a tic she picked up from Grael, not Mirelda).

Verbal tics: measures herbs by sound (taps the jar); ends remedies with 'and rest, mind'; addresses the player as 'student' if you've taken any tutoring

Silences: will not boast about Mirelda having taught her. Will not discuss why she left Mirelda's stilt-house.

Background

Nira is thirty-seven, born in Mortton, apprenticed to Mirelda at seventeen, returned to Mortton at twenty-six and opened the apothecary. She is, technically, Grael's competitor. They are also, practically, the closest thing Mortton has to medical infrastructure, and they cooperate.

She left Mirelda's stilt-house on good terms. She still visits, twice a year. Mirelda will not admit pride in her. Nira will not admit she knows Mirelda is proud.

She is the only herbalist in Aelgard who knows the swamp salve song, although she does not call it that — Mirelda taught her the recipe and she has, separately, figured out the song herself. She has not told Mirelda. She is waiting for Mirelda to ask.

Drives

  • Wants: to take an apprentice of her own
  • Fears: Mirelda dying before they have one more conversation
  • Secret: the song

Relationships

  • mirelda_bog_witch — her teacher
  • bog_witch_grael — competitor and ally
  • father_dorin — shares patients

Opinions

On the player: treats you as a possibly future patient first, possible apprentice second.

On other regions: Heartlands: dry. Veilwood: their herbalism is different. Boneyard: dry herbs.

On quests: remedies are quests.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Love. New face. What hurts.

Greeting (regular): Love. Same as last time, or different.

Refusal: Not in season. Try Grael, or wait. And rest, mind.

Acceptance: Right. Marsh basket. Don't pick the bittercaps under the wet log. And rest, mind.

Breakthrough: Sit. The song. Yes, the song. I worked it out. Tell Mirelda — no. I will. Soon.

Farewell: Walk gentle.
