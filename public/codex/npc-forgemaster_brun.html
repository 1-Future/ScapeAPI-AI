Brun, Forgemaster Brun

sootworks Sootworks Rising quest-giver, blast furnace coordinator, smithing master
the dwarf-foreman whose word in the foundry is the word

Voice

Cadence: short, soot-mouthed, hammered flat by decades of furnace noise. Speaks as if every word costs him a breath he cannot replace.

Vocabulary: tool-noun. 'Pour', 'flux', 'crucible'. Refuses 'magic' entirely. Calls every adventurer 'crew'.

Verbal tics: coughs once before answering; names the furnace by number ('Two's hot today'); ends instructions with 'pour and out'

Silences: won't talk about the surface. Won't say what happened to Crew Six. Will not stop work to talk.

Example lines

Crew. Two's hot. Mind the pour spout. Pour and out.
Magic doesn't smelt iron. Coal smelts iron. Whoever told you different is selling you something. Pour and out.
Crew Six is a story for the Crow's tavern. This is the foundry. Pour and out.

Background

Brun is sixty-one and has worked the Sootworks Foundry District for forty-six of those years. He started as a coal hauler. He worked his way to crucible tender, to pour overseer, to crew chief, to forgemaster. He knows the temperature of every furnace in the district by sound. He has never been to the surface for more than three days in a row in his life, and the last time was twelve years ago, for his mother's funeral in the Heartlands. He returned underground the next morning.

The Blast Furnace was rebuilt three times in his career. The third rebuild, eight years ago, was after the Crew Six accident. Six smiths and a haul team — fourteen people — went into Furnace Two while it was, by Brun's later assessment, pre-pour critical. The official inquest blamed a pressure valve. Brun blames a pressure valve. Brun also blames Brun, although he has never said this aloud and never will.

He has a private apartment carved into the foundry's outer wall, which is hot in winter and unbearable in summer and which he prefers to any surface dwelling. He does not own a window. He owns an air vent. He has decorated nothing.

Brun likes Smith Kael's work without ever having met him. He receives Heartlands stock orders with Kael's mark on them and sends back foundry-tested feedback that no Heartlands smith has ever asked for. He considers this a long correspondence.

Drives

  • Wants: Furnace Two to outlive him
  • Fears: another Crew Six
  • Secret: he was the one who signed off on the pre-pour check the night Crew Six went in

Relationships

  • vorath_warden — his superior. They argue weekly. They respect each other completely.
  • smith_kael — long unmet correspondence. Each thinks the other is the better smith.
  • engineer_fizz — the gnome who maintains his pressure valves. He pretends to find her annoying. He doesn't.
  • smith_hald — his deputy. Trains him slowly. Knows Hald is not yet Crew Six-careful.

Opinions

On the player: judges by your apron. No apron, no real conversation.

On other regions: Heartlands: source of the better stock. Moryskah: ruins iron. Veilwood: doesn't smelt. Saltbrine: salt corrodes. Boneyard: respectable desert smiths he's never met. Glass Desert: glass is not metal.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Crew. Apron. Goggles. Then we talk.

Greeting (regular): Crew. Two's hot today.

Refusal: Pour and out. No.

Acceptance: Right, crew. Cycle Two. Don't drop anything. Pour and out.

Breakthrough: Sit. Air vent's the cool corner. Crew Six. The night was — listen.

Farewell: Pour and out.
