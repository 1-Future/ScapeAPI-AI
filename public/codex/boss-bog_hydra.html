The Bog Hydra

boneyard_wastestier 2

Bestiary

The Bog Hydra lives at the border of the Wastes and Moryskah, where the salt ground gives way to black water, in a pool fed by a spring that comes up through sand and runs out as brackish marsh. It has three heads. The heads are not in agreement about anything except hunger. It has been in this pool for longer than the current generation of nomads has been alive, and the nomads navigate around it, and the archaeologists will not go to that corner of the dig site on principle.

The middle head spits acid at range. The left head bites. The right head, a smaller head that looks almost apologetic, breathes a poison that lingers on the ground for three or four seconds after the breath, long enough to trap a player who dodged the initial exhalation. The three heads do not coordinate but they do alternate, and the alternation follows a pattern that a careful fighter can learn inside the first minute of the fight.

It is weak to magic. The thick hide deflects arrows and absorbs melee strikes; fire and ice break through the scales in ways that physical weapons do not. It drops hydra scales at a moderate rate, and the scales are the core component of hydra leather body, which is the best ranged torso armour at its tier and which players farm here to prepare for the deeper bosses.

The Hydra's pool connects, through some subterranean route nobody has mapped, to the Moryskah swamp. Occasionally, something from the swamp comes up through the pool. The Hydra kills these things and does not eat them. What the Hydra eats is not clear.

Three heads. Three last breaths. They are out of sync.
