Blood Rites

experienced long id: blood_rites
moryskah
Moryskah. Four villages on the swamp's edge have been raided in a pattern that is not random. The pattern is the shape of an old road that is not on any current map, and at the end of the road is Castle Malachar, which has been sealed for decades. Father Dorin has a letter. It is addressed to you, in a hand he says is two hundred years old, and it is an invitation.

Premise

Vampyres do not raid for food. They raid to remind. The four villages are the four corners of a ritual circle, and the raids are the ritual's opening phrases. If the circle is allowed to finish, something sealed in Castle Malachar wakes up. Father Dorin has spent forty years keeping the seal locked; the invitation with your name on it is somebody inside the castle telling Dorin that they are about to lose.

To interrupt the ritual you will need Protect from Undead, which the Church has not taught in a century because it requires a kind of certainty most priests cannot muster. Dorin will teach you, if you help him. You will also need to visit the Barrows mounds in the south of Moryskah and earn the respect of the six ancient warriors buried there. They will not help, but they will not hinder, and sometimes that is all.

Steps

[1] Visit the four raided villages. Do not comfort. Listen. Map what the survivors saw
Where: Moryskah, four villages on the swamp edge
Requires: a notebook, steady hands
The survivors are calm. Too calm. Whatever came to them did not panic them, which is the wrong sort of mercy.
[2] Walk the Barrows mounds. Do not open them. Pay your respects at each. Leave a coin
Where: Moryskah, the Barrows
Requires: six coins, respect
The mounds hum very faintly when you walk between them. A sound you notice only in retrospect.
[3] Learn Protect from Undead from Father Dorin. He will teach it by asking you questions, not by handing you a prayer
Where: Moryskah, Dorin's chapel
Requires: honesty, time
The prayer works the first time only if you have told Dorin the truth. It has to work the first time.
[4] Walk the old road to Castle Malachar. Do not ride. Do not teleport. The road must be walked
Where: the old road to the Castle
Requires: your feet, your prayers
The road is stone in places, mud in places, bone in one long stretch. You do not ask about the bone.
[5] Enter the castle. Interrupt the ritual before the final phrase. The vampyre lord will insist on a formal challenge; accept
Where: Castle Malachar, the great hall
Requires: Protect from Undead active, combat at the right levels
The hall is lit by candles that do not cast shadows. You cast a shadow. You are the only one.
[6] Defeat the lord or drive them off. Break the circle. Return to Dorin
Where: the great hall, then the chapel
Requires: the broken ritual pieces
Dorin does not ask what happened. He has read it off your face already.

Twist

The invitation was written by the sealed thing itself, not by the vampyre lord. It did not want you to stop the ritual. It wanted you to finish it on its behalf, by a path that looks like stopping. You will know, mid-combat, whether you are stopping or finishing. The correct answer is whichever Dorin would weep about.

Resolution

The Barrows open to the player, not as an invasion but as a courtesy; the six warriors buried there permit you to fight their echoes for their gear, over and over. Dorin files away the invitation in a box he keeps under a loose floorboard. He does not thank you. He nods.

What it unlocks

The Barrows are open to you — six degradable sets of equipment, repeatable, the best set-bonus mid-game gear — and Protect from Undead is in your prayerbook, which is the prayer you will use every time you come back. Moryskah opens new roads when you travel; the old road is walkable now, not only in dreams.

Dialogue

Father Dorin
The prayer is not a spell. A spell works because you are clever. The prayer works because you are certain. If you are not certain, do not say it. You will be answered wrong.
the sealed thing
You are very helpful. Very helpful. Stop, if you can. You may not be able to.
