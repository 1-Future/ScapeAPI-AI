Song of the Elves (Aelgard)

grandmaster epic id: song_of_the_elves_aelgard
veilwood
Veilwood Inner Sanctum. The elven elder will not meet you at the outer grove this time. She has sent word: come to the inner sanctum, where the anvils sing, and bring every skill you have ever trained, because the trial she has for you is the one the elves use on their own.

Premise

The Inner Sanctum is the elven heart. Outsiders do not go there; those who have are either dead, or elves now, or not returning. Elara will admit you because the forest has asked her to, which is not a thing Elara argues with. The trial is the one the elves take when they turn thirty — a comprehensive check of every craft, every combat style, every gathering skill. Pass, and you earn access to the Sanctum's anvils, which are crystal-enhanced and make the finest equipment in Aelgard.

The trial is not adversarial. It is diagnostic. You will demonstrate melee, ranged, magic, mining, smithing, crafting, woodcutting, agility, herblore, construction, fletching, farming, and hunter at levels comparable to an elf of appropriate age. The trial will call out what you are weak at. You will have to go away and train, and come back. The trial does not expire. It waits.

Steps

[1] Meet Elara at the Sanctum's outer veil. Step through the veil without explaining yourself
Where: Veilwood, Inner Sanctum veil
Requires: the forest's consent, which is either present or absent
The veil is warm on the way in, cool on the way out.
[2] Combat trial. Three elven champions, three styles. Defeat each without cheating
Where: Sanctum's combat grove
Requires: melee, ranged, magic
The champions do not smile. They do not frown either. They are professional in a way that predates professionalism.
[3] Gathering trial. Mine, chop, hunt, fish, harvest. In the order the Sanctum names
Where: Sanctum's gathering lane
Requires: every gathering tool you own
The order is the order of respect, which is not what you expected.
[4] Crafting trial. Smith an elven weapon. Craft an elven gem-setting. Fletch an elven bow. Build an elven loom
Where: Sanctum's workbench
Requires: smithing, crafting, fletching, construction
The Sanctum's anvils glow when used correctly. You will feel them before you see them.
[5] Agility trial. Navigate the lost elven ruins without falling. Falling once is forgivable, twice is not
Where: lost elven ruins
Requires: agility, a light foot
The ruins shift, slightly, the way a body shifts in sleep.
[6] Herblore and farming trial. Grow, brew, deliver a potion the Sanctum asks for by taste
Where: Sanctum's green lane
Requires: herblore, farming
The potion is an honest one. No enhancement, no concealment. Just exactly what it says it is.
[7] The Crystal Seed. Grow one in your own pocket, to completion, in the Sanctum's presence
Where: Sanctum's heart
Requires: every skill the trial has tested
The seed hums. It is listening to your heart rate.

Twist

At the end of the trial, Elara will ask you to choose: one skill to be marked, permanently, as your favoured skill, which will train faster forever. The choice is yours and irrevocable. Whatever you choose, the Sanctum will note your face with it, and other elves will treat you accordingly.

Resolution

The Inner Sanctum's anvils are yours. The crystal tools are yours to craft, to buy, to wield. The Sanctum will not open again for you to re-take the trial; it does not take; you have either passed or you have not.

What it unlocks

The Veilwood Inner Sanctum is open to you — the best anvils in the game, the best crystal gear crafted or bought, the best mining XP at the Sanctum's crystal nodes (with maximum attention required; the nodes are not idle miners' work). Crystal crafting and crystal tools are yours. A favoured-skill marking rides in your quest log: that skill trains a fraction faster now, forever.
