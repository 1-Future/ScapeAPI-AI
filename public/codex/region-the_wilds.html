Prestige Goals

Prestige Goal: Coronation of the Revenant King

Defeat the Revenant King deep in the Wilderness. Survive multiple PKer ambushes. Emerge with the Wilderness Crown.

Required skills: attack 85 ranged 80 prayer 75 hitpoints 85

Bosses: revenant_king

Unique rewards: wilderness_crown, revenant_ether_mace

Sub-areas

  • The Wilds — Lawless PvP zone north of Aelgard. No requirements to enter, but anything you carry can be taken. Superior training methods and rare resources justify the risk.
  • Revenant Caves — Multi-combat PvP dungeon. Revenants drop unique PvP weapons. Extremely dangerous. [requires: hitpoints 40]
  • Wilderness Resource Area — Dark crabs, runite ore, magic trees. 7,500gp entry fee. Deep wilderness.

Quirky Interactions (2)

Ambient world objects that grant tiny XP. Discoverable, flavorful, mathematically terrible — but sometimes a lifeline.
  • Climb the Fallen Statue [agility] — 2 xp/click. The statue is massive. Climbing it exposes you to any PKer in the zone.
  • Place a Revenant Ward [prayer] — 1.8 xp/click. You hope no revenants notice. Or PKers. Mostly PKers.
