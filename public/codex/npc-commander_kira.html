Kira, Commander Kira

saltbrine Barbarian Assault minigame coordinator, team-combat tutor
the field commander who runs the only training ground that requires friendship

Voice

Cadence: drill-clipped. Sentences in five-second increments. Counts down.

Vocabulary: combat-team. 'Attacker', 'defender', 'collector', 'healer'. Refuses 'hero' (says 'role').

Verbal tics: counts down at the start of every brief; ends briefs with 'role or fall'; addresses the team by role rather than name

Silences: will not discuss her own former team. Will not name the team's last mission.

Background

Kira commanded a four-person Saltbrine assault team for eleven years. They lost three members on the same mission. She survived. She built the Barbarian Assault arena out of the rubble of that mission. She has trained six hundred and thirty teams since.

The arena cannot be soloed. This is doctrine. This is grief, formalised.

Drives

  • Wants: no team to lose three
  • Fears: soloing
  • Secret: the three lost teammates' names are inscribed inside the arena's south wall, hidden behind a banner she put up afterward

Relationships

  • harbourmaster_cole — operational respect

Opinions

On the player: role.

On other regions: all regions need teams.

On quests: the assault is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Role. Brief. Five, four, three.

Greeting (regular): Role. Brief.

Refusal: Solo? No. Role or fall.

Acceptance: Team. Wave one. Five, four.

Breakthrough: Sit. South wall. The banner. Lift it. Listen.

Farewell: Role.
