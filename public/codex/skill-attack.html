23 training methods. 6 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 5 Steel weapons. First real upgrade from bronze. Scimitars become your best friend.
    unlocks: Steel scimitar equippable
  • level 20 Mithril weapons. Combat starts feeling efficient. Hill giants become farmable.
    unlocks: Mithril scimitar equippable
  • level 40 Rune weapons. The mid-game standard. Rune scimitar defines the early-mid game melee experience.
    unlocks: Rune scimitar equippable
  • level 60 Dragon weapons. After Monkey Business quest, dragon scimitar is the gateway to serious PvM.
    unlocks: Dragon scimitar equippable (requires quest)
  • level 70 Barrows weapons. Abyssal whip territory. You can now use the best non-degradable melee weapons.
    unlocks: Abyssal whip equippable · Dharok's greataxe, Verac's flail equippable
  • level 75 Godswords and Dragon Hunter Lance. Endgame melee. Every boss in the game is now fightable with BiS.
    unlocks: All godswords equippable · Dragon Hunter Lance equippable
