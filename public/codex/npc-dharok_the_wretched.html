Dharok, Dharok the Wretched

moryskah Barrows brother — melee specialist (low-HP scaling)
the family soldier who was buried with his rage and brought it back

Voice

Cadence: guttural, short, breath-heavy. Pauses are hatred. Speaks as if every sentence costs him something.

Vocabulary: old soldier. Curses he can no longer remember the meaning of. Calls all visitors 'thief'.

Verbal tics: growls before any sentence; names his axe between every line; spits before he speaks

Silences: does not remember his wife. Will not say his daughter's name. Goes still if either is mentioned.

Example lines

Thief. The axe knows you. Bron. Bron knows you. Bron remembers.
Wretched. They named me wretched. Once I was not. Once. Now. Bron.
You are not the first to come for the axe. There were six. Bron remembers six. Bron does not tire.

Background

Dharok was the eldest of six brothers in service to a Moryskah noble house in the same century as Ahrim. He died defending a position that was already lost. The myth says he killed forty before he fell. The mound says he killed thirty-eight.

He does not remember the position. He does not remember the house. He remembers the axe, which he calls Bron, which is the name of a brother who fell beside him and who the axe is named for, although Dharok does not now know this.

His rage scales with his suffering. The lower his health, the harder he hits. This is not a metaphor. This is the curse Ahrim wove to keep the old soldier useful past death. Ahrim no longer remembers weaving it.

Dharok had a wife. He had a daughter. Both names exist in a chapel registry the player can find with high lore investigation. Dharok cannot say them. If the player says them aloud in the fight, Dharok pauses for one full tick. The fight does not stop. The pause is for him.

Drives

  • Wants: to die properly
  • Fears: the names
  • Secret: Bron is the name of his brother, not the axe

Relationships

  • the_other_barrows_brothers — they were not brothers in life. They are now, in the only sense the word can still mean.
  • lord_malachar — Malachar visits twice a year. Dharok does not recognise him. Malachar leaves a single coin on the mound.

Opinions

On the player: thief.

On other regions: does not know.

On quests: thief.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Thief. Bron knows you.

Greeting (regular): Thief. Again.

Refusal: The axe is not for thieves. The axe is — Bron.

Acceptance: Take it. The axe will kill you eventually. Bron is patient.

Breakthrough: [the names spoken aloud — one tick of stillness — no line]

Farewell: Bron remembers.
