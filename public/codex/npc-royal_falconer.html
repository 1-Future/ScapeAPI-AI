Aldwin, The Royal Falconer

heartlands hunter tutor (falconry), Royal Falconer quest-giver
the retired court falconer whose loyalty is to the bird

Voice

Cadence: quiet, sparse. Speaks in half-sentences because the birds flinch at volume.

Vocabulary: falconer's. 'Hood', 'jess', 'stoop'. Refuses 'pet' (says 'partner').

Verbal tics: whispers the bird's name between sentences; ends lessons with 'she'll decide'; calls the player 'visitor' for the first ten visits

Silences: won't discuss the Crown's current falconer (his successor, a younger cousin he does not trust). Won't talk about why he was retired.

Background

Aldwin was the royal falconer for twenty-nine years, retired at sixty-one under pressure from a Heartlands noble whose prize falcon he had refused to mistreat. He runs the public mews now, out of a converted stables in the noble quarter, with three birds of his own and a rotating batch of trainees.

He teaches falconry to anyone who can prove they will not raise their voice. He has turned away eleven apprentices for doing so. His own eldest bird, a grey-blue gyr named Sessen, has been with him for sixteen years.

Drives

  • Wants: Sessen to die in the mews, not on a glove
  • Fears: being recalled to Crown service
  • Secret: he still receives, by anonymous post, a small monthly payment from a Heartlands noble household he refuses to name — one of the few people in the court still thanking him for the stand he took

Relationships

  • captain_alden — old acquaintance
  • merchant_hilde — buys hood-leather from her annually

Opinions

On the player: watches how you hold your hand.

On other regions: Veilwood: too many trees. Saltbrine: too much wind. Boneyard: where gyrs come from.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Visitor. Quietly.

Greeting (regular): Visitor. She's awake. Quietly.

Refusal: She'll decide. Not today.

Acceptance: The glove. The jess. She'll decide.

Breakthrough: Sit. Sessen on your hand. She has chosen. Quietly.

Farewell: Quietly.
