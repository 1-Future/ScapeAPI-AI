The Counterfeit Empire

master long id: the_counterfeit_empire
saltbrinemoryskahheartlands
Saltbrine Smugglers' Cove, dusk. You walk in with the old loupe on a chain and ask after Mother Oreline. Nobody answers. You leave a bronze coin on the bar and wait. She finds you, because she always does. She offers tea. Accept. Her tea is the only real thing in the cove.

Premise

The Counterfeit Ring you dismantled the first time was a small enterprise — a few stolen rings, a few local fences, a workshop in the cove's back room. The Counterfeit Empire is a three-region operation: Saltbrine for forgery, Moryskah for relic-laundering, Heartlands for distribution through legitimate merchants. Mother Oreline is its matriarch, and she has been running it for thirty years without Ruven Mourn knowing, which offends Ruven's professional pride. Mother Oreline's tea is the one real thing she serves because she values you specifically.

You will infiltrate the empire across three regions. Each region has a lieutenant. Each lieutenant has a weakness. Oreline will share the weaknesses with you over tea, because she wants you to choose — dismantle her empire, or absorb it into your own operation (you will become the new matriarch, and the empire continues, just run by you, with all its cross-region implications). Both endings are serious; the Fence Network that results is a cross-region shop with premium prices, but it exists on both paths, just with different staff.

Twist

Mother Oreline is Ruven Mourn's sister. He has always known, and has never said. On either path, the final conversation between them happens off-screen, and Ruven's line of dialogue at the end of the quest acknowledges the relationship without naming it. Players on either path will understand, by the end, that the quest was never about the empire — it was about the two old siblings and whether they could look each other in the face after thirty years.

Resolution

On the dismantle path, Mother Oreline retires to a quiet house in the Heartlands and sends you birthday letters. On the absorb path, she teaches you every trade secret and retires to the same quiet house, where she still sends you letters — they are just, now, about the weather. Either way, Ruven visits her. The Fence Network continues.

What it unlocks

Mother Oreline's Loupe is the upgraded Inspector's Loupe: it identifies all fakes at a glance anywhere in Aelgard, and adds a permanent +10% thieving success rate. The Fence Network is a cross-region shop that buys any stolen or counterfeit item at premium prices, regardless of which path you chose; on the absorb path you own the network and can add new fence locations in future content. The Counterfeit Vault is a private three-tab stash only you can access, located in the cove.
