The Fencepost Problem

novice short id: the_fencepost_problem
heartlands
Heartlands ranch country. Every morning, three fenceposts on Arun Bellfield's eastern pasture are lying on the ground, and no animal has knocked them down. Arun has stayed up at night with a lantern and a strong drink and seen nothing. He is convinced, with the calm of an exhausted man, that his fence hates him personally. He would like a second opinion.

Premise

The Bellfield ranch has stood where it is for four generations, and the eastern fence has always been the problem fence. Arun's grandmother built it without asking the field what it thought. The field has opinions. It has been politely raising its fenceposts out of the ground, one at a time, for longer than anyone alive can remember, and Arun is the first to notice in several decades because he is the first to need the pasture for real cattle rather than sheep.

The fix is not more fence. The fix is a small bell hung on a particular post, rung once at dawn, and then left alone. Arun's grandmother left instructions in a drawer nobody has opened since she died. The bell is also in the drawer. It is very small and it looks like a joke.

Steps

[1] Walk the fence with Arun at dawn and watch it happen. Then ask him if anyone in his family left any notes
Where: Heartlands, Bellfield eastern pasture
Requires: none
The post in the middle lifts itself out of the earth like a tooth giving up.
[2] Find the grandmother's instructions in the drawer nobody will open, and the bell in the jar behind them
Where: Bellfield ranch house, the back room
Requires: permission, gently asked for
The drawer sticks. A hairpin in the keyhole will not do what a polite word does.
[3] Hang the bell on the post the grandmother marked. Ring it once. Do not ring it twice
Where: the middle of the eastern fence
Requires: the bell
The field sighs, which is a sound you will pretend you did not hear.

Twist

The bell is not on the post the grandmother described. Her directions are correct, but the field moved while you were gone. You will have to walk the pasture until you find where the post wants to be, and set the bell there instead. If you get it wrong the bell rusts through within a week.

Resolution

Arun watches you get it right, which is to say he watches his pasture stop fighting him, and for the first time in two years he looks unfrightened. He gives you a keyring with a bell on it that is a miniature of the big one, and he tells you the gate of the ranch will open to anyone wearing that bell, no matter where they are in the Heartlands.

What it unlocks

The Heartlands ranch is yours to travel to, bell in hand, as if the road had folded itself up to bring you there. You also carry a Rancher's Bell on a cord; ring it somewhere quiet, and a pet cow comes plodding over, which is cosmetic and very much welcome, and which sets a farming timer you will rely on.

Dialogue

Arun Bellfield
She left a drawer I was told not to open. I have respected this for three decades. Today I will disrespect it.
grandmother's note
Ring once. The field is not deaf, it is tired. Tired things do not like to be shouted at.
