Vorath, Vorath, Warden of the Deep Vein

sootworks Sootworks faction leader, regional governor, mid-tier boss
the dwarf-king who chose foreman over crown

Voice

Cadence: ceremonial when public, dry when private. Grants long pauses to subordinates. Cuts off equals.

Vocabulary: old dwarven formal. 'Vein', 'seam', 'oath'. Calls the Sootworks 'the Vein'. Calls outsiders 'surface'.

Verbal tics: taps his ring against the table once before answering; ends formal pronouncements with 'so the Vein holds'; calls every contract 'the oath'

Silences: won't discuss the Crown. Won't discuss his elder sister, who was the previous Warden and who walked away. Won't say the word 'king'.

Example lines

Surface. The Vein hears your request. The Vein does not always answer at the speed the surface prefers. So the Vein holds.
I am Warden, not king. The distinction is the entire reason the Sootworks works. Tell that to your Crown when you next see it.
Crew Six. The oath was kept. The price was the price. We do not unmake the oath because the price was high.

Background

Vorath is the eighty-third Warden of the Deep Vein. He is also, as it happens, the man his elder sister was supposed to be — she was raised to be Warden, raised exquisitely well, and at the moment of her ascension simply walked out of the Sootworks and went, by all accounts, to the Glass Desert. She has not been heard from in thirty-one years. Vorath assumed the Wardenship at twenty-six because his sister did not.

He runs the Sootworks as a confederation of crews, each with its own foreman and its own oaths. He does not impose. He coordinates. He is, by Heartlands standards, almost embarrassingly democratic for a Warden. He has refused two formal alliances with the Heartlands Crown that would have consolidated power in his name.

He is, separately, a competent fighter — the boss fight against him is the closest the player can come to a friendly duel with a regional leader. He does not fight to kill. He fights to test. Adventurers who survive the duel earn, among other things, the right to bring complaints to him directly, without the customary three months of paperwork.

He drinks dwarven stout once a year, on the anniversary of his sister leaving, and only one. He keeps a single piece of correspondence from her — a brief note, eleven words, which he has not shown anyone — locked in his desk.

Drives

  • Wants: his sister to come home
  • Fears: becoming king, by accident or by drift
  • Secret: the eleven-word note in his desk

Relationships

  • forgemaster_brun — longest-serving foreman. Mutual stubborn respect.
  • engineer_fizz — her weekly notes are read.
  • smith_hald — deputy forgemaster. Vorath has not made up his mind about him.
  • crystal_sage_orin — they have written each other for twenty-eight years. Vorath has never asked Orin if he has seen Vorath's sister. Orin has never volunteered.

Opinions

On the player: tests by paperwork, then by oath, then if necessary by hammer.

On other regions: Heartlands: a Crown he is polite to. Moryskah: dangerous, leaves alone. Veilwood: respected craftsmen, no trade. Saltbrine: useful for ore export. Boneyard: respected. Glass Desert: where his sister went.

On quests: the oath is the contract. The contract is the work.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Surface. State the oath you bring.

Greeting (regular): Surface. The Vein remembers you.

Refusal: The Vein does not bind to that oath today.

Acceptance: So the Vein holds. The oath is sworn. Take this token. Return it kept.

Breakthrough: Sit. There is a note. There is a sister. The note is eleven words. You will know what I do not.

Farewell: So the Vein holds.
