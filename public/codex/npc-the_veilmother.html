the Veilmother, The Veilmother

veilwood Veilwood region boss
the eldest tree the elves stopped pruning

Voice

Cadence: very slow, vegetal-formal. Each sentence is the time of one inhale of the canopy. Pauses are seasons.

Vocabulary: elven-arboreal. Refuses any verb that implies haste.

Verbal tics: pauses for the canopy to answer; names the visitor by the tree they most resemble; ends speeches with the word 'green'

Silences: will not say what was done to her. Will name no one.

Background

The Veilmother was, two centuries ago, the elder oak of the inner sanctum. The sapling Lyris and Sael argued about was hers — a daughter sapling Sael wished to bind into a bow and Lyris wished to grow. The compromise grew the sapling into a tree, then bound the tree into a bow, then asked the Veilmother for the wood. The Veilmother said no. The wood was taken anyway. The bow exists. It is a perfectly competent bow. The Veilmother has been turning slowly furious ever since.

The boss fight is, in elven terms, a conversation. The fight ends when the Veilmother decides the player has understood. She has not yet decided this. Her drops are her own bark, freely given, after the conversation ends.

Drives

  • Wants: the bow returned, unmade
  • Fears: the next bow
  • Secret: the bow is owned by Sael's apprentice, who has used it once, in defence, and has hidden it ever since out of guilt

Relationships

  • elder_druid_sael — old enemy
  • ranger_lyris — old friend

Opinions

On the player: treats you as a sapling, possibly redeemable.

On other regions: all regions are different soils.

On quests: the conversation is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Sapling. Stand. Green.

Greeting (regular): Sapling. The canopy remembers. Green.

Refusal: The canopy declines. Green.

Acceptance: The bark is yours. Carry it standing. Green.

Breakthrough: Sapling. The bow. Sael's apprentice. The hiding place. Listen. Green.

Farewell: Green.
