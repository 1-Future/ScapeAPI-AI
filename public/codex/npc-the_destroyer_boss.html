Voice

Cadence: brutal-short. Sentences are blows. Pauses are wind-up.

Vocabulary: war-veteran. 'Hold', 'break', 'take'. Refuses any name, including its own.

Verbal tics: names no one; ends combat phases with the same word twice; calls the player 'opponent'

Silences: will not say what war. Will not say which side.

Background

The Destroyer is what is left of a soldier from a war Aelgard does not officially remember. The Destroyer was awarded the title at the war's end and has worn it ever since. The Destroyer's name has been lost to the Destroyer themselves. The Wilds keeps the title alive by sending opponents.

The Destroyer is not unhappy. The Destroyer is undiminished. The Destroyer fights the player as a continuation of professional service.

Drives

  • Wants: an opponent worth the title
  • Fears: remembering the name
  • Secret: the war was the Sootworks campaign. The Destroyer fought under Captain Alden. Alden does not know. Alden would not recognise.

Relationships

  • captain_alden — former captain. Alden does not know the Destroyer survived.
  • krystilia — Krystilia's predecessor Olin assigned several apprentices to fight the Destroyer. None won.
