The Silent Hunt

intermediate medium id: the_silent_hunt
veilwoodboneyard
Veilwood perimeter. A creature that has no business in the open desert has crossed out of the canopy and into the dunes, and Ranger Hale can see the tracks fading into sand that should not hold them. The creature is a Shade Lynx. It is not supposed to exist outside the forest. It is getting smaller, somehow, the farther it walks.

Premise

A Shade Lynx is a forest-bound creature that feeds on ambient green. In the Boneyard Wastes it will starve, slowly, and become more dangerous as it does — small, fast, bitter. Hale wants it taken alive and brought back to the forest. He will not chase it himself; his tracking is better than his running. He needs a partner with a trap kit and a tolerance for two biomes in one afternoon.

The trick is adjusting your method between regions. Veilwood tracking is silent, soft, top-down. Boneyard tracking is loud and flat and you follow smell and the refraction of heat. The pitfall traps you build from Veilwood timber and vine rope are the Veilwood trap. Box traps near an oasis at dusk are the Boneyard trap. The lynx is drawn to water once it is thirsty. Patience is the whole plan.

Steps

[1] Meet Hale at the Veilwood perimeter and read the tracks leaving the canopy
Where: Veilwood perimeter
Requires: hunter eye
The tracks go from clear to wishful over ten paces of sand.
[2] Craft pitfall traps from Veilwood timber and vine rope, then follow the trail through the canopy shortcuts
Where: Veilwood canopy
Requires: crafting, agility
The canopy shortcuts are a series of agreements. Each branch you use, you thank.
[3] Cross into the Boneyard Wastes. Change your method. Set box traps near the oasis the lynx will go to at dusk
Where: Boneyard Wastes, oasis
Requires: hunter, a box trap
The sand is hot enough to fry your palms. Your box trap hisses when set.
[4] Wait for nightfall. Approach the lynx without spooking it
Where: Boneyard oasis
Requires: stealth, patience
The lynx is smaller than it was. Its eyes are brighter.
[5] Capture it alive. Carry it home. Do not let it out of the cage until you are under canopy again
Where: Veilwood perimeter
Requires: a reinforced cage
The lynx does not scratch the cage. It waits.

Twist

The lynx will speak once during the return journey, in a language neither of you know, and you will have to decide whether to report this to Hale. He will not ask. He has had lynxes speak to him too, long ago, and he has his own rule about it.

Resolution

The lynx, back under canopy, becomes a lynx again. Hale hands you a wand carved with a looped noose, which is the ranger's tool and the ranger's badge. He does not say welcome. He nods.

What it unlocks

You carry a ranger's noose wand, which is an acknowledged tool in two regions now. Your hunter training in the Veilwood canopy is faster and Boneyard box traps yield better catches when you are the one setting them. Hale does not teach you further, but he will answer questions. That is the thing that changes.
