The Plague Road

master epic id: the_plague_road
moryskahboneyardheartlandsveilwood
Moryskah, Hollow Mire. Four villagers have died of a sickness the village healer has never seen. The sickness travels. Its next stop is the Heartlands, and after that, Veilwood. A pandemic is three weeks away if nobody walks the road that the sickness has been walking.

Premise

The plague originated in the Boneyard Wastes — an ancient spore released by miners who dug where miners were warned not to dig. It has travelled along trade roads to Moryskah, and from Moryskah it will travel to the Heartlands and Veilwood unless inoculated. The antidote must be grown from the spore's own root system, brewed with desert herbs and Moryskah swamp water, and administered in the right order: the garrison at Heartlands first, because soldiers travel, and the Veilwood elves last, because they will take the longest to trust.

You will collect plague samples, identify the pathogen, travel to the Boneyard to grow the counter-agent in a breached tomb, brew the antidote, and travel across three regions administering it. In Heartlands, you will cook medicinal broth for the soldiers. In Veilwood, you will pray at the elves' shrine to earn trust. The plague recedes in the order of your route.

Steps

[1] Investigate the first victims in Moryskah's Hollow Mire. Collect plague samples and identify the pathogen
Where: Hollow Mire
Requires: herblore
The samples are warm. They should not be warm.
[2] Travel to the Boneyard Wastes. Locate the breached tomb where the spore was disturbed
Where: Boneyard Wastes, breached tomb
Requires: hunter tracking
The tomb breath smells of fruit that is past its best.
[3] Grow a counter-agent from the spore's own root system
Where: Boneyard tomb
Requires: farming
The counter-agent grows slowly. You can watch it.
[4] Brew the antidote using desert herbs and Moryskah swamp water
Where: Hollow Mire apothecary
Requires: herblore at high level
The brew fights you. You are patient.
[5] Travel to Heartlands. Inoculate the garrison. Cook medicinal broth for the soldiers
Where: Heartlands garrison
Requires: cooking
The soldiers drink the broth before they notice it is medicine.
[6] Travel to Veilwood. Pray at the shrine. Administer the cure to the elves
Where: Veilwood shrine
Requires: prayer
The prayer takes an hour. The cure takes a minute. Both are required.
[7] Return to Moryskah's healers with a full report
Where: Hollow Mire apothecary
Requires: honesty
The healers will archive your report for reference. It will be cited, later, without your knowledge.

Twist

The miners who released the spore are alive, and in hiding. A villager in Hollow Mire will ask you, privately, not to hunt them. You may honour the request or ignore it. Honouring buys you a small unlock from the villager. Ignoring gives you a different unlock from the Wastes authorities.

Resolution

The plague recedes across three regions. The healers of Moryskah, the Heartlands, and Veilwood each write to you. You wear the plague doctor's mask as a mark of respect; a doctor is a person who walks into rooms other people are leaving.

What it unlocks

You wear the plague doctor's mask — a cosmetic and a practical item; it reduces the effectiveness of certain poisons against you, and NPCs in hospital settings recognize it. The three regions' healers will treat you as a colleague from now on; their teaching rates for herblore improve slightly whenever you are present.
