Prestige Goals

Prestige Goal: The Pharaohs Reckoning

Defile the sealed tomb of Pharaoh Senekhet. Defeat his mummy lord guardians, solve the pyramid puzzles, and claim the Ankh of Rebirth.

Required skills: thieving 75 agility 65 magic 60 prayer 55

Bosses: pharaoh_senekhet, three_mummy_lords

Unique rewards: ankh_of_rebirth, pharaohs_crown

Sub-areas

  • The Boneyard Wastes — Post-apocalyptic desert. Pyramids, fossils, sand elementals. Hot climate damages unprepared players. [requires: mining 15, firemaking 10, quest: sand_and_secrets]
  • Boneyard Pyramid Interior — Ancient pyramid dungeon. Agility course, prayer training, and access to Ancient Magicks. [requires: agility 30, thieving 25, quest: desert_treasure]

Quirky Interactions (3)

Ambient world objects that grant tiny XP. Discoverable, flavorful, mathematically terrible — but sometimes a lifeline.
  • Sift the Dig Site Sand [thieving] — 0.8 xp/click. The archaeologist ignores you but leaves the sand pile accessible.
  • Re-wrap the Mummy Display [crafting] — 0.3 xp/click. The curator supervises. "NOT too tight."
  • Poke the Desert Fire Pit [firemaking] — 0.4 xp/click. The traveler nods. "Firemaking is a practiced art, stranger."
