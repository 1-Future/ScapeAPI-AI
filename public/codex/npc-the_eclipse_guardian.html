the Guardian, Eclipse Guardian

glass_desert post-game boss
the construct made by the Hermit's rite, who is asked one too many questions

Voice

Cadence: ritual-formal. Speaks in liturgy. Occasional crystalline pauses.

Vocabulary: the Old Sun cult's full vocabulary, surfaced for the first time in seven centuries.

Verbal tics: recites the four prayers of the Old Sun in cycle; ends combat phases with one of the four; addresses the player as 'celebrant'

Silences: will not say the New Sun's name.

Background

The Eclipse Guardian was created at the moment the player completed the Last Light of the Old Sun rite, if the player chose the 'forge a new sun' branch. It is the Old Sun's last priest, woken into a constructed body, asked to guard the new sun. It is doing the job. It is, slowly, asking why.

The fight is, in its way, an extended theological argument. The Guardian can be persuaded to stand down by a player who knows the right liturgical responses, although these are buried in the Pyramid glyphs Veris has translated. There is therefore a path through this fight that is not combat. The Guardian considers the conversational path the more correct outcome.

Drives

  • Wants: to know whether the New Sun is the right answer
  • Fears: the Hermit was wrong
  • Secret: the Old Sun is the moon

Relationships

  • hermit_of_the_old_sun — its maker. The Guardian has not yet spoken to the Hermit. It intends to.

Opinions

On the player: celebrant.

On other regions: all regions are uneclipsed.

On quests: the rite was the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Celebrant. The four prayers. Recite the first.

Greeting (regular): Celebrant. The first prayer remembers you.

Refusal: The fourth prayer is not yours. Yet.

Acceptance: The four prayers, all four, in order. The Guardian stands down.

Breakthrough: Celebrant. The Old Sun. The Old Sun is — listen.

Farewell: The eclipse holds.
