The Song Before Words

experienced long id: the_song_before_words
veilwood
Veilwood amphitheatre. Lyricist Aelwen holds a bow that is not finished being a bow yet, and she is humming a melody she has not heard since her grandmother died. There is a song, she says, older than language, that shaped the forest. She has been reconstructing it in her head for twenty years, one note at a time.

Premise

The Song Before Words is a melody carried by five standing stones in the Veilwood. Each stone emits a tone when struck by an arrow from a resonance bow made of singing-wood. Four stones still stand. The fifth was buried during the old war. Reconstructing the melody requires fletching a bow of very specific timber, harvesting bowstring vines that only grow under certain moons, and locating the buried fifth stone by magical means.

When the song plays, the forest responds. A hidden grove opens — the First Glade, where the elves first awoke — and within it sits the memory of the First Elf, preserved in amber light. Aelwen will not go to the Glade herself. She says the song is not for the singer; the song is for the forest to hear.

Steps

[1] Speak with Lyricist Aelwen. Learn what a resonance bow is, and agree to fletch one
Where: Veilwood amphitheatre
Requires: fletching plans
Aelwen hums while she writes. The notes are on the page and also in the air.
[2] Harvest bowstring vines under the correct moon. Fletch the resonance bow from singing-wood
Where: Veilwood forest; amphitheatre workshop
Requires: fletching, woodcutting
The singing-wood vibrates when cut. You will finish the cut quickly, to be kind.
[3] Fire the resonance bow at each of the four standing stones. Record the notes in order
Where: Veilwood standing stones
Requires: ranged
Each stone holds a note. The notes do not harmonize with each other; they harmonize with the fifth, once it is found.
[4] Use magic to locate the buried fifth stone. Unearth it
Where: Veilwood forest floor, unknown
Requires: magic, digging
The earth gives up the stone with a soft sound like a held breath releasing.
[5] Capture the fifth note. Play the full five-note melody at the amphitheatre
Where: Veilwood amphitheatre
Requires: the resonance bow, the five notes
The forest goes still. Then it responds.
[6] Enter the First Glade and speak with the memory of the First Elf
Where: Veilwood, First Glade
Requires: silence, respect
The memory is warm. It does not ask for anything.

Twist

The fifth note is wrong. The stone sounds a note a half-tone off from what Aelwen expected. You may play it as it is, which opens the Glade but subtly changes what the memory of the First Elf says. Or you may re-carve the stone to match Aelwen's expectation, which is faster but is a kind of forgery the forest will notice.

Resolution

The Glade opens. The First Elf's memory shares a single sentence that will sit in your quest log forever. Aelwen accepts a singing-wood longbow from you in exchange for your newfound access; she crafts a second for herself and sings you out of her amphitheatre.

What it unlocks

You carry a singing-wood longbow, which is a bow of remarkable range and a quiet tone. The First Glade is open to you — a shrine, a training ground, a place you may sit and be. Aelwen will teach you advanced Veilwood fletching at her own pace, which is unhurried and excellent.

Dialogue

Lyricist Aelwen
Play it true. If the fifth note is wrong, it is wrong. I would rather know what the stones actually sing than hear what I expect to hear.
the First Elf
We were awake before we were named. The forest remembers the waking. It does not remember the naming. Either is fine.
