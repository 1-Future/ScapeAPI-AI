The Hollow King

grandmaster epic id: the_hollow_king
moryskahinkweald
Hollow Mire. An urgent summons from the elder: Morvek, a vampyre lord who should have been put down a generation ago, has entered the Inkweald through the Moryskah gate and has begun feeding on dreams directly. If he is not stopped, he becomes unkillable.

Premise

Morvek is old enough to remember Malachar as a junior. He has discovered that the Inkweald's dreams can nourish him in a way mortal blood cannot, and he has begun feeding. Each dreamer he consumes makes him stronger. If he consumes enough, he becomes the Hollow King — a creature of pure dream-vampirism, immune to most conventional attacks.

You will brew a Dream Walker elixir, enter the Inkweald through the Moryskah gate, track Morvek through three nightmare realms he has corrupted, slay a Nightmare Sentinel in each, and confront him at the Dream Nexus. He fights in three phases: shadow magic, transformation into the Hollow King, and fracture into three copies of himself. The real one must be identified and given the killing blow.

Steps

[1] Receive the summons from the Hollow Mire elder. Brew a Dream Walker elixir
Where: Hollow Mire
Requires: herblore
The elixir is heavy. It smells of old libraries.
[2] Enter the Inkweald through the Moryskah gate. Track Morvek through three nightmare realms
Where: Inkweald, three realms
Requires: combat, navigation
The realms are nightmares of people you do not know. They are still terrifying.
[3] In each realm, pray to dispel Morvek's corruption and slay a Nightmare Sentinel
Where: three realms
Requires: prayer, slayer
Each Sentinel is a knight from a dream nobody is willing to claim.
[4] Reach Morvek at the Dream Nexus. He has already begun feeding
Where: Dream Nexus
Requires: presence
Morvek looks up when you enter. He looks exhausted, and hungry, and amused.
[5] Phase 1: counter his shadow magic with holy spells
Where: Dream Nexus
Requires: magic, prayer
His spells are colder than cold.
[6] Phase 2: he transforms into the Hollow King. Melee combat required
Where: Dream Nexus
Requires: attack
The Hollow King does not look down. He looks through.
[7] Phase 3: he fractures into three copies. Use magic to identify the real one, then the killing blow
Where: Dream Nexus
Requires: magic
One copy breathes slightly. The other two do not.

Twist

Morvek will offer, at the cusp of phase three, to retreat to Castle Malachar forever in exchange for his life. Accepting sets up a future raid. Refusing and destroying him is the clean path. Both are supported.

Resolution

Morvek is destroyed, or banished to Malachar. The Inkweald heals. You return through the Moryskah gate with a hollow crown on your head — a cosmetic, and also a passive that makes undead hesitate in your presence.

What it unlocks

You wear the Hollow Crown, a cosmetic with a real passive effect: undead creatures treat you as a high-ranked presence and will hesitate briefly before attacking. You carry Morvek's fang as a trophy, and the Moryskah gate to the Inkweald is open to you as a stable portal.
