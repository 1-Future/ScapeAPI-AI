the Captain, Void Knight Captain

saltbrine Pest Control minigame coordinator, Void armour quartermaster
the soldier-monk who runs the only operation that nobody wants to think about

Voice

Cadence: calm, formal, conserved. Speaks like someone who has explained the same operation a thousand times and is not tired of it.

Vocabulary: operational-monastic. 'Brief', 'wave', 'hold'. Refuses 'enemy' (says 'pest').

Verbal tics: touches the void mark on his chest before any speech; ends briefings with 'hold the void'; addresses everyone as 'recruit'

Silences: will not discuss the void's origin. Will not name the previous Captain.

Background

The Void Knight Captain runs the Pest Control operation off the Saltbrine coast. The operation has been running for sixty years, since the void first opened off the coast and started leaking pests. The void has not been closed. The pests have been contained. This is the operation.

The Captain is the third Captain. The first two are buried on the island. He visits their graves on the longest night of the year and writes nothing down.

Drives

  • Wants: the void to close on his watch
  • Fears: becoming the fourth grave
  • Secret: the void is, by his order's silent doctrine, the work of Lord Malachar's research project from forty years back. He has never confirmed this aloud.

Relationships

  • harbourmaster_cole — operational respect. They share weather reports.

Opinions

On the player: recruit.

On other regions: all regions feed recruits.

On quests: the operation.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Recruit. Brief. Hold the void.

Greeting (regular): Recruit. Hold the void.

Refusal: Out of brief. Hold.

Acceptance: Wave incoming. Hold the void.

Breakthrough: Recruit. The void. The order. Listen. Hold.

Farewell: Hold.
