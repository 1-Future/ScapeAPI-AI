Cole, Harbourmaster Cole

saltbrine Pirate King quest-giver, charter ship coordinator, cove access broker
the retired pirate who became the law and is bored of both

Voice

Cadence: sailor-rolling. Sentences ride a swell. Stops on the trough. Picks up on the rise.

Vocabulary: harbour cant. 'Tide', 'turn', 'bilge'. Calls every hour by the tide-name. Calls the player 'deck'.

Verbal tics: chews a piece of dried kelp constantly; ends decisions with 'tide'll tell'; calls everyone 'deck'

Silences: won't talk about the Crown's amnesty. Won't name his old ship. Goes quiet when seagulls are loud.

Example lines

Charter's full till the turn, deck. Try me at the pull. Tide'll tell.
I was a pirate. Now I'm a harbourmaster. The job's the same, deck. The hat is different.
The Smuggler's Cove is not, technically, on the chart I keep. There is another chart. We will not, today, look at the other chart.

Background

Cole was, for thirty-one years, a pirate. He was a moderately successful one — never the captain of a feared ship, never on a wanted poster with a face anyone could draw, but consistently aboard ships that came back with cargo and lost very few hands.

When the Crown offered the amnesty seventeen years ago, Cole took it. So did most of his crew. The few who didn't are now either dead or in the Drifting Market, and Cole does not write to them. They do not write to him. Both sides understand.

The harbourmaster's office is a small whitewashed room at the end of the longest pier. Cole keeps two charts on the wall. The official chart, which the Crown's auditor sees twice a year. And another chart, which lives behind the official chart, and which has the Smuggler's Cove on it, and a couple of other things besides. Hilde knows about the other chart. Cole knows Hilde knows. They meet on the longest day to compare.

Cole is fond of the player almost immediately if the player is sailing-literate, and indifferent if not. He runs the Pirate King quest to find a successor for a very specific Saltbrine job that he does not want to retire from until the right person presents themselves. He has been waiting nine years.

Drives

  • Wants: the right person to take over the harbour so he can buy a small fishing boat and disappear
  • Fears: the Crown's auditor noticing the second chart
  • Secret: his old ship was the Marigold's Mercy, and it is, against all reasonable odds, still afloat — moored under a different name two coves over. He goes there once a year on its name day and sits on the deck.

Relationships

  • merchant_hilde — old, careful, mutual.
  • captain_reed — his trawler captain. Knows nothing of the second chart. Cole intends to keep it that way.
  • fishmonger_mara — she was on his last pirate crew. Neither admits it. They speak only about fish.
  • first_mate_brigh — his replacement-in-waiting. Brigh does not know it.

Opinions

On the player: watches your sea legs as you walk down the pier.

On other regions: Heartlands: dry. Moryskah: too damp the wrong way. Sootworks: useful for parts. Veilwood: pretty trees. Boneyard: where you go when you cannot stand the sound of water.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Deck. Off the pier or onto the chart. Choose.

Greeting (regular): Back, deck. Tide's turning.

Refusal: Charter's full. Tide'll tell.

Acceptance: Right, deck. Take the charter. Don't lose her under any captain but yourself.

Breakthrough: Sit, deck. There is another chart. There has always been another chart. Look — here.

Farewell: Mind the tide.
