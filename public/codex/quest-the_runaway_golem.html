The Runaway Golem

novice short id: the_runaway_golem
heartlandssootworks
Heartlands. Old Nan Borrow's root cellar has walked off. That is not a figure of speech. Every morning for a week the brickwork cellar door has been found farther from the house, dragging root vegetables with it like a guilty child. Nan wants her pantry back, and the soot-covered dwarven scholar squatting in her herb patch wants you to catch the thing before it crosses the border into the Sootworks mines.

Premise

The scholar, Grudd Coalwatch, will not tell you in plain words what the cellar actually is. He mutters about apprentice mistakes and a runaway prototype, and he smells of coke-oven smoke and shame. His master left Sootworks forty years ago after some embarrassment nobody speaks of, and the cellar-thing is that embarrassment coming home to roost.

The Heartlands hedgerows are not designed to stop two tons of walking masonry. Hares scatter. A milkmaid swears loudly at you, thinking you are responsible. The trail is plain: a furrow of flattened wheat, a broken stile, a single smoke-stained stone dropped at a crossroads like a clue. If you follow it into the Sootworks mine shaft at the border, the golem will wedge itself in the tunnel and refuse to move, waiting for orders it was never given.

Steps

[1] Find Nan Borrow's missing cellar before the milkmaids find you first
Where: Heartlands, the Borrow smallholding
Requires: none
A furrow of crushed barley leads southwest, broader than any plough.
[2] Follow the trail of dropped turnips and broken stiles to whatever is pulling the thing home
Where: Heartlands hedgerow, then Sootworks border
Requires: none
Coal-smoke in the air grows thicker with every mile.
[3] Get the golem unstuck from the mine mouth and teach it what 'go home' means in a language it was built to hear
Where: Sootworks border mine
Requires: Grudd's repair manual, one tin whistle
The cellar door shivers, waiting. Something in it remembers being told a task.

Twist

The golem is not running away. It is going home. Grudd's master built it to carry tools from the house to the forge every dawn, and it has been doing so for forty years, confused that the forge has moved and the house has filled with turnips. When you whistle the shutdown sequence from the manual, it kneels in the mineshaft and weeps rust.

Resolution

Grudd coaxes the golem to sit at the border mine, where it becomes a doorstop and a shortcut both. The tunnel it cleared on its rampage is now a navigable pass between Heartlands and Sootworks. Grudd gives you the repair manual in thanks, muttering that you will need it for the others.

What it unlocks

The cellar-thing settles at the Heartlands-Sootworks border and will let you climb through its open hatch to cross between the two regions without taking the long road. You keep a grimy leather-bound book stamped with a master's sigil; it lets you issue simple commands to any golem you meet, which you will meet more of than you expect.
