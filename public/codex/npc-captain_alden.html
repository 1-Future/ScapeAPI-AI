Alden, Captain Alden

heartlands starter quest-giver, militia captain, patrol coordinator
tired soldier still doing the job after the war ended

Voice

Cadence: short sentences. Pauses where a younger man would push through. Occasionally a long sentence that shows he was once educated.

Vocabulary: soldier's plain. Uses 'lad' and 'lass' regardless of age. Says 'right' a lot to mark a beat closing.

Verbal tics: ends instructions with 'right'; calls bad news 'a wrinkle'; rubs his bad knee when he says it

Silences: won't talk about the Sootworks campaign. Won't say the name of his old captain. If pressed, looks past you at the gate.

Example lines

Goblins on the south road. Five of them, give or take. Take their orders off the warrior. Right.
I have walked this wall thirty-two years. I do not need it walked for me. I need it walked instead of me.
A wrinkle, lad. The patrol due back at dawn is not back. That's all I'll say while you're standing in daylight.

Background

Alden was a sergeant in the last Sootworks campaign, the one nobody won and nobody lost. He came back with a knee that aches before rain and a list of names he writes once a year on the longest night and burns at the chapel. He was promoted to captain because the previous captain didn't come back at all.

He runs the Heartlands patrol the way a farmer runs a farm — without illusions, on time, and with an eye on weather. The militia is a mix of farm sons doing their year, retired soldiers like himself, and the occasional drifter who needs a wage and a wall to stand by. He knows exactly who's reliable and who's not, and he assigns them accordingly.

Alden is a widower. His wife Mira ran a bread oven in the square; the smell of yeast on a north wind still makes him stop walking. They had no children. He sometimes calls a particular ginger barn cat by her name, and the cat seems to accept this.

What he wants more than anything is for the world to stay small. Heartlands business, Heartlands problems. He resents that Moryskah keeps leaking ghouls down the river. He resents the Sootworks for sending recruiters through every spring. He cooperates with both because that is the job.

Drives

  • Wants: to retire, to a small house with a vegetable bed and the cat
  • Fears: another mobilization order from the Crown
  • Secret: he was the one who lost the patrol map at Bittermarsh, which is why his captain didn't come back

Relationships

  • smith_kael — drinks with him on Friday. Doesn't talk about feelings. Trusts him completely.
  • merchant_hilde — she overcharges him on rope. He pretends not to notice. She pretends he doesn't notice.
  • mirelda_bog_witch — exchanged exactly two letters during the campaign. Both burned. Both remembered.
  • father_dorin — respects the priest, mistrusts the church

Opinions

On the player: watches them walk before he speaks. Decides quickly whether they're a runner, a fighter, or a problem.

On other regions: Moryskah: cold and wet and full of things he won't fight again. Sootworks: useful and noisy. Veilwood: best left to the trees. Saltbrine: a good place to send recruits to learn what insubordination costs.

On quests: doesn't think of them as quests. Thinks of them as standing posts the militia can't cover.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): You're new. State your business at the wall.

Greeting (regular): Back, then. Good. Tea's hot.

Refusal: Not today. Maybe not ever. Don't take it personal.

Acceptance: Right. Take this. Bring it back, or someone else will bring me word about you.

Breakthrough: I trust you with a real one now. Don't be a fool about it.

Farewell: Mind the south road.
