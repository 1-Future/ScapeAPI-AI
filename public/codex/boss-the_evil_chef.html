The Evil Chef

heartlandstier 2

Bestiary

The Culinaromancer was a title, once — an honorific for the senior chef of the Heartlands Grand Hall, back when the Hall held feasts for six hundred and the kitchens ran three shifts. The last Culinaromancer was a woman named Vessa, who retired into a small private kitchen after the court dissolved, and who vanished from that kitchen one winter, leaving a pot on the stove that was still warm when her assistant found it two weeks later.

The Evil Chef is what came back. Not Vessa. Not entirely not Vessa. The kitchen stayed warm for a decade. Then there was a soup, in the kitchen, that had not been made, and the soup was good, and the soup was tasted and found to contain ingredients that had not been brought to the Hall in a hundred years, and the kitchen was sealed. The Evil Chef emerged from the kitchen some time after that, by a route nobody saw, and began haunting the Grand Hall.

The Evil Chef's combat is a three-course meal. The first phase is Soup: the Chef hurls ladles of scalding broth, and the floor fills with steaming puddles that must be stepped around rather than through. The second phase is the Main: the Chef wields a cleaver the size of a door and attacks in heavy arcs that can be parried but must be timed. The third phase is Dessert: the Chef produces a pastry — a glossy, dark thing, iced with something that is not icing — and hurls it, and the pastry explodes, and the explosion spreads an effect across the room that drains any buff or potion the player is relying on.

The Chef is armoured in an apron. The apron is not ordinary cloth. The Chef is slow. The Chef is not intelligent in the ordinary sense — it does not strategise — but it does recognise food in an adventurer's inventory, and the Chef will prioritise attacks on any adventurer who is holding something edible. There is an obvious tactical consequence. Come hungry.

Defeat awards the Culinaromancer Gloves, which are the best melee gloves available before Barrows-tier equipment, and access to the Grand Hall, which restores the Heartlands' central teleport hub. The Evil Chef drops the gloves from a severed hand, and the severed hand is Vessa's hand, and the Lamplighters at the Chapel of the Last Light will, if asked, tell the player how to bury it.

The kitchen falls silent. Somewhere, a pot stops boiling.
