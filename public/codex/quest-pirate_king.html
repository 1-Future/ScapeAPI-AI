Pirate King

intermediate long id: pirate_king
saltbrineheartlands
Saltbrine harbour, under a sky the colour of a bruise. The harbour is closed because three pirate frigates sit at its mouth and nobody has yet worked out how to politely ask them to leave. Harbourmaster Cole drinks his breakfast from a tankard and tells you the only way into the pirate flagship is to be invited, and the only way to be invited is to lose a fight to their bosun.

Premise

The Pirate King is not a king. He is an administrative invention — the captain the other captains defer to because he has the best cook, the best charter, and the only copy of the trade wind almanac that is not ten years out of date. If he moves his fleet, Saltbrine opens. If he refuses, Saltbrine starves. Cole has been trying to negotiate for a week. The pirates are bored. They would like a duel.

You will lose the duel on purpose because the winner gets chained in the bilge and the loser gets the captain's table. Then you will eat, drink, steal the almanac, and decide whether to betray the Pirate King, befriend him, or replace him. The charter-ship network of Aelgard's coasts runs on his signature. However you leave that ship, the signature has to come with you.

Steps

[1] Find the bosun on the dock and lose a fight to him without looking like you meant to lose
Where: Saltbrine harbour, the main wharf
Requires: a willingness to bleed, an understanding of pride
The bosun is enormous, cheerful, and smells of butter. He apologises while he hits you.
[2] Accept the invitation to the captain's table. Eat everything offered. Drink what you can
Where: Pirate flagship, great cabin
Requires: appetite, manners
The cook is as good as advertised. The almanac is in a bookcase behind the captain's chair.
[3] Find a reason to be left alone in the great cabin for four minutes
Where: Pirate flagship, great cabin
Requires: an excuse, a fast hand
A steward returns after three minutes. You have the almanac or you do not.
[4] Decide how you are leaving the ship. Each way pays differently
Where: Pirate flagship
Requires: a decision, probably made already
The Pirate King has not asked you a direct question. He is waiting to see if you will ask one of him.
[5] Return to Cole at the harbour. Tell him what arrangement you reached, and what signature came with you
Where: Saltbrine harbour
Requires: the almanac or an agreement
Cole listens without interrupting, which for him is extraordinary.

Twist

The Pirate King is Cole's sister. She was the harbourmaster before he was, and she resigned to go to sea because somebody in the city wanted her quietly dead. Cole will not tell you. The King will. You have to decide whether Cole is told, when Cole is told, and in whose voice.

Resolution

The fleet moves. Saltbrine breathes. Cole reads the almanac at his own desk and does not thank you, which is his way. The Pirate King sends a gift: a hook-handle in brass, and a charter-ship pass stitched into the sleeve of a coat that fits you.

What it unlocks

The Saltbrine Reach is open to you and the charter-ship network is yours — you can pay passage between coastal ports as a regular traveller, which collapses the map. You carry the Captain's Hook, a cosmetic blade and a real benefit: your fishing goes faster when you are working from a boat. People on the docks give you the nod, the one that means you know the King.

Dialogue

Harbourmaster Cole
Lose the fight. Do not let him see you lose on purpose. He would rather lose on accident to an honest hand than win against a liar.
the Pirate King
I do not take the harbour. I visit. When I visit, the harbour chooses to close. That is a different thing and it matters.
