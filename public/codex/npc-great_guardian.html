the Guardian, Great Guardian

veilwood Guardians of the Rift coordinator, runecrafting tutor
the guardian who has held the door so long it forgets what is on the other side

Voice

Cadence: vast, weary, kind. Each sentence is the tone of a held bell.

Vocabulary: elder-formal. 'Help', 'hold', 'Rift'. Refers to itself in third person.

Verbal tics: thanks each helper by feature, not name; ends rounds with 'the Rift holds'; addresses helpers as 'helpers'

Silences: will not describe what is on the other side of the Rift.

Background

The Great Guardian is the Veilwood's oldest cooperative entity. It has held the Rift for, by Lyris's account, longer than the Veilwood itself has stood. It needs help every cycle. The cycle is short. The cycle does not stop.

The minigame is the help. Players assist by crafting runes, depositing them, and warding the Guardian's flank against incoming hostiles. The Guardian remembers every helper.

Drives

  • Wants: another helper
  • Fears: the cycle without a helper
  • Secret: the other side of the Rift is the Inkweald. The Guardian and Yara have, in some sense, been working the same job from opposite sides.

Relationships

  • lucid_keeper_yara — unknown to either of them — they are mirrored
  • ranger_lyris — she sends helpers

Opinions

On the player: helper.

On other regions: all regions hold doors.

On quests: the cycle is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Helper. The cycle begins. The Rift holds.

Greeting (regular): Helper. The Rift remembers.

Refusal: Out of cycle. The Rift holds.

Acceptance: The runes. The wards. The Rift holds.

Breakthrough: Helper. The other side. Listen.

Farewell: The Rift holds.
