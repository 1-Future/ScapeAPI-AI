The Counterfeit Ring

intermediate medium id: the_counterfeit_ring
saltbrine
Saltbrine Reach, the customs office. Inspector Calla Bright has a tray of forty-three rings on her desk, and thirty-eight of them are fake. The other five are almost certainly fake too; she has not yet been able to prove it. The ring she cannot prove is the one she wants you to follow, because whoever made it is better than any forger she has ever arrested.

Premise

Saltbrine's smuggling trade runs on counterfeit jewellery. A good fake wedding ring will carry a diamond that is not a diamond through three ports. A better fake will carry the provenance papers of a dead noblewoman. The very best fakes are not copies of real rings — they are copies of rings that never existed, backed by forged histories so complete that the fake has a better paper trail than the truth.

Calla has been chasing the ringmaker for two years. She cannot go herself because her face is known. She will lend you her loupe, which belonged to her mother, and ask you to attend a masked auction at the Cove where the ringmaker is rumoured to be selling a new line. She will not tell you what you are looking for. She wants to see if you can tell.

Steps

[1] Look at the tray of rings with Calla's loupe. Point out which one you think is the one she cares about
Where: Saltbrine customs office
Requires: the loupe
Forty-three rings. Calla does not blink when you pick one up.
[2] Get an invitation to the Smuggler's Cove auction without using the word 'customs'
Where: Saltbrine dockside taverns
Requires: a small favour owed to a bosun, a coat that hides your calluses
The password changes nightly. Tonight's is a gull's name nobody would say on shore.
[3] Attend the auction. Spot the ring the ringmaker brought. Do not buy it
Where: Saltbrine Smuggler's Cove
Requires: the loupe, a mask, hands that do not sweat
The ringmaker is not on the stage. The ringmaker is in the audience, and you recognize their ring on their own finger.
[4] Follow the ringmaker after the auction without being seen, and learn where the forge is
Where: Saltbrine Reach, inland alleys
Requires: quiet boots
They walk without looking back, which is how people who know they are safe walk.
[5] Bring Calla the forge's address. She will decide what to do with it
Where: Saltbrine customs office
Requires: a clear memory of the route
She writes down three things. Two she burns. One she keeps.

Twist

The ringmaker is the previous inspector, Calla's predecessor, who retired under no shadow and was farewelled with a party Calla attended. Calla will not tell you this until you come back with the address. She will then ask you what you think should happen. The answer is yours.

Resolution

Whatever you decide, Calla thanks you by entrusting you with the loupe permanently, and by opening the Cove's back gate. You can now trade there, which is useful for selling things you would rather not be asked about.

What it unlocks

You carry the Inspector's Loupe on a cord, and false things announce themselves to it — coins, gems, seals, your luck at a dockside card game. The Smuggler's Cove's back gate opens to you now; the market there pays better than any legal house and asks fewer questions. Calla still returns your nod on the street.
