Breakpoints

The "this changes everything" moments. Every significant threshold that permanently changes how you play.

Transformative (62)

The biggest moments. You remember these forever.
  • attack 70 Barrows weapons. Abyssal whip territory. You can now use the best non-degradable melee weapons.
  • ranged 75 Toxic blowpipe. The most iconic ranged weapon. Fast, venomous, game-changing DPS.
  • prayer 43 All three protection prayers. This is THE breakpoint. Bosses that were impossible become farmable. The game permanently changes.
  • prayer 70 Piety. 20% attack, 23% strength, 25% defence boost. THE endgame melee prayer. All serious PvMers need this.
  • magic 55 High Level Alchemy. Convert any item to coins. The backbone of the entire economy. EVERYTHING has a floor price.
  • magic 94 Ice Barrage (Ancient Magicks). The most iconic spell. AoE freeze + massive damage. PvP and PvM endgame.
  • runecrafting 77 Blood runes. AFK runecrafting. Profitable. Fuels blood barrage for endgame PvM. The skill is finally worth training.
  • herblore 38 Prayer potions. The most important potion in the game. Sustains protection prayers. Changes ALL bossing.
  • slayer 55 Slayer helm craftable. 16.67% accuracy and damage boost on task. Slayer becomes THE combat training method.
  • slayer 95 Hydra. Alchemical Hydra drops Hydra claw (Dragon Hunter Lance component). Best slayer gp/hr.
  • construction 67 Gilded altar with two incense burners. 3.5x prayer XP. The reason most people train construction.
  • construction 83 Rejuvenation pool (ornate). Restore HP, prayer, stats, special attack, run energy. Between every trip.
  • quest: desert_treasure Desert Treasure. Unlocks Ancient Magicks spellbook. Ice Barrage, Blood Barrage, Smoke Barrage. The game splits into "before Ancient Magicks" and "after."
  • quest: lunar_diplomacy Lunar Diplomacy. Unlocks Lunar Spellbook. Vengeance, Heal Group, NPC Contact, Humidify. Utility magic that changes every activity.
  • quest: dragon_slayer_aelgard Dragon Slayer. Anti-dragon shield. Rune platebody equippable. The original "you beat the game" moment.
  • quest: rfd_finale Recipe for Disaster. Barrows Gloves. BiS hand slot for melee. The mid-game achievement everyone works toward.
  • quest: sins_of_malachar Sins of Malachar. Castle Malachar raid. Lord Malachar boss. Scythe of Malachar — the most powerful melee weapon.
  • quest: the_last_light The Last Light. Post-game content. Eclipse Guardian boss. Solar Blessing prayer. The game after the game.
  • item: Barrows Gloves Barrows Gloves. The culmination of Recipe for Disaster. BiS hands. A rite of passage every account completes.
  • item: Scythe of Malachar Scythe of Malachar. Hits 3 times per swing on large monsters. The most powerful melee weapon. The endgame flex. Degrades.
  • item: Fire cape Fire cape. Proof you completed the Fight Caves. BiS melee cape. Every serious player has one.
  • item: Infernal cape Infernal cape. The hardest PvM challenge in the game. Replaces Fire cape. The ultimate achievement.
  • quest: the_culinaromancers_curse Grand Feast complete. Culinaromancer Gloves equipped. Best melee gloves until Barrows. This IS the Heartlands prestige moment.
  • quest: ghosts_ahoy Ectofuntus unlocked. Prayer XP rate jumps 4x. Every bone you own becomes 4x more valuable. Transformative.
  • quest: the_haunted_mine Salve Amulet equipped. +16.7% damage vs undead. Moryskah combat efficiency DOUBLES overnight.
  • quest: barrows_brothers_legend Barrows unlocked. THE Moryskah prestige content begins. 6 bosses, mid-game degradable gear, Amulet of the Damned path.
  • runecrafting 77 Blood rune crafting at the Moryskah altar. Profitable rune training + feeds Scythe/Sanguinesti upkeep. The iconic RC breakpoint.
  • firemaking 70 Deep-coal furnace unlocked. 245k firemaking XP/hr. Sootworks becomes the fastest firemaking region in Aelgard.
  • quest: the_iron_tongue_heresy Brass Choir open. Industrial prayer XP — 140k/hr with relics. Sootworks prayer becomes the best outside Moryskah.
  • quest: the_clockwork_heretic Clockwork Heretic defeated. Deep-coal furnace, vent-bloom master brewing, and rune-imbue press all unlock together.
  • quest: lighthouse_reignited Signal-Fire Network active. Six lighthouses lit. Teleport between coastal ports and shipping lanes. She does not need the charter-voucher — she holds the wind.
  • quest: the_drowned_king Captain's Bond Relic equipped. Kraken-Warding prayer active. The Reach opens — the Drowned do not come while the Bond holds. She is the signature Saltbrine prestige moment.
  • quest: moon_cycle_apprentice Mooncourt unlocked. Lunar runes at 52. Blood-rune-equivalent escape hatch from the RC Misery Zone. Dream-runes at 74. The forest taught you the tide.
  • quest: what_the_forest_said Dream-Tongue spellbook acquired. Phase-arrow through one guard. Kin-call any Veilwood NPC. Stag-step ignores one obstacle. The forest answered.
  • quest: she_sang_to_the_loom Loom Sanctum opened. Court-cloth recipes unlocked. Every court-tier armour path begins HERE — cross-region crafting demand rises.
  • runecrafting 52 Lunar rune crafting. After weeks of the RC treadmill, the Mooncourt altar opens. Low-attention, profitable — the Veilwood escape hatch.
  • quest: the_water_numbered_wind The Veiled Grave opens. Prayer-points become something the desert understands. Water-blessing cures poison in one tick. The single most important Boneyard moment.
  • quest: pyramid_of_the_salted_king The Bone Pyramid opens. The Salted King can be called hour by hour. Sun-King Cape unlocks the region's slayer cap. The desert names you killer.
  • quest: the_burnt_library_index Three scroll-spells unique to your character. Your neighbor has three others. Non-degenerate: the Library chooses. Prayer-binding spellcasting becomes the Boneyard mage identity.
  • quest: hyena_market_apprentice Cipher-tongue acquired. Sand-script on every wall becomes readable. The disappeared-trade opens. Thieving XP rates double overnight in the Boneyard.
  • mining 90 Crystal mining cap reached. Prismstone + lens-quartz unlock the full Lens Forge chain. The desert yields its clear.
  • runecrafting 92 Light-rune binding unlocks. The only rune that burns instead of breaks. BiS for endgame combat magic.
  • herblore 92 Prism elixir distills. +15% spell damage for five minutes. The BiS magic consumable in Aelgard.
  • quest: the_edge_remembers The Edge-Keeper remembers you. BiS endgame combat training opens. Strength, defence, HP, prayer all at their peak rates.
  • quest: the_mirrored_spire_quest The Spire reflects. Prism-burst magic, light-arrow mastery, prism elixir, crystal-imbued firemaking, master vault thieving all open at once.
  • quest: the_lens_forge_quest The Lens Forge fires. BiS smithing bar, plate, and weapon blank all open. The desert hammers its own edge.
  • quest: the_door_that_opens_last The door opens last. Pageturn Court unlocked. The forest's geometry starts reading like a bound volume. Transformative moment.
  • quest: mirror_stag Answered Antler Cape equipped. Mirror-Glade combat trials open. You can fight yourself now. You begin to win.
  • quest: the_library_that_reads_back Dreambinding spellbook. Three random scrolls per character — no two players the same. Non-degenerate variety baked in.
  • quest: by_page_by_margin_by_self Dream-warding prayer book. True-Name Binding and Margin-Walk change how you move through the forest. Identity shift.
  • ? Level 50 wilderness. Teleblocked deep. Twenty-three tiles from any glory, minimum. Revenant caves unlock.
  • ? Level 80 wilderness — the long road. Teleblocked by every mage who sees you. Vet'ion, venenatis, deep-rev only here.
  • quest: the_mage_arena_trial God spells unlocked. Mage Arena cape equipped. The wilds mage identity crystallizes — autocast god spells, no ditch count needed.
  • quest: enter_the_abyss Abyss opened. Fastest RC route in Aelgard. PKers camp the portal, but RC XP rates double overnight.
  • quest: blade_of_the_wilds_third Blade of the Wilds (final). Wilderness Crown lineage complete. The throne of wilds teleport unlocks. Wilds-maxed player identity.
  • runecrafting 95 Wrath rune altar unlocked. Deepest wild. Level-fifty-two wilderness. Two tiles to the throne pocket. The only wrath rune source in Aelgard.
  • item: wilderness_crown Wilderness Crown equipped. Peak wilds identity. +20% damage bonus inside wilds. The risk king.
  • prayer 92 Soulsplit prayer unlock threshold. Heal for 25% of damage dealt. The defining wilds prayer.
  • quest: pharaohs_reckoning Ankh of Rebirth acquired. One revive. THE Boneyard prestige moment — earned after a 3-phase pharaoh fight.
  • runecrafting 90 Soul rune crafting in Inkweald. Most profitable RC method. Cross-region endgame magic demand.
  • item: infernal_cape Infernal Cape. The ultimate prestige item. Proves you beat the hardest content in Aelgard.
  • quest: coronation_of_the_revenant_king Wilderness Crown equipped. Risk king of Aelgard. Transformative identity shift.

By Skill

attack (6)

  • level 5 Steel weapons. First real upgrade from bronze. Scimitars become your best friend.
  • level 20 Mithril weapons. Combat starts feeling efficient. Hill giants become farmable.
  • level 40 Rune weapons. The mid-game standard. Rune scimitar defines the early-mid game melee experience.
  • level 60 Dragon weapons. After Monkey Business quest, dragon scimitar is the gateway to serious PvM.
  • level 70 Barrows weapons. Abyssal whip territory. You can now use the best non-degradable melee weapons.
  • level 75 Godswords and Dragon Hunter Lance. Endgame melee. Every boss in the game is now fightable with BiS.

strength (4)

  • level 10 Max hits visibly increase. First taste of "I am getting stronger."
  • level 50 Granite maul special attack threshold. PvP becomes accessible.
  • level 70 Dharok's full set. At low HP, you can hit 60+ with the greataxe. Changes how you think about HP.
  • level 99 Max melee strength. The grind that defines RS. Strength cape gives +4 invisible boost.

defence (3)

  • level 1 Pure builds stay at 1. A conscious choice that defines PvP. Defence is optional — that is the design.
  • level 40 Rune armour. Green dragonhide. You stop dying to random mobs. The world opens up.
  • level 70 Barrows armour. The endgame defensive standard. Torag, Guthan, Verac, Dharok plate.

hitpoints (3)

  • level 40 Enough HP to eat a full shark and sustain through mid-game bosses.
  • level 70 Bossing threshold. 70 HP means you can take a big hit and brew back. GWD becomes viable.
  • level 99 Max HP. Anglerfish can overheal to 121. You are as tanky as you will ever be.

ranged (5)

  • level 20 Willow shortbow. Ranged becomes a real combat style, not just a novelty.
  • level 40 Green dragonhide armour. Yew shortbow. Ranged is now competitive with melee for many encounters.
  • level 61 Rune crossbow. One-handed ranged weapon with shield slot free. Changes the meta for prayer-using fights.
  • level 70 Armadyl crossbow. Crystal bow. The ranged endgame begins.
  • level 75 Toxic blowpipe. The most iconic ranged weapon. Fast, venomous, game-changing DPS.

prayer (9)

  • level 1 Chaos Altar is accessible at ANY prayer level. 7x XP. THE wilderness economy of prayer training.
  • level 13 Protect Item. You keep one more item on death. PvP and PvM risk calculation changes.
  • level 37 Protect from Magic. The first protection prayer. Mages in PvP can no longer freeze-kill you.
  • level 43 All three protection prayers. This is THE breakpoint. Bosses that were impossible become farmable. The game permanently changes.
  • level 60 Chivalry. 15% attack, 18% strength, 15% defence boost. Massive DPS increase if you can sustain the drain.
  • level 70 Piety. 20% attack, 23% strength, 25% defence boost. THE endgame melee prayer. All serious PvMers need this.
  • level 74 Rigour. 20% ranged accuracy, 23% ranged strength, 25% defence. The ranged equivalent of Piety.
  • level 77 Augury. 25% magic accuracy, 25% defence. The magic equivalent of Piety.
  • level 92 Soulsplit prayer unlock threshold. Heal for 25% of damage dealt. The defining wilds prayer.

magic (7)

  • level 7 Lvl-1 Enchant. Sapphire jewelry becomes Ring of Recoil. First taste of enchanting.
  • level 25 Varrock teleport. Free instant travel. The world shrinks. Everything is closer.
  • level 33 Telekinetic Grab. Items behind fences. Wine of zamorak without angering monks. Clever interactions.
  • level 43 Superheat Item. Smelt bars without a furnace. Mining + Smithing in one inventory. Huge efficiency.
  • level 55 High Level Alchemy. Convert any item to coins. The backbone of the entire economy. EVERYTHING has a floor price.
  • level 70 Ice Burst (Ancient Magicks). AoE freeze spell. Slayer tasks go from single-target to mass-killing. XP rates double.
  • level 94 Ice Barrage (Ancient Magicks). The most iconic spell. AoE freeze + massive damage. PvP and PvM endgame.

runecrafting (15)

  • level 25 Soot-Library opens. Industrial runes (soot-cant scrolls) craft 2x per forge-crystal. Sootworks magic comes online.
  • level 27 Cosmic runes. Required for all enchant spells. Your crafted jewelry becomes enchanted jewelry.
  • level 44 Nature runes. Self-sufficient high alchemy. You craft your own nature runes, alch your own items. Full circle.
  • level 52 Lunar rune crafting. After weeks of the RC treadmill, the Mooncourt altar opens. Low-attention, profitable — the Veilwood escape hatch.
  • level 54 Law runes. Self-crafted teleports. Total mobility independence.
  • level 65 Death runes. The currency of burst and barrage magic. Slayer efficiency multiplier.
  • level 72 Name-rune crafting. Mid-tier Lunar-kin RC. Feeds Dream-warding prayers and Library magic simultaneously.
  • level 77 Blood runes. AFK runecrafting. Profitable. Fuels blood barrage for endgame PvM. The skill is finally worth training.
  • level 77 Blood rune crafting at the Moryskah altar. Profitable rune training + feeds Scythe/Sanguinesti upkeep. The iconic RC breakpoint.
  • level 77 Salt-Pan runecrafting paired with Moryskah blood altar — the Aelgard RC endgame. Water rune at scale. Cross-region: Salt-Pan feeds the charter-conjure economy across all ports.
  • level 77 Rune-salt double-binding at the Salt Cistern. The only honest rune in the game, the prophet says. The Boneyard runecrafter's ascension.
  • level 90 Soul runes. The highest altar. Used in the most powerful spells.
  • level 90 Soul rune crafting in Inkweald. Most profitable RC method. Cross-region endgame magic demand.
  • level 92 Light-rune binding unlocks. The only rune that burns instead of breaks. BiS for endgame combat magic.
  • level 95 Wrath rune altar unlocked. Deepest wild. Level-fifty-two wilderness. Two tiles to the throne pocket. The only wrath rune source in Aelgard.

construction (5)

  • level 20 First house rooms. You have a HOUSE. It is empty and sad but it is yours.
  • level 50 Portals in your house. Teleport to major cities from your own home.
  • level 67 Gilded altar with two incense burners. 3.5x prayer XP. The reason most people train construction.
  • level 82 Jewellery box (ornate). All teleport jewelry in one interface. Never carry jewelry again.
  • level 83 Rejuvenation pool (ornate). Restore HP, prayer, stats, special attack, run energy. Between every trip.

agility (8)

  • level 10 Run energy restores faster. The world stops feeling sluggish.
  • level 40 Several key shortcuts unlock. The Falador dungeon shortcut saves minutes per trip.
  • level 52 Wilderness agility course opens. The one-way escape route. The dispenser at the end drops skull-gold each lap.
  • level 60 Dune-vault speed run opens. The sand becomes a musical instrument. The marks of grace rain down if the tempo holds.
  • level 70 Most endgame shortcuts accessible. God Wars Dungeon shortcut. Bossing efficiency jumps.
  • level 70 Wilds shortcut network fully usable. Every shortcut chained into one route. Escape becomes a skill the agility-capped keep.
  • level 75 Deep gearwalk unlocked. 80k agility XP/hr + pumpman seals. The tick-perfect endgame Sootworks course.
  • level 92 Hallowed Sepulchre floor 5. The best agility training in the game and one of the best money-makers.

herblore (8)

  • level 3 Attack potions. Your first potion. Herblore stops being a mystery.
  • level 38 Prayer potions. The most important potion in the game. Sustains protection prayers. Changes ALL bossing.
  • level 45 Super attack potions. 5 + 15% attack boost. The super potion series begins.
  • level 63 Super restores. Restores prayer AND all stats. The premier sustain potion for bossing.
  • level 69 Antifire potions. Required to fight dragons. Without this, dragonfire kills you. Whole boss category unlocked.
  • level 81 Saradomin brews. Heals 16 per dose. The endgame healing potion paired with super restores.
  • level 90 Overloads (if raids herblore). The ultimate combat potion. Boosts all combat stats massively.
  • level 92 Prism elixir distills. +15% spell damage for five minutes. The BiS magic consumable in Aelgard.

thieving (4)

  • level 28 Fruit stalls. Good XP, zero attention. Your first real AFK skilling method.
  • level 50 Coal-Throat strongboxes open. 140k/hr coin + cinderbar ingots. The iconic Sootworks thieving money grind.
  • level 55 Blackjacking. The highest thieving XP/hr in the game. Requires insane clicking.
  • level 91 Master farmers become very profitable. Rare herb seeds every few pickpockets.

crafting (6)

  • level 10 Spinning flax into bowstrings. Your first cross-skill connection. Crafting feeds Fletching.
  • level 20 Cut sapphires. Gem cutting begins. Uncut gems from mining become cut gems for jewelry.
  • level 43 Diamond jewelry. The first serious crafting money. Diamond rings, necklaces, amulets.
  • level 63 Green dragonhide bodies. The profitable crafting method. Buy hides, craft bodies, alch or sell.
  • level 80 Inner Sanctum crystal armour crafting. Endgame armour path — crystal shards meet court-cloth meet moonsong. Veilwood's crafting peak.
  • level 84 Dragonstone amulets and fury amulets. The best combat amulet you can make.

fletching (5)

  • level 1 Arrow shafts. 15 per log. Your first batch processing — logs become ammunition.
  • level 50 Maple shortbow. The efficient fletching training tier. Cut, string, repeat.
  • level 70 Yew longbow. THE classic high-alch item. Yew logs -> yew longbow (u) -> string -> alch = profit.
  • level 72 Crystal bow fletching. ONLY source of crystal bows in Aelgard. Endgame ranged weapon start.
  • level 85 Magic longbow. Highest fletching product. Magic logs -> magic longbow -> alch for max gold.

slayer (8)

  • level 1 Slayer begins. The skill that makes combat structured. Kill assignments, earn points, unlock new monsters.
  • level 40 Moryskah Slayer Tower access. Aberrants, gargoyles, nechryaels — the mid-game slayer grind core.
  • level 50 Scuttler Pits slayer tier open. Anchor-Sworn gives the first salt-wraith task. The Reach's slayer ladder begins here — brine-troll to salt-vampire to salt-wraith.
  • level 55 Slayer helm craftable. 16.67% accuracy and damage boost on task. Slayer becomes THE combat training method.
  • level 72 Skeletal wyverns. First serious slayer money-maker. Drops are worth millions per task.
  • level 85 Abyssal demons. Drops the Abyssal Whip — the iconic weapon. This is the goal people grind slayer for.
  • level 91 Cerberus. Drops primordial, pegasian, eternal crystals. BiS boots for all 3 combat styles.
  • level 95 Hydra. Alchemical Hydra drops Hydra claw (Dragon Hunter Lance component). Best slayer gp/hr.

hunter (4)

  • level 53 Chinchompas. Stackable ranged weapon. AoE ranged training in dungeons. Ranged training gets 3x faster.
  • level 65 Ghost-camel chase unlocks. The dune chain at the hour the wind has no name. The camels drop pyre-bones, which burn for prayer.
  • level 73 Black chinchompas. Best ranged XP in the game (via chinning). Caught in the Wilds — PvP risk for PvM reward.
  • level 75 Crystal-hunter falconry opens. The crystal-hunter returns with what it has seen. Endgame hunter flagship.

mining (9)

  • level 15 Iron ore. First ore worth smelting solo. Power-mining iron becomes the go-to training method for years.
  • level 30 Coal. The key ingredient for steel, mithril, adamant, and rune bars. Opens the Smithing web.
  • level 55 Mithril ore. First "valuable" ore. Mining starts generating real money.
  • level 70 Adamantite ore. Serious mining money. You feel rich for the first time.
  • level 75 Lunar-adjacent crystal mining. Moon-cycle bonus yield. Higher tier than Veilwood. Inkweald becomes the crystal king.
  • level 85 Runite ore. The rarest ore. Two rocks in the Wilds, contested 24/7. Mining becomes PvP.
  • level 85 Wilderness rune rocks accessible. Two-times the respawn rate of any safe zone. Thirteen rocks in a node. Level-forty-six wilderness pocket.
  • level 90 Crystal mining cap reached. Prismstone + lens-quartz unlock the full Lens Forge chain. The desert yields its clear.
  • level 92 Crystal endgame mining. Post-mastery mining. Crystal dust feeds all endgame crystal gear.

smithing (8)

  • level 15 Iron bars. The 50% fail chance creates early frustration that Ring of Forging solves — your first "I need another skill."
  • level 30 Steel bars. Steel platebodies for alching. Smithing starts feeding the economy.
  • level 30 Blast Furnace access. Smithing XP rate triples. Halves coal requirements. Sootworks prestige.
  • level 50 Mithril bars. Mithril platebodies are a real product. You can make gear other players want.
  • level 55 Cannon Foundry unlocks sea-shot bolts. The only source in Aelgard. Ranged economy shifts — the crow's nest demands sea-shot; the Foundry demands smithing.
  • level 85 Runite bars. You can smelt the highest-tier bar. Rune platebody smithing is the endgame flex.
  • level 90 Vesta reforging threshold. The Wilds Blast Forge accepts the fragments. Peak melee lineage begins.
  • level 99 Rune platebody. The iconic smithing goal. 5 runite bars. The original flex.

fishing (6)

  • level 20 Fly fishing (trout/salmon). Active but fast. The best early training method for any gathering skill.
  • level 40 Lobsters. First fish that heals well in combat. You can now sustain through real fights.
  • level 62 Monkfish. Heals 16. The efficient PvM food. Balance of cost and healing.
  • level 76 Sharks. Heals 20. The iconic endgame food. "Bank full of sharks" means you are ready for anything.
  • level 82 Anglerfish. Heals above max HP. The only food that overheals. Endgame PvM and PvP staple.
  • level 82 Anglerfish fishing. Overheal food that pushes past max HP. Essential for endgame bossing.

cooking (3)

  • level 40 Lobsters without burning (at the Cooking Guild). Reliable mid-game food production.
  • level 80 Sharks. Cook without burning at 99, but even at 80 the burn rate is low enough to profit.
  • level 84 Anglerfish. The overheal fish. Cooking these for yourself is a point of pride.

firemaking (3)

  • level 30 Willow logs. The efficient firemaking training tier.
  • level 50 Access to the Wintertodt minigame. Firemaking goes from "why bother" to "this is actually fun."
  • level 70 Deep-coal furnace unlocked. 245k firemaking XP/hr. Sootworks becomes the fastest firemaking region in Aelgard.

woodcutting (4)

  • level 15 Oak trees. First tree worth cutting. Oak logs feed construction and fletching.
  • level 30 Willow trees. The AFK woodcutting standard. You will cut willows for hours.
  • level 60 Yew trees. Real money from woodcutting. Yew logs for fletching -> longbows -> high alch = profit pipeline.
  • level 75 Magic trees. The slowest, most valuable logs. Magic longbows are the best alch item in the game.

farming (5)

  • level 9 Guam seeds plantable. The herblore pipeline begins. Farm -> herb -> potion -> combat.
  • level 32 Ranarr seeds. The money herb. Each ranarr herb run is 50k-100k profit. Farming funds everything.
  • level 32 Ranarr seeds via Heartlands rotation. THE farming money breakthrough — each run nets ~80k profit at this level.
  • level 62 Snapdragon seeds. Higher profit, higher herblore value. Super restores become self-sufficient.
  • level 85 Torstol seeds. The highest herb. Saradomin brew ingredients from your own farm.
