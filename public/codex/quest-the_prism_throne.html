The Prism Throne

grandmaster epic id: the_prism_throne
glass_desert
Glass Desert. At the centre of the labyrinth sits a throne made of living crystal. It has been empty for millennia. When you approach, the crystals hum in a chord, and the chord is impatient. The throne is waiting for someone, and the someone is possibly you, and you are not certain you would like to be sat upon.

Premise

The Glass Desert was once an ocean. A god crystallized it in a single breath to trap something sleeping beneath. The Prism Throne was placed at the centre as a warden's seat, and the warden has been absent for too long. The crystals are getting impatient because the thing beneath is beginning to dream toward wakefulness.

To approach the throne you will fight the Spire Guardian, mine prismatic ore from the throne's base (the hardest material in Aelgard), and smith the ore into a Crown of Refraction. Placing the crown on the throne projects the desert's origin memories. A deep-seeing spell then reveals what lies beneath. The throne will demand a warden. You may sit it yourself — a commitment that binds you to a weekly check-in ritual — or refuse, letting the thing below stir very slightly more.

Steps

[1] Navigate the Glass Desert labyrinth to reach the Crystal Spire at the centre
Where: Glass Desert labyrinth
Requires: patience, a sense of direction that does not give up
The labyrinth does not repeat. It rhymes.
[2] Defeat the Spire Guardian, surviving its refractive beam attack
Where: Crystal Spire
Requires: defence, hitpoints
The beam will fracture against your shield if you angle the shield correctly. You will learn this by failing once.
[3] Mine prismatic ore from the throne's base
Where: throne base
Requires: mining at its highest tier
The ore sings under the pick. Your hand warms.
[4] Smith the ore into a Crown of Refraction
Where: Glass Desert smithing bench
Requires: smithing
The crown does not reflect you. It refracts you into three.
[5] Place the crown on the throne and cast a deep-seeing spell to view what lies beneath
Where: Prism Throne
Requires: magic
The desert was an ocean. The ocean was crystallized to trap a titan. The titan sleeps.
[6] Decide: sit the throne, or refuse
Where: Prism Throne
Requires: a choice
The crystals do not pressure. They simply wait.

Twist

Sitting the throne binds you to a weekly check-in quest — a small, repeatable ritual that lasts five minutes and keeps the titan asleep. Skipping the check-in has consequences felt across Aelgard. Refusing the throne has a different weight: the titan stirs, not enough to wake, but enough that the Glass Desert becomes slightly harder for everyone, forever.

Resolution

Whichever path you choose, the crystals hum. You leave with a Prism Shard in your pocket, which catches the light differently than other crystals and occasionally hums when you are thinking.

What it unlocks

You carry a Prism Shard, a token of the throne. If you sat the throne, a weekly warden check-in quest is added to your log, permanent and small. If you refused, the Glass Desert carries a faint increased difficulty which is a background hum rather than a wall. Either way, the throne's chamber is a travel node, and you may return.
