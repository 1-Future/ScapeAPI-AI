The Kraken of Saltbrine

saltbrine_reachtier 3

Bestiary

The Kraken was, for most of its life, far from shore. It lived in the deep trench that runs the length of the Reach's outer coast, and the trench was deep enough that no Reach boat had the ballast to dive for it, and the Kraken had no interest in surfacing. This arrangement worked. Something changed. The oldest fishermen say the singing from the trench has been getting louder for forty years, and in the last year the singing has moved, and the Kraken has moved with it, and the Kraken is now under the harbour.

Under, not at. The fight takes place in a flooded cave below the dock, reached through a tunnel that the cooperatives do not advertise and that can be navigated only with a waterbreathing potion prepared in Moryskah. The cave is lit by a glow from the Kraken itself, which is bio-luminescent in a way the old records did not mention.

The Kraken is size five. Most of the Kraken is out of range at any given time; the player fights tentacles, four of them, which protrude from the central body and which must be severed before the body becomes vulnerable. Each tentacle has its own hit points. Each tentacle has its own attack, mostly a grabbing strike but occasionally a whip. The body, when exposed, casts water magic — compressed spouts that punch through shields and staff spells that drain air from the lungs.

Weakness is slash. Tentacles cut. The body itself is tougher and takes reduced damage from magic — unlike most creatures of its scale, the Kraken is magical in its own right, and it resists the energies it wields. Ranged is viable but slow. Slash, specifically with a blessed weapon, clears the fight fastest.

It drops a severed tentacle as a weapon, which is itself a top-tier slash sword; the Tidal Amulet, which grants a magic-damage bonus underwater; and the Abyssal Trident, a powered staff that casts water magic without consuming runes. The Kraken also drops raw shark in respectable quantities, which has kept Mara's stall supplied through the last season's market shortfall.

The water in the cave stops moving. The singing, which the fishermen have been hearing for a year, stops, and does not resume.
