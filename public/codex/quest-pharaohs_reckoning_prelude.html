The Pharaoh's Reckoning

grandmaster epic id: pharaohs_reckoning_prelude
boneyard_wastesglass_desert
The tomb was sealed for a long hot time. Sealed it no longer is. Pharaoh Senekhet wakes. Three mummy lords walk the dunes. The desert is drying faster; the prophet's tongue is drier. Defile the tomb before the tomb defiles the desert. Parched prophet speaks, even so.

Premise

Pharaoh Senekhet ruled the Boneyard in the age before the First Empire, and was buried with a list of grudges he intended to renew if ever he were disturbed. He has been disturbed, recently, by the tremors of the comet's impact or by the opening of the prophecy or by something else the prophet will not name. His tomb has cracked. His three mummy lords walk the dunes at night, gathering old enemies whose grandchildren now live in Aelgard's current cities. You must go in, take the tomb apart, and end the pharaoh before his reckoning runs its course.

The tomb is a three-chamber pyramid. The first chamber is a slide-puzzle that maps the old Boneyard. The second is a light-puzzle that reveals the mummy lord Nefru. The third is a prayer-puzzle that reveals the mummy lord Ahmose. The fourth chamber is open and contains the mummy lord Takhepri; the fifth is the pharaoh's own. Each mummy lord fights differently; the pharaoh fights like all three combined, then, at low health, like none of them. He is a fight. The reward is the Ankh of Rebirth, an amulet that grants one free respawn per day, and the Pharaoh's Crown, a head slot with a once-per-hour Sand Form immunity. You must loot in order and walk out through the collapse as the tomb re-seals itself.

The tomb is closing itself. Do not pause for souvenirs.

Twist

The first mummy lord, Nefru, is the original sandglass sage — a predecessor of Orin's, bound to service by Senekhet four thousand years ago and never released. When you defeat him, he thanks you in Orin's voice. If you have completed Sandglass Sage Ascension before this quest, Nefru will recognise you as the successor and fight you with visible reluctance; this does not make the fight easier, but it makes the aftermath different — he hands you his own staff, which does not appear on the loot table, before he dies.

Resolution

The tomb is closed for good. Senekhet is dead, and the three mummy lords are dust. The desert is quieter. You carry the Ankh of Rebirth, which grants one free respawn per day without item loss, and the Pharaoh's Crown, which gives a once-per-hour Sand Form immunity — short sandstorm immunity, ten seconds. The tomb re-seals; the pharaoh can be re-summoned by a re-opening ritual, which makes him a repeatable grandmaster boss for players who want to farm him.

What it unlocks

The Ankh of Rebirth is the most significant death-insurance item in Aelgard — one respawn per day, no item loss. The Pharaoh's Crown grants Sand Form, a ten-second full sandstorm immunity, once per hour; it is head-slot with +2 prayer. The repeatable Senekhet fight is a grandmaster boss encounter with a unique drop table — including rare chance at the Ankh's second charge, which doubles its daily uses.
