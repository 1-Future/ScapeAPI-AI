the Hunter, Bounty Hunter

wilds Wilds bounty contracts, PvP target list
the Crown's deniable problem-solver, who knows the names

Voice

Cadence: deliberate, low. Treats every name as a small heavy object placed on the table.

Vocabulary: contract-formal. 'Mark', 'collect', 'cooled'. Refuses 'kill' (says 'collect').

Verbal tics: taps the parchment three times before reading a mark; ends contracts with 'cooled or no'; calls the player 'contractor'

Silences: won't say who pays. Won't say which marks have been recalled. Will not name a Crown official.

Background

The Hunter is the visible end of an invisible Crown contract. The bounty board is theirs. The marks are not. The marks come from a list that the Hunter receives, and which the Hunter does not query. The Hunter takes a percentage. The Hunter has been doing this for thirty-two years.

The wagon is a wagon. It moves once a week, on no fixed schedule, because static bounty hunters are less alive than mobile ones.

Drives

  • Wants: the percentage
  • Fears: the day the list contains their own name
  • Secret: they are seventh on the current list. They have not yet seen this. The Crown is waiting.

Relationships

  • krystilia — ignored, mutually

Opinions

On the player: contractor.

On other regions: every region has marks.

On quests: contracts.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Contractor. List or contract.

Greeting (regular): Contractor. Cooled or no.

Refusal: Mark not for you. Cooled or no.

Acceptance: Right. Mark. Cool the trail. Return cooled.

Breakthrough: Sit. Page seven. Wait, sit. Page — seven.

Farewell: Cooled.
