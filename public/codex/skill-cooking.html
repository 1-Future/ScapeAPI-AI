Cooking

33 training methods. 5 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 40 Lobsters without burning (at the Cooking Guild). Reliable mid-game food production.
    unlocks: Cook lobsters reliably
  • level 70 Palace Kitchen service opens. Court food is never burned. The palace range cooks perfectly every time.
    unlocks: Palace Kitchen Service
  • level 70 The Moonless Inn mortuary kitchen opens. Nothing burns; nothing has, since the wake ran past the wake and kept going.
    unlocks: Mortuary Inn Kitchen
  • level 80 Sharks. Cook without burning at 99, but even at 80 the burn rate is low enough to profit.
    unlocks: Cook sharks
  • level 84 Anglerfish. The overheal fish. Cooking these for yourself is a point of pride.
    unlocks: Cook anglerfish — heals above max HP
