The Haunted Lighthouse

intermediate medium id: the_haunted_lighthouse
saltbrinemoryskah
Saltbrine Reach. The cliff-path lighthouse has gone dark, and two ships have wrecked in a week. A new keeper named Lira has volunteered — she has a lamp, a tinderbox, and a visible fear — and she needs an escort along the haunted path to the door, because the last keeper fled screaming about ghosts.

Premise

The lighthouse is haunted by a drowned sailor who has been trying to get home to Moryskah for a hundred years. He does not mean to keep the beacon dark; his grief displaces the flame. Lira will not walk alone. You will walk with her — through three waves of ghost attacks on the cliff-path, past the spectral lock at the door, through three ectoplasm-filled floors, to the top where the beacon sits cold.

To restore the light, you must first lay the drowned sailor to rest. A prayer of passage, spoken at the correct turn of the beacon, will free him. Then Lira's tinderbox will take.

Steps

[1] Meet Keeper Lira at Saltbrine harbour. Escort her along the cliff path
Where: Saltbrine harbour; cliff path
Requires: defence
Lira clutches her lamp like a child clutches a toy.
[2] Protect Lira from three waves of ghost attacks
Where: cliff path
Requires: defence
The ghosts do not howl. They whisper.
[3] Pray to weaken the spectral lock on the lighthouse door
Where: lighthouse door
Requires: prayer
The lock loosens but does not let go.
[4] Clear each floor of ectoplasm while keeping Lira safe
Where: lighthouse interior
Requires: combat, patience
Each floor is colder than the last.
[5] Put the drowned sailor's ghost to rest. Relight the beacon
Where: lighthouse top
Requires: prayer, firemaking
The ghost thanks you in a voice as far away as Moryskah.

Twist

The drowned sailor asks you to deliver a small token to his family in Moryskah. The token is a ring. Delivering it unlocks a minor side-quest there; pocketing it gives a small ranged bonus but costs a shade of honour.

Resolution

The beacon burns. Ships return safely. Lira takes up her post with a key of her own — and hands you one too, because she is grateful.

What it unlocks

You carry the lighthouse keeper's key, which unlocks the Saltbrine lighthouse as a teleport destination. The Saltbrine-Moryskah cliff-path is safer for you now; ghosts recognize your prayer and pass by.
