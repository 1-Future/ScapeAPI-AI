The Wandering Plague

experienced long id: the_wandering_plague
heartlandsmoryskahboneyard_wastes
A plague walks the caravan roads between the Heartlands, Moryskah, and the Boneyard. It does not care for borders, weather, or prayers. Trace its carrier. Find its origin. Cure it or contain it before the next caravan dies coughing. The Bog Witch has brewed a starter-potion; she cannot travel. Her patch will not let her.

Premise

The plague is a three-region contagion: it originated in a specific Moryskah village, travelled by caravan into the Heartlands, and has spread toward the Boneyard through a cursed well. The disease is not magical; it is biological, which is unusual for Moryskah, and which means it can be cured by an antidote — not a prayer, not a blessing, an actual herbalist's antidote, compounded from ingredients that can only be gathered in all three regions. The Bog Witch has brewed a starter; you must complete it with ingredients from the other two regions, identify which of three Moryskah survivors is lying about how the plague started (one is covering for a family member), and deliver the antidote to the caravan routes before the next caravan season begins.

The cure is a repeatable recipe; every future epidemic in Aelgard, if any, can be cured by brewing it. The Plague Doctor's Mask, awarded on completion, grants poison immunity and +5% herblore success — it is a niche capstone for herblorists.

Steps

[1] Examine a dying caravaner at the Heartlands border post
Where: Heartlands border post
Requires: herblore 65
The symptoms are unusual — biological, not magical. The caravaner is coherent, which is worse.
[2] Follow the backtrail to Moryskah — the plague started in a village behind the fog
Where: Moryskah, fog-shrouded village
Requires: prayer 55
The village is quiet. The fog is thicker than it should be.
[3] Interview the three survivors. One is lying
Where: village common room
Requires: thieving 50, attention
The lie is small. It is also critical. Listen to the second survivor's second sentence.
[4] Track the true source into the Boneyard
Where: Boneyard, a specific well
Requires: agility 50, tracking
The well has been spat into by a specific thing. The spit is still wet.
[5] Secure the well. Brew the three-region antidote at the Bog Witch's cottage
Where: Bog Witch's cottage
Requires: herblore 65
The Witch will not help with the brew. She will watch, which is her way of helping.
[6] Deliver the antidote to the caravan routes. Administer personally to the worst-hit
Where: three caravan stops across three regions
Requires: none
Some will thank you. Some will spit. A few will try to pay you.

Twist

The liar among the three survivors is a child, covering for her grandfather. The grandfather is the actual origin of the plague — he drank from the Boneyard well while on a pilgrimage, and carried it home. He is not malicious; he is ashamed, and would rather die than be blamed. Curing him requires you to extract a commitment from him to speak publicly about the well, which he will not do easily. The child's lie is out of love. Handle it with care; if you expose the child, the grandfather refuses treatment.

Resolution

The plague is cured. The cursed well is sealed. The caravan routes reopen. The three-region antidote recipe is now in the Bog Witch's book, transferable to any herblorist you share it with. The Plague Doctor's Mask sits in your inventory; it grants poison immunity and a permanent +5% herblore success. The Plague Doctor as an NPC now travels the three regions, dispensing daily rare-herb assignments — a repeatable daily content hook for herblorists.

What it unlocks

The Three-Region Antidote is an herblore recipe requiring ingredients from Heartlands, Moryskah, and Boneyard; it cures every non-unique disease in Aelgard and is the gate for future epidemic-themed content. The Plague Doctor's Mask is head-slot with full poison immunity and +5% herblore success, which is a breakpoint for poisoned-food farming. The Plague Doctor NPC gives daily rare-herb assignments across the three regions, feeding into the herblore economy.
