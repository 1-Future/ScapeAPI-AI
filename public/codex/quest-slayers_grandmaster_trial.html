The Slayer's Grandmaster Trial

grandmaster epic id: slayers_grandmaster_trial
moryskahheartlandsboneyardveilwoodsootworkssaltbrineinkwealdglass_desertthe_wilds
Moryskah. Varrek hands you a sealed envelope in both hands and tells you it is not a task. It is a list. Nine names. One per region. Finish it, or give the creed-ring back. He does not say which outcome he prefers. He pours the whisky while you read.

Premise

The grandmaster trial is an informal promotion, because the slayer order does not have formal promotions. What it has are nine creatures nobody wants to touch, one per region, each a stain on the order's reputation because every earlier master either failed to assign them or assigned them and the slayer did not come back. Varrek has been keeping the list in his drawer since his own master gave it to him. He will tell you, if pressed, that his master was still carrying it when he died.

Each creature on the list is documented in the envelope with a single line. The lines are short and wrong on purpose — the creed demands that you not be told what you are hunting, only that something must be hunted. You will be cold in the Wilds, hot in the Boneyard, wet in Saltbrine, confused in the Inkweald, tired in the Veilwood, scorched in the Sootworks, lost in the Glass Desert, seen in the Heartlands, and preyed-upon in Moryskah. You will be all of those at once, because time is not generous, and the creatures are patient.

Steps

[1] Open the envelope. Read the nine lines. Pick a first
Where: Moryskah, Varrek's study
Requires: none
Varrek watches without speaking. The whisky is not poured yet.
[2] Heartlands: hunt the null-hound — a dog that forgets itself when looked at
Where: a Heartlands shepherd's field at dusk
Requires: slayer 85, a cold nerve
Look away to strike. Look back to aim. It is a hard habit to learn in one fight.
[3] Boneyard: hunt the sandwraith, who eats water not blood
Where: Boneyard deep dunes
Requires: a full waterskin you do not need
Offer the water. She will come close. Close is where she is killable.
[4] Moryskah: hunt the laundry-wight, who washes the dead and does not stop
Where: Moryskah, a graveyard's cold pool
Requires: prayer 70, no clean clothes
She will try to wash you. Let her, briefly. The cloth she uses is her only soft spot.
[5] Veilwood: hunt the moonhawk-king, who remembers your face
Where: Veilwood canopy, the moonhawk run
Requires: ranged 75, nothing bright
The canopy will watch. The canopy will not help. You will have to take him in the air.
[6] Sootworks: hunt the rust-snake in a cooling pipe that must not cool
Where: Sootworks deep forge
Requires: attack 80, a tolerance for heat
Do not break the pipe. The forge will collapse on you both, and only one of you is coming out.
[7] Saltbrine: hunt the grief-eel in the wake of the Lost Mary
Where: Saltbrine, a specific shipwreck
Requires: diving, fishing 70
The eel lives on mourning. Bring none, or bring it all. Halfway is the worst option.
[8] Inkweald: hunt the sleep-fox that dreams of being hunted by you
Where: Inkweald, the second-door clearing
Requires: magic 75, a light sleep
If you kill it awake you fail. If you kill it asleep you fail. Kill it in its own dream of your killing it.
[9] Glass Desert: hunt the prism-cat — it refracts into three, kill all three inside a minute
Where: Glass Desert, any flat silica plain
Requires: magic 75, patience
Three is not the maximum. It is the usual. Do not look surprised.
[10] Wilds: hunt the throne-crow before it crowns you
Where: deep Wilds, the throne clearing
Requires: ranged 80, prayer 70
It will land on your head if you are slow. The crown is a kind of death. You would prefer the other kind.
[11] Return the nine trophies to Varrek and cut the creed-mark from your palm onto the stone
Where: Moryskah, Slayer Tower basement
Requires: the creed-ring, a knife
The cut does not hurt. The giving does.

Twist

Your name on the stone now includes a small carved symbol that was not there when you finished The Slayer's Creed. The symbol is the old master's mark. Varrek has been expecting you to earn it; he did not. The grandmaster trial is not actually about the nine creatures. It is about which ones you killed silently, which you killed honourably, and which you let go after realising they deserved to live. The symbol is awarded by the stone based on what it saw.

Resolution

Varrek retires when you return. He does not make a fuss; he hangs his own ring on the nail behind his chair and tells you the assignment room is yours to sit in if you ever want company. The slayer order promotes by reputation, and your reputation is now an entire stone. The ledger he hands you is thick and half-written.

What it unlocks

The Grandmaster Slayer Helmet now fits your head; it combines the creed-ring and a full helm slot, and no other item in Aelgard gives +15% slayer damage in every region. Varrek's ledger means you may assign yourself slayer tasks at any master location. Self-assigned tasks pay half XP, but they let you pick your target, and the creed-mark will glow for any creature the stone has ever known.
