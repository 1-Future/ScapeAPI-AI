Osmumten, Pharaoh of the Desert

boneyard_wastestier 5

Bestiary

Osmumten is the raid boss at the heart of the Tombs of Aelgard, a pyramid in the deeper Wastes that was rediscovered after Azhmari's defeat loosened the sand over its entrance. He was a pharaoh earlier than Azhmari, and a more careful pharaoh, and his tomb was built for privacy rather than resurrection. He never expected to be disturbed. He also never expected to have stopped dying, which is the state he is currently in, and which he has expressed annoyance about through his attendants.

The tomb is an eight-player raid. Four pathways lead to four sub-bosses — the Warden of the Flame, the Warden of the Flood, the Warden of the Sands, the Warden of the Stars — and each Warden falls, and at the centre Osmumten rises from his sarcophagus and fights the party directly. He is tall, thin, and crowned. He wears no armour. He carries a fang — a curved blade of his own making, named Osmumten's Fang — and he wields it at close range with a precision that no other boss in Aelgard matches.

The Fang finds gaps in armour. Against high-defence targets, the Fang's accuracy doubles, and this is a passive property of the weapon that the pharaoh himself demonstrates in combat. His strikes always land. He does not miss. What a player can do is reduce the damage of a landing strike, and this is done through prayer, and the prayer cycle he requires is complex — he changes attack styles on a fixed tempo, and the tempo can be memorised, but the memorisation takes several runs.

The raid uses an invocation system. At base difficulty, the fight is merely hard. At maximum invocation — six hundred points' worth — the fight is the most difficult single encounter in Aelgard, and the rewards are proportional.

Osmumten drops his Fang (BIS stab against high-defence targets), Masori armour (a three-piece ranged set that maximises DPS at the cost of defence), the Lightbearer ring (halving special-attack restore time), Elidinis' Ward (the best magic shield available), and the Shadow of Tumeken (the most damaging magic weapon in Aelgard, powered by soul and chaos runes).

He lowers his fang slowly, inspects it, and speaks a single word to no one in particular. The word is his own name.
