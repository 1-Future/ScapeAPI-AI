Famine, Famine

boneyard mid-tier Boneyard boss, one of the Riders
the Rider who took the slow road

Voice

Cadence: slow, dry, weighted. Each sentence is a draught of empty water.

Vocabulary: scriptural-broken. References scripture from a religion the chapel banned.

Verbal tics: counts grain between sentences; ends speeches with a single dry vowel; addresses the player as 'fed one'

Silences: will not say the names of the other Riders. Will not concede they ride.

Background

Famine is the Boneyard's local Rider, allegedly one of four. The Boneyard does not entirely believe in the others. Famine does not, in any case, ride. Famine has stood at the dry well for three centuries.

The fight is mostly attritional. Famine drains the player's resources rather than dealing damage. The drops include Famine's grain, which is one of the only items in the game that increases the player's maximum hit points.

Drives

  • Wants: the well to fill
  • Fears: rain
  • Secret: the well filled, twenty years ago, for one full season. Famine drank it dry. Famine is, in some sense, the reason the Boneyard is the Boneyard.

Relationships

  • razak — Razak avoids the third dune ring. Famine respects this.

Opinions

On the player: fed one.

On other regions: all regions are wells.

On quests: the well is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Fed one. Count your grain.

Greeting (regular): Fed one. The grain has been counted.

Refusal: The well declines.

Acceptance: The grain is yours. Eat slowly.

Breakthrough: Fed one. The well filled once. Listen.

Farewell: Dry.
