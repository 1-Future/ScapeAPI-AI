Woodcutting

21 training methods. 4 breakpoints.

Breakpoints

  • level 15 Oak trees. First tree worth cutting. Oak logs feed construction and fletching.
    unlocks: Oak tree cutting
  • level 30 Willow trees. The AFK woodcutting standard. You will cut willows for hours.
    unlocks: Willow tree cutting — AFK standard
  • level 60 Yew trees. Real money from woodcutting. Yew logs for fletching -> longbows -> high alch = profit pipeline.
    unlocks: Yew tree cutting — feeds the alch economy
  • level 75 Magic trees. The slowest, most valuable logs. Magic longbows are the best alch item in the game.
    unlocks: Magic tree cutting — top-tier logs
