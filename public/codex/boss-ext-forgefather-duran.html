Bestiary

Duran was a Heartlands smith, and not a bad one — competent enough to be promoted, weak enough to resent the promotion, and stubborn enough to turn that resentment into a project. He was given the Heartlands Smithing Guild in his forty-second year, and he declined to run it the way the previous Forgefather had run it, and within five years he had left the guild under circumstances the guild's records describe as mutually agreed and which were not. He went north. He took his hammer and his apprentice, and the apprentice is still missing, and the hammer is now the burning hammer which Duran wields today as the primary feature of his combat. He built a private forge in the hills above the Heartlands and he began to smith weapons that he sold to anyone who asked, at prices that undercut the guild, and he did this until the guild sent a delegation to ask him to stop, and the delegation did not return, and a second delegation was not sent. The burning hammer is the Forgefather's signature. It is soot-iron, forged by him personally, and it holds heat at a temperature that should have melted it a long time ago. How he maintains this is not known. Several Heartlands scholars have theorised that he bargained with something at the Sootworks for the privilege, and that the bargain has since come due in ways that are showing. Duran's face is thinner than it was. His apprentice is long gone. The forge behind him is lit by no visible fire. Players encounter him in the hills and find him willing, almost eager, to fight. He does not taunt. He does not bargain. He swings, and the hammer swings with a weight that is heavier than iron should be, and a swing catches fire as it lands. His armour is heavy and his skin is bright-red where the heat of the hammer has reached it over the years. He is, in the strict sense, still a human being. He is what human beings become when they build something in anger and will not stop tending it. Survive him with a piercing weapon — stab through the armour gaps at the joints, because slash will not get through and crush will not do enough damage fast enough. Melee gloves and adamant gear are standard. He drops his hammer occasionally, and the hammer cools within a minute of his death, and the hammer can then be taken.

He says the name of his apprentice. You do not know who that is.
