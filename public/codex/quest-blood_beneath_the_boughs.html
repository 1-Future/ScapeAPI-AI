Blood Beneath the Boughs

experienced long id: blood_beneath_the_boughs
moryskah
Moryskah, Hollow Mire. The swamp trees are bleeding. The sap is red and it is alive, and it is climbing the trunks, and the local vampyres — who do not frighten easily — have sent a delegation to the village elder asking what is going on. The elder thinks you might help before the vampyres start being helpful in their own ways.

Premise

The Bloodwood parasite is an organism that predates the swamp. It was buried by the Moryskah dead, and it has waited in the root network, feeding on the swamp's ambient magic, growing. It has now surfaced as a Bloodwood Heart — a creature that fuses with the swamp itself and cannot be killed by ordinary weapons. The vampyres fear it because it feeds on what they feed on, and there is not enough for both.

Silver-tipped axes cut its tendrils. An herblore antidote purifies its central root. You will need to track the root system through the bog, slay the Heart, sever five tendrils before they regenerate, and purify the centre. The vampyres will send an emissary afterwards to thank you uneasily, which is the most vampyric gesture there is.

Steps

[1] Investigate the bleeding trees near the village of Hollow Mire and collect sap samples
Where: Moryskah, Hollow Mire
Requires: herblore
The sap moves in your flask. It is not water.
[2] Identify the sap. Discover the parasite is alive and rooted
Where: Hollow Mire apothecary
Requires: herblore
The identification takes three hours and makes the apothecary ill.
[3] Track the root system through the bog using agility shortcuts
Where: Moryskah deep bog
Requires: agility
The roots pulse when you step over them. They are listening.
[4] Slay the Bloodwood Heart. It is immune to normal weapons
Where: Moryskah, root nexus
Requires: slayer, a silver-tipped axe
The Heart does not scream. It sighs.
[5] Sever five root tendrils before they regenerate. Purify the central root with your antidote
Where: root nexus
Requires: axe, antidote
Each tendril cut makes the bog quieter. By the fifth, you can hear your own breathing.
[6] Report to the Hollow Mire elder. Receive the vampyre emissary's uneasy thanks
Where: Hollow Mire
Requires: manners
The emissary does not sit. She does not uncross her hands. She thanks you anyway.

Twist

The vampyres will ask, through the emissary, for a piece of the Bloodwood Heart for their own research. Granting it makes Moryskah politics slightly easier for you forever. Refusing it costs you nothing immediate but will cost you small courtesies in Moryskah down the line. Either choice is survivable.

Resolution

The swamp quiets. The Hollow Mire village breathes. You carry a Bloodwood stake — a trophy and a weapon — and the vampyres, whether you gave them their piece or not, nod to you now when you pass their watchtowers.

What it unlocks

You carry a Bloodwood stake, which does extra damage against swamp aberrants and against a small category of things the stake remembers fighting. Hollow Mire is open to you as a home-village; the elder will rent you a room. The vampyre emissary will see you at short notice, which is not a friendship but is also not nothing.

Dialogue

Hollow Mire elder
We buried it wrong, a long time ago. Somebody's great-grandmother did the burial, and she was in a hurry. This is what hurrying looks like, a hundred years later.
the vampyre emissary
We do not say 'thank you'. I will say it once, and you will pretend I did not. It cost me.
