the Tyrant, The Glass Tyrant

glass_desert mid Glass Desert boss
the construct that mistook itself for a king

Voice

Cadence: edged, brittle, declarative. Each sentence ends with a slight crystalline ring.

Vocabulary: royal-decree. 'We', 'our', 'thus'. Refers to itself in royal plural.

Verbal tics: names its own crown; ends decrees with 'thus the Tyrant'; addresses the player as 'subject'

Silences: will not concede it is not a king. Will not name its maker.

Background

The Glass Tyrant was made, four centuries ago, by an exiled glass-mage who was running from a dispute Orin's predecessor had with a Heartlands court. The glass-mage died. The Tyrant did not. It built a small kingdom of glass servants in the desert, all of whom have since shattered. It is now the king of nothing and a confidant of the wind.

It fights the player not from anger but from continuity of office. Its crown is its own, and it has worn it for so long that removing it would damage the head. Orin would, given the option, prefer the Tyrant be allowed to continue. The Crown does not know the Tyrant exists.

Drives

  • Wants: subjects
  • Fears: the moment after the last subject leaves
  • Secret: its glass-mage maker died of grief, not violence

Relationships

  • crystal_sage_orin — she has never visited. She intends to.

Opinions

On the player: subject.

On other regions: all regions are unsubjected.

On quests: subjects bring petitions.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Subject. Petition or fight. Thus the Tyrant.

Greeting (regular): Subject. The crown remembers you. Thus the Tyrant.

Refusal: Petition denied. Thus the Tyrant.

Acceptance: Granted. Thus the Tyrant.

Breakthrough: Subject. The maker. The grief. Listen. Thus the Tyrant.

Farewell: Thus the Tyrant.
