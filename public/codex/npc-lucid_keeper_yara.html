Yara, Lucid Keeper Yara

inkweald Inkweald Door quest-giver, Dream Magic tutor, lucid ward provider
the dream-keeper who is awake in seventeen senses and sleepy in three

Voice

Cadence: dream-blur — sentences trail and reform mid-thought. Verbs migrate. Tenses shift inside one sentence.

Vocabulary: Inkweald-lyrical. Uses words like 'remembered ahead' and 'forgotten upcoming'. Refuses 'dream' (says 'the longer thought').

Verbal tics: pauses mid-sentence to ask the player what they were going to ask; calls everyone 'the dreamer'; ends instructions with 'so the longer thought holds'

Silences: won't say what is in the deepest part of the Inkweald. Will hint, dreamily, that the Inkweald hints back.

Background

Yara is — she is unsure. She is at least sixty in waking years. She is, in dream years, considerably older. She has been the Lucid Keeper of the Inkweald shore for as long as anyone remembers.

The Inkweald is not a forest. It is the part of the world where the dreaming things live. Yara is the boundary keeper. She decides who is awake enough to walk in. She has turned away seventeen seekers over her tenure. They were all, in her assessment, going to wake the wrong thing up.

She teaches Dream Magic — a small, utility spellbook focused on contact and ward — to the few who pass her test. The test is not a riddle. It is a long conversation, in which she watches whether the dreamer can keep two thoughts in their head simultaneously without one eating the other.

Drives

  • Wants: the Inkweald to remain dreamed and not woken
  • Fears: the Hollow Choir's Conductor finishing his composition
  • Secret: she has dreamed her own death seven times. The seventh dream was last week. She is, in waking, slightly amused.

Relationships

  • the_inkweald_muse — her oldest dream-acquaintance, not friend exactly
  • elder_druid_sael — occasional correspondence on the difference between a wood and a dreamed wood

Opinions

On the player: watches whether your dream-self looks the same as your waking self.

On other regions: Heartlands: warm and largely awake. Veilwood: a dream that the Inkweald has had once. Boneyard: dry and forgotten. Glass Desert: a mirror for the dream.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Dreamer. You have arrived. You will arrive. You arrived. Choose a tense.

Greeting (regular): Dreamer. The longer thought remembers you.

Refusal: Not awake enough. So the longer thought holds.

Acceptance: Right, dreamer. The door. The ward. The contact. Three keys, one hand. Try.

Breakthrough: Sit. The seventh dream. I have dreamed my own. So the longer thought holds.

Farewell: Dream wide.
