The Werewolf's Reckoning

grandmaster epic id: the_werewolfs_reckoning
moryskahheartlands
An invitation arrives, folded in silver paper, delivered by a child who cannot yet pronounce the name of the person who sent it; the matriarch of the werewolf family, who is older than the city and who has been locked in the high tower of the estate since before the city had its present name, has asked for you by title, and the family, who are not in the habit of declining her, are not in the habit of imagining that you will either.

Premise

The matriarch has been waiting for the right person for a considerable portion of the century. The matter she would like to settle is a pact made before the city was a city, between her line and another line which broke the pact in a particular way, in a particular generation, and which has been, ever since, a small ache at the back of her long life. She would like you to close the pact by finding the last living descendant of the pact-breaker and settling with them, in whatever way seems appropriate to you after you have met them. She does not care how you close it. She cares that it is closed.

The matriarch is enormous, polite, and tired. She will serve you tea that you must drink. The tea is not poisoned but it is binding: once you drink it you cannot lie to her during the visit. This is not a trap; this is a courtesy she extends to both of you. Her tower, the high tower of the estate, has more stairs each time you look up, and it is a matter of habit, not magic — the staircase was built by a family that does not like visitors, and it knows when it is being measured. At the top, she will pour the tea, and she will hear whatever you have learned in the werewolf arc up to this point, and she will make an offer that has been waiting to be made for a century.

Steps

[1] Present yourself at the estate gate at the hour the tower door will open, which is not every hour
Where: Moryskah werewolf estate
Requires: the family token
The gate opens itself. The staircase is visible. The tower top is not.
[2] Climb the tower, which has more stairs each time you look up
Where: the high tower
Requires: agility 50 or patience, not both
Do not count. The stairs are counting you back.
[3] Meet the matriarch, who is enormous, polite, and offering tea
Where: the top of the tower
Requires: open hands
The teacup is the size of a soup bowl. Drink all of it.
[4] Hear the matter of the pact out, without interrupting, while you drink
Where: the parlour at the tower top
Requires: none
She will be thorough. She will be brief. She is never both. Today she is brief.
[5] Track down the last living descendant of the pact-breaker (Thieving 70)
Where: Aelgard-wide search
Requires: thieving 70, patient interviewing
The descendant does not know about the pact. This will be your first piece of news to them.
[6] Settle with them — fight, offer, negotiate, or marry them in (player choice)
Where: wherever you find them
Requires: attack 80 if fight path, charisma otherwise
All outcomes close the pact. None of them are the same outcome.
[7] Return to the matriarch with proof, and do not lie about any part of it
Where: the tower top
Requires: the tea is still binding; speak plainly
She will know if you soften any detail. Do not.
[8] Accept the marrow-right, which is given only once a generation
Where: the tower top
Requires: open hands again
It is a small bone-pin, polished, with a carved sigil. It will hum if she is near.

Twist

The last living descendant of the pact-breaker is, depending on the player's prior choices, either the child from The Werewolf's Lineage or the cousin whose house was burnt down four generations ago — who was, it turns out, not entirely dead. The matriarch has been tracking them for a century, could have settled this herself at any time, and did not, because the pact required the settlement to be made by someone with no blood in the family. That is why she waited for you.

Resolution

The pact is closed. The matriarch drinks the last of her tea and does not pour another cup. She is, visibly, lighter — which is to say she is slightly smaller than when you arrived, and the tower is slightly shorter. She hands you the marrow-right, a bone-pin worn on the cloak, which summons her as a combat ally for one minute once per day. She tells you, with great formality, that you may call her by her given name, though you may not share it with anyone outside the family. You are now, in a sense she would not use out loud, one of the family.

What it unlocks

You wear the Matriarch's Tooth as an amulet — a permanent +5 prayer and a sharpening of sight that lets you pick out werewolves in crowds, in fogs, and in dreams. The marrow-right prayer calls the matriarch to your side for one minute once per day; she fights like someone who has been still for a century and would like to be less so. The tower top is open to you for sparring whenever she is in residence, which is most of the time, and the spar is a repeatable 1-on-1 fight you will not win for a long time.
