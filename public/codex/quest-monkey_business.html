Monkey Business

master long id: monkey_business
saltbrine
Saltbrine harbour. A sailor in an earring the size of a napkin ring hands you a map to an island nobody can find twice. The island has monkeys on it. The monkeys talk. The monkeys have their own king and their own religion and, apparently, the best smiths south of the Sootworks, and you will need to pretend to be a monkey to get there.

Premise

The monkey island drifts. It is not a stationary place; it floats on a cycle that only the Saltbrine harbourmaster's oldest charts reflect. The monkeys there are sapient, and they are not friendly to sailors, and they hold a grudge against the Heartlands that you cannot explain away because nobody involved is willing to describe the original offence. Their smiths make the best scimitars in the world. Dragon scimitars. If you want one — and you do — you will need a monkey talisman. The monkeys do not sell it. They award it.

You will reach the island on a charter that will certainly crash, survive the crash, smuggle yourself into the monkey city in a disguise that looks absurd from the shoulders down, prove yourself at their agility trial, and, if you are patient, earn the trust of their elder. The elder keeps the talismans in a gourd he wears on his shoulder. He will not give you one; he will bet you one on a contest of cleverness. Lose with grace, win with humility. They have words for both.

Steps

[1] Take the map to Harbourmaster Cole. Charter the ship. Do not ask what the sailors are laughing about
Where: Saltbrine harbour
Requires: coin, nerve
The charter comes with a lifebelt and an apology in advance.
[2] Survive the crash. Make landfall. Start walking. The monkeys are watching from the trees and scoring you
Where: the monkey island, southern beach
Requires: basic survival, a sense of humour
A monkey holds up a sign with your landing score on it. The score is poor.
[3] Craft a disguise from island materials. Infiltrate the city. Do not speak unless spoken to; your accent is hilarious
Where: the monkey city
Requires: thieving, crafting
The disguise is convincing from a distance. The distance is not far.
[4] Complete the agility trial. Do not cheat; the monkeys award talismans by merit and by grievance both
Where: monkey city, the trial canopy
Requires: agility, legs that do not care about bruises
The final jump is not scheduled. It appears.
[5] Find the temple. Pray there honestly. The monkey gods share a great-grandparent with the Heartlands ones
Where: monkey island, the old temple
Requires: prayer, an open attitude
The temple is tended by a very small monkey in very large robes.
[6] Defeat the demon that guards the elder's gourd, or outwit it. The elder will prefer one over the other
Where: the elder's tree
Requires: combat or riddle-work
The demon is not very good at demons. It got the job on family.
[7] Accept the talisman from the elder. Return to Saltbrine. Do not mention the island to anyone for a week
Where: Saltbrine harbour
Requires: the talisman
Cole nods at your shoulder, where the talisman warms.

Twist

The elder will make a third offer before the talisman is handed over: to adopt you into the monkey lineage. Accepting gives a different cosmetic, a different shop discount, and a cousin who writes to you on feast days. Refusing is also fine. The elder prefers neither; he prefers that you pick quickly.

Resolution

The talisman is yours. The dragon scimitar shop is open to you, which is a sentence that will mean more to you in two weeks than it does now. The island recedes from the harbourmaster's charts on cue. You will find it again, by talisman, whenever you please.

What it unlocks

You carry a Monkey Talisman that lets you teleport to the monkey island at your leisure, and the dragon scimitar shop is open to you — the BIS melee weapon for the mid-game, not sold anywhere else. You can equip dragon scimitars now; the skill is not the gate, the talisman is.
