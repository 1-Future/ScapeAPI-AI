The Infernal Challenge

grandmaster epic id: infernal_challenge
glass_desert
Glass Desert, the deepest cave. The entrance to the Inferno is a basin where you lay down your Fire Cape and it burns to nothing while you watch. If you leave the Fire Cape on your back, the entrance refuses. The cape is the toll, and the Inferno will not negotiate.

Premise

The Inferno is the hardest solo content in Aelgard. Sixty-nine waves. A mechanical depth that requires prayer switching at the level of a twitch reflex, positioning that plans three waves ahead, resource management that cannot afford a single mistake. It is the shrine players build toward for years and most do not reach. It is the Infernal Cape at the end, and the Infernal Cape is the only BIS cape in the game.

You do not quest your way in. You earn your way in, by having done the Fight Caves, and then by burning the Fire Cape. You cannot be carried. You cannot respawn. You must bring everything you need for potentially eight hours and be willing to be killed on wave sixty-eight.

Steps

[1] Lay your Fire Cape in the basin. Watch it burn
Where: Glass Desert, deepest cave
Requires: the Fire Cape
It burns like a cape, not like a paper.
[2] Survive waves 1-68 with prayer switching, positioning, and resource management at a level you have not yet tested
Where: Inferno
Requires: attack, ranged, magic, prayer, hitpoints, hours, silence
Waves 1-40 are the entrance exam. Waves 41-60 are the exam. Waves 61-68 are the wrong answers.
[3] Defeat the final boss on wave 69
Where: Inferno, wave 69
Requires: everything you have
The boss has three forms. Two are fair. The third is a test of what is left of you.
[4] Claim the Infernal Cape
Where: Inferno, reward chamber
Requires: being alive
The cape is cool to the touch. This is the whole point.

Twist

The Inferno records your attempt. After ten failed attempts the cave begins to greet you, very slightly, as if it respects you — a small torch will light itself as you enter, a dropped item will be picked up by a waiting imp who hands it back at wave start. None of this helps you win. It just acknowledges that you keep trying.

Resolution

You leave the cave with the cape on, and the Glass Desert nods once, and the veteran at the camp nods twice. You are, in this as in few things, measurably among the best.

What it unlocks

The Infernal Cape is yours — BIS cape in the entire game, the ultimate prestige, the cape other players look at first. There is no higher cape. There is no next challenge at this tier.
