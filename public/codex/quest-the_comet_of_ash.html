The Comet of Ash

master long id: the_comet_of_ash
boneyard_wastesheartlandssootworksveilwoodglass_desert
Crystal Sage Orin's reading-table has a new entry, drawn in ash: a comet fell seven nights ago, out of the wrong sky, and landed in the Boneyard. Five regions report strange weather. Track the comet. Understand what it was. Decide what to do with what it left. Orin, for the first time in memory, looks alarmed.

Premise

The comet is not a comet. It is a message, carried by a slow piece of rock from a place Aelgard has never had dealings with, which is deeply unusual for a cosmology as small as Aelgard's. The comet left a crater in the Boneyard, residue in five other regions, and a note written in ash that Orin can read but only partially. To decipher the message you must sample five regions: a crop circle of ash in the Heartlands where the comet grazed the sky; a singed deep-forge chimney in Sootworks; disoriented moonhawks flying in reversed spirals in the Veilwood; the crater itself, still warm, in the Boneyard; and the expected arrival site in the Glass Desert, where the sandglass sage had foreseen the comet without knowing what it was.

The message, once combined at Orin's table, is a greeting and an invitation from a distant cosmology. You have three choices: bury the comet (the greeting is ignored, Aelgard stays isolated), broadcast its message (every region receives the greeting, with world-affecting consequences in future content), or hold it as a private secret (only you and Orin know, with unique gameplay benefits). This is a world-affecting choice. The Codex records it. Server-wide.

Twist

The greeting's two questions are: (a) what is your name, and (b) will you answer? The name is straightforward. Whether to answer is not. If you broadcast, Aelgard answers as a whole, and the distant cosmology knows about Aelgard for future content. If you bury, the question is declined politely, and the cosmology leaves. If you hold the secret, you personally answer, in your own name, and you are known — individually — to the cosmology, with unique consequences in future content. No choice is wrong. All choices are remembered.

Resolution

Orin writes the chosen outcome into the reading-table. The Codex logs it. You carry the Comet Shard Pendant, an amulet with +3 to magic and prayer and a once-per-day message to any player on the server. You gain three Minor Prophecy cantrips appended to your current spellbook: read item, read intent, read weather. The Comet Ash runecrafting method is a rare node using comet residue as a substrate for unique runes with cross-cosmology implications — which is to say, future content will use them.

What it unlocks

The Comet Shard Pendant is an amulet, +3 magic and +3 prayer, with a once-per-day server-wide message function — you may send a short line of text to any named player in Aelgard, regardless of region. Minor Prophecy appends three cantrips to your current spellbook that read item history, intent of an NPC, and weather in any region. Comet Ash runecrafting uses comet residue as a substrate for rare rune types, producing runes that feed into future cross-cosmology spellbooks.
