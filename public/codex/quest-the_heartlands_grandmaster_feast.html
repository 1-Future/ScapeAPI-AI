The Grand Feast

grandmaster epic id: the_heartlands_grandmaster_feast
heartlands
Master Chef Oleander has heard of you. Specifically, she has heard that you survived the Culinaromancer. She would like to commission the Grand Feast. Ten courses. Heartlands ingredients only. The Royal Court will attend. This is the Heartlands prestige goal. It is both an honour and a very public failure waiting to happen.

Premise

The Grand Feast is the Heartlands' prestige capstone. It is a ten-course dinner served to the Royal Court, using only ingredients sourced from within the Heartlands — no imports, no shortcuts, no Sootworks clockwork kitchens. You must source every ingredient yourself (farms, rivers, woods, hunts), plan the menu in consultation with Master Chef Oleander and the pastry second, Delphine, cook every course yourself within two hours on feast night, and serve the Court. A rogue chef poisons the ninth course — you must catch it (Herblore 70) before it is served, or the whole feast is a disaster. After service, the rogue chef ambushes you in the kitchen. Do not break the plates.

The Grand Feast title is awarded by Oleander personally, who, if you earn it, hands you her apron. The apron halves burn rate at any range across Aelgard and adds +10% cooking XP permanently. The Pristine Culinaromancer Gloves are the upgrade of the RFD Barrows Gloves with cooking bonuses on top. The Private Kitchen in the Guild is your own range, your own farm plot, and your own fishing pond — a personal zone inside the Cooking Guild.

Twist

The rogue chef is the Culinaromancer's apprentice — a small surviving remnant from Recipe for Disaster, hidden inside the Royal Kitchen since you defeated the Culinaromancer. Killing her releases a final fragment of the Culinaromancer's curse, which manifests as a cookbook that lands on the pass. The cookbook contains the one recipe the Culinaromancer never shared, which unlocks the pristine tier of Barrows gloves — the final reward of the Heartlands cooking arc.

Resolution

Oleander hands you her apron. The Royal Court applauds, which Oleander will tell you later is a rare thing. The Private Kitchen in the Guild is yours — a range, a plot, a pond. The Pristine Culinaromancer Gloves are the upgraded Barrows Gloves with cooking bonuses. The Master Chef title sits on your character sheet permanently.

What it unlocks

Master Chef's Apron is chest-slot with burn-rate halved at any range in Aelgard and a permanent +10% cooking XP. Pristine Culinaromancer Gloves are the upgraded Barrows Gloves — the melee hand BIS for most of the midgame, now with cooking bonuses layered on. The Private Kitchen in the Guild has your own range, your own farm plot, and your own fishing pond; it is the only combined cooking-farming-fishing training hub in Aelgard, and all three feed into each other.
