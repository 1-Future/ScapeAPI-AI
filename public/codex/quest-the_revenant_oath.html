The Revenant Oath

experienced medium id: the_revenant_oath
the_wilds
Wilds. North. Cold. Obelisk. You kneel. Speak the oath. The revenants remember oaths. You will make one. Keep it. Or don't.

Premise

The Wilds voice is cold and clipped because the Wilds do not have time for sentence structure. Ranger Tomas, who taught you Into the Wilds, taught you only the first half of the oath. The second half requires kneeling at the black obelisk in the deep Wilds. Once spoken, the oath binds you to not initiate combat against any player who does not initiate against you first — an unusual constraint in a PvP zone. It is not enforced by the game code. It is enforced by the relic, which bleeds, visibly and unmistakably, if you break the oath.

The oath is rewarded by revenant cooperation. Revenants in the deep Wilds will not attack you on sight; they will test you, and if you pass, you may hunt them for unique ether drops without the normal aggro. The test is simple: accept a task from the first revenant who approaches, kill three PKers who attack you unprovoked, and return to the obelisk alive with three skulls in a sack. The oath is also inherited: the relic can be passed down to other players, which creates an in-game subculture of oath-keepers in the Wilds.

Twist

The third PKer who attacks you is not a PKer. It is Ranger Tomas, in disguise, testing whether you will break the oath to defend yourself against your own teacher. If you do, the oath is broken, the relic bleeds immediately, and the quest fails visibly. If you do not — if you let him kill you, or fight him in self-defence only without finishing him — he reveals himself, apologises in his clipped way, and the revenant acknowledges you with a nod. This branch is silent-delivery: no quest text warns you. Word of mouth will spread it.

Resolution

You carry the Revenant Oath Relic in your pocket slot. It grants +15% damage against revenants and bleeds visibly if you break the oath (initiate combat against another player). The Black Obelisk is now a safe point in the deep Wilds — a bank access, a one-way teleport out. Sworn revenant hunting opens as a PvP-safe combat training method against revenants while the oath is unbroken, with unique ether drops that feed into wilderness gear.

What it unlocks

The Revenant Oath Relic sits in your pocket slot: +15% revenant damage, visible bleeding if you break the oath. The Black Obelisk is your only safe point in the deep Wilds — bank access and a one-way teleport to the Wilds border. Sworn revenant hunt is a repeatable training method with unique ether drops; the ether is used in wilderness-only smithing and craft recipes.
