The Clockwork Heart

grandmaster epic id: sootworks_grandmaster_titan
sootworks
Sootworks. Deep forge. The Titan's been standing inert for three generations. Brun's been pacing. Soot's waiting. Big bastard needs a heart. Five bars of fire-iron. Two of silver. One of you. Hammer in your hand and the furnace hotter than a prayer.

Premise

The Sootworks Titan is a clockwork golem the size of a small tower, built in three generations of work by Brun's father, grandfather, and great-grandfather, and left inert since his great-grandfather died of a sudden heart attack on the forge floor. The family joke, not told aloud, is that the heart went with him. Brun has been pacing in front of the Titan for twenty years waiting for someone worth telling this to. Today he told you.

The Clockwork Heart must be smithed at the Foundation Furnace using five fire-iron bars, two silvered-steel bars, and one Sootworks clockwork core; it takes one uninterrupted hour at Smithing 90, and if you walk away the work cools and you must restart. You install it in the Titan, wake the Titan (which will not like you at first, and will pace), and fight the Titan, hand to hammer, with no summons, no food, no prayer switches — a clean fight on honour. Win, and it kneels, and it is yours as a summon. Lose, and Brun pulls you out before the hammer comes down.

Twist

Brun's family joke — that the heart went with his great-grandfather — was literal. The great-grandfather's actual heart is inside the Titan's inert chest cavity, embalmed, where he had placed it as a spare heart for the Titan he knew he would not live to finish. When you install your Clockwork Heart, you place it next to the family heart. The Titan wakes with two hearts, which is why its first minute of pacing is confused — it is not angry, it is surprised. Brun will not say any of this. He will cry briefly when the Titan kneels.

Resolution

The Titan is yours as a summoned companion — permanent, one summon per fight, one fire-iron bar per summon to fuel. It rests on the Titan Floor, a hangar deep below the Sootworks, which is yours to visit whenever you wish. The Steampunk Greataxe, a two-handed slash weapon that consumes fire-iron bars as charges, is BIS in that slot until the wyrm tier. Brun hands you the greataxe with both hands, which, for him, is ceremony.

What it unlocks

The Sootworks Titan is a permanent combat summon, once per fight, fuelled by fire-iron bars, which you can mine yourself. The Steampunk Greataxe is a two-handed slash weapon, BIS until the wyrm tier, and consumes fire-iron bars as charges — it is the reason the Foundation Furnace matters for endgame economy. The Titan Floor is a private hangar below the Sootworks, with a bank and a rest cot for your Titan. Brun will, if asked politely, sit with you there.
