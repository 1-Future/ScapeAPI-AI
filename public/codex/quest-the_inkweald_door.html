The Inkweald Door

experienced long id: the_inkweald_door
inkweald
Inkweald boundary. The border is not a line, it is a mood: the trees on the inside are wrong in a way you cannot point at, and the trees on the outside are watching them. Lucid Keeper Yara sits in a tent pitched where the mood changes, and she has been sleeping too little, and she wants to know if you have had any unusual dreams lately.

Premise

The Inkweald is a dreaming, and Yara is its hinge. She is the Keeper, which means she sits where the dream meets the world and keeps the door open — not for travellers, but for the dream itself, which must breathe. The door is weakening because somebody inside the Inkweald is holding their breath. If the door closes wrong, the Inkweald collapses in on itself and everything inside it becomes a memory nobody owns.

To stabilize the door you must enter it and find the person who is holding their breath, and convince them to let it out. Yara cannot go herself because she is the hinge. She will teach you the first verses of dream magic — NPC-contact, dream-teleport, lucid-ward — if you can close the door without slamming it.

Steps

[1] Sleep where Yara tells you to sleep. Describe the dream you have, in detail, before you are fully awake
Where: the Keeper's tent
Requires: an open mind
Yara writes your dream on a slate. She does not look at you while you tell it.
[2] Step through the door on the second night. Do not use your name inside. Dreams are fond of names
Where: the Inkweald threshold
Requires: a small token to exchange for the use of a name
The air on the other side feels like reading.
[3] Find the person holding their breath. They will not be visible until you stand close enough to hear them
Where: Inkweald, wherever the breath-holding is loudest
Requires: patience, quiet
A painter. A printer. A mapmaker. A child who has not blinked in some time. You decide which, and you will be mostly right.
[4] Convince them to breathe. Do not use logic. Dreams do not respect logic
Where: Inkweald, the breath-holding place
Requires: a story you have not told anyone
They breathe out, eventually. You breathe in. It is not fair.
[5] Return through the door. Tell Yara only what she asks. Do not volunteer what you saw
Where: the Keeper's tent
Requires: discretion
Yara nods once. She writes a second slate and sets it on top of the first.

Twist

The person holding their breath is you, in a sense — a dream-version of you who was left behind on a previous visit to the Inkweald you cannot remember having made. You must convince yourself to breathe. You will know how to do this only after you have tried the wrong ways.

Resolution

The door stabilizes. Yara teaches you the first verses of dream magic, which are strange and helpful and do not always work in the way you would expect. The Inkweald opens to you as a region, and the border is no longer a mood — it is a door, with your name on the inside.

What it unlocks

The Inkweald is open to you, every shifting acre of it, and the Dream Magic spellbook is yours — a utility spellbook, full of the sort of spells that save you steps rather than killing anything quickly. NPC-contact to speak across the map. Dream-teleport between places you have actually dreamt. Lucid-ward to keep things out while you sleep. None of it glamorous, all of it indispensable.

Dialogue

Lucid Keeper Yara
The Inkweald is not dangerous. It is unwell. The difference matters. You are not going to fight it, you are going to sit with it.
the breath-holder
If I breathe out I am not sure what shape I will be next. I would rather be this shape. You understand.
