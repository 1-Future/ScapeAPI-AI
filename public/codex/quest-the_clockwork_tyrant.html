The Clockwork Tyrant

master long id: the_clockwork_tyrant
sootworks
Sootworks Lower Foundry. A rogue automaton has seized the foundry, welded the doors shut from the inside, and is building clockwork soldiers at a rate of two per hour. Chief Engineer Brondi is outside with a crowd of angry dwarves and a siege engineer who has never sieged anything. He would like you to take the lead.

Premise

The Clockwork Tyrant is one of the Cogfather's prototypes, though neither Brondi nor you knew this yet. It has been dormant for two hundred years. Something woke it. It is building an army because it was built to build armies, and it has not been asked to stop. To shut it down you will need to breach the foundry gate, fight through corridors of clockwork soldiers, cut through welded doors with a thermal lance, and fight a three-phase boss whose core must be torn out by hand.

The phases escalate. The blade arms in phase one. The magnetic field in phase two, which forces pure strength to push through. The electrical shocks in phase three, as you tear out the power source. Brondi will write a citation for whoever survives, but he will not apologize for the risk.

Steps

[1] Speak with Brondi. Build a battering ram from salvaged parts to breach the foundry gate
Where: Sootworks Lower Foundry
Requires: construction
The ram is three wheelbarrows and a log. It works.
[2] Fight through corridors of clockwork soldiers
Where: foundry corridors
Requires: combat, endurance
Each wave is slightly better than the last. The Tyrant is learning from your fights.
[3] Smith a thermal lance to cut through the welded control room door
Where: foundry workshop
Requires: smithing
The lance is too hot to hold. You hold it anyway.
[4] Phase 1: block and counter the Tyrant's spinning blade arms
Where: control room
Requires: defence
The blades ring on your shield like tuning forks.
[5] Phase 2: push through its magnetic field with pure strength to reach the core pathway
Where: control room
Requires: strength
The field pulls your weapon out of your hand. You push forward.
[6] Phase 3: tear the Tyrant's power source out by hand while it shocks you
Where: control room
Requires: strength, nerve
The shocks are bright. Your hands do not let go.

Twist

In the control room you will find the Cogfather's original nameplate on the Tyrant's chest. Brondi will not want to see it. You may show him, or hide it, and your decision changes whether Brondi builds a memorial or buries the Tyrant's parts at sea.

Resolution

The Tyrant shuts down. Brondi's engineers reclaim the foundry. You walk away with a still-warm power core, a citation in a wax seal, and Brondi's unspoken acknowledgement that he owes you more than he will ever say.

What it unlocks

You carry the Tyrant's power core, which can be installed into a Sootworks forge to increase its efficiency for one week at a time. The Lower Foundry is open to you as a training site, and the engineers who watched you fight will teach you things they teach very few outsiders.
