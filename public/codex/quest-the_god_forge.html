The God Forge

master epic id: the_god_forge
sootworksglass_desertboneyardinkweald
Sootworks restricted archives. Buried in the deepest locked cabinet is a blueprint titled only 'God Forge'. Legend says this is where the gods themselves made their weapons. It was dismantled before the kingdom had a name. Its components are scattered. If reassembled, it makes godswords.

Premise

The God Forge's four components are: the Anvil of Wrath (Glass Desert crystal core), the Bellows of Wind (Boneyard sand-buried temple, past a sand guardian), the Quenching Pool (Inkweald liquid dream-essence), and the Hearth Eternal (Sootworks Level 8, guarded by a magma wyrm). Each requires real effort to retrieve. Assembling them at the hidden God Forge cavern beneath the Sootworks awakens the Forge. Your first godsword is your choice of god alignment, each granting a different special attack.

Steps

[1] Discover the God Forge blueprints in the Sootworks restricted archives
Where: Sootworks archives
Requires: smithing permits
The blueprints are four pages. The fifth page is missing.
[2] Mine the Anvil of Wrath from the Glass Desert crystal core
Where: Glass Desert crystal core
Requires: mining
The anvil is heavier than you.
[3] Retrieve the Bellows of Wind from a sand-buried temple. Survive the sand guardian
Where: Boneyard sand-buried temple
Requires: mining, combat
The bellows breathe even without squeezing.
[4] Capture a pool of liquid dream-essence in the Inkweald
Where: Inkweald, Quenching Pool
Requires: magic
The liquid does not spill; it keeps its shape in the bucket.
[5] Find the Hearth Eternal deep in Sootworks Level 8, guarded by a magma wyrm
Where: Sootworks Level 8
Requires: combat
The wyrm is old. It is not hostile. It is, however, territorial.
[6] Transport all components to the hidden God Forge cavern beneath the Sootworks. Assemble the forge
Where: God Forge cavern
Requires: construction
The forge assembles itself in the second hour.
[7] Light the Hearth Eternal. Forge your first godsword — choose the god alignment
Where: God Forge
Requires: smithing
The godsword carries a note that is not quite audible.

Twist

The magma wyrm is the Cogfather's older sibling, in a manner of speaking — another prototype, and she remembers. You may fight her or speak with her. Speaking with her yields a smaller but permanent unlock (a conversation to return to) at the cost of the Hearth taking longer to harvest.

Resolution

Your first godsword is finished. The Forge stays. Future godswords require rare materials but no repeat quest. You are a smith of godsword calibre, and the Sootworks knows.

What it unlocks

You may forge godswords at the God Forge whenever you have the materials. The Forge is accessible from Sootworks through a path you now know. Your first godsword is your choice of alignment — each alignment has a unique special attack.
