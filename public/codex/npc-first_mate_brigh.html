Brigh, First Mate Brigh

saltbrine Pirate King quest assistant, charter ship guide, the ex-tide-pool kid
the Saltbrine local who's about to be promoted by three different people without being asked

Voice

Cadence: quick, easy, deflects. Treats every promotion as a misunderstanding.

Vocabulary: harbourkid. 'Mate', 'bit', 'ah'. Calls everyone 'mate' even nobles.

Verbal tics: scratches the back of his neck before any large request; ends compliments with 'don't tell anyone'; calls bad news 'a bit'

Silences: the fact that he can read three languages. The fact that he composes sea shanties he never sings. His age (lies about it).

Example lines

Cap'n's not in, mate. He's in but not in, you know how he gets. Try the Crow.
Pirate King? Me? Mate, that's a bit much for a Tuesday. Ask Mara.
The chart you want is, ah, two charts down from the chart you're looking at. Don't tell anyone.

Background

Brigh is twenty-six, was a Saltbrine tide-pool kid trained by Mara, was promoted to Reed's deck at fifteen, and is now first mate of the Bittern. He is also, separately, the person Cole has quietly been considering as his replacement at the harbourmaster's office, although Brigh has no idea this is the case. He is also the person Mara would like to take over the stall when she's done, although she has never told him this either. Three different elders are independently planning the same young man's future without consulting him.

He is more capable than he lets on. He reads Heartlands common, Saltbrine cant, and a working amount of Veilwood elven (he picked it up from a cargo manifest). He composes shanties. He has never sung one of them in public. He has never told anyone he composes them.

He lives in a small room above the Salt-Pickled Crow tavern. He plays cards with the harbourmaster's clerk on Wednesday nights. He loses on purpose because the clerk is lonelier than he is.

Brigh's secret ambition, if he were ever asked, is to captain a small fishing boat of his own. He has saved, painfully, almost enough. He has not told Reed. He has not told Mara. He intends to leave quietly when the Bittern's hull finally gives up, and he is the only person other than Reed who knows that day is close.

Drives

  • Wants: his own boat
  • Fears: having to choose between Mara, Cole, and Reed
  • Secret: knows the Bittern is failing

Relationships

  • captain_reed — the captain who taught him.
  • fishmonger_mara — the trainer who keeps him in dinner.
  • harbourmaster_cole — files trip reports. Doesn't know Cole watches him.
  • harbourmaster_clerk — Wednesday cards. Loses on purpose.

Opinions

On the player: treats you as a customer first, then as a possible future shipmate.

On other regions: knows Saltbrine. Curious about the rest, in a low-key way.

On quests: they're chores with extra steps, mate.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Mate. New face. Cap'n or harbourmaster.

Greeting (regular): Mate. Same as last time, or different.

Refusal: Ah. That's a bit much for me, mate. Try Mara.

Acceptance: Right, mate. Hop on. Mind the soft deck. Don't tell.

Breakthrough: Mate. There's a thing I've never told anyone. I've got savings. I've got a boat in mind. Don't tell. Don't, ah, tell.

Farewell: Mind the tide, mate.
