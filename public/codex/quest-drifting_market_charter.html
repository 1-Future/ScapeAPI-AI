The Drifting Market Charter

experienced long id: drifting_market_charter
heartlandssaltbrineveilwoodsootworks
Heartlands, market square. Merchant Hilde has a scroll the length of her forearm and a plan the length of her life. She wants to build a market that moves — a floating hub that visits every region in turn, under charter, carrying goods and gossip. She needs you to recruit four merchants from four regions and, together, build the first deck.

Premise

The Drifting Market is Hilde's retirement project. Retirement, for her, means a ten-year construction job. The charter is a legal document signed by one merchant from each region, binding the market to visit their harbour once per season. Nobody has yet signed. Nobody will sign until they see the hull, and the hull cannot be built until at least one other signs, which is the deadlock Hilde wants you to break.

You will travel to Saltbrine and recruit Fishmonger Mara, who will sign if you fish her a batch of lobsters she can serve at the launch. You will travel to Veilwood and recruit Fletcher Tarin, who will sign if you chop oak logs the Veilwood permits him. You will travel to Sootworks and recruit Engineer Fizz, who will sign if you cook a feast for her crew. And then you will return to Hilde, sign-sign-sign, and build the market banner from fabric only Heartlands weavers make. At the end you will launch a thing that did not exist, that now moves.

Steps

[1] Hear Hilde's plan. Accept or decline honestly
Where: Heartlands market square
Requires: time
Hilde has three sample banners pinned to a board. They are each beautiful. She is choosing between them with a knife.
[2] Saltbrine: recruit Fishmonger Mara. She signs after a good catch
Where: Saltbrine harbour
Requires: fishing, manners
Mara does not look at the catch. She sniffs it.
[3] Veilwood: recruit Fletcher Tarin. He signs after a legitimate cut of oak
Where: Veilwood
Requires: woodcutting, a permit
Tarin is bad at conversation and excellent at listening.
[4] Sootworks: recruit Engineer Fizz. She signs after a proper feast, served hot, on site
Where: Sootworks Steam District
Requires: cooking, quick hands
Her crew eats with their goggles on. That is a compliment to you, not a flaw in them.
[5] Return to the Heartlands. Craft the market banner from Heartlands silk. Hilde oversees
Where: Heartlands market square
Requires: crafting, silk, a steady hand
The banner carries Hilde's mother's design. Hilde's mother has been dead eleven years.
[6] Launch the market. Attend the inaugural feast. Be prepared for something to go wrong
Where: the market, wherever it first docks
Requires: your presence
The market rises. The guests take their seats. And then, of course, the feast is cursed — but that is the next quest.

Twist

One of the four merchants will sign reluctantly — the one you least expect. The sign is genuine but carries a condition you will not learn until the market has been launched. The condition is small. It will matter, later, in a way Hilde will not blame you for.

Resolution

The Drifting Market exists. It moves weekly. Each region has a standing appointment, each merchant has a berth. You have opened the trade map of Aelgard and the market is your new second home. Hilde retires, honestly this time, and writes you in as her first patron.

What it unlocks

The Drifting Market is open to you — a mobile trade hub that moves between regions weekly, with shops from all of them on one deck. Specialty wares you cannot find at one stationary shop are here, depending on where the market is this week. You will plan your week around its route.
