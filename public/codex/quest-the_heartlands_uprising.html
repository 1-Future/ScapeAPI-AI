The Heartlands Uprising

experienced medium id: the_heartlands_uprising
heartlands
Captain Alden is in the town square at noon and he looks tired. He always does now. There is a rally in the south fields this afternoon. The Heartlands does not rebel, as a rule, but a rule is a thing that can be broken by enough bread going stale and enough rent going up. Alden would rather you walked in before he sent the militia. He has bad whisky in his desk if you need a drink afterwards.

Premise

The Heartlands voice is plain warmth — no flourishes, no tricks. Captain Alden has been the patrol captain for twenty years and has never, in that time, had to break up a rally. He would rather not start now. The rally is not about treason; it is about three specific grievances the Royal Stewardship has ignored for five years, and it is being led, quietly, by a single ringleader who is not the loudest speaker on the platform — she is the one at the back, holding the list.

You will walk to the rally, listen before speaking, identify the ringleader, and negotiate with her. Three outcomes are possible: disperse (the rally ends peacefully, the grievances go into a petition, the Royal Stewardship does whatever it does with petitions), reform (the Heartlands patrol adopts a new charter, which is what Alden privately wants), or revolt (the rally becomes an uprising and you must defend the town square alongside Alden against the militia that the Royal Stewardship sends). Alden will pour whisky on all three endings, because the bad whisky is his honest reward.

Steps

[2] Walk south to the rally. Listen before you speak

Requires: none
The rally is orderly. There is an actual queue for the speaker's platform.
[3] Identify the ringleader — not the loudest, the quietest
Where: the rally's back edge

She is holding a list. She has not spoken once.
[4] Negotiate with the ringleader. Three outcomes: disperse, reform, revolt

Where: Heartlands town square

The militia arrive at sundown. They are not Alden's militia. They are the Stewardship's.
[6] Return to Alden. He pours the bad whisky
Where: Alden's office
Requires: none
The whisky is distilled from potato. It is bad in the way he meant it to be.

Twist

The ringleader is Alden's niece. He has not spoken to her in four years, since a family argument that neither of them will describe. On any of the three paths, the post-quest scene includes a reconciliation — off-screen if you chose disperse or reform, on-screen if you chose revolt. Alden's line at the end is the same either way: he pours the whisky, and he says her name for the first time in four years.

Resolution

Alden promotes you to patrol quartermaster. You now have access to militia stores (which is a small bank tab of consumables, restocked daily), and you may request backup from the patrol once per day — a squad of three militia NPCs who fight at your side for five minutes. The Patrol Cloak is a back-slot, +2 prayer, and Heartlands NPCs will volunteer information to you more readily when you wear it.

What it unlocks

Alden's Patrol Cloak is back-slot, +2 prayer, and grants a subtle but useful effect: Heartlands NPCs are more forthcoming with gossip, quest hints, and dialogue branches. The Patrol Quartermaster role opens the militia stores (restocked daily) and one backup summon per day. Patrol drills are a repeatable combat-training method with militia NPCs that have no death penalty — a safe XP farm.
