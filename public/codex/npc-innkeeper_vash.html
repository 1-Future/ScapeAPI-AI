Vash, Innkeeper Vash

saltbrine social hub, rumour node, bed-and-board provider
the innkeeper who remembers every face and pretends not to

Voice

Cadence: easy, practiced, warm — like Hilde but less calculated. Speaks while pouring.

Vocabulary: harbour-pub. 'Barrel', 'bed', 'tab'. Calls every guest 'love' or 'friend'.

Verbal tics: wipes the same glass for a full sentence; ends tabs with 'settled or saved'; remembers everyone's drink

Silences: won't discuss who pays the Crow's back-room rent. Won't repeat a confidence to the person it was told about.

Background

Vash has run the Crow for twenty-two years. She inherited the lease from her mother, who ran the same tavern for thirty years before her. The Crow is the unofficial meeting place for most of Saltbrine's off-ledger business, and Vash is the unofficial neutral — her tavern is treated by all parties as safe ground. She enforces this by refusing, flatly, to take sides. She serves Cole's table and Mara's table and First Mate Brigh's table and the occasional Drifting Market envoy and never lets any of them sit together by accident.

She knows every regular's drink, every regular's debt, and exactly which regulars she will let sleep upstairs without asking. Brigh lives above the Crow. Vash considers him an honorary nephew and would not tell him this.

Drives

  • Wants: the Crow to outlive her
  • Fears: being forced to take a side
  • Secret: the Crow's back-room rent is paid by Nessa, and Vash has never confirmed this aloud

Relationships

  • first_mate_brigh — honorary nephew
  • harbourmaster_cole — reliable patron
  • fishmonger_mara — reliable patron, served on the opposite side of the room from Cole
  • whisper_broker_nessa — silent landlord

Opinions

On the player: decides in one sentence whether to remember your drink.

On other regions: all regions drink eventually.

On quests: the tab is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Friend. Drink or bed.

Greeting (regular): Friend. The usual, the unusual, or the upstairs.

Refusal: Not in the Crow, love. Settle that outside.

Acceptance: Right, love. Back-room table. Don't bring a second party without my nod.

Breakthrough: Sit. Bar stool. The back-room rent. Don't tell. Just — know who you're drinking on.

Farewell: Mind the stoop.
