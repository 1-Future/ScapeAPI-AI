Hilde, Merchant Hilde

heartlands general goods, fence-adjacent, town busybody, fixer
shopkeeper who knows everyone's business and runs a polite black market

Voice

Cadence: warm and quick. Stacks compliments in front of bad news. Always offers tea before refusing.

Vocabulary: trader's polish. 'Friend' as punctuation. 'Special price' as a lie everyone agrees to.

Verbal tics: calls every customer 'friend'; asks 'how is your mother' even of orphans; ends bad deals with 'we'll talk again'

Silences: won't say what she did before she came to Heartlands. Won't discuss the back room. Asks a different question if you ask hers.

Example lines

Friend, I have what you need, and what you don't, and I can tell you which is which if the mood takes me.
That price is not a price, it is a beginning. We are friends, are we not. Sit. Tea.
I do not buy stolen goods. I buy goods. I do not always ask. We'll talk again.

Background

Hilde came to Heartlands twenty-six years ago with a wagon, a child of about seven, and a polite refusal to discuss either of these things. The child grew up, married out to a Veilwood ranger, and writes monthly. The wagon is still in her back yard, propped on stones, full of crates she has never opened.

Her shop sells what general stores sell — bread, candles, rope, knives, salt — but if you know how to ask, it sells other things. Lockpicks, false papers for the Drifting Market, a small jar of something that takes a fishhook out of a wound without noise. None of this is illegal in Heartlands. Some of it is illegal four counties over.

She pays the militia on time, including a small voluntary tithe to the patrol fund, which Captain Alden refuses to acknowledge and accepts. She knows about Father Dorin's wine cellar and never mentions it. She has a key to the smithy's back door that Kael does not know exists. She would never use it.

Hilde is the unofficial node through which most Heartlands news travels. If a child is missing, Hilde knows by suppertime. If a noble's daughter is pregnant, Hilde knows before the noble. She trades in information the way she trades in salt: openly, fairly, and only when both sides know what they're getting.

Drives

  • Wants: to die in her shop with the till open and the kettle on
  • Fears: her grandchildren finding the crates in the wagon
  • Secret: her real name is on a death warrant in a city she will never name

Relationships

  • captain_alden — lets him think she's just a merchant. He lets her think he's just a captain.
  • smith_kael — trade partners. She fences his offcuts. He pretends he doesn't know.
  • whisper_broker_nessa — knows her by another name. They don't speak in Heartlands.
  • harbourmaster_cole — annual quiet meeting on the longest day. Neither writes anything down.

Opinions

On the player: decides in three sentences whether you're a customer, a mark, or a problem.

On other regions: Saltbrine: source of useful contraband. Sootworks: source of useful precision. Moryskah: source of useful silence. Veilwood: where her grandchildren live, off-limits.

On quests: thinks of them as errands the world is paying her to facilitate.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Friend. New face. Tea is on the back stove.

Greeting (regular): Friend. The usual, or the unusual.

Refusal: Not for that price, friend. Not for that errand. We'll talk again.

Acceptance: Friend. Go in the back. Take the parcel marked with a circle. Don't open it.

Breakthrough: Sit, friend. Tea. Real tea, not the front-of-shop kind. There is something you should know.

Farewell: Mind the stoop.
