Sins of Malachar

master epic id: sins_of_malachar
moryskah
Moryskah. Castle Malachar opens its gates for the first time since Blood Rites, and what comes out is a procession of servants dressed for a formal dinner. They are carrying invitations. Only one of them is for you. The others are decoys, and picking the wrong invitation turns your blood into a commodity Lord Malachar has been collecting for decades.

Premise

Lord Malachar has decided, on a timescale no mortal can influence, that he would like to be killed. He is tired, and he is bored, and he is so old that the idea of continuing is more repulsive to him than the idea of not. He will, however, resist being killed, because that is his nature — and because he wishes to be killed by someone worth it. The invitation is formal. It proposes a raid on his castle. It gives the rules. It is polite.

The raid is the Aelgardi equivalent of Theatre of Blood — a sequence of rooms, each a named encounter, that demands a full party of coordinated adventurers. You will fight the Bloodhound, the Chalice, the Organ, the Twins, and the Scythe before you reach Malachar himself. None of them are optional. Each room is tied to a vice Malachar has spent his undeath indulging and regretting, and Malachar himself is only fightable after the five vices are returned to him.

Steps

[1] Pick the correct invitation from the procession. Read it carefully. Assemble a party you trust
Where: Moryskah, road to the Castle
Requires: a steady hand, companions
The invitations smell of different things. One smells of your mother's kitchen. That is not a trap; Malachar did his research.
[2] The Bloodhound's chamber. The beast is a loyalty made animal. Defeat it without letting it keep any of you it bites
Where: Castle Malachar, dog-room
Requires: melee, coordination
The hound is so old its teeth are yellow and its love is complete.
[3] The Chalice. A gauntlet of thirst. You may drink or you may carry the Chalice. Choose carefully
Where: Castle Malachar, refectory
Requires: herblore, discipline
The Chalice is full at your arrival. It empties only by being drunk from.
[4] The Organ. A puzzle of sound. Play the right sequence or the walls close
Where: Castle Malachar, chapel
Requires: a good ear, quick hands
The organ has eighty keys. Seven of them are black in colour and blacker in effect.
[5] The Twins. Fight two at once. They swap between ranged and melee every time you blink
Where: Castle Malachar, hall of mirrors
Requires: ranged, melee, a party
They wear identical rings. One ring burns. The other ring does not.
[6] The Scythe. A combat puzzle: stay close to the blade, never close enough to be cut
Where: Castle Malachar, the orchard
Requires: agility, bravery
The scythe is not held by anyone. That is not an improvement.
[7] Return the five vices to Lord Malachar at his feet. Then, and only then, fight him
Where: Castle Malachar, the throne
Requires: all five tokens
He smiles. He has not smiled in a very long time and it shows.

Twist

Malachar's last ability — which he will activate at the cusp of defeat — is to offer his Scythe to whichever party member landed the killing blow on any of the five vices. Taking the Scythe makes you his heir for one heartbeat. The party must agree in advance whether to refuse or accept, and the ruling matters.

Resolution

Malachar falls, either by your hand or by his own, depending on what the party did with the Scythe. His castle becomes a raid site — repeatable, valuable, dangerous. The servants disperse without being told. They will cook for you, for gold, in a kitchen somewhere east.

What it unlocks

Castle Malachar is open as an endgame raid — the Theatre-of-Blood equivalent of Aelgard, repeatable, with loot tables that include Malachar's own effects. The boss fight itself drops the Scythe of Malachar, which is a weapon for people who have accepted what the weapon asks in return.
