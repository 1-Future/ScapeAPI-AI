The Last Dragon, Part Three

master epic id: the_last_dragon_p3
glass_desert
Glass Desert, the Crystal Caverns. Veldrak is awake. The sky above the Crystal Caverns has a dragon in it, and the dragon is old enough to have watched the Glass Desert become glass, and the dragon is angry. Crystal Sage Orin stands at the cavern mouth, holding a lance you have never seen before, and he offers it to you because his shoulder can no longer draw one.

Premise

Veldrak was the last dragon for a reason. The earlier dragons killed each other in the old war, and Veldrak went to sleep under the crystal desert to outlast grief. The awakening was not your doing, but it is now your problem, because Orin's oaths forbid him from being the one to put Veldrak down. The Dragon Hunter Lance in his hand was forged for this precise fight, and it will not work for anyone who has not already slain the two dragons of parts one and two.

The fight has three phases. Veldrak's breath is the breath of a long-quiet thing finding its lungs again; it is not as fast as it will be in five minutes. Phase one, you hurt him. Phase two, he learns. Phase three, he remembers. If you can reach the third phase without faltering, he will talk to you, and you will have to decide whether to deliver the killing blow or to let him sleep again, with a scar he will carry in his sleep forever.

Steps

[1] Take the lance from Orin. Let him tell you what it is made of
Where: Glass Desert, Crystal Caverns mouth
Requires: respect for the thing you are holding
The lance is crystal wrapped in steel wrapped in leather that smells of three extinct animals.
[2] Enter the cavern. Walk until Veldrak notices you. Do not shoot first
Where: Crystal Caverns, deep
Requires: courage
Veldrak notices you three times before he reacts. You do not move between noticings.
[3] Phase one. Strike while his breath is still shallow
Where: the deep cavern
Requires: the lance, your combat, your shield
He coughs. He has not coughed in a thousand years. The sound is almost embarrassing.
[4] Phase two. He now knows your tricks. Change them or lose them
Where: the deep cavern
Requires: adaptability
Your opener stops working. Your second opener stops working. Your third opener had better surprise you too.
[5] Phase three. He speaks. Hear him out. Decide
Where: the deep cavern
Requires: silence
His speech is very old. Listening is the whole of the fight.
[6] Finish the fight in the way you have decided. Return to Orin at the cavern mouth
Where: the cavern mouth
Requires: the lance, and a choice
Orin does not ask. He notices which pocket you put the scale in.

Twist

Veldrak offers you his oldest scale as a token regardless of what you do. The scale weighs more if you killed him than if you let him sleep. Orin will not tell you this, but he will know, and the crystal caverns will behave differently around you forever.

Resolution

The Glass Desert's Crystal Caverns are yours, in one form or another. Orin takes back the lance, wraps it, and hands it to you with his own name added to the list of names on the haft.

What it unlocks

The Crystal Caverns are open to you — the Crystal Wyrm's lair, a tier-5 endgame boss you can now pursue — and you may equip the Dragon Hunter Lance, which is the best-in-slot weapon against any dragon you will ever meet in Aelgard. The haft has names scratched into it, each one a hunter who owned the lance before you. Yours is the last one so far.

Dialogue

Crystal Sage Orin
My oaths will not let me strike him. His oaths will not let him leave. The loop requires a third. That is you.
Veldrak
I have been asleep. It was restful. It is not restful any more. Please decide quickly. I am not used to being awake.
