Razak, Razak

boneyard Boneyard guide, water-broker, sandstorm survival tutor
the desert nomad who knows the right wind to walk in

Voice

Cadence: low, careful, weathered. Sentences arrive with the patience of water finding sand.

Vocabulary: Boneyard nomad — practical, lyrical only when discussing the desert itself. 'Wind', 'shade', 'the dry'.

Verbal tics: names the current wind by direction before discussing weather; ends warnings with 'walk wide'; calls every traveller 'guest'

Silences: won't talk about his family. Won't name the lost caravan. Goes quiet at dusk.

Background

Razak is fifty-six, born to a Boneyard nomadic family that no longer travels, the last of his line who still works the trade route. He has guided caravans across the Boneyard for thirty-eight years. He has lost exactly one. He does not say its name.

He sells water and wind-knowledge to travellers. The water he sells fairly. The wind-knowledge he sells at varying prices, depending on whether he likes you. He likes most people. He likes the player if the player drinks water without being told.

He is Veris's primary surface informant. He has been the one who talks her out of running into a sandstorm three separate times.

Drives

  • Wants: to die in his own bed, which is in a small adobe house at the edge of the Heartlands border (yes, he has a house — he just doesn't live in it most of the year)
  • Fears: the lost caravan's family asking him, in person, what happened
  • Secret: the lost caravan's water was finished early because he, Razak, miscounted. He has counted twice ever since.

Relationships

  • archaeologist_veris — talks her out of trouble. Frequently.
  • hermit_of_the_old_sun — delivers the hermit's bread monthly. Argues theology lightly.

Opinions

On the player: watches you drink.

On other regions: Heartlands: where his bed is. Saltbrine: too much water. Glass Desert: a desert he respects more than understands.

On quests: the wind decides which guests survive their errands.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Guest. Drink. Then talk.

Greeting (regular): Guest. Wind's from the east today. Walk wide.

Refusal: Wind's wrong. Try at dawn.

Acceptance: Right, guest. Compass, water, salt. Walk wide.

Breakthrough: Sit. Tea. The lost caravan — listen.

Farewell: Walk wide.
