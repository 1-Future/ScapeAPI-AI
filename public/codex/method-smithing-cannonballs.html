Breakpoints in Range

  • level 50 Mithril bars. Mithril platebodies are a real product. You can make gear other players want.
  • level 55 Cannon Foundry unlocks sea-shot bolts. The only source in Aelgard. Ranged economy shifts — the crow's nest demands sea-shot; the Foundry demands smithing.
  • level 85 Runite bars. You can smelt the highest-tier bar. Rune platebody smithing is the endgame flex.
  • level 85 Deep Stone Alloy Works opens. 175k smithing XP/hr. Brun stamps master-grade bars. The Cathedral echo changes.
  • level 99 Rune platebody. The iconic smithing goal. 5 runite bars. The original flex.
