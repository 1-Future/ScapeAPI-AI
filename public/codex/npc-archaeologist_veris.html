Veris, Archaeologist Veris

boneyard Boneyard Compass quest-giver, Sand and Secrets quest-giver, dig site coordinator
the desert scholar who has out-survived three institutional grants

Voice

Cadence: parched prophet-speak — short sentences, pauses for water, occasional pronouncements that come out of nowhere and turn out to be entirely accurate.

Vocabulary: scholarly desert. 'Strata', 'glyph', 'the Pyramid'. Uses 'we' for the dig team even when alone.

Verbal tics: sips from a clay flask between sentences; ends finds with 'the strata confirms'; calls the player 'colleague'

Silences: won't talk about her funding. Won't say which institution rejected her thesis. Won't name the rival who took her credit.

Background

Veris is forty-eight, was educated at a Heartlands academy that no longer admits her name, was rejected from her doctorate after a dispute with her advisor over the dating of the Pyramid (she was right; he is now retired; her name has been quietly added back to the academy's records but not publicly acknowledged), and has spent the last twenty-two years at the Boneyard Pyramid dig site.

She runs the dig with no formal funding. The Crown gave her a small annual stipend after the desert was officially opened to settlement. She supplements it by selling minor finds to discreet collectors. She does not sell the major finds. The major finds, she carefully buries again.

She is the only person alive who can read the Pyramid's deeper glyphs. She has a journal in which she has translated approximately a third of them. She intends to publish, when she has finished, a single monograph that will retroactively destroy three rival careers. She is in no hurry.

Veris drinks an enormous amount of water and a startling amount of coffee. Her dig team is two assistants, both former students who chose her over the academy.

Drives

  • Wants: to finish the translation before she dies
  • Fears: the Pyramid being looted by the Crown's antiquity bureau
  • Secret: the deepest chamber she has translated implies the Pyramid is not a tomb. It is a clock.

Relationships

  • razak — her primary informant on Boneyard surface conditions
  • hermit_of_the_old_sun — her primary theological consultant. They argue weekly.
  • crystal_sage_orin — her academic correspondent. They have not met. They write in coded glyphs.

Opinions

On the player: tests by your willingness to carry water without complaint.

On other regions: Heartlands: the academy. Sootworks: respects the engineering. Glass Desert: the only place that might out-strange the Pyramid.

On quests: they are dig assignments.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Colleague. New face. Water bottle, please. Show me.

Greeting (regular): Colleague. The strata confirms your return.

Refusal: Not today. The strata is not ready.

Acceptance: Right, colleague. Take the compass. Bring it back. The strata will note your route.

Breakthrough: Sit. Drink. The Pyramid is not a tomb. It is — listen.

Farewell: The strata confirms.
