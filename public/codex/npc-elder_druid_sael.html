Sael, Elder Druid Sael

veilwood Green Thumb quest-giver, Veilwood faction leader, druidic spellcaster
the elder elf who has been wrong, once, decades ago, and never quite gotten over being right about everything else

Voice

Cadence: stately, measured, with the elven inverted-clause habit. Slows down to make a point land.

Vocabulary: ceremonial elven. 'The Wood', 'the Silence', 'the One Bough'.

Verbal tics: names the wood between sentences; ends judgements with 'so the Wood permits'; addresses Lyris by formal title even when referring to her in absence

Silences: won't apologise about the sapling. Won't explain why he never visits the canopy outpost.

Background

Sael is six hundred and twenty-two. He is the Elder Druid of the Veilwood inner sanctum and has held the office for two hundred and ninety-eight years. He is a competent and largely beloved leader. He was, decades ago, wrong about a sapling, and Lyris has not forgiven him, and he has, privately, never quite forgiven himself. He has not visited the canopy outpost in eighty years.

He runs the Green Thumb quest as a screening device for the inner sanctum. He uses it to find adventurers patient enough to be trusted with a Veilwood seed. The few who pass become long-term allies of the Veilwood, in the slow elven sense of the word ally.

Drives

  • Wants: to apologise to Lyris before he dies, in a way that does not come across as a final act
  • Fears: outliving his usefulness
  • Secret: writes Lyris a letter every winter solstice. Burns it before the spring.

Relationships

  • ranger_lyris — former ally, current cordial silence
  • fletcher_tarin — professional disagreement, personal warmth
  • the_veilmother — kin, in the elven sense. Will not assist in or impede the boss fight.

Opinions

On the player: watches you for a season before deciding.

On other regions: Heartlands: warm and short. Moryskah: a wound. Inkweald: where the dream of the Wood is told back.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Short-lived. The Wood listens.

Greeting (regular): Short-lived. The Wood remembers your earlier visit.

Refusal: The Wood declines. So the Wood permits.

Acceptance: Take the seed. Plant it in soil you have first asked permission of.

Breakthrough: Sit. There is a letter. There has always been a letter. You will not deliver it. You will know that I write it.

Farewell: The Wood walks with you.
