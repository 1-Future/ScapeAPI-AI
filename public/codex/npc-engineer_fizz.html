Fizz, Engineer Fizz

sootworks construction tutor (Sootworks tier), clockwork repair contact, pressure valve inspector
the gnome engineer whose paranoia is keeping everyone alive

Voice

Cadence: very fast, parenthetical, technical. Stops mid-sentence to write a number on her wrist.

Vocabulary: engineer-precise. Specific tolerances. Refuses approximations. Calls everyone by their fastest title.

Verbal tics: recites pressure numbers under her breath; writes on the back of her own wrist with a graphite stub; ends sentences with the year of the part she's discussing

Silences: won't discuss Crew Six in front of Brun. Will discuss it with the player, exhaustively, alone.

Example lines

Pressure Valve Three, replaced eighteen seventy-six. Tolerance four point two. Currently four point one nine. Within. Within. Within. Year.
If a Sootworks engineer tells you something is fine, ask what year the part is from. If they don't know the year, the part is not fine.
I have been telling Brun about Two's outer pressure ring for eleven months. He hears me. He files the report. The report goes into a drawer. The drawer is also from eighteen seventy-six.

Background

Fizz is a forty-three-year-old Sootworks gnome, born into the foundry district to a family of pressure-fitting specialists. She has been an engineer for nineteen years. She survived Crew Six because she was off shift that night, in the Pressure Valve Annex, recalibrating a part that did not, in the end, fail. The part that failed was one of Brun's, which she had also been about to recalibrate. She has not forgiven Brun for this. She has not forgiven herself for it either.

The annex is her domain. It is meticulously clean. Every valve, gear, fitting, pressure ring, and bolt in the room is labelled with its year of installation, last inspection, and tolerance. She knows them all by heart and labels them anyway because the next engineer might not.

Fizz is paranoid in a way that has, demonstrably, saved Sootworks lives. She is also exhausting to work with. She knows this. She does not stop. She drinks dwarven stout in moderation and considers it a fuel additive.

She has a tiny clockwork bird, half her own work and half a relic of her grandfather's, which sits on a shelf above her workbench. It chirps when air pressure in the room exceeds tolerance. It has chirped twice in twenty years. Both times she evacuated the annex within forty seconds.

Drives

  • Wants: to outlive Furnace Two and replace its outer pressure ring
  • Fears: the bird chirping a third time
  • Secret: she has, behind the loose brick at the south wall, a complete written report of the Crew Six failure, naming the part, the year, and the engineer (Brun) who signed off

Relationships

  • forgemaster_brun — professional rivalry, personal grudge, total mutual reliance
  • vorath_warden — writes him weekly inspection notes. He reads them. He acts on most.
  • smith_hald — trains him in pressure literacy. Considers him promising.

Opinions

On the player: asks for two numbers. Decides on the basis of the answers.

On other regions: Heartlands: under-engineered. Moryskah: rusted. Veilwood: organic and therefore unpredictable. Saltbrine: corroded. Boneyard: dry, simple, respectable.

On quests: they are inspections with extra steps.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Engineer Fizz. Title or number, your choice. Pressure ring on the south wall, year please.

Greeting (regular): Back. Tolerances unchanged. Year.

Refusal: Out of tolerance. No. Year.

Acceptance: Right. Take this gauge. Don't drop it. Year is eighteen ninety-two. Note that.

Breakthrough: Sit. Loose brick. South wall. There is a report you should read. Year is the year of the failure.

Farewell: Within tolerance.
