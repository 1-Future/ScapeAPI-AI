Coronation of the Revenant King

grandmaster epic id: the_wilds_grandmaster_crown
the_wilds
King. Revenant. Deep Wilds. Old. Crowns the dying. You will crown him ended. Go fast. PKers come.

Premise

The Revenant King is the oldest revenant in the Wilds. He does not attack the living; he crowns the dying. Any player who falls in the deep Wilds with under a breath of health becomes, briefly, crowned — a passive buff that does not help them, but which the King considers his gift. The gift is actually a toll: once crowned, you will, on your next death anywhere in Aelgard, respawn in the Wilds instead of your usual spawn point. The Wilds' population has been quietly rising for decades because of this.

You will fight him to end his reign. Equip the Wilderness Blade and the Revenant Oath Relic and enter the Wilds from any direction but south (south is his sight-line). Reach his throne in the deepest Wilds through ambushes — other players will be on you. Do not speak with him; he will try to crown you. Attack within ten seconds of arrival. Phase one is his rusted sword. Phase two is six revenant knights. Phase three is the crown, dropped. Pick it up. Leave before the throne collapses. Ranger Tomas will refuse the crown. Put it on.

Twist

The crown is not the only regalia; there is also the Revenant Ether Mace, which the King drops after phase two and which you will not notice until you are already outside — because the Mace only manifests to someone wearing the crown. If you leave the Wilds without the Mace in your inventory, it will return to the throne, and you must re-do the fight. Some players, not knowing this, have re-done the fight twice. Word of mouth has since spread the warning.

Resolution

You wear the Wilderness Crown. Revenants across Aelgard will not attack you first. The throne-clearing is now your private camp — a PvP-safe bank, a small shrine, and a cache. The Revenant Ether Mace is a crush weapon, BIS Wilds-only, and consumes ether per hit; the ether is gathered from sworn revenant hunting, which ties the whole Wilds arc together.

What it unlocks

The Wilderness Crown is head slot, +15% damage in the Wilds, and revenants will not initiate against you. The Revenant Ether Mace is crush-damage, BIS in the Wilds only; ether is produced by sworn revenant hunting, which requires the Oath Relic and which creates a closed-loop economy inside the Wilds for this entire arc. The Revenant Throne is a PvP-safe bank and shrine inside the deep Wilds — the only such safe point past the Black Obelisk.
