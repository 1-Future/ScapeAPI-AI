Prestige Goals

Prestige Goal: The Barrows Brothers Challenge

Defeat all 6 Barrows Brothers in a single run. Claim the full set of degradable armor. Lay them to rest permanently.

Required skills: attack 75 magic 75 prayer 43 defence 70

Bosses: dharok, guthan, verac, ahrim, karil, torag

Unique rewards: amulet_of_the_damned, all_barrows_sets

Sub-areas

  • Moryskah — Gothic horror swamp. Undead, werewolves, vampires. Holy items provide protection. [requires: prayer 20, attack 25, magic 15, quest: the_bog_witchs_bargain]
  • Moryskah Slayer Tower — Multi-floor slayer dungeon. Aberrant spectres, gargoyles, nechryaels, abyssal demons. [requires: slayer 10, quest: slayers_gauntlet]
  • The Barrows — Six ancient warrior tombs. Repeatable boss encounters. Drops degradable set equipment. [requires: prayer 43, magic 50, quest: blood_rites]
  • Castle Malachar — Vampire stronghold. Late-game Moryskah content. Theatre of Blood equivalent raid. [requires: attack 70, prayer 60, agility 50, quest: sins_of_malachar]

Quirky Interactions (4)

Ambient world objects that grant tiny XP. Discoverable, flavorful, mathematically terrible — but sometimes a lifeline.
  • Stir the Bog Witchs Cauldron [cooking] — 0.5 xp/click. The Bog Witch hums while you stir. "Faster, slower, faster. Never stop."
  • Make Crypt Rubbings [crafting] — 0.4 xp/click. Some of the names feel familiar somehow. You havent been here before.
  • Practice Exorcism Chant [prayer] — 1 xp/click. The chapel grows slightly darker each time. Probably just your eyes.
  • Feed the Bat Colony [hunter] — 0.8 xp/click. The bats no longer swarm you. Progress.
