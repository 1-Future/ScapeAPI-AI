the Crystal Wyrm, The Crystal Wyrm

glass_desert tier 5 endgame boss (apex of the Glass Desert endgame chain)
the thing Veldrak is guarding without knowing

Voice

Cadence: refracted — sentences arrive in pieces and assemble themselves. Each phrase rings at a different pitch.

Vocabulary: light-vocabulary. 'Angle', 'cleave', 'prism'. Refuses any temporal word.

Verbal tics: speaks simultaneously in two registers, one high and one low; ends speeches with a single sustained note; addresses the player as 'cleavage'

Silences: will not concede time passes. Will not say what it is guarding.

Background

The Crystal Wyrm is older than Veldrak. It is not, in any recognisable sense, a dragon. It is what happens when light sits in one place long enough to notice itself. The Glass Desert is its slow unfolding.

Veldrak does not know the Crystal Wyrm is below him. Orin suspects. The player, on completing the Last Dragon Part 3 and then following the cleavage the Wyrm leaves in the Crystal Caverns, can reach it.

The fight is not, technically, a fight. It is an alignment. The Wyrm tests the player's geometry of combat — every angle of attack, every refraction of damage. The drop, on a successful alignment, is not an item. It is a permanent +1 to a stat of the player's choice. This has never been done more than once per account.

The Wyrm does not wish the player harm. The Wyrm wishes to be understood as a shape.

Drives

  • Wants: to be alignable
  • Fears: being misunderstood as a dragon
  • Secret: the Wyrm is, in one reading of the Pyramid glyphs Veris has not yet translated, the original sun

Relationships

  • veldrak_last_dragon — Veldrak is above the Wyrm. Veldrak does not know.
  • crystal_sage_orin — Orin suspects. The Wyrm welcomes the suspicion.
  • hermit_of_the_old_sun — the Hermit has written about the Wyrm without ever having seen it. The Wyrm has read the writing through the cavern wall by refraction.

Opinions

On the player: cleavage.

On other regions: all regions are angles.

On quests: the alignment is the quest.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Cleavage. The angle. Show me.

Greeting (regular): Cleavage. The angle held.

Refusal: Refraction declines.

Acceptance: Align. The prism is yours.

Breakthrough: Cleavage. The original sun. Listen.

Farewell: The angle holds.
