The Lost God Returns

grandmaster epic id: the_lost_god_returns
heartlandsmoryskahboneyard_wastesveilwoodsaltbrinesootworksglass_desert
Six regions are reporting a stranger at the crossroads. The stranger is the same person — same face, same voice, same quiet promise — in all six regions, simultaneously, which is impossible. The old histories name her the Lost God. She is testing you across six regions at once. The world will be changed by your answers. The Codex will record them. The server will remember.

Premise

The Lost God was a minor divinity in Aelgard's oldest histories — walked out of the world during the First Empire's fall, reasons unknown. She has returned, and she is testing you because you are the only mortal in Aelgard who has completed the three prerequisite world-quests that made her aware of you: Recipe for Disaster's finale, Desert Treasure, and Lunar Diplomacy. She will meet you at six crossroads, one per region, and each meeting will ask you to do a small morally ambiguous thing: lie to a stranger, leave a grave unvisited, pour out your water, cut down a tree you promised not to, burn an unread letter, forge a weapon you will never wield. Each of these is a test of whether you can hold a self without being told what to do.

The seventh meeting is in the Glass Desert. She will not name a seventh crossroad task. She will ask you what you learned. There is no correct answer. She will then offer apotheosis. Accept, refuse, or propose a third thing. The server remembers. Your Lost God's Sigil (the permanent amulet you walk away with) will be bound to your choice and will have stats varying by path. The Lost God's Mercy prayer is once-per-week; any single death is undone, provided you keep the Sigil in inventory. The Crossroad Shrine appears at a seventh crossroad accessible only to you.

Steps

[1] Meet the Lost God at the Heartlands crossroad. She asks you to lie to a stranger on her behalf
Where: Heartlands crossroad
Requires: thieving 70
The stranger is a farmer. The lie is small. It will hurt him for a week and heal after.
[2] Meet her at the Moryskah crossroad. She asks you to leave a grave unvisited
Where: Moryskah crossroad
Requires: prayer 85
The grave is your own, from a past mistake you made in an earlier quest. Leaving it unvisited is a harder choice than it sounds.
[3] Meet her at the Boneyard crossroad. She asks you to pour out your water
Where: Boneyard crossroad
Requires: herblore 70
You have a full skin of water. The sun is high. The pour is deliberate.
[4] Meet her at the Veilwood crossroad. She asks you to cut down a tree you promised not to
Where: Veilwood crossroad
Requires: woodcutting 70
The tree is one of the covenant-trees. The druids will not see, but the Veilwood will.
[5] Meet her at the Saltbrine crossroad. She asks you to burn a letter unread
Where: Saltbrine crossroad
Requires: firemaking 60
The letter is addressed to the pirate king. He has been expecting it. You must burn it before he sees it.
[6] Meet her at the Sootworks crossroad. She asks you to forge a weapon you will never wield
Where: Sootworks crossroad
Requires: smithing 80
The weapon is a short blade. You will not use it. Someone else will, eventually.
[7] Meet her at the Glass Desert crossroad. She asks what you learned. There is no correct answer
Where: Glass Desert crossroad
Requires: honesty
She listens all the way through whatever you say. She does not react until the end.
[8] She offers apotheosis. Accept, refuse, or propose a third thing
Where: Glass Desert crossroad
Requires: honesty
The apotheosis is literal. So is the refusal. The third option is whatever you name.

Twist

Each of the six small tasks has a hidden consequence that does not resolve for several in-game weeks. The lied-to farmer gets stronger when he discovers the lie. The unvisited grave grows a strange flower. The poured-out water feeds a patch of desert that was dying. The cut tree produces a seedling that grows three times faster than its parent. The burned letter was one the pirate king would have been hurt by. The forged short blade becomes, eventually, a child's heirloom. You do not know about any of this while you do the tasks; the Lost God's test is whether you choose well when you cannot see the consequences.

Resolution

Whatever you chose at apotheosis is permanent. The Lost God's Sigil sits on your neck; its stats are bound to your path — apotheosis grants divine stats (+5 to every skill), refusal grants mortal stats (full prayer regeneration on kill), the third path grants variable stats based on whatever third thing you proposed. The Lost God's Mercy prayer is once-per-week; any single death is undone, provided the Sigil is in inventory. The Crossroad Shrine appears at a seventh crossroad accessible only to you; it contains a memory of each task and a small altar for private reflection.

What it unlocks

The Lost God's Sigil is a neck slot with stats varying by path: apotheosis grants +5 to every skill (the largest flat buff in Aelgard), refusal grants full prayer regeneration on any kill (a combat-prayer breakpoint), the third path grants a stat package determined by whatever you proposed (GM-discretion flavor, but always useful). The Lost God's Mercy prayer undoes one death per week, provided the Sigil is in inventory — which is the most valuable death insurance in the game. The Crossroad Shrine is a private temple at a seventh crossroad that does not exist on any map, accessible only through a dream prompt or by the Sigil's passive.
