Waking the Dreaming One

grandmaster epic id: the_inkweald_grandmaster_dream
inkweald
Sleeps still, the Dreaming One does — not soundly, however, and never quite silently. Yours, the choice, now is: wake her, and take the dream into the waking world, or bind her deeper, and let the Inkweald stay a dream. Walked, the dream labyrinth must be, first. Three nights of preparation, begin.

Premise

The Dreaming One is the oldest presence in the Inkweald — she was not made, she simply was, and she began dreaming before the continent of Aelgard had a shape worth mapping. Her dream became the Inkweald, which is why the Inkweald's grammar is dream-blur and why its regions move when you are not looking. To reach her you will need to walk the Dream Labyrinth behind the second door, which consists of five lucid-riddle rooms, each of which is a dream you had and forgot. The riddles will be yours; they will not be identical for any two players, and they will take material from your recent gameplay.

At the labyrinth's heart she is waiting, and she speaks in your own voice. She will offer you a choice of three outcomes. Wake her, and the world gains a new kind of magic permanently — her dream spills into reality, and certain breakpoints across Aelgard become easier, certain others harder. Bind her deeper, and the world stays stable, and you gain her sigil — permanently. Walk out without choosing, which is the rarest and least-taken path, and she remains asleep, and you carry out the one item no player who chose either other path can get — her unspoken name.

Twist

Whichever path you take, the Dreaming One remembers you afterwards. If you wake her, she becomes a minor NPC who occasionally gives you cryptic advice in your own voice. If you bind her, her sigil wakes you from dreams that another player is trying to borrow from you. If you walk out without choosing, her unspoken name appears on your inventory as a quest-item you cannot drop, and the game will know it for later quests that have not yet been written.

Resolution

The Inkweald is subtly different after you leave the heart chamber. Players who chose different paths will notice different environmental details — a change of moon, a new bird, a missing tree — and the Codex will record which path you chose, for server-wide reference. Your own unlocks depend on the choice: Lucid Staff and Dream Talisman on the wake path, Dreaming One's Sigil on the bind path, and the unspoken-name quest-item on the walk-out path, which gates at least one future quest that has not been written yet.

What it unlocks

The Lucid Staff is the BIS magic staff for lucid and dream spells, with +15% spell damage anywhere in the Inkweald. The Dream Talisman teleports you to the last place you dreamed of — which is any location your character spent more than five minutes in during the last seven in-game days — and the Codex tracks this. The Dreaming One repeatable boss appears in whichever ending state you chose; its drop table matches the path (combat on bind, puzzle on wake, none on walk-out, because the walk-out path does not refight her).
