The Inkweald Mirror

experienced short id: the_inkweald_mirror
inkweald
A mirror there is, in the Inkweald's third grove, that shows not your face but the face of what you will have become, if you continue. Sought, few have. Walked away, fewer still. Laughed, none. At noon, the third grove pretends to be awake. The mirror will be covered. Uncover it alone.

Premise

The Inkweald Mirror is a capstone miniquest for the Inkweald arc — short in steps, permanent in effect. The mirror shows you a specific version of your character six months into their future, assuming no major course correction. The glimpse is unsettling, precise, and, for most players, a significant narrative beat that either affirms their current play style or prompts them to change it. The mirror must be uncovered alone (no party members), looked at without flinching until the mirror finishes (forty in-game seconds), and named — the name you speak will determine the shape the mirror takes for future glimpses (once per session).

The reward is the Mirror Shard, a pocket item that reveals one PKer's intent per day (whether they are hostile or passive, regardless of their actual combat state) and a once-per-session glimpse of one possible future for any other player or NPC. This is a high-utility social and strategic item. The third grove itself becomes a meditative training zone for prayer, with unique scaling based on your relationship with your mirrored future.

Where: the mirror niche

The shard is small. It is what you will have cost yourself.

Twist

The mirror shows a future that the game tracks partially in real time. If you significantly change your play style (major skill rebalance, moving regions, taking on a hitherto-refused faction), the mirror's image shifts on your next visit, and the shard's effects shift slightly with it. Players who never return to the mirror get a static future. Players who return often get an evolving one.

Resolution

You carry the Mirror Shard. The third grove is yours to meditate in. Once per day you may identify one PKer's hostility intent. Once per session you may glimpse one possible future for any other player or NPC, which makes it a socially-important item — watch which PKers have it, and which merchants do, and which quest-givers.

What it unlocks

Mirror Shard is a pocket slot: identifies one PKer's hostility intent per day, and glimpses one possible future for any player or NPC once per session. This is both a PvP safety tool (use on suspicious players near the Wilds border) and a social tool (use on merchants to see if they will raise prices). Mirror meditation is an AFK prayer method in the third grove, scaling with your mirrored-future — aggressive futures give combat-prayer bonuses, peaceful futures give utility-prayer bonuses.
