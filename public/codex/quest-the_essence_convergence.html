The Essence Convergence

intermediate medium id: the_essence_convergence
heartlandsinkweald
Heartlands Runecrafting Guild. Wizard Theron's altars are weakening on a schedule he can predict to the minute. His own runes come out wrong now, a colour he will not name. He thinks the answer is in the Inkweald, where the magic leaks sideways, and he would like you to bring back a bottle of that leakage.

Premise

Runic altars run on essence, and essence runs on an underground current that has, for the last decade, thinned. Theron's theory is that a new kind of essence — Dream Essence — can be found in the Inkweald, and that the altars can be taught to accept it with an infusion. His colleagues think he has lost his mind, which has happened to runecrafters before and is not disqualifying. He will teach you enough to test his theory.

You will mine rune essence beneath the guild, to demonstrate competence. You will craft a batch of air runes, to demonstrate more. Then you will walk into the Inkweald, stabilize three unstable nodes around an essence rift, collect five Dream Essence fragments, and infuse the Heartlands altar with them. The first Dream Rune is yours to craft, and only yours.

Steps

[1] Speak with Wizard Theron. Mine fifteen rune essence beneath the guild, and craft a test batch of air runes
Where: Heartlands Runecrafting Guild
Requires: mining, runecrafting
Theron watches you mine without watching you. It is a skill of his.
[2] Enter the Inkweald and navigate the shifting paths to the Essence Rift
Where: Inkweald
Requires: patience, a dream-anchor
The rift hums. The hum has lyrics; you do not hear them clearly.
[3] Channel magic through three unstable nodes to stabilize the rift
Where: Inkweald, rift nodes
Requires: magic
Each node resists for a different reason. You will not know the reasons until afterwards.
[4] Collect five Dream Essence fragments from the stabilized rift
Where: Inkweald rift
Requires: runecrafting, a containment pouch
The fragments sing, quietly, at different pitches. They make a chord when combined.
[5] Return to the Heartlands. Infuse the altar. Craft the first Dream Rune
Where: Heartlands Runecrafting Guild
Requires: runecrafting, magic
The altar changes colour once, briefly, before settling. Theron laughs aloud.

Twist

One of the rift nodes refuses to stabilize. The refusal is not a puzzle; it is a boundary. You may return, later, with a higher level, and stabilize it then — or you can accept four out of three nodes stable and complete the quest imperfectly, which gives the Dream Rune but also gives a small occasional misfire when you craft it.

Resolution

Theron writes a paper his colleagues will not read. You walk out with a pouch of Dream Runes and a way of producing more. The altar now takes Dream Essence, which means your runecrafting has one more flavour.

What it unlocks

Dream Runes are in your pouch, and you know how to craft more. A small essence pouch rides on your belt for overflow. The Heartlands altar accepts Dream Essence now, and a trickle of the Inkweald's leaks runs through the guild in a way that changes the colour of their lamps. Theron credits you in the margins.
