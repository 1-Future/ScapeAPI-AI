Foundations of the Fallen

intermediate medium id: foundations_of_the_fallen
heartlandssootworks
Heartlands, the eastern moor. The Chapel of the Last Light has been a pile of stone for a hundred years, and everyone in the village of Wellcote has been pretending it is not there. A mason called Perla Goodknee wants to rebuild it. She has asked every guild in the Heartlands, and every guild has said the same thing: the old chapel was built by Sootworks hands, and nobody up here knows the joints.

Premise

The Chapel of the Last Light fell because the villagers took the altar slab to make a millstone and the roof followed a year later. It was built in the old Sootworks joint-style, where each stone is cut so that its neighbour supports it directly and mortar is barely needed. Nobody in the Heartlands remembers the cuts. The dwarves still use them, but they do not teach them to outsiders. Foreman Grukk in the Steam District will consider it, if you prove you can smith the bracket-iron his apprentices use to hold a joint-cut in place overnight.

Rebuilding the chapel is the job. Discovering what the villagers are pretending not to know is the other job. The altar slab was not taken for a millstone. There is a crypt under the chapel floor, and the slab was moved to cover the stair.

Steps

[1] Walk the ruin with Perla and decide with her what can be re-used. Mine limestone from the chapel quarry only if you can name the bed it came from
Where: Heartlands, Chapel of the Last Light
Requires: a pick, an eye for stone
Perla kneels by a fallen lintel and runs a fingernail along its underside. She whistles, low.
[2] Travel to the Sootworks Steam District. Convince Foreman Grukk you are not wasting his time by smithing the test brackets without complaining
Where: Sootworks, Steam District
Requires: iron, a willingness to sweat
The forge is loud enough that Grukk's criticism is almost inaudible. Almost.
[3] Learn the joint technique. Lay the foundation in the old way. Raise four wall segments correctly — Perla will check each one
Where: Heartlands, Chapel of the Last Light
Requires: joint-cut stones, bracket-iron, bad language
Perla presses her palm flat against each wall and listens with her ear to the stone. Two she nods at. One she tells you to take down.
[4] Build a pulley yourself and raise the roof beam without help. Light the chapel lantern when it is up
Where: Heartlands, Chapel of the Last Light
Requires: ropes, timbers, a firemaking kit
The lantern catches. Something under the floor moves, very slightly.
[5] Investigate what the lit lantern revealed beneath the altar. Report honestly to the Heartlands Archives
Where: the crypt under the chapel
Requires: courage, a notebook
The crypt is dry. That is the first wrong thing about it.

Twist

The Sootworks joint-style the dwarves teach you is an older form than the chapel actually used. When you lay the foundation, one joint does not fit, and you will realize — either on your own or when Grukk arrives uninvited — that the chapel was built by a dwarf who had been banished for inventing a new cut. Grukk wants to know what the old heretic built, and what is under it.

Resolution

The chapel stands again. Perla weeps politely and pretends she is not weeping. Grukk examines the crypt and takes notes in a book he does not let you see. You have rebuilt a shrine and uncovered a secret, and both are now yours to revisit.

What it unlocks

The Sootworks is open to you as a region, not just a border mine. You have the joint-cut technique in your hands, and construction XP flows better when you use it — a small bonus per wall, per beam, per rebuilt doorway. The hidden crypt beneath the chapel is yours to enter when you are ready, and it has its own quiet appointments.
