The Underground Pass

experienced long id: underground_pass
heartlandsinkweald
Heartlands, west of the city. A guard at the mouth of the Underground Pass has been posted there for twelve years and nobody has relieved him. He will let you in. He will not come with you. The Pass is full of traps, puzzles, and an ancient evil, and the bodies of the people who went in before have not been brought out.

Premise

The Underground Pass is a long tunnel that connects the Heartlands to the Inkweald, and it is genuinely dangerous. Traps. Pit bridges. A three-orb puzzle that is unique to each player. A shifting maze of fear. A demon at the bottom who is not particularly interested in being killed. The Temple of Light at the Pass's deepest point is the halfway mark; after that, the Inkweald boundary.

Nobody will help you. You will bring your own ranged, your own agility, your own willingness to solve puzzles in the dark. The guard at the entrance will promise to remember your name if you do not come back, which is the most he can offer.

Steps

[1] Speak with the guard. Enter the Pass. Navigate the first section of traps
Where: Pass entrance
Requires: agility
The guard does not wave.
[2] Cross the pit of despair using the rope bridge
Where: the pit
Requires: nerves
The bridge is one rope. You pass your belt over it. You go hand-over-hand.
[3] Solve the three-orb puzzle — it is unique to you
Where: orb chamber
Requires: thinking
The orbs hum at different pitches. Match them to the chord above the door.
[4] Navigate the maze of fear, whose walls shift every thirty seconds
Where: the maze
Requires: a clock in your head
You will be wrong twice. Do not be wrong three times.
[5] Reach the Temple of Light. Defeat the demon of the Pass, preferably by ranged
Where: Temple of Light
Requires: ranged, endurance
The demon is impatient. He has been waiting.
[6] Find the exit to the Inkweald boundary. Return to the guard with proof of passage
Where: Inkweald boundary; Pass entrance
Requires: proof
The guard finally waves.

Twist

Midway through the maze of fear you will encounter a corpse that is not old. The person was going the other way. You can leave them or carry them out; carrying them out slows you but gives you a minor unlock (the victim's family opens a shop to you in the Heartlands).

Resolution

You have walked the Pass. The guard promotes you in his own ledger. The shortcut between Heartlands and Inkweald is yours to use; you never have to walk the full route again.

What it unlocks

The Underground Pass is a shortcut between Heartlands and the Inkweald boundary, at your disposal. The guard at the entrance remembers your name. A few families in the Heartlands whose relatives you may have recovered extend trade privileges to you.

Dialogue

the guard
I have been here for twelve years. I have seen four people go in. You are the fifth. I have seen one come out. Maybe you are the second.
the demon
I was not waiting for you specifically. I was waiting for anyone. You will do.
