The Bog Witch's Errand

novice short id: the_bog_witchs_errand
moryskah
Moryskah. A woman in a coat made of crow feathers and hessian is standing in a swamp up to her knees, and she is waiting for you specifically, though you have never met. She is the Bog Witch's hedge-daughter Merenna, and she has a shopping list from her mother, and her mother does not like to come into town.

Premise

The Bog Witch does not do her own shopping because the last time she did, three stalls closed and one horse refused to ever be ridden again. Merenna is her errand-girl, and Merenna is tired, and Merenna wants a proper adventurer to run the list today because her feet are swollen and the swamp tea has done nothing.

The list is not a normal list. It is read out loud, not handed over, and it changes depending on what the witch is brewing. You will be told a set of things. Most of them are everyday. One or two are strange. All of them have to come back clean. If you run the list well, the witch will teach her hedge-daughter to teach you, which is how herblore gets passed down in this part of the world.

Steps

[1] Memorize Merenna's list without writing it down — she will not repeat it, and ink attracts flies in the bog
Where: Moryskah swamp edge
Requires: none
A rusted hinge. Three salt-cured eels. A bell no-one has rung in a century. The thing your mother threw away when you were nine.
[2] Gather the items. Do not explain what they are for if asked, because you do not know
Where: Moryskah village, the abandoned chapel, and somewhere only you know about
Requires: patience; a polite refusal if the miller gets curious
The bell in the belfry is heavier than it has any right to be.
[3] Return to Merenna before the sun drops below the line of cattails, or the witch starts the brew without you
Where: Moryskah swamp edge
Requires: all the listed items, dry
Merenna counts them with a finger. Her finger does not touch them.

Twist

One of the listed items cannot be purchased: the thing your mother threw away when you were nine. The quest journal will not help. Merenna will not help. You must decide, yourself, what that was, find something that stands for it, and bring it. The witch will know if you lied.

Resolution

The brew bubbles green and then silver and then a colour you do not know the word for, and the Bog Witch — finally visible from a shack that was not there when you arrived — grunts once and nods. Merenna smiles for the first time that day. The witch hands you a small pot of salve that smells of turned earth and mint, and a short list of plants that will grow for you, not for other people, in a herb patch that is now yours to tend.

What it unlocks

You walk away from the swamp with a bruise-coloured salve that keeps undead at arm's length long enough to matter, and the recipe for it, which uses no ingredient you cannot acquire. A corner of the Moryskah bog now grows swamp herbs for you, the sort that hedge-witches swap at harvest time and never sell. The Bog Witch does not wave. She never waves.

Dialogue

Merenna
I am not going to tell you the list twice. If you forget a thing, pick something that rhymes with it. She hates that, but she respects it.
the Bog Witch
The ninth thing is the only real one on the list. The other eight are a courtesy.
