The Drowned Cartographer

experienced long id: the_drowned_cartographer
saltbrine
Saltbrine harbour, midnight. A ghost ship fades into being between the pilings, and on its deck a ghost in a coat with too many pockets is unrolling a map that is not finished. He has been dead for two hundred years. He is not angry about it. He would like help finishing his atlas.

Premise

Cartographer Maelvin drowned with his fleet in a storm that swallowed the whole Saltbrine expedition of two centuries ago. He has spent every century since returning on one specific midnight to work on the atlas he never completed. This year, the midnight is yours to attend. He needs coast drawings from three places he never reached alive, sketched properly, with the right parchment and the right eye.

The ghost ship is surrounded by spectral sea serpents that will eat you unless they are distracted by fresh bait. You will fish that bait, board the ship, listen to Maelvin, sketch his coasts using charcoal and parchment you craft, pray over his spirit so he can read what you drew, and watch him complete the atlas. The atlas reveals an island that rises once per century — and its rise is soon.

Steps

[1] Witness the ghost ship at Saltbrine harbour at midnight
Where: Saltbrine harbour
Requires: presence, patience
The ship fades in the way a photograph develops, if you have seen a photograph.
[2] Row out. Fish for bait to distract the spectral sea serpents around the hull
Where: Saltbrine harbour waters
Requires: fishing
The serpents do not sink your rowboat. They are waiting for the bait.
[3] Board the ship. Speak with the ghost of Cartographer Maelvin
Where: ghost ship
Requires: courage
Maelvin's accent is wrong. He has been dead long enough for the accent to slide.
[4] Sketch the Saltbrine cliffs using charcoal and parchment you craft, and sketch the reef from the harbour lighthouse
Where: Saltbrine cliffs; harbour lighthouse
Requires: crafting
The charcoal runs differently at night. The sketches look accurate in moonlight and slightly off in day.
[5] Pray over Maelvin so he can read your sketches
Where: ghost ship
Requires: prayer
Maelvin peers at the sketches. He smiles. His smile is longer than his face.
[6] Watch Maelvin complete the atlas. Take the atlas from his hands as he fades
Where: ghost ship
Requires: both hands
The atlas is warm. His hands were cold.

Twist

Maelvin's final addition to the atlas is a place he should not know about — a coast he drew while dead, from memory of a place no living cartographer has mapped. The coast matches a dream you have had but will not immediately remember having.

Resolution

The ghost ship fades. The atlas sits in your bag. Maelvin's century is complete. The next cartographer on this midnight appointment is someone else, and their name will appear in the atlas after yours in time.

What it unlocks

You carry Maelvin's atlas, which shows an island that rises once per century. The island is due to surface soon; the atlas marks it, and the marking is yours alone. Saltbrine ghost-fishing is open to you at low tide, and a discount at the charcoal-maker follows your association with Maelvin's lineage.

Dialogue

Cartographer Maelvin
I drew many coasts. I never drew this one. You have. It is a better coast than mine, and I am glad of it.
