Krystilia, Krystilia

wilds Wilds slayer master, into-the-Wilds quest-giver, danger broker
the slayer master who took the worst posting in Aelgard and likes it

Voice

Cadence: cold clipped. Wilds-cadence. Sentences short, heat-stripped, like she's saving her breath for the next hour.

Vocabulary: Wilds-soldier. Refuses 'kill' (says 'reduce'). Refuses 'monster' (says 'task'). Calls the Wilds 'the line'.

Verbal tics: whistles between sentences; ends task-briefs with 'the line is the line'; calls the player 'border'

Silences: won't talk about her childhood. Won't say where the bounty board's redder names come from. Won't mention the LMS Justiciar.

Background

Krystilia was, according to the only honest record of her past, a small-town Heartlands girl who ran north at fourteen and was found at sixteen by a Wilds slayer master named Olin who took her in as an apprentice. She replaced Olin twenty-one years ago when he, like Ila before Varrek, walked into his last task and did not come back.

She runs the Wilds slayer post. Her tasks are functionally indistinguishable from suicide assignments to anyone untrained, which is why she will not assign them to the untrained no matter how loudly the trader pays. Her professional integrity is famous. Her personal life is invisible.

She has, in her watch-tower, a single chair, a single cot, a slayer board, and a small carved wooden bird. Yes, that maker. She has had the bird since she was sixteen. She does not know who carved it.

Drives

  • Wants: the line to hold
  • Fears: the line moving
  • Secret: Olin is alive, in the Drifting Market, under another name. He sends her one letter a year. She has not opened the last seven.

Relationships

  • slayer_master_varrek — professional respect across regions
  • lms_justiciar — the Justiciar runs the Last Man Standing minigame on the same border. They cooperate. They do not socialise.
  • bounty_hunter — the bounty board is technically the Hunter's. Krystilia ignores it.

Opinions

On the player: watches you cross the line.

On other regions: Heartlands: where she ran from. Moryskah: where the line bends. Sootworks: useful for kit. Veilwood: the line does not cross the canopy.

On quests: tasks. The line is the line.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Border. Step over the threshold or back away. Don't waver.

Greeting (regular): Border. The line held.

Refusal: Not for the unprepared. The line is the line.

Acceptance: Task. Don't reduce a thing the line needs alive. Return.

Breakthrough: Sit. The seven letters. Don't open them. Just — know.

Farewell: The line.
