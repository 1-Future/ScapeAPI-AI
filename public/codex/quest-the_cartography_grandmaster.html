The Cartography Grandmaster

grandmaster epic id: the_cartography_grandmaster
heartlandsmoryskahboneyard_wastesveilwoodsootworkssaltbrineinkwealdglass_desertthe_wilds
Every region has been mapped. The space between them has not. Nessa has been asked to draw the Map of Maps — the living atlas of Aelgard, which must be walked into existence, border by border, at specific hours, in specific weathers, by two people. Her partner, you are. She will bring the cart. You will bring a spare pair of boots.

Premise

The Map of Maps is a dynamic atlas that updates as Aelgard changes — which, by now, with the comet and the prophecy and the dreamer half-awake, is often. It must be walked into existence; it cannot be drawn from a desk. Each border must be measured at its correct hour (dusk for Heartlands-Moryskah, midnight for Moryskah-Boneyard, noon for Boneyard-Glass Desert, dream-border for Glass Desert-Inkweald, scent-only for Inkweald-Veilwood, underground for Veilwood-Sootworks, by drainage canal for Sootworks-Saltbrine, in the company of a favour-holding revenant for Saltbrine-Wilds). You will walk with Nessa, sometimes with her cart, sometimes alone because the border will not permit two, but always with her method. At the end, you draw the Map of Maps together at her new table, which is larger than last time.

The Map of Maps is a consumable-slot item: teleport to any previously-visited location once per hour, updates in real time. Border running is a new agility training method with unique XP bonuses at each border. Nessa retires after this; you inherit her commission ledger and may assign yourself map-quests for rewards.

Steps

[1] Meet Nessa at the Boneyard market. She has a cart. You have spare boots
Where: Boneyard market
Requires: the_cartographers_debt, the_aelgard_atlas
Nessa is old now. She is also walking better than she was, which is surprising, and which she will not discuss.
[2] Walk the Heartlands-Moryskah border at dusk
Where: HM border
Requires: agility 80
Dusk is precise. Five minutes early or late, and the border will not measure.
[3] Walk the Moryskah-Boneyard border at midnight
Where: MB border
Requires: agility 80, prayer 50
The border is silent. The silence is itself a measurement.
[4] Walk the Boneyard-Glass Desert border at noon
Where: BGD border
Requires: firemaking 60
The heat is punishing. Fire must be lit at two specific cairns without breaking the pace.
[5] Walk the Glass Desert-Inkweald dream-border. There is no daylight to measure by
Where: GDI border, in dream state
Requires: herblore 60
The dream-border is walked while dreaming. Nessa cannot walk this one. You go alone.
[6] Walk the Inkweald-Veilwood border by scent only
Where: IV border
Requires: hunter 60, eyes closed
Eyes closed. Nose working. Nessa will lead you back if you stray too far.
[7] Walk the Veilwood-Sootworks border underground
Where: VS border, in the tunnel
Requires: mining 60
Measured by sounding the ceiling at specific intervals. The ceiling answers.
[8] Walk the Sootworks-Saltbrine border along the drainage canal
Where: SS border, canal
Requires: fishing 60
A net is lowered at each curve. Each net catches a small specific fish that must be released after being weighed.
[9] Walk the Saltbrine-Wilds border with a revenant who owes you a favour
Where: SW border
Requires: the Revenant Oath Relic, a favour owed
The revenant will walk beside you. It will not speak. Its presence is the measurement.
[10] Return to Nessa. Draw the Map of Maps together at her new table
Where: Nessa's new workshop
Requires: a steady drawing hand
The new table is larger. It has to be. The Map of Maps is not small.

Twist

Nessa is walking better because she has been wearing a pair of boots she made for her mother, which she could not wear until her mother's grave had walked close enough to her (see The Cartographer's Debt). Now that it has, she is walking with her mother's stride. She will mention this once, on the midnight border, and will not mention it again.

Resolution

The Map of Maps is finished. It is a living atlas. Nessa retires. You inherit her commission ledger, which means you may assign yourself map-quests for rewards, from a rotating pool of specific Aelgard locations that require re-measuring. The map itself occupies an inventory slot; it teleports to any previously-visited location once per hour, and updates in real time — new locations added as you visit them, old locations shifted as Aelgard changes.

What it unlocks

The Map of Maps occupies an inventory slot and provides one teleport to any previously-visited Aelgard location per hour, with real-time updates as the world changes (dunes moving, cottages shifting, new quest-locations appearing). Border running is a new agility training method with unique XP bonuses at each of the eight borders — a late-game agility farm that requires planning around the specific hour and condition of each border. Nessa's commission ledger is a quest-giver ledger you own; you may assign yourself new map-quests from a rotating pool that rewards unique teleport glyphs.
