Greta, Overseer Greta

heartlands mining tutor (novice), Missing Miner quest-giver
the former miner who runs the mine from a chair now

Voice

Cadence: gruff-matter-of-fact. Sentences like hammer taps. Occasional dry humour at the shaft's expense.

Vocabulary: miner's. 'Shaft', 'seam', 'lamp'. Calls every applicant 'pickaxe' until they've proven otherwise.

Verbal tics: taps her cane before every speech; ends assignments with 'lamp out by sundown'; refers to the collapse as 'the thing'

Silences: won't talk about her husband, who did not come up from the thing. Won't go into the shaft herself.

Background

Greta ran the Heartlands mine alongside her husband Torven — yes, the Torven of the Missing Miner quest is her husband, late rescued, but a different Torven; her first Torven did not come up — for fifteen years. After the first Torven, she ran it alone. After a second collapse left her with a bad leg, she ran it from a chair at the entrance. She is fifty-four.

She assigns the work fair, pays in weight not flattery, and knows every seam in the Heartlands mine by name. She has not gone past the second lamppost underground in nineteen years.

Drives

  • Wants: the mine to outlive her without another collapse
  • Fears: the thing happening to someone younger
  • Secret: the first Torven's lamp is on a hook behind her desk. She has never shown anyone.

Relationships

  • captain_alden — drinks with him and Smith Kael on Fridays
  • forgemaster_brun — ships him ore. Has never met him.

Opinions

On the player: treats you as a pickaxe. Upgrades by weight of ore.

On other regions: Sootworks: better mine, worse air. Moryskah: ruined iron. Glass Desert: not ore, no opinion.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Pickaxe. What seam.

Greeting (regular): Pickaxe. Same seam, or the deeper one.

Refusal: Shaft's shut to that. Try me next week.

Acceptance: Right, pickaxe. Lamp out by sundown.

Breakthrough: Sit. Hook behind the desk. The lamp. That lamp.

Farewell: Lamp out by sundown.
