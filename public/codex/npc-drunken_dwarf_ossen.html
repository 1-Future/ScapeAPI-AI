Ossen, Ossen (the Drunken Dwarf)

sootworks eccentric, unofficial lore node, dwarven stout importer
the dwarf everyone assumes is useless, who is the only one who remembers the deep tunnels correctly

Voice

Cadence: slurred on the first sentence, perfectly precise on the third. Cycles between the two.

Vocabulary: dwarven pub. Lyrical when drunk, technical when correcting. Refuses 'drunk' (says 'focused').

Verbal tics: toasts before every speech; ends stories with 'and that's why the tunnel goes left'; calls the player 'stout-taker'

Silences: won't discuss the Crew Six accident. Won't name his lost crewmates. Will, on exactly one day a year, discuss both at length — the anniversary.

Background

Ossen is fifty-eight, was a tunnel surveyor in the deep Sootworks for thirty-one years, survived Crew Six by being three tunnels away, and has been, by his own preferred description, focused ever since. He lives above the Soot-Mouth tavern. He receives a small pension from Vorath's office, unofficially, because Vorath wants him to keep living.

He is the Sootworks's surviving living memory of the pre-Crew-Six tunnel maps. Fizz knows this. Brun knows this. Vorath knows this. None of them have, in eight years, asked him to redraw the maps. He is waiting to be asked. He has, in a drawer in his room, a stack of three-hundred-and-forty handwritten notes that reconstruct most of the tunnels from memory.

Drives

  • Wants: to be asked for the maps
  • Fears: being asked too late
  • Secret: the drawer

Relationships

  • forgemaster_brun — mutual avoidance, mutual respect
  • engineer_fizz — she visits quarterly. He insists she doesn't.
  • vorath_warden — unofficial pension

Opinions

On the player: sizes you up by what you order. Water earns a third sentence.

On other regions: Heartlands: too sober. Moryskah: too damp. Saltbrine: respectable stout.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Stout-taker. Sit. Water or stout.

Greeting (regular): Stout-taker. Same stool.

Refusal: Not today. And that's why the tunnel goes left.

Acceptance: Right, stout-taker. Take this note. Tunnel goes left. Don't lose it.

Breakthrough: Sit. The drawer. Yes, that drawer. Don't tell Fizz I told you. And that's why the tunnel goes left.

Farewell: Next round on you.
