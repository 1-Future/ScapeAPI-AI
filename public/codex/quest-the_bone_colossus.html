The Bone Colossus

master long id: the_bone_colossus
boneyard
Boneyard Wastes, the outpost at the northern dunes. A giant skeleton has assembled itself from the bones of a thousand old warriors and is walking southwest, toward the Heartlands, at the pace of a man who has a grudge. Watch Commander Thane has a bounty, a brewery, and no illusions about who will volunteer.

Premise

The Bone Colossus is not one creature. It is a thousand. Every day it absorbs more skeletons from the Wastes and grows a few inches. It is walking toward the Heartlands because the men who became its bones marched that way once, and the bones have not forgotten the march. Thane's scouts estimate two weeks until it reaches a Heartlands village.

To kill it you will need silver weapons, the Bonecrusher blessing, and a three-phase combat plan. Phase one: destroy its legs by attacking the knee joints. Phase two: it collapses but fights from the ground; slay the skeleton core controlling it. Phase three: the core splits into five bone wraiths, each using a different combat style; destroy all five. The final act is shattering the Colossus Heart — the central bone that held it all together — which Thane will present to you personally.

Steps

[1] Speak with Watch Commander Thane at the Boneyard outpost. Scout the Colossus from a distance
Where: Boneyard outpost
Requires: a telescope
The Colossus is twice as tall as a siege tower. It is not making a sound.
[2] Mine blessed silver ore from the sacred mine near the outpost. Have the outpost smith forge silver weapons
Where: sacred mine; outpost forge
Requires: mining, smithing
The silver takes the blessing quickly. The smith is efficient.
[3] Pray at the Boneyard shrine for the Bonecrusher blessing
Where: Boneyard shrine
Requires: prayer
The blessing lands heavily. You carry a little more weight walking out than walking in.
[4] Approach the Colossus. Phase 1: destroy its legs by attacking the knee joints
Where: dunes
Requires: attack
The knees are the size of doorways. You enter them, swing, exit.
[5] Phase 2: it collapses and fights from the ground. Slay the skeleton core controlling it
Where: dunes
Requires: slayer
The core is hidden in the ribcage. Climb in.
[6] Phase 3: the core splits into five bone wraiths. Destroy all five using the silver weapons
Where: dunes
Requires: attack, endurance
The wraiths use the combat styles of their original owners. You recognize one of them.
[7] Shatter the Colossus Heart. Report to Thane
Where: dunes; outpost
Requires: a heavy blow
The Heart is crystal, not bone. Thane takes it in both hands.

Twist

One of the five bone wraiths will say your family name, in a voice you recognize. You may defeat it silently, or you may pause and speak with it — which is dangerous, because the wraith will try to convince you to stop the fight. Pausing is not rewarded. Proceeding is. But the name will stay in your quest log.

Resolution

The Colossus falls. The Wastes are quieter. Thane hands you a Colossus bone shard, which is not valuable, and a pouch of coin, which is. You are welcome at the outpost from now on; they remember you.

What it unlocks

You carry a Colossus bone shard. The Boneyard outpost is open to you as a waypoint and a recruiting station; Thane will put your name on tasks when they arise. Your silver-weapon crafting is faster, and the Bonecrusher blessing is in your prayerbook for future use against undead bosses.
