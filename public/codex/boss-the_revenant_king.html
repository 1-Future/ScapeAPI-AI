The Revenant King

the_wildstier 5

Bestiary

The Revenant King is the subject of the Wilds' deepest rumour and is, therefore, the Wilds' most valuable target. A revenant in a crown, in a crypt, in a ruin that is not on any surviving map of the old garrison. He is said to be what is left of the regent who led the Kingsfall expedition, though this is not confirmed. He is said to wear a crown that was made during the expedition, from a circlet the regent brought and from the teeth of the first revenant the regent killed, and this is also not confirmed. What is confirmed is that players who reach his chamber describe the same room, and the same figure, and the same crown, and the descriptions have been consistent across the small handful of reports spanning twenty years.

To reach him, a player must complete the Coronation quest, which requires finding the crypt — the crypt's location does not stay the same between expeditions and must be re-located on foot — and opening a door that the last few travellers to reach it declined to open. Opening the door is the quest. What comes after is the boss.

He fights as a spectral warrior. He moves silently. His strikes drain life and transfer it to him, and he heals faster than a single player can damage him. The fight is intended for a group of three or more; a solo player can win, but will need to have prepared extensively. He does not have phases in the conventional sense; he has moods, and his mood changes when his health drops below certain thresholds, and his attacks shift accordingly. He is faster at low health. His strikes are heavier when he is angry. He is angry most of the time.

He is weak to crush. The spectral form has no material skeleton but has a residual structure, and the structure shatters under blunt-force weapons the way a frozen thing shatters under a hammer. Slash passes through. Ranged lands but does reduced damage. Magic is variable.

He drops the Wilderness Crown, which grants a twenty percent damage bonus inside the Wilds and which does nothing at all outside them, and the Revenant Ether Mace, which is vampiric inside the Wilds and an ordinary mace outside. He also drops a quantity of gold and a map — hand-drawn, in his own script — of a part of the Wilds that is not on any existing crown map. The map has not been deciphered.

A long hollow breath. The crown, settled on his head a moment before, falls to the floor with the weight of something heavier than gold.
