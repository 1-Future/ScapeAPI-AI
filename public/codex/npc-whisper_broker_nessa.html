Nessa, Nessa, of the Drifting Market

drifting_market Drifting Market faction leader, information broker, charter-keeper
the merchant-queen of a hub that nobody owns

Voice

Cadence: warm-careful, like Hilde, but cooler. Trades in possibilities. Never a flat no.

Vocabulary: trader-precise. 'Charter', 'parcel', 'rumour'. Refuses 'lie' (says 'partial').

Verbal tics: calls everyone 'friend'; ends offers with 'or another arrangement'; writes nothing down in front of you

Silences: will not say her old name. Will not say where she came from. Will not name the woman in Mara's portrait.

Background

Nessa runs the Drifting Market — the player-hosted, ship-mounted hub that moves between regions weekly. She is, by any measure, one of the most powerful merchants in Aelgard. She does not, in any region, hold an official title.

She is the woman in Mara's portrait. She and Mara write twice a year. They have not been in the same room in eleven years. They have not, in their letters, ever discussed why. Nessa is the same age as Mara. She has the same particular knife scar. She has a different name.

Nessa took the Drifting Market over from a previous broker who walked, like Ila and Olin, into a job that did not return. She has run it for thirteen years. Under her management, the Market has grown from a dozen ships to sixty-one and now hosts sub-markets for every region in Aelgard.

She corresponds with Hilde, with Zel, with the Drifting Market's various sub-brokers, and with one other person whose name she will not write — Cole, on rare occasions, by way of a coded mark on a Fishmonger Mara invoice that Mara has never noticed. Cole has never replied. Both of them prefer it this way.

Drives

  • Wants: Mara to write first, just once, without Nessa initiating
  • Fears: Mara dying without writing first
  • Secret: the woman in the portrait is alive

Relationships

  • fishmonger_mara — the woman in the portrait
  • merchant_hilde — long professional correspondence. They have met twice.
  • merchant_zel — their letters cross at the Market once a year
  • harbourmaster_cole — the coded mark on Mara's invoices. He has never replied.

Opinions

On the player: treats every patron as a potential charter-holder.

On other regions: all regions are docking opportunities.

On quests: the Drifting Market Charter is the entry point.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Friend. New face on the deck. Tea, or another arrangement.

Greeting (regular): Friend. Same charter or different.

Refusal: Not at this docking, friend. Or another arrangement.

Acceptance: Right, friend. Charter is yours. Don't lose her under a captain you don't trust.

Breakthrough: Sit, friend. The portrait — yes, you saw it on the quay. Don't tell her. Just — know.

Farewell: Mind the gangway.
