Dragon Slayer of Aelgard

experienced long id: dragon_slayer_aelgard
sootworks
Sootworks, the Iron Vein foundry. A family of metal dragons has moved into the deep mines, which is not unprecedented, and they are not eating the miners, which is. They are, however, moving the ore. Forgemaster Brun wants the dragon-head off in a polite and structured way, because killing the mother-dragon wrong will make the whole family worse.

Premise

Metal dragons are hatched, not born; their mothers are often as surprised as anyone. The Iron Vein family has an adamant matriarch who is old enough to remember when the Sootworks was a hole. She is not attacking miners. She is relocating them to a chamber that she wants to keep, and she expects them to understand why. Brun has read enough dragon-lore to know that a polite slaying is the best any of them will get, and he would like it done before the matriarch has time to lay again.

To kill the matriarch you will need dragonbane ore, a shield that does not melt, and a willingness to fight in a heat that will strip a non-blessed weapon in minutes. You will also need to give the matriarch a chance to refuse the fight. Brun insists on this. It is not kindness. It is good practice: a dragon who was given a chance to leave and did not is a dragon whose hoard you have clear title to afterwards.

Steps

[1] Meet Brun. Hear the structured-slaying rules. Agree or decline honestly; this is not a job for a fast yes
Where: Sootworks, Iron Vein foundry
Requires: patience for the protocol
Brun reads the rules off a plaque on the wall. He has them memorised. He reads them anyway.
[2] Mine five dragonbane ore nodes in the restricted shaft. The ore does not come out clean the first strike
Where: Sootworks, restricted shaft
Requires: mining, a light hand
The ore sings on the second strike. On the third it answers you.
[3] Smith an anti-dragon shield to fit your arm, not a standard one. And smith a dragonbane sword that does not melt
Where: Sootworks, long forge
Requires: smithing, patience
The shield's curve has to be yours. Somebody else's shield will turn aside the wrong way.
[4] Descend into the den. Give the matriarch the chance to leave. Speak the Sootworks formula
Where: Sootworks, deep den
Requires: the formula, a clear voice
She blinks at you slowly, which is not yes and not no. It means: continue.
[5] Fight her. Keep the shield up. Aim for the throat-vent rather than the face. Win, or retreat; do not lose
Where: Sootworks, deep den
Requires: the shield, the sword, defence and attack at the right levels
The fire is white. The shield runs in a trickle but holds.
[6] Take the core from the matriarch's chest. Leave the other chambers alone. Report to Brun with the core
Where: Sootworks, deep den and then the foundry
Requires: the core
Brun weighs the core and says something quiet in a language you do not know.

Twist

The matriarch was hatched from an egg that Brun found as a young smith and kept in his hearth for a season. He did not know until she told him, mid-fight, in a language he thought he had forgotten. He will not tell you whether this changes anything. You decide whether to take the hoard for yourself or to leave half of it where it is.

Resolution

The dragon-head is off. The Iron Vein family leaves the Sootworks in an orderly procession. Brun reopens the deep mines and writes your name in the ledger beside his own. The shield you carry was made to your arm; it will not fit anyone else.

What it unlocks

You wear your own anti-dragon shield — required to fight any dragon without taking the full brunt of the breath — and you know the dragonfire shield recipe, which is the best shield in the game for a dragon fight. The Sootworks deep mines are open to you as a training area; metal dragons, runite ore, and the occasional surprise.

Dialogue

Forgemaster Brun
The protocol is not for her benefit. It is for yours. A dragon who is slain without an offer of leaving is a dragon who is murdered. That has consequences we have not yet measured.
the matriarch
You smell like him. I suppose he is still angry. Tell him I am not.
