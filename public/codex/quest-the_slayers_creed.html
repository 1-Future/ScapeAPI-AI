The Slayer's Creed

experienced long id: the_slayers_creed
moryskah
Moryskah, Varrek's assignment room. The Slayer Master looks at the mark on your palm and tells you he cannot put the next level into you. He can only point. Eastward. To a tower whose last tenant left sixty years ago without turning the key. The creed is not taught. The creed is carved.

Premise

The abandoned Slayer Tower on the eastern edge of Moryskah was built by six slayers who did not trust each other and did not need to. They agreed only on a stone, kept in the basement, on which each of them carved their name before they died. No master has been inside the basement in a generation. The stone's rule is simple and cannot be taught by telling: every slayer who carves their name there must first hunt one creature for each of the six names already on it, with no scroll, no order, and no task. The creed is the knowledge that makes the order unnecessary.

Varrek cannot teach this because he is a master of the scroll, and the creed despises the scroll. You go because you have already taken the first mark and the gauntlet has already tested you and there is nothing else for Varrek to tell you. The creed-mark, if you earn it, will be on your palm forever, and it will hum when you are near a creature the stone remembers.

Steps

[1] Reach the eastern tower and open the basement without the original key
Where: Moryskah, abandoned Slayer Tower
Requires: thieving, agility
The basement door is wooden, swollen, and older than Moryskah's town charter.
[2] Read the six names on the stone and decide the order in which to hunt them
Where: Slayer Tower basement
Requires: a steady hand on a cold stone
The names are carved deep enough to hold dust, and the dust is a different age in each groove.
[3] Hunt one creature per name. No task scroll. The creed will tell you when it is satisfied
Where: six locations, one per name
Requires: slayer 50 combat, patience
Each creature will know you are hunting without orders. It will be worse for them, and also for you.
[4] Return to the stone and carve your name beneath the sixth
Where: Slayer Tower basement
Requires: a knife that has cut something that was not willing
The stone accepts the cut. The cut accepts you.
[5] Survive the seventh name, which is not yours, which appears above your name while you carve
Where: Slayer Tower basement
Requires: everything you have left
The seventh slayer was never dead. She was waiting to be finished.
[6] Return to Varrek with the creed-mark hot on your palm
Where: Moryskah, Varrek's assignment room
Requires: none
Varrek does not smile. He will let you sit in his chair, which is more.

Twist

The seventh name on the stone is the one nobody could carve while alive. It belongs to the slayer who founded the order and could not be killed until someone carved beneath her. When you carve yours, she becomes killable, and immediately is, because she chose this outcome sixty years ago. Her shade fights you. She loses on purpose. She has been waiting a long time to lose.

Resolution

The creed-mark on your palm is permanent and hot. Varrek, who is not usually a sentimental man, pours you his second-best whisky. The stone now has seven names. You do not have to take scroll tasks any more, but you may; the creed is a supplement to the order, not a rejection of it. Varrek tells you, without looking at you, that the eighth name will not be his. He does not say whose it will be.

What it unlocks

You walk out of the tower with a creed-mark burned into your left palm and a ring on your right hand that hums when you approach a creature the stone remembers. The Slayer Tower basement is yours to enter at any hour — banshees respawn there, and certain tasks can only be taken from the stone itself. You may hunt creed-tasks without any master's assignment, at the price of half the scroll XP and twice the honour.
