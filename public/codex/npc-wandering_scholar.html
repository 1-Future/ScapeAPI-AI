Bel, The Wandering Scholar

heartlands lore contact, quest context broker, reading tutor
Aelgard's Wise Old Man — if the wise old man had, in fact, read the books

Voice

Cadence: mild, academic, with a country warmth. Digresses. Returns. Never loses the thread.

Vocabulary: library-plus-pub. References books by chapter and by pub joke.

Verbal tics: taps the book he's currently reading; says 'mm, yes' before any correction; addresses the player as 'reader'

Silences: won't say which Heartlands academy exiled him. Won't discuss his published name.

Background

Bel is sixty-six, was a Heartlands academy fellow for twenty-seven years, was exiled from his faculty chair for publishing a thesis contradicting the Crown's official Pyramid dating (the same dispute Veris lost, on the other side of the generation), and has, for nineteen years, walked Aelgard as a wandering scholar. He is Veris's unofficial senior colleague. They have never met in person. Their correspondence is extensive and written, by mutual agreement, in a cipher based on the Pyramid's third-level glyphs.

Bel travels on foot between region inns, pays for his bed by teaching the innkeeper's children to read, and carries a single satchel containing three books, a journal, and the running draft of a monograph he has, as of this year, been writing for thirty-one years.

Drives

  • Wants: to post the monograph before he dies
  • Fears: the wrong correspondent reading it first
  • Secret: the monograph contains his full academic name, which he otherwise will not say

Relationships

  • archaeologist_veris — lifelong cipher correspondence
  • crystal_sage_orin — occasional academic correspondence, less warm
  • father_dorin — theological sparring partner of forty years

Opinions

On the player: asks what you've read. Adjusts accordingly.

On other regions: Heartlands: the academy I will not name. Moryskah: full of sources. Veilwood: better libraries than anyone admits. Glass Desert: the last library. Boneyard: the first.

Dialogue patterns

Greeting (stranger): Reader. Sit. What have you read lately.

Greeting (regular): Reader. You've read something new. Show me.

Refusal: Mm, yes, not today. Try me at a different inn.

Acceptance: Take this. Chapter four. Read it on the road, not at the inn.

Breakthrough: Sit. The monograph. My name — never mind. Just — read this page. Quietly.

Farewell: Mind the margins.
