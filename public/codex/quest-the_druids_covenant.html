The Druid's Covenant

experienced long id: the_druids_covenant
veilwood
The Veilwood, dusk. Silver, the moss has gone. Met, you have, the druids' circle — briefly, in passing, when the Green Thumb led you there. Offered now, a covenant is. Inverted, its grammar. Plain, its demand. Become, they would have you, their kin in all but blood — or leave with what you already have, which is substantial, and which they do not grudge you.

Premise

The Veilwood speaks by preference in a specific grammar — the main verb at the end, the subject shifted to nuance the statement — and the druids, who have lived among the trees long enough to have learned from them rather than the other way round, speak the same way, in the sense that their instructions to outsiders will always require two hearings. The covenant they are offering is not initiation into their order; it is a binding to the woods themselves, such that the woods will know you, help you, and expect you at certain hours.

The covenant requires three works: a staff grafted from seven covenant-trees, each cutting taken with permission from the tree itself; a draught brewed from three patches you planted in The Green Thumb, which by now will have grown; and a ceremony spoken backwards, which is the only way it is heard. You may refuse at any point up to the drinking. The Veilwood will remember either answer.

Steps

[1] To the druids' circle go, at the hour the moss turns silver
Where: Veilwood druid circle
Requires: none
The circle will receive you without preamble. They do not do preamble.
[2] Of the seven covenant-trees, a cutting from each take — permission, first, you must ask
Where: seven scattered trees in the Veilwood
Requires: woodcutting 50, patience
Four will agree without hesitation. Two will take some time. One will require a gift.
[3] Graft the seven cuttings onto a covenant-staff
Where: druid circle workbench
Requires: woodcutting 50, farming 60
The grafts must take overnight. They will not take if you sleep near them. Sleep elsewhere.
[4] From three patches you planted during The Green Thumb, the draught brew
Where: Veilwood herblore table
Requires: herblore 55
The patches will be ready. They have been waiting.
[5] Drink the draught and speak the covenant backwards — the only way it is heard
Where: the druid circle
Requires: a steady voice, a steadier mind
Forwards you will read it twice. Backwards you will speak it once. The druids will not help.
[6] Accept or refuse — either choice, the Veilwood remembers
Where: the circle's centre
Requires: honesty
You may walk out. The staff will remain. The woods will nod.

Twist

The seventh covenant-tree, which required a gift, is not a tree at all. It is a druid, transformed long ago at her own request, who still accepts cuttings because the cuttings keep her remembered. Your gift to her, which will be whatever you brought without knowing you were bringing it, is recorded in the Veilwood's long memory, and will come back to you, in a later quest, as recognition or as debt.

Resolution

You carry the Druid's Covenant Staff, grafted of seven covenant-trees, which heals on hit during daylight within the Veilwood. The covenant is struck. You may walk the druid circle at any hour, and the moonhawk who roosts above the circle will not leave when you approach. A seven-part farming rotation opens to you, unique to Veilwood, with bonus seed drops the other patches do not give.

What it unlocks

The Druid's Covenant Staff is main-hand for druid builds; it heals you on hit during daylight within Veilwood, and it scales to your woodcutting level for bonus damage against forest creatures. The covenant-patches — seven farming spots, one per covenant-tree — yield rare seeds you cannot buy. The Covenant Rest prayer restores full prayer points instantly when you stand on living Veilwood grass, which is a niche but important breakpoint for Veilwood-only training runs.
